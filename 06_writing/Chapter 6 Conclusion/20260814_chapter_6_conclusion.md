# Chapter 6 — Conclusion

# 第 6 章 — 结论

Short-term oil price volatility affects budgeting, hedging and market-risk decisions, yet weekly Brent forecasts remain difficult to improve on a simple no-change rule. Using weekly data from 2019 to 2025, this study examined whether satellite remote sensing and maritime shipping provide predictive information beyond conventional financial indicators. It also tested whether modality-aware representation-level modelling performs better than flat feature fusion. Under a common evaluation framework, each added data source was compared with both a finance-only model and the no-change benchmark.

短期油价波动会影响预算、套期保值和市场风险决策，但周度 Brent 预测仍然很难优于简单的不变预测规则。本研究使用 2019 至 2025 年的周度数据，考察卫星遥感和海上航运能否在传统金融指标之外提供预测信息。同时检验模态感知的表征级建模是否优于扁平特征融合。在统一的评价框架下，每一种新增数据源都分别与仅金融模型和不变预测基准进行比较。

The results show that adding more data or using more complex models does not guarantee better forecasting performance. Remote sensing provides no consistent improvement, either when added alone or together with shipping data. Shipping provides clearer incremental predictive value, although this improvement is mainly observed in the Deep pathway. In the main run, the Deep model using financial and shipping data records a modest improvement over the no-change benchmark, while none of the Flat models outperforms the benchmark. Across the same information sets, the Deep pathway generally performs better than the Flat models. This supports the value of preserving the structure of different data types and modelling them at the representation level. Model interpretation further shows that forecasts continue to rely mainly on financial and EIA information. Shipping makes a smaller contribution, and its importance varies across disruption types, periods and locations in the transport network.

研究结果表明，增加更多数据或使用更复杂的模型并不能保证更好的预测表现。遥感无论单独加入还是与航运一并使用，都没有带来稳定改善。航运提供更明确的增量预测价值，但这种改善主要出现在 Deep 路径中。在主要运行中，使用金融和航运数据的 Deep 模型相对不变预测基准取得了小幅改善，而所有 Flat 模型均未优于该基准。在相同信息集上，Deep 路径总体优于 Flat 模型。这支持了保留不同类型数据结构并在表征层面建模的价值。模型解释进一步表明，预测仍主要依赖金融和 EIA 信息。航运贡献较小，其重要性随扰动类型、时期以及运输网络中的位置而变化。

The main contribution of this study is to provide a framework and set of criteria for assessing when new types of data provide meaningful predictive value. By comparing each new data source with both established financial information and a simple no-change benchmark, the framework distinguishes incremental value from overall forecasting performance. The study also shows that the value of a data source should be assessed according to how well its scale, frequency and structure match the analytical task. Remote sensing may be more suitable for monitoring facility activity or regional production, while shipping data are better suited to capturing disruptions and adjustments across global supply networks. Monitoring value and predictive value should therefore be assessed separately. For applications such as energy-security monitoring, trade planning and inflation-sensitive budgeting, spatial data such as remote sensing and shipping can complement conventional market information by revealing facility activity, transport disruptions, rerouting and changes in supply networks. The value of spatial data therefore extends beyond price forecasting to the monitoring of physical activity and the identification of potential risks. This broader monitoring role connects the study to wider agendas of energy security and oil-market transparency reflected in official oil-market monitoring and initiatives such as JODI. Better observation of chokepoints, rerouting and facility activity can support these agendas even when incorporating such signals does not enable weekly forecasts to outperform a no-change benchmark.


本文的主要贡献是提供一套框架和评价标准，用于判断新类型数据何时能够提供有意义的预测价值。通过将每一种新增数据源同时与既有金融信息和简单的不变预测基准进行比较，该框架能够区分增量价值与整体预测表现。本研究还表明，数据源的价值应根据其尺度、频率和结构与分析任务的匹配程度来评价。遥感可能更适合监测设施活动或区域生产，而航运数据更适合捕捉全球供应网络中的中断和调整。因此，监测价值与预测价值应分别评价。对于能源安全监测、贸易规划和对通胀敏感的预算管理等应用，遥感和航运等空间数据可以通过揭示设施活动、运输中断、航线改道以及供应网络变化，补充传统市场信息。因此，空间数据的价值并不限于价格预测，也体现在实物活动监测和潜在风险识别方面。这种更广泛的监测作用使本研究与能源安全和石油市场透明度等更广泛议程相联系，这些议程也体现在官方石油市场监测和 JODI 等倡议中。即使纳入这些信号无法使周度预测优于不变预测基准，对咽喉、航线改道和设施活动的更好观测仍可服务于这些议程。

Future research can extend this study in three areas: temporal and spatial coverage, forecasting tasks, and practical applications. First, a longer time span and broader geographic coverage would help test whether the value of shipping information remains stable across different periods and regions. Second, the same research framework could be applied to other energy and commodity markets, including WTI crude oil, liquefied natural gas, natural gas, grains and metals. Remote sensing could also be evaluated for tasks that better match its spatial and temporal scale. Finally, future research should further assess the practical value of the framework, including its use in energy-security monitoring, trade planning and budgeting. Evaluating specific policies would require combining the forecasting framework with causal or decision-analysis methods.

未来研究可从三个方面扩展本研究：时间和空间覆盖、预测任务以及实际应用。首先，更长的时间跨度和更广泛的地理覆盖有助于检验航运信息的价值能否在不同时期和地区保持稳定。其次，可以将相同研究框架应用于其他能源和商品市场，包括 WTI 原油、液化天然气、天然气、粮食和金属。遥感也可以用于与其空间和时间尺度更匹配的任务。最后，未来研究应进一步评估该框架的实际价值，包括其在能源安全监测、贸易规划和预算管理中的作用。评价具体政策则需要将预测框架与因果或决策分析方法结合。
