## Research Motivation / 研究动机

### English

Official merchandise-trade statistics are usually released monthly or quarterly and often involve publication delays of several months. These delays limit policymakers' ability to identify current turning points in trade and economic activity.

Automatic Identification System data provide near-real-time information about commercial vessels, including vessel identity, location, speed, vessel type, deadweight tonnage, draught, arrival time, and departure time.

Because a large proportion of international merchandise trade is transported by sea, the paper asks whether AIS-based vessel activity can be transformed into timely indicators of physical trade flows.

The central measurement logic is:

\[
\text{AIS vessel activity}
\rightarrow
\text{physical cargo movement}
\rightarrow
\text{trade-volume indicator}
\]

The paper therefore focuses on nowcasting physical trade activity rather than predicting trade values or financial-market prices.

### 中文

官方商品贸易统计一般按月度或季度发布，而且通常存在数月的公布滞后。这种滞后限制了政策制定者及时识别贸易活动和经济周期转折点的能力。

自动识别系统（AIS）能够近实时地提供商业船舶信息，包括：

- 船舶身份；
- 地理位置；
- 航速和航向；
- 船舶类型；
- 载重吨；
- 吃水深度；
- 到港与离港时间。

由于国际商品贸易中有很大一部分通过海运完成，论文研究的核心问题是：

> 能否将 AIS 船舶活动转化为及时、可靠的实体贸易量指标？

其核心测量逻辑是：

\[
\text{AIS 船舶活动}
\rightarrow
\text{实体货物移动}
\rightarrow
\text{贸易量代理指标}
\]

因此，这篇论文研究的是实体贸易活动的实时监测或临近预测，而不是直接预测贸易价值或金融市场价格。

## Research Question / 研究问题

### English

Can real-time AIS-based port-call data be filtered and aggregated to construct reliable high-frequency indicators of merchandise trade activity before official trade statistics become available?

## Core Method / 核心方法

### English

The paper follows a two-stage methodology:

\[
\text{Raw AIS port calls}
\rightarrow
\text{Filtering of non-trade activities}
\rightarrow
\text{High-frequency indicator construction}
\rightarrow
\text{Comparison with official statistics}
\]

The study does not estimate an OLS model, panel regression, ARIMA model, or machine-learning model.

Its methodology is more accurately described as:

- rule-based data cleaning;
- economic indicator construction;
- descriptive time-series comparison;
- correlation-based validation.

### 中文

论文采用两阶段方法：

\[
\text{原始 AIS 港口记录}
\rightarrow
\text{过滤非贸易活动}
\rightarrow
\text{构造高频指标}
\rightarrow
\text{与官方统计比较}
\]

论文没有估计：

- OLS 回归；
- 面板回归；
- ARIMA 模型；
- 机器学习模型。

更准确的方法概括是：

- 基于领域规则的数据清洗；
- 经济指标构造；
- 描述性时间序列比较；
- 基于相关系数的有效性验证。

## Port-Call Filtering / 港口记录过滤

### English

Raw port calls cannot be directly interpreted as merchandise-trade events. Vessel movements may represent bunkering, anchorage movements, repairs, maintenance, ship-to-ship transfers, or data-recording errors.

The authors apply three filtering rules.

#### Rule 1: Remove anchorage and bunkering activity

The authors remove movements related to:

- bunkering services;
- anchorage activity;
- short movements between ports and nearby anchorages;
- some ship-to-ship transfer activity.

These movements may generate AIS port-call records without representing a new import or export transaction.

A total of 3,861 observations are removed under this rule.

#### Rule 2: Match arrivals with departures

A valid port call should normally contain both an arrival and a corresponding departure.

Records with an arrival but no matching departure are removed because they may indicate incomplete AIS coverage or data errors.

A total of 418 observations are removed under this rule.

#### Rule 3: Restrict port-stay duration

The paper retains vessels staying in port for:

\[
5\text{ hours}\leq\text{dwell time}\leq60\text{ hours}
\]

Vessels staying fewer than five hours may not have had sufficient time to load or unload cargo.

Vessels staying longer than sixty hours may have entered the port for repairs, maintenance, storage, or other non-trade services.

A total of 7,771 observations are removed under this rule.

#### Final sample

The filtering process removes:

\[
12{,}050\text{ port calls}
\]

This represents:

\[
22.8\%
\]

of the original dataset.

The final valid sample contains:

\[
40{,}813\text{ port calls}
\]

### 中文

原始港口靠泊记录不能直接等同于商品贸易事件。船舶移动可能对应：

- 加油服务；
- 锚地等待；
- 维修或维护；
- 船对船转运；
- 港口与锚地之间的短距离移动；
- AIS 数据记录错误。

作者采用了三项主要过滤规则。

#### 规则一：删除锚地与加油活动

作者删除以下活动：

- 船舶加油；
- 锚地活动；
- 港口与附近锚地之间的短距离移动；
- 部分船对船转运活动。

这些移动虽然可能产生 AIS 港口记录，但并不一定代表新的进口或出口交易。

该规则删除了3,861条记录。

#### 规则二：匹配到港与离港记录

正常的港口靠泊通常应当同时存在：

- 到港记录；
- 对应的离港记录。

如果只有到港而没有匹配的离港记录，可能说明 AIS 覆盖不完整或存在数据错误，因此予以删除。

该规则删除了418条记录。

#### 规则三：限制停港时间

论文只保留满足以下条件的船舶：

\[
5\text{小时}\leq\text{停港时间}\leq60\text{小时}
\]

停留少于5小时的船舶可能没有足够时间完成装卸货。

停留超过60小时的船舶可能正在进行维修、维护、储存或其他非贸易活动。

该规则删除了7,771条记录。

#### 最终样本

过滤过程共删除：

\[
12{,}050\text{条港口记录}
\]

占原始数据的：

\[
22.8\%
\]

最终保留：

\[
40{,}813\text{条有效港口记录}
\]

## Constructed Indicators / 构造的指标

### 1. Cargo Number Indicator / 船舶数量指标

#### English

The cargo number indicator counts the number of incoming cargo vessels during each week:

\[
CN_t=\sum_i I(\text{ship }i\text{ arrives in week }t)
\]

The indicator is simple and timely, but it assigns identical weight to every vessel.

Consequently, it does not distinguish between:

- small and large vessels;
- loaded and empty vessels;
- vessels with different cargo capacities.

#### 中文

船舶数量指标统计每周到港货船的数量：

\[
CN_t=\sum_i I(\text{船舶 }i\text{ 在第 }t\text{ 周到港})
\]

该指标简单且及时，但会对所有船舶赋予相同权重。

因此，它无法区分：

- 大型船舶与小型船舶；
- 满载船舶与空载船舶；
- 不同载货能力的船舶。

---

### 2. Cargo Load Indicator / 货运量指标

#### English

The cargo load indicator combines vessel capacity with changes in draught.

A simplified representation is:

\[
CWI_t=
\sum_i
DWT_{i,t}
\times
\frac{
\left|
d^{D}_{i,t}-d^{A}_{i,t}
\right|
}{
\max(d_i)
}
\]

where:

- \(DWT_{i,t}\) is the deadweight tonnage of vessel \(i\);
- \(d^A_{i,t}\) is the draught reported at arrival;
- \(d^D_{i,t}\) is the draught reported at departure;
- \(\max(d_i)\) is the maximum observed draught for that vessel.

The absolute change in draught is used as a proxy for the amount of cargo loaded or unloaded.

The ratio:

\[
\frac{
|d^{D}_{i,t}-d^{A}_{i,t}|
}{
\max(d_i)
}
\]

is interpreted as an approximate capacity-utilisation measure.

The indicator therefore follows the logic:

\[
\text{Vessel capacity}
\times
\text{Estimated load change}
\approx
\text{Cargo loaded or unloaded}
\]

The absolute value is used because the paper aims to measure gross trade activity:

\[
\text{imports}+\text{exports}
\]

rather than net imports or net exports.

#### 中文

货运量指标将船舶载重能力与吃水变化结合起来。

其简化形式为：

\[
CWI_t=
\sum_i
DWT_{i,t}
\times
\frac{
\left|
d^{D}_{i,t}-d^{A}_{i,t}
\right|
}{
\max(d_i)
}
\]

其中：

- \(DWT_{i,t}\) 表示船舶 \(i\) 的载重吨；
- \(d^A_{i,t}\) 表示到港时报告的吃水；
- \(d^D_{i,t}\) 表示离港时报告的吃水；
- \(\max(d_i)\) 表示该船在样本中观察到的最大吃水。

到港与离港吃水的绝对变化被用作装卸货量的代理变量。

比率：

\[
\frac{
|d^{D}_{i,t}-d^{A}_{i,t}|
}{
\max(d_i)
}
\]

可以被理解为近似的船舶容量利用率。

因此，该指标的基本逻辑是：

\[
\text{船舶载重能力}
\times
\text{估计的载货变化}
\approx
\text{装卸货物数量}
\]

作者使用绝对值，是因为论文希望测量总贸易活动：

\[
\text{进口}+\text{出口}
\]

而不是净进口或净出口。

## Temporal Frequency / 时间频率

### English

The two indicators are constructed at weekly frequency over 211 weeks.

This is much more timely than official merchandise-trade statistics, which are generally monthly, quarterly, or annual.

The paper uses a five-term centred moving average to smooth some figures. This smoothing procedure is intended for descriptive visualisation rather than real-time forecasting.

### 中文

两个指标均按周度构造，样本共覆盖211周。

与通常按月度、季度或年度发布的官方贸易统计相比，AIS 指标具有明显的时效性优势。

论文在部分图表中使用五期中心移动平均进行平滑。该方法主要用于历史图形展示，而不是严格的实时预测。

## Evaluation / 评估方法

### English

The paper evaluates the AIS indicators through:

- graphical comparison with official statistics;
- comparison of turning points;
- correlation coefficients;

- rolling out-of-sample forecasts;
- RMSE;
- MAE;
- MAPE;
- directional accuracy;
- Diebold-Mariano tests;
- formal forecast-encompassing tests.

- 计算相关系数；
- 比较贸易伙伴的地理分布。

因此，该研究证明的是 AIS 指标的测量有效性和临近预测潜力，而不是严格的样本外预测优势。

## Key Findings / 主要发现

### Finding 1: AIS vessel counts track official maritime activity  
### 发现一：AIS 船数能够跟踪官方港口活动

#### English

The cargo number indicator follows the general dynamics of official vessel-arrival statistics.

The correlation coefficient between the two series is:

\[
r=0.75
\]

However, the AIS-based vessel count is approximately 20–30% higher than the official count.

Possible explanations include:

- broader AIS coverage;
- repeated visits by the same vessel;
- inclusion of vessels that official statistics exclude;
- incomplete official port statistics;
- remaining transit or non-trade observations.

The correlation of 0.75 refers to AIS vessel counts versus official vessel counts. It is not the correlation between AIS activity and trade volume.

#### 中文

船舶数量指标能够较好地跟踪官方船舶到港统计的总体变化。

两者的相关系数为：

\[
r=0.75
\]

但是，AIS 记录的船舶数量通常比官方统计高约20%–30%。

可能原因包括：

- AIS 覆盖更全面；
- 同一船舶被记录为多次到港；
- AIS 包含了官方统计未纳入的船舶；
- 官方港口统计可能不完整；
- 数据中仍存在部分过境或非贸易活动。

需要强调的是，0.75表示 AIS 船数与官方船数之间的相关性，而不是 AIS 活动与贸易量之间的相关性。

---

### Finding 2: The cargo load indicator tracks trade volume  
### 发现二：货运量指标能够跟踪官方贸易量

#### English

The cargo load indicator broadly follows Malta's official gross trade-volume index.

It captures:

- the substantial increase in trade activity in mid-2016;
- the subsequent deceleration;
- several broad movements in official trade volume.

The reported correlation is:

\[
r=0.65
\]

This result suggests that a DWT- and draught-adjusted AIS indicator can provide a timely proxy for physical trade volume.

However, the result is an in-sample correlation, not an out-of-sample forecasting score.

It should not be interpreted as:

- 65% forecasting accuracy;
- an out-of-sample \(R^2\);
- evidence that AIS always improves forecasts;
- evidence of a causal relationship.

#### 中文

货运量指标能够较好地跟踪马耳他官方总贸易量指数。

该指标捕捉到了：

- 2016年中期贸易活动的大幅上升；
- 随后的明显下降；
- 官方贸易量中的若干总体变化趋势。

论文报告的相关系数为：

\[
r=0.65
\]

这一结果说明，经 DWT 和吃水变化调整后的 AIS 指标可以作为实体贸易量的及时代理变量。

但是，该结果只是样本内相关系数，不是样本外预测得分。

不能将其解释为：

- 65%的预测准确率；
- 样本外 \(R^2\)；
- AIS 一定能够改善预测；
- AIS 活动与贸易量之间存在因果关系。

---

### Finding 3: Last port does not identify cargo origin  
### 发现三：上一个港口不能代表货物来源国

#### English

The trade-partner distribution derived from AIS last-port information differs substantially from the official partner-country distribution in IMF DOTS.

The reasons include:

- container ships visit multiple ports;
- cargo may originate in a country different from the last port;
- goods from landlocked countries pass through foreign ports;
- transshipment hubs distort geographical attribution;
- maritime chokepoints systematically affect observed routes;
- cargo ownership may change during transportation.

Therefore:

\[
\text{Last port}\neq\text{Country of cargo origin}
\]

This problem is especially important for oil because crude oil may be:

- sold while at sea;
- stored temporarily;
- transferred ship-to-ship;
- blended;
- re-exported;
- sold several times before final delivery.

#### 中文

基于 AIS 上一个港口信息得到的贸易伙伴分布，与 IMF DOTS 官方贸易伙伴分布存在明显差异。

原因包括：

- 集装箱船通常停靠多个港口；
- 货物原产国可能与船舶上一个港口不同；
- 内陆国家的商品需要通过其他国家港口运输；
- 中转港会扭曲贸易地理归属；
- 海上咽喉航道会系统性影响观测到的航线；
- 运输过程中货物的经济所有权可能发生变化。

因此：

\[
\text{上一个港口}\neq\text{货物原产国}
\]

这一问题对于石油贸易尤其重要，因为原油可能在运输过程中：

- 在海上被出售；
- 被临时储存；
- 进行船对船转运；
- 被混合处理；
- 被再次出口；
- 在最终交付前被多次转售。

---

### Finding 4: AIS is more suitable for volume than value  
### 发现四：AIS 更适合测量贸易量而非贸易价值

#### English

The paper argues that vessel data are most suitable for measuring:

- trade in goods rather than services;
- physical trade volume rather than nominal trade value;
- gross activity rather than net trade;
- broad commodity groups rather than detailed products.

AIS data may be particularly informative for relatively homogeneous commodities such as oil and natural gas.

### 中文

论文认为，船舶数据更适合测量：

- 商品贸易，而不是服务贸易；
- 实体贸易量，而不是名义贸易价值；
- 总贸易活动，而不是净贸易；
- 宽泛商品类别，而不是高度细分的具体商品。

对于石油和天然气等相对同质化的商品，AIS 数据可能具有更高的解释价值。

## Contribution / 论文贡献

### English

The paper makes four main contributions.

1. It demonstrates how raw AIS port calls can be transformed into economically interpretable indicators.

2. It shows that filtering non-trade vessel movements is central to the validity of AIS-derived economic variables.

3. It introduces a capacity- and draught-adjusted trade-volume indicator rather than relying only on vessel counts.

4. It validates the indicators against official maritime and trade statistics.

The study is therefore primarily a contribution to alternative-data measurement and real-time economic monitoring.

### 中文

论文主要有四项贡献。

1. 展示了如何将原始 AIS 港口记录转化为具有经济含义的指标。

2. 说明过滤非贸易船舶活动是保证 AIS 经济指标有效性的核心步骤。

3. 提出了结合载重能力与吃水变化的贸易量指标，而不是只依赖船舶数量。

4. 使用官方港口和贸易统计验证了 AIS 指标。

因此，这篇论文主要贡献于替代数据测量和实时经济监测，而不是机器学习预测模型设计。

## Important Leakage Warning / 重要的数据泄漏警告

### English

The paper uses a five-term centred moving average in some figures:

\[
CMA_t=
\frac{
x_{t-2}+x_{t-1}+x_t+x_{t+1}+x_{t+2}
}{5}
\]

This procedure uses future observations \(x_{t+1}\) and \(x_{t+2}\).

It is appropriate for retrospective visualisation but cannot be used as an input feature in a real-time forecasting exercise.

The dissertation should instead use trailing transformations:

\[
TMA_t=
\frac{
x_t+x_{t-1}+\cdots+x_{t-k+1}
}{k}
\]

All transformations, scaling, imputation, and feature selection must be fitted using training data only within each rolling or expanding window.

### 中文

论文在部分图表中使用了五期中心移动平均：

\[
CMA_t=
\frac{
x_{t-2}+x_{t-1}+x_t+x_{t+1}+x_{t+2}
}{5}
\]

该方法使用了未来观测值 \(x_{t+1}\) 和 \(x_{t+2}\)。

它适合历史图形展示，但不能作为实时预测模型的输入特征。

你的项目应使用后向滚动变换：

\[
TMA_t=
\frac{
x_t+x_{t-1}+\cdots+x_{t-k+1}
}{k}
\]

所有变换、标准化、缺失值填补和特征选择都必须在每个滚动或扩展窗口内仅使用训练数据完成。

## Limitations / 局限性

### 1. Single-country case study / 单一国家案例

#### English

The empirical analysis is limited to Malta and two major ports. Malta's geographical and economic characteristics may not generalise to large economies or complex multi-port systems.

#### 中文

实证研究仅覆盖马耳他和两个主要港口。马耳他的地理和经济特征未必能够推广到大型经济体或复杂的多港口体系。

---

### 2. Short sample period / 样本时间较短

#### English

The sample covers only four years, from 2015 to 2018. This limits the ability to assess long-run stability and performance across multiple economic regimes.

#### 中文

样本只覆盖2015年至2018年四年，难以检验指标的长期稳定性，也无法充分覆盖多种经济周期和市场状态。

---

### 3. No formal forecasting experiment / 缺乏正式预测实验

#### English

The paper relies mainly on contemporaneous graphical comparisons and correlations. It does not conduct a rolling out-of-sample nowcasting experiment.

#### 中文

论文主要依赖同期图形比较和相关分析，没有进行严格的滚动样本外临近预测实验。

---

### 4. Draught measurement error / 吃水数据存在测量误差

#### English

Draught is manually entered by vessel crews and may be:

- missing;
- inaccurate;
- updated with delay;
- changed only after departure;
- inconsistent across vessels.

The observed maximum draught is also not necessarily the vessel's true designed maximum draught.

#### 中文

吃水信息由船员手工输入，可能存在：

- 缺失；
- 录入错误；
- 更新延迟；
- 离港后才修改；
- 不同船舶之间口径不一致。

样本中观察到的最大吃水也不一定等于船舶设计上的真实最大吃水。

---

### 5. Equal loading and unloading problem / 等量装卸问题

#### English

If a vessel unloads and loads approximately the same weight of cargo during one port call, arrival and departure draught may be similar.

The indicator may then report little or no activity even though substantial trade occurred.

#### 中文

如果一艘船在同一次靠港中卸下和装上大致相同重量的货物，其到港和离港吃水可能接近。

此时，即使发生了大量贸易活动，该指标也可能错误地记录为很少或没有活动。

---

### 6. Gross flows rather than direction / 测量总流量而非方向

#### English

The absolute draught change measures total loading and unloading activity but loses directional information.

This is acceptable for gross trade monitoring but less suitable for distinguishing:

- imports from exports;
- supply from demand;
- inflows from outflows.

#### 中文

吃水变化的绝对值可以衡量总装卸活动，但会丢失方向信息。

这适合监测总贸易活动，却不适合区分：

- 进口和出口；
- 供给和需求；
- 流入和流出。

---

### 7. Last-port attribution problem / 上一个港口归属问题

#### English

The last port cannot reliably identify cargo origin or economic ownership, especially in container shipping and oil trading.

#### 中文

上一个港口无法可靠识别货物原产地或经济所有权，尤其是在集装箱运输和石油贸易中。

---

### 8. AIS coverage and intentional signal loss / AIS 覆盖与主动关闭问题

#### English

AIS coverage can be weak in some regions. Vessels may also switch off or manipulate AIS signals, particularly around sanctioned or politically sensitive trade.

#### 中文

部分地区的 AIS 接收覆盖可能较弱。在制裁贸易或政治敏感活动中，船舶还可能主动关闭或操纵 AIS 信号。

### 9. Trade volume is not oil price / 贸易量不等于油价

#### English

The paper validates AIS as a proxy for physical trade volume. It does not establish a direct relationship between vessel traffic and crude-oil prices.

The dissertation's complete mechanism is longer:

\text{physical oil flow}
\rightarrow
\text{supply-demand balance}
\rightarrow
\text{inventory and expectations}
\rightarrow
\text{Brent price}
\]

Each link may involve:

- nonlinear effects;
- time lags;
- reverse causality;
- expectations;
- market regimes;
- confounding macroeconomic shocks.

#### 中文

论文验证的是 AIS 对实体贸易量的代理能力，并没有建立船舶活动与原油价格之间的直接关系。

你的项目所需检验的完整机制更长：

\[
\text{AIS 油轮活动}
\rightarrow
\text{实体石油流量}
\rightarrow
\text{供需平衡}
\rightarrow
\text{库存与预期}
\rightarrow
\text{Brent 价格}
\]

其中每个环节都可能涉及：

- 非线性关系；
- 时间滞后；
- 反向因果；
- 市场预期；
- 市场状态变化；
- 宏观冲击等混杂因素。

## Final Assessment / 最终评价

### English

This paper is highly relevant to the dissertation, but primarily as a methodological reference for M3 data construction and proxy validation.

Its central lesson is not that AIS data automatically predict oil prices. Its central lesson is that raw vessel movements must be transformed into economically meaningful indicators through:

1. domain-informed filtering;
2. vessel-type classification;
3. capacity weighting;
4. directional interpretation;
5. validation against independent official data.

The dissertation can extend this framework by testing whether these physical-market indicators produce stable and statistically significant improvements in leakage-free, rolling out-of-sample Brent forecasts.

### 中文

这篇论文与项目高度相关，但其主要价值在于为 M3 数据构造和代理变量验证提供方法参考。

论文的核心结论不是“AIS 数据能够自动预测油价”，而是原始船舶移动必须通过以下步骤转化为具有经济含义的指标：

1. 基于航运知识的活动过滤；
2. 船舶类型分类；
3. 容量加权；
4. 方向性解释；
5. 与独立官方数据进行验证。

你的项目可以进一步扩展该框架，检验这些实体市场指标能否在无数据泄漏的滚动样本外 Brent 预测中，产生稳定且具有统计显著性的预测改进。
