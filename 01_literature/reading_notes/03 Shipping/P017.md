## Paper Positioning / 文献定位

### English

This paper is not primarily an oil-price forecasting study. It examines whether the effect of oil-price changes on tanker port-call activity varies across oil-price regimes.

The main relationship tested is:

$$
\text{Oil-price changes}
\rightarrow
\text{Tanker port-call activity}
$$

The paper is particularly relevant to research using AIS or PortWatch data because it:

1. treats tanker port calls as a proxy for physical oil-shipping activity;
2. tests whether the oil–shipping relationship contains threshold effects;
3. distinguishes global activity from activity in Asia, Europe, America and Africa;
4. demonstrates that the relationship is geographically heterogeneous;
5. combines threshold regression with a VAR-based Granger-causality test.

### 中文

这篇论文并不是严格意义上的“油价预测论文”。它研究的是：油价变化对油轮靠港活动的影响，是否会随着油价所处状态的不同而发生结构变化。

论文检验的主要方向是：

$$
\text{油价变化}
\rightarrow
\text{油轮靠港活动}
$$

它对 AIS 和 PortWatch 研究特别重要，因为它：

1. 将油轮靠港次数作为原油实体运输活动的代理变量；
2. 检验油价与航运活动之间是否存在门槛效应；
3. 区分全球、亚洲、欧洲、美洲和非洲的油轮活动；
4. 证明不同地区的油价—航运关系具有明显异质性；
5. 将门槛回归与基于 VAR 的 Granger 因果检验结合起来。

## Research Background / 研究背景

### English

Tankers are a major mode of international crude-oil transportation because they offer:

- relatively low transportation costs;
- large carrying capacity;
- flexible routes;
- lower infrastructure commitment than pipelines;
- the ability to connect geographically dispersed exporters and importers.

The authors argue that oil prices reflect changes in the global supply and demand for crude oil. Changes in oil prices can therefore influence:

- crude-oil import and export decisions;
- refinery and petrochemical purchasing;
- shipping-company deployment decisions;
- the number and frequency of port visits;
- regional oil-trade routes;
- tanker demand and port congestion.

However, the response of tanker activity may not be constant at all oil-price levels. For example, a price increase in a low-price environment may have a different meaning from an increase during an already high-price period.

This motivates the use of threshold regression rather than a model imposing one constant linear coefficient.

### 中文

油轮是国际原油运输的主要方式之一，原因包括：

- 运输成本相对较低；
- 运载能力较大；
- 航线灵活；
- 与管道相比，不需要固定的大规模基础设施投入；
- 能够连接分布在世界各地的原油出口国和进口国。

作者认为，油价反映了全球原油供需状况。油价变化可能进一步影响：

- 原油进口和出口决策；
- 炼厂及石化企业的采购行为；
- 航运公司的船舶调度；
- 船舶进入港口的次数和频率；
- 地区间的原油贸易路线；
- 油轮需求和港口拥堵。

但是，油轮活动对油价的反应未必在所有价格水平下保持不变。例如，低油价阶段的一次价格上涨，与油价已经很高时的进一步上涨，可能代表完全不同的市场环境。

因此，作者没有只估计一个固定的线性系数，而是采用门槛回归识别不同油价状态下的关系。

## Research Questions / 研究问题

### English

The paper addresses four main questions:

1. Does the effect of oil-price changes on tanker port-call activity vary across oil-price regimes?
2. Is there an endogenously identified oil-price threshold at which the relationship changes?
3. Are the threshold effects different across continents?
4. Do oil-price changes and economic policy uncertainty Granger-cause changes in global tanker port calls?

论文主要回答四个问题：

## Conceptual Mechanism / 理论机制

### English

The broad mechanism proposed by the authors is:

$$
\text{Oil-market supply and demand}
\rightarrow
\text{Oil price}
\rightarrow
\text{Trade and shipping decisions}
\rightarrow
\text{Tanker port calls}
$$

Oil prices can influence tanker activity through several possible channels.

### Demand channel

A change in oil prices may reflect or produce changes in refinery demand, industrial activity and crude-oil imports.

### Supply and trade channel

Oil-price conditions may change production, exports and the geographical allocation of oil trade.

### Shipping-cost and profitability channel

Oil prices can affect voyage costs, shipping-company profitability and fleet-deployment decisions.

### Regime channel

The economic meaning of an oil-price increase may differ between low-price and high-price markets. A uniform linear coefficient may therefore hide changes in sign, magnitude or significance.

### Regional heterogeneity channel

Different regions have different roles in the oil supply chain:

- exporters versus importers;
- refining centres versus production regions;
- mature markets versus rapidly growing markets;
- regions with relatively rigid demand versus more price-sensitive demand.

The oil-price coefficient may therefore differ substantially across regions.

### 中文

作者提出的总体机制可以表示为：

$$
\text{石油市场供需}
\rightarrow
\text{油价}
\rightarrow
\text{贸易与运输决策}
\rightarrow
\text{油轮靠港活动}
$$

油价可以通过以下渠道影响油轮活动。

### 需求渠道

油价变化可能反映或引发炼厂需求、工业活动和原油进口量的变化。

### 供给与贸易渠道

油价环境可能影响原油生产、出口以及全球石油贸易的地理分配。

### 航运成本与利润渠道

油价可能影响航程成本、航运公司的利润以及船队调度决策。

### 市场状态渠道

低油价阶段的油价上涨与高油价阶段的上涨，其经济含义可能不同。一个统一的线性系数可能掩盖系数符号、大小和显著性的改变。

### 地区异质性渠道

不同地区在石油供应链中的角色不同：

- 出口地区与进口地区；
- 炼油中心与生产地区；
- 成熟市场与高速增长市场；
- 需求相对刚性的地区与价格敏感地区。

因此，油价系数可能在不同地区之间存在显著差异。

## Data Transformation and Preliminary Tests / 数据处理与前期检验

### Logarithmic transformation / 对数转换

The authors report using logarithmic transformations because they:

- reduce scale differences between variables;
- may reduce heteroskedasticity;
- allow some coefficients to be interpreted as elasticities;
- reduce the influence of extreme values.

作者对主要变量进行对数处理，理由包括：

- 缩小不同变量之间的量纲差异；
- 缓解潜在异方差；
- 使部分系数具有弹性解释；
- 降低极端值的影响。

### Stationarity / 平稳性

The authors conduct stationarity tests before regression.

They report that global port-call numbers from 2010 to 2019 are stationary when a trend is included, but the COVID-19 outbreak changes the time-series properties.

作者在回归之前进行了平稳性检验。

论文指出，2010—2019年的全球靠港次数在包含趋势项后表现为平稳，但 COVID-19 冲击改变了序列特征。

### Multicollinearity / 多重共线性

Variance inflation factors are used to diagnose multicollinearity.

Reported VIF values include approximately:

- $\Delta \ln RealPrice$: 1.821
- $\ln EPU$: 1.554

The authors conclude that there is no serious multicollinearity because the values are well below 10.

作者使用方差膨胀因子检查多重共线性：

- $\Delta \ln RealPrice$：约1.821
- $\ln EPU$：约1.554

由于数值明显低于10，作者认为不存在严重的多重共线性。

## Core Method / 核心方法

### Threshold regression / 门槛回归

The main model can be represented as:

$$
Activity_t =
\begin{cases}
\delta_{01}
+\delta_{11}\Delta\ln RealPrice_t
+\delta_{21}\ln EPU_t
+\delta_3D_t
+\delta_4Trend_t
+\varepsilon_t,
& \ln RealPrice_t \leq \gamma \\[6pt]
\delta_{02}
+\delta_{12}\Delta\ln RealPrice_t
+\delta_{22}\ln EPU_t
+\delta_3D_t
+\delta_4Trend_t
+\varepsilon_t,
& \ln RealPrice_t > \gamma
\end{cases}
$$

where:

- $Activity_t$ is monthly tanker port-call activity;
- $\Delta\ln RealPrice_t$ is the change in the log real oil price;
- $\ln EPU_t$ represents economic policy uncertainty;
- $D_t$ is the COVID-19 dummy;
- $Trend_t$ is a time trend where included;
- $\gamma$ is the estimated oil-price threshold;
- $\varepsilon_t$ is the error term.

The model:

1. searches for the threshold that minimises the residual sum of squares;
2. divides observations into low-price and high-price regimes;
3. estimates coefficients using least squares within the regimes;
4. tests whether the coefficients differ across regimes.

Threshold regression can therefore be understood as a data-driven piecewise linear model.

### 中文

论文的主要门槛回归模型可以表示为：

$$
Activity_t =
\begin{cases}
\delta_{01}
+\delta_{11}\Delta\ln RealPrice_t
+\delta_{21}\ln EPU_t
+\delta_3D_t
+\delta_4Trend_t
+\varepsilon_t,
& \ln RealPrice_t \leq \gamma \\[6pt]
\delta_{02}
+\delta_{12}\Delta\ln RealPrice_t
+\delta_{22}\ln EPU_t
+\delta_3D_t
+\delta_4Trend_t
+\varepsilon_t,
& \ln RealPrice_t > \gamma
\end{cases}
$$

其中：

- $Activity_t$：月度油轮靠港活动；
- $\Delta\ln RealPrice_t$：实际油价的对数变化；
- $\ln EPU_t$：经济政策不确定性；
- $D_t$：COVID-19 虚拟变量；
- $Trend_t$：在部分模型中加入的时间趋势；
- $\gamma$：由数据估计的油价门槛；
- $\varepsilon_t$：随机误差项。

门槛回归的基本过程是：

1. 寻找使残差平方和最小的门槛值；
2. 将样本划分为低油价和高油价两个区间；
3. 分别使用最小二乘法估计两个区间的系数；
4. 检验不同区间的系数是否存在显著差异。

因此，门槛回归本质上可以理解为一种由数据确定断点的分段线性模型。

## VAR and Granger-Causality Test / VAR 与 Granger 因果检验

The authors additionally estimate a three-variable VAR containing:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
GlobalPortCalls_t \\
\Delta\ln RealPrice_t \\
\ln EPU_t
\end{bmatrix}
$$

The general VAR specification is:

$$
Y_t =
\alpha_0+
\beta_1Y_{t-1}
+\cdots+
\beta_kY_{t-k}
+u_t
$$

The lag length is selected as:

$$
k=3
$$

based on the LR, FPE and AIC criteria.

The paper then applies what it describes as a time-varying biased Granger-causality test.

### 中文

作者进一步建立了一个包含三个变量的 VAR 模型：

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
GlobalPortCalls_t \\
\Delta\ln RealPrice_t \\
\ln EPU_t
\end{bmatrix}
$$

一般形式为：

$$
Y_t =
\alpha_0+
\beta_1Y_{t-1}
+\cdots+
\beta_kY_{t-k}
+u_t
$$

作者根据 LR、FPE 和 AIC 准则选择：

$$
k=3
$$

即三个滞后期。

随后，论文进行了其所称的 time-varying biased Granger-causality test。

## Empirical Results / 实证结果

## 1. Global port calls / 全球靠港次数

The estimated global threshold is:

$$
\gamma=84.52
$$

| Variable / 变量 | Low-price regime / 低油价区间 | High-price regime / 高油价区间 |
|---|---:|---:|
| $\Delta\ln RealPrice$ | $-0.1438^{***}$ | $0.1036$ |
| $\ln EPU$ | $0.0105$ | $0.1358^{***}$ |
| Constant / 常数项 | $7.1036^{***}$ | $6.5051^{***}$ |

Additional estimates:

- Trend: $0.0035^{***}$
- COVID-19 dummy: $-0.2449^{***}$
- Adjusted $R^2$: 0.8594
- Durbin–Watson statistic: 1.7608

### English interpretation

When the real oil price is below the threshold, an increase in the real oil price is significantly associated with fewer global tanker port calls.

Above the threshold, the oil-price coefficient becomes positive, but it is not statistically significant at conventional levels.

It is therefore too strong to claim that oil-price increases definitely raise port calls above the threshold. The safer conclusion is that the sign and magnitude of the relationship change across regimes.

EPU has little effect in the low-price regime but a significant positive coefficient in the high-price regime.

The COVID-19 dummy is strongly negative, indicating a substantial decline in global tanker activity during the pandemic period.

### 中文解释

当实际油价低于84.52的门槛时，油价上涨与全球油轮靠港次数下降显著相关。

当实际油价高于门槛时，油价系数由负转正，但在常规显著性水平下并不显著。

因此，不能直接说“油价超过门槛后，油价上涨一定增加靠港次数”。更稳妥的结论是：两个油价区间中的系数方向和大小发生了变化。

EPU 在低油价区间影响不明显，但在高油价区间呈显著正向关系。

COVID-19 虚拟变量显著为负，说明疫情期间全球油轮活动出现大幅下降。

### Asia / 亚洲

#### English

No statistically significant threshold is identified for Asia. The oil-price coefficient is relatively small and negative.

The authors argue that major oil importers and petrochemical producers are located in Asia. Relatively rigid industrial demand may reduce the likelihood of an abrupt change in port-call behaviour at a single oil-price threshold.

#### 中文

亚洲没有发现显著门槛。油价系数较小并呈负向关系。

作者认为，亚洲拥有多个主要原油进口国和大型石化产业。相对刚性的工业需求可能使亚洲靠港活动不会在某一个油价水平上突然发生结构变化。

### Europe / 欧洲

#### English

The estimated European threshold is 115.29.

Below the threshold, the oil-price coefficient is negative but insignificant. Above the threshold, the coefficient becomes positive and is weakly significant at the 10% level.

Europe therefore provides the clearest evidence of a regime-related sign reversal.

Possible interpretations include:

- high prices coinciding with strong demand;
- changes in sourcing regions;
- increased trade-route adjustments;
- more frequent or smaller shipments;
- changes in refinery procurement.

- 运输批次变小但频率增加；

#### English

## Granger-Causality Results / Granger 因果检验结果

The reported results for the global-port-call equation are:

| Excluded variable / 被排除变量 | F-statistic | p-value |
|---|---:|---:|
| $\Delta\ln RealPrice$ | 4.3595 | 0.0059 |
| $\ln EPU$ | 2.4264 | 0.0687 |
| All variables jointly / 联合检验 | 2.9351 | 0.0103 |

### English interpretation

Past values of real-oil-price changes contain statistically significant information for global tanker port calls.

EPU is significant only at approximately the 10% level.

The tested direction is:

$$
\text{Past oil-price changes}
\rightarrow
\text{Future global port calls}
$$

This is not evidence that tanker port calls predict future oil prices.

Granger causality means incremental time-series predictability conditional on the model. It does not establish structural or economic causality.

### 中文解释

实际油价变化的过去值，对全球油轮靠港次数具有显著的增量解释信息。

EPU 只在约10%的显著性水平上成立。

论文检验并支持的方向是：

$$
\text{过去的油价变化}
\rightarrow
\text{未来的全球靠港次数}
$$

这一结果并不能证明油轮靠港次数能够预测未来油价。

Granger 因果表示在给定模型条件下的时间序列增量预测关系，并不等同于结构性因果或严格的经济因果。

## Key Findings / 主要发现

### English

1. The effect of oil-price changes on tanker port-call activity is not adequately represented by a single global linear coefficient.

2. A global oil-price threshold of 84.52 is identified.

3. Below the global threshold, higher real oil prices are significantly associated with fewer tanker port calls.

4. Above the global threshold, the oil-price coefficient becomes positive but is statistically insignificant.

5. Threshold effects are geographically heterogeneous:
   - thresholds are identified globally, in Europe and in America;
   - no threshold is identified in Asia or Africa.

6. Europe shows the clearest sign reversal across oil-price regimes.

7. In America, the coefficients remain negative on both sides of the threshold and differ only moderately.

8. Economic policy uncertainty is more strongly associated with tanker activity in high-price regimes.

9. COVID-19 has a large negative association with tanker port-call activity.

10. Oil-price changes Granger-cause global tanker port calls in the paper’s VAR framework.

## 8. Avoid look-ahead bias / 避免前视偏差

### English

For every AIS or PortWatch series:

1. identify when the observation becomes available;
2. align it to the forecasting timestamp;
3. do not backfill revised data into earlier dates;
4. forward-fill low-frequency data only after its release date;
5. estimate thresholds and transformations using training data only;
6. fit scalers and imputers within each training fold;
7. never use future observations to define regimes.

### 中文

## Literature-Review Paragraph / 文献综述段落

### English version

Mi et al. (2023) investigate the relationship between crude-oil prices and tanker shipping activity using monthly AIS data from January 2010 to June 2021. Tanker activity is measured by port-call frequencies at the global level and across Asia, Europe, America and Africa. Using a threshold-regression framework, the authors find that the effect of changes in the real Brent oil price varies across oil-price regimes and geographical regions. A threshold is identified for global, European and American port calls, whereas no significant threshold is found for Asia or Africa. The results suggest that a single linear coefficient may conceal regime-dependent and spatially heterogeneous relationships between oil-market conditions and tanker activity. The study therefore provides an important justification for incorporating tanker-specific and regionally disaggregated AIS indicators into oil-market models. However, its primary direction of analysis is from oil prices to shipping activity, and it does not establish that port calls predict future oil prices. The present dissertation extends this literature by testing whether strictly lagged AIS and PortWatch indicators provide incremental out-of-sample information for forecasting future Brent returns.

### 中文版本

Mi et al.（2023）使用2010年1月至2021年6月的月度 AIS 数据，研究原油价格与油轮运输活动之间的关系。论文以全球及亚洲、欧洲、美洲和非洲的油轮靠港频率衡量航运活动，并采用门槛回归检验实际 Brent 油价变化的影响是否随油价状态和地理区域发生改变。研究在全球、欧洲和美洲样本中识别出油价门槛，但在亚洲和非洲没有发现显著门槛。结果说明，单一线性系数可能掩盖石油市场状态与油轮活动之间依赖市场状态且具有空间异质性的关系。因此，该研究为在石油市场模型中引入 tanker-specific 和地区拆分的 AIS 指标提供了重要依据。然而，论文的主要分析方向是油价影响航运活动，并未证明靠港次数能够预测未来油价。本研究在此基础上进一步检验：经过严格滞后处理的 AIS 和 PortWatch 指标，能否为未来 Brent 收益提供增量样本外预测信息。
