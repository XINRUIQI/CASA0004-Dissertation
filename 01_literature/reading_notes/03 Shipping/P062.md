## Research Objective / 研究目标

### English

This paper develops a system for forecasting weekly crude-oil maritime traffic flows at major importing, exporting, and trading regions.

The study addresses two limitations of conventional maritime traffic forecasting:

1. Geofence-based vessel counts may include vessels that only pass through, refuel, resupply, wait, or anchor near a port, rather than completing crude-oil loading or unloading operations.
2. Single-port time-series models ignore the spatial and economic connections among regions in the global crude-oil transportation network.

The authors therefore combine:

- AIS vessel trajectories;
- crude-oil berth locations;
- a domain-informed maritime transportation graph;
- Graph Convolutional Networks;
- Long Short-Term Memory networks.

### 中文

本文构建了一套用于预测主要原油进口区、出口区和贸易中转区周度海运流量的系统。

文章主要解决传统海运流量预测中的两个问题：

1. 基于地理围栏的船舶计数，可能把只是经过、加油、补给、等待或锚泊的船舶，也错误地视为港口原油装卸活动。
2. 单港口时间序列模型忽略了全球原油运输网络中不同国家和地区之间的空间联系与经济联系。

因此，作者综合使用：

- AIS 船舶轨迹；
- 原油泊位位置；
- 融合原油供需知识的运输网络；
- 图卷积神经网络；
- 长短期记忆网络。

                            ↓

                            ↓

                            ↓

                            ↓

                    ↓

                    ↓

                    ↓

                    ↓
    GCN 提取空间与网络关系

## Target Variable / 预测目标

### English

The target is not the crude-oil price.

The model predicts:

> The number of crude-oil vessels completing loading or unloading operations at each country or regional node in the next week.

At week \(t\), the traffic-flow state of all nodes is represented as:

\[
X_t=(x_{t,1},x_{t,2},\ldots,x_{t,V})
\]

where \(x_{t,i}\) is the number of crude-oil vessels completing operations at node \(i\) during week \(t\).

The forecasting task is:

\[
[X_{t-T},\ldots,X_t]
\rightarrow
\hat{X}_{t+1}
\]

### 中文

本文预测的并不是 Brent 或 WTI 原油价格。

模型的预测目标是：

> 每个国家或区域节点在下一周完成装油或卸油作业的原油船舶数量。

在第 \(t\) 周，全部节点的交通流状态表示为：

\[
X_t=(x_{t,1},x_{t,2},\ldots,x_{t,V})
\]

其中，\(x_{t,i}\) 表示节点 \(i\) 在第 \(t\) 周完成装卸作业的原油船舶数量。

预测任务可以写为：

\[
[X_{t-T},\ldots,X_t]
\rightarrow
\hat{X}_{t+1}
\]

## Feature Construction / 特征构建

### 1. Identification of Berthing Operations / 靠泊作业识别

### English

The paper does not treat every vessel entering a port area as a completed crude-oil operation.

For each AIS observation, the authors first select low-speed vessel records:

\[
SOG < 0.5
\]

The distance between the vessel and each crude-oil berth is then calculated.

A vessel record is treated as a possible berthing point when:

\[
distance_{ship,berth}<d
\]

where \(d\) is a distance threshold.

If one vessel position is located close to more than one berth, it is assigned to the nearest berth:

\[
i^*=\arg\min_i distance_{t,i}
\]

The authors then group consecutive AIS records into individual berthing operations according to temporal-gap thresholds.

For each identified operation:

- the earliest timestamp is treated as the arrival time;
- the latest timestamp is treated as the departure time;
- the complete group of records is treated as one loading or unloading operation.

### 中文

本文并没有把所有进入港口区域的船舶都视为完成了原油装卸作业。

对于每条 AIS 记录，作者首先筛选低速船舶轨迹：

\[
SOG < 0.5
\]

随后计算船舶位置与每个原油泊位之间的距离。

当以下条件成立时，该轨迹点被视为潜在靠泊点：

\[
distance_{ship,berth}<d
\]

其中，\(d\) 是预先设定的距离阈值。

如果同一船舶位置同时靠近多个泊位，则将其分配给最近的泊位：

\[
i^*=\arg\min_i distance_{t,i}
\]

作者再根据相邻 AIS 记录之间的时间间隔，将连续记录划分为不同的靠泊作业。

对于每一次识别出的作业：

- 最早的时间被定义为到港时间；
- 最晚的时间被定义为离港时间；
- 完整的连续记录被视为一次装油或卸油作业。

### 2. Weekly Aggregation / 周频聚合

### English

Daily crude-oil vessel activity is highly sparse because many countries and regions do not receive crude-oil vessels every day.

The authors therefore aggregate vessel operations to the weekly level.

Weekly aggregation helps to:

- reduce zero inflation;
- reduce the sparsity of the node-feature matrix;
- lower the size of the input data;
- generate more stable physical-activity indicators.

### 中文

原油船舶活动在日频尺度上非常稀疏，因为许多国家或地区并不是每天都有原油船靠泊。

因此，作者将船舶作业汇总为周频数据。

- 缓解节点特征矩阵的稀疏性；
- 降低输入数据规模；
- 得到更加稳定的物理活动指标。

## Model Architecture / 模型结构

### 1. Graph Convolutional Network / 图卷积网络

### English

At each time step, the transportation relationships among regions are represented by an adjacency matrix:

\[
A_{ij}
=
\begin{cases}
1, & \text{if nodes }i\text{ and }j\text{ are connected}\\
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\]

Self-loops are added so that each node retains its own information during graph propagation.

A graph-convolution layer is expressed as:

\[
H_t^{(l)}
=
\sigma
\left(
\tilde{D}^{-1/2}
\tilde{A}
\tilde{D}^{-1/2}
H_t^{(l-1)}
W^{(l-1)}
\right)
\]

The paper uses a two-layer GCN.

The GCN allows each node to combine:

- its own weekly vessel flow;
- information from connected exporting regions;
- information from connected importing regions;
- information from important trading and transit nodes.

### 中文

在每个时间点，不同区域之间的运输关系由邻接矩阵表示：

\[
A_{ij}
=
\begin{cases}
1, & \text{节点 }i\text{ 和 }j\text{ 相连}\\
0, & \text{否则}
\end{cases}
\]

作者加入自连接，使每个节点在图传播过程中能够保留自身信息。

图卷积层可以表示为：

\[
H_t^{(l)}
=
\sigma
\left(
\tilde{D}^{-1/2}
\tilde{A}
\tilde{D}^{-1/2}
H_t^{(l-1)}
W^{(l-1)}
\right)
\]

文章使用两层 GCN。

GCN 使每个节点能够同时融合：

- 节点自身的周度船舶流量；
- 相连出口区域的信息；
- 相连进口区域的信息；
- 重要贸易与中转节点的信息。

---

### 2. Long Short-Term Memory Network / 长短期记忆网络

### English

After graph convolution is applied at every weekly time step, the graph-enhanced representations are reorganised into node-specific temporal sequences:

\[
[h_{i,t-T},\ldots,h_{i,t}]
\]

These sequences are then passed into an LSTM.

The two components have different functions:

- **GCN** models spatial and network relationships among regions;
- **LSTM** models temporal dependence and persistence in regional vessel flows.

The complete logic is therefore:

\[
\text{Network structure}
+
\text{Historical evolution}
\rightarrow
\text{Next-week vessel flow}
\]

### 中文

在每个周度时间点完成图卷积之后，经过图增强的节点表示被重新排列为节点层面的时间序列：

\[
[h_{i,t-T},\ldots,h_{i,t}]
\]

随后，这些时间序列被输入 LSTM。

两个模块具有不同作用：

- **GCN** 用于建模区域之间的空间关系和运输网络关系；
- **LSTM** 用于建模区域船舶流量的时间依赖和持续性。

完整逻辑可以概括为：

\[
\text{运输网络结构}
+
\text{历史时间变化}
\rightarrow
\text{下一周船舶流量}
\]

## Evaluation / 评估方法

### Test Nodes / 测试节点

The forecasting experiment focuses on four major nodes:

- China;
- Russia;
- Middle East;
- America.

预测实验主要评估四个节点：

- 中国；
- 俄罗斯；
- 中东；
- 美洲或美国节点。

### Evaluation Metrics / 评估指标

- Root Mean Squared Error:

\[
RMSE
=
\sqrt{
\frac{1}{n}
\sum_{t=1}^{n}
(y_t-\hat{y}_t)^2
}
\]

- Mean Absolute Percentage Error:

\[
MAPE
=
\frac{100}{n}
\sum_{t=1}^{n}
\left|
\frac{y_t-\hat{y}_t}{y_t}
\right|
\]

The reported results are based on ten experimental runs.

论文使用以下两个指标：

- 均方根误差 RMSE；
- 平均绝对百分比误差 MAPE。

模型比较结果基于十次实验运行。

## Key Findings / 核心发现

### 1. Joint Spatiotemporal Modelling Improves Forecasting  
### 1. 联合建模时空关系能够改善预测表现

### English

L-GCN achieves the lowest RMSE and MAPE for China, Russia, and the Middle East.

This result suggests that regional crude-oil traffic flows are influenced by both:

- their own historical evolution;
- the states of connected importing, exporting, and trading regions.

GCN and LSTM therefore capture complementary information.

### 中文

L-GCN 在中国、俄罗斯和中东节点上均取得最低的 RMSE 和 MAPE。

这一结果表明，区域原油船舶流量同时受到以下因素影响：

- 节点自身的历史变化；
- 与其相连的进口区、出口区和贸易区的状态。

因此，GCN 与 LSTM 提取的是互补信息。

---

### 2. Spatial Information Is Not Equally Valuable for Every Node  
### 2. 空间网络信息并非对所有节点都同样重要

### English

For the American node, LSTM and L-GCN produce the same forecasting performance:

\[
RMSE=5.89,\qquad MAPE=6.53\%
\]

The authors explain that American weekly crude-oil traffic flow is comparatively stable.

When a node can already be predicted accurately from its own temporal history, adding graph information may provide limited marginal improvement.

### 中文

对于美国节点，LSTM 与 L-GCN 的预测结果完全相同：

\[
RMSE=5.89,\qquad MAPE=6.53\%
\]

作者认为，这是因为美国周度原油船舶流量相对稳定。

当某个节点仅依赖自身历史序列便能得到较好预测时，加入图网络信息可能不会产生明显的边际改善。

---

### 3. Berth-Based Counts Are More Meaningful Than Geofence Counts  
### 3. 基于泊位的作业计数比地理围栏计数更有经济意义

### English

The paper compares geofence-based counts with berth-based operation counts in Singapore.

For VLCC vessels during the selected weeks:

- geofence counts were approximately 42–48 vessels per week;
- berth-operation counts were approximately 2–5 vessels per week.

The difference is substantial because many vessels near Singapore may only:

- transit through the area;
- refuel;
- resupply;
- wait;
- anchor without completing crude-oil loading or unloading.

Using geofence counts would substantially exaggerate Singapore's node value.

Because node information is propagated through the graph, this measurement error would also affect forecasts for connected nodes such as China.

### 中文

文章比较了新加坡的地理围栏计数与泊位作业计数。

在论文选取的几周中，VLCC 船舶数量大约为：

- 地理围栏计数：每周 42–48 艘；
- 泊位作业计数：每周 2–5 艘。

二者差异很大，因为许多出现在新加坡附近的船舶可能只是：

- 通过该区域；
- 加油；
- 补给；
- 等待；
- 锚泊但没有进行原油装卸。

使用地理围栏计数会显著高估新加坡节点的流量。

由于图模型会把节点信息传播到相邻节点，这种测量误差还会影响中国等相关节点的预测。

---

### 4. Correct Node Classification Improves Network Representation  
### 4. 正确划分节点贸易角色能够改善网络表示

### English

The United States and the United Kingdom cannot be accurately represented as purely importing or purely exporting nodes.

The paper therefore introduces an oil-balance category.

Incorrectly treating a balance node as an import-dependent node changes the graph structure and the information propagated to neighbouring nodes.

The experiments show that this increases forecast error.

### 中文

美国和英国不能被准确地描述为纯进口节点或纯出口节点。

因此，文章设置了原油进出口平衡节点。

如果错误地把一个平衡节点视为进口依赖节点，就会改变图结构以及向相邻节点传播的信息。

实验结果表明，这种错误分类会增加预测误差。

---

### 5. XGBoost Performs Relatively Poorly in This Specific Experiment  
### 5. XGBoost 在本文特定实验中的表现相对较弱

### English

XGBoost performs worse than L-GCN for all four evaluated nodes.

The authors argue that the input variables are highly homogeneous and mainly contain:

- time index;
- vessel count;
- country or node identity.

This feature structure offers fewer heterogeneous interactions for tree-based models to exploit.

However, this result should not be generalised to crude-oil price forecasting with heterogeneous financial, macroeconomic, remote-sensing, and shipping variables.

### 中文

XGBoost 在四个测试节点上的表现均弱于 L-GCN。

作者认为，主要原因是本文输入变量高度同质，主要包括：

- 时间索引；
- 船舶数量；
- 国家或节点身份。

这种特征结构没有为树模型提供丰富的异质变量交互。

但是，这一结果不能直接推广到同时包含金融、宏观、遥感和航运变量的原油价格预测任务。

## Main Contributions / 主要贡献

### English

1. **Operation-based measurement of crude-oil shipping activity**

   The study measures vessels that actually complete berthing, loading, or unloading operations instead of counting all vessels present within a geographic area.

2. **Domain-informed maritime network construction**

   The graph incorporates crude-oil supply and demand, import and export roles, shipping routes, and special trading nodes.

3. **Joint spatial and temporal forecasting**

   The proposed L-GCN combines graph convolution with LSTM-based temporal modelling.

4. **Evidence that spatial and temporal signals are complementary**

   Comparisons among LSTM, GCN, and L-GCN show that temporal and network information contribute differently to forecasting performance.

### 中文

1. **基于真实作业测量原油航运活动**

   文章识别真正完成靠泊、装油或卸油的船舶，而不是统计出现在某一地理区域内的所有船舶。

2. **融合领域知识构建原油海运网络**

   图结构同时考虑原油供需、进口和出口角色、船舶航线以及特殊贸易节点。

## Limitations / 局限性

### 1. The Target Is Shipping Flow Rather Than Oil Price  
### 1. 预测目标是船舶流量而不是原油价格

The paper demonstrates that network information improves crude-oil vessel-flow forecasting.

It does not directly demonstrate that shipping graphs improve Brent or WTI price forecasts.

The following transmission mechanism still needs to be tested:

\[
Shipping\ activity
\rightarrow
Physical\ supply/demand
\rightarrow
Inventory\ expectations
\rightarrow
Crude\ oil\ price
\]

本文证明的是，网络信息可以改善原油船舶流量预测。

它没有直接证明航运图能够改善 Brent 或 WTI 价格预测。

仍需检验以下传导机制：

\[
航运活动
\rightarrow
实际供需
\rightarrow
库存预期
\rightarrow
原油价格
\]

---

### 2. Short and Unusual Sample Period  
### 2. 样本期较短且具有特殊性

The sample covers only January 2020 to July 2021.

This period is heavily affected by the COVID-19 pandemic and contains a relatively small number of weekly observations.

Potential risks include:

- overfitting;
- weak external validity;
- limited regime coverage;
- excessive dependence on pandemic-era shipping patterns.

样本仅覆盖 2020 年 1 月至 2021 年 7 月。

这一时期受到 COVID-19 疫情的显著影响，周频观测数量也相对有限。

潜在问题包括：

- 过拟合；
- 外部有效性较弱；
- 覆盖的市场状态有限；
- 对疫情时期航运结构过度依赖。

---

### 3. Static Graph Structure  
### 3. 使用静态图结构

The model uses a fixed adjacency matrix:

\[
A_t=A
\]

However, crude-oil transportation networks change over time because of:

- sanctions;
- wars;
- supply cuts;
- trade redirection;
- port disruptions;
- new transportation routes.

A more realistic model would allow:

\[
A_t\neq A_{t+1}
\]

模型使用固定邻接矩阵：

\[
A_t=A
\]

但现实中的原油运输网络会因为以下因素不断变化：

- 制裁；
- 战争；
- 减产；
- 贸易转移；
- 港口中断；
- 新运输路线形成。

更合理的模型应允许：

\[
A_t\neq A_{t+1}
\]

### 4. 节点特征较为单一

- dwell time;
- congestion;
- sailing speed;

节点的核心特征主要是完成作业的船舶数量。

模型没有充分加入：

### 5. Coarse Regional Aggregation  
### 5. 区域聚合较粗

Thousands of berths and ports are reduced to only 11 regional nodes.

This reduces computational complexity but removes important local heterogeneity.

For example:

- different Middle Eastern terminals serve different routes;
- different Chinese ports connect to different refinery systems;
- Hormuz, Suez, and Malacca have different positions in the crude-oil supply chain.

大量泊位和港口最终被压缩为 11 个区域节点。

这种方法降低了计算复杂度，但也损失了重要的局部异质性。

### 6. No Probabilistic Forecasting  
### 6. 缺少概率预测

The study evaluates point forecasts using RMSE and MAPE but does not report:

### 7. 没有油价预测中的强基准

## Final Assessment / 最终评价

### English

This paper is an important methodological reference for the shipping component of the dissertation because it establishes three principles:

1. shipping indicators should represent meaningful physical operations rather than generic vessel presence;
2. crude-oil maritime activity should be represented as an interconnected global supply-chain network;
3. temporal and network information can provide complementary forecasting signals.

Its main value is therefore to support:

- M3 shipping-feature construction;
- weekly aggregation;
- the Shipping Encoder;
- graph-based ablation experiments;
- mechanism validation of shipping indicators.

However, it does not prove that shipping variables can outperform a no-change forecast in Brent-price forecasting.

That question must be evaluated through:

- M0–M4 ablation experiments;
- rolling out-of-sample testing;
- comparison with strong price benchmarks;
- Diebold–Mariano or related forecast-comparison tests;
- robustness analysis across different market regimes.

### 中文

这篇文章是本项目航运部分的重要方法学参考，因为它说明了三个核心原则：

1. 航运指标应代表具有经济意义的真实物理作业，而不是一般性的船舶出现；
2. 原油海运活动应被表示为相互连接的全球供应链网络；
3. 时间信息与网络信息能够提供互补的预测信号。

因此，它最主要的价值是支持：

- M3 航运变量构建；
- 周频聚合；
- Shipping Encoder 设计；
- 基于图结构的消融实验；
