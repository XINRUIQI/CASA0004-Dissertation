## One-Sentence Summary / 一句话总结

**English:**  
The paper develops a country-level maritime trade nowcasting framework that converts AIS-derived physical shipment volumes into monthly import and export value and volume estimates, which can be released about seven working days after month-end.

**中文：**  
本文建立了一套国家层面的海运贸易即时预测框架，将 AIS 船舶数据估算出的实物货运量转换为月度进出口价值和贸易量，并可在月末后约 7 个工作日发布。



## Research Question and Motivation / 研究问题与动机

### Research question / 研究问题

**English:**  
Can high-frequency AIS vessel-movement data from IMF PortWatch be transformed into timely and comparable country-level estimates of maritime imports and exports, before official trade statistics become available?

**中文：**  
IMF PortWatch 的高频 AIS 船舶运动数据，能否在官方贸易统计发布之前，被转换为及时、可跨国比较的国家层面海运进出口估计？

### Motivation / 研究动机

**English:**

Official merchandise-trade statistics are often published with substantial and uneven delays. Some countries release monthly estimates shortly after month-end, whereas others may take several months. This limits real-time monitoring of trade shocks, supply-chain disruptions, tariff changes and geopolitical events.

AIS data provide a high-frequency alternative because they record vessel identity, location, type, draught and port activity. However, vessel observations are not automatically equivalent to customs trade statistics. The paper therefore develops a statistical bridge from vessel movements to physical cargo volumes, trade values and constant-price trade volumes.

**中文：**

官方商品贸易统计通常存在较长且各国不一致的发布滞后。有些国家能在月末后不久发布月度数据，另一些国家则可能需要数月。这限制了对贸易冲击、供应链中断、关税变化和地缘政治事件的实时监测。

AIS 数据能够记录船舶身份、位置、类型、吃水和港口活动，因此可提供高频替代信息。但船舶观测并不天然等同于海关贸易统计。本文的核心工作，就是建立从船舶运动到实物货运量、贸易价值和不变价贸易量的统计转换链条。


## Main Contribution / 主要贡献

**English:**

## Core Method / 核心方法

The paper does **not** estimate a standard regression, XGBoost model or deep-learning model. It applies a three-step accounting and index-construction nowcasting methodology.

本文**不是**使用普通回归、XGBoost 或深度学习直接预测贸易，而是采用三阶段核算和指数构造型即时预测方法。

### Step 1 — AIS-derived physical shipment volume  
### 第一步——由 AIS 估算实物货运量

For country \(c\), time \(t\), trade direction \(i\), and vessel type \(j\), the paper aggregates estimated physical shipment volumes:

\[
U_{c,t}^{i,j}
\]

where:

- \(i \in \{x,m\}\): exports or imports;
- \(j \in \{tn,bc,cs\}\): tanker, bulk carrier or containership;
- the quantity is measured in metric tonnes.

The estimate is derived from vessel draught rather than from vessel count alone.

论文在国家、月份、进出口方向和船型层面汇总实物货运量。关键物理变量来自船舶吃水估计，而不是简单的船舶数量。

### Step 2 — Physical volume to trade value  
### 第二步——由实物量转换为贸易价值

\[
V_{c,t}^{i}
=
\sum_j U_{c,t}^{i,j} \times UV_{c,t}^{i,j}
\]

where \(UV_{c,t}^{i,j}\) is the average unit value of goods transported by vessel type \(j\).

Base-period unit values are calculated from CEPII BACI value and weight data. They are updated using vessel-type-related prices:

- Tankers: IMF fuel price index excluding coal.
- Dry bulk: IMF non-fuel commodity index, including coal but excluding precious metals.
- Container and other cargo: WTO manufactured-goods price index, supplemented by bridging indicators for missing recent months.

贸易价值等于各船型实物货运量乘以相应平均单位价值。基期单位价值来自 BACI，随后按船型对应的商品价格指数更新。

### Step 3 — Trade value to constant-price trade volume  
### 第三步——由贸易价值转换为不变价贸易量

\[
Q_{c,t}^{i}
=
\frac{V_{c,t}^{i}}{P_{c,t}^{i}}
\]

where \(P_{c,t}^{i}\) is an import or export price deflator.

The paper compares:

1. **Constructed Laspeyres-type deflators**; and
2. **Official country-level deflators**.

Japan is the main case where official deflators substantially improve the fit.

通过进出口价格平减指数剔除价格变化后，得到统计意义上的贸易量。论文比较自建 Laspeyres 型指数和官方国家平减指数，日本是官方平减指数显著改善结果的主要案例。



## Validation Strategy / 验证策略

### Four-stage validation / 四阶段验证

**English:**

1. Review whether all major ports are included and whether port polygons are correctly defined.
2. Compare PortWatch port calls with official port-level data.
3. Compare PortWatch physical shipment volumes with official port- and country-level cargo data.
4. Compare nowcast trade values and constant-price volumes with official trade statistics.

**中文：**

1. 检查是否覆盖所有主要港口，以及港区多边形是否合理。
2. 将 PortWatch 的港口调用次数与官方港口数据比较。
3. 将 PortWatch 实物货运量与港口和国家层面的官方货运量比较。
4. 将即时预测的贸易价值和不变价贸易量与官方贸易统计比较。

- Main comparison period: January 2020 to December 2024.
- Main form: monthly year-on-year changes.
- Metrics:
  - Pearson correlation for trend alignment.
  - Normalised RMSE for level accuracy.
- Indicative performance criteria:
  \[
  \text{Correlation} > 0.8
  \]
  \[
  \text{Normalised RMSE} < 1
  \]

**Critical point / 关键提醒:**  
The paper explicitly states that this year-on-year comparison is **not a formal out-of-sample forecasting test**.

论文明确指出，这种月度同比拟合评估**并不等同于正式的样本外预测检验**。

## Data and Methodological Adjustments / 数据与方法修正

### 1. Port coverage / 港口覆盖

The study adds 64 ports:

- Brazil: 21
- Jamaica: 2
- Japan: 37
- United States: 4

Small ports may be unimportant globally but material for country-level totals.

研究新增 64 个港口。某些港口在全球尺度占比很小，但对单个国家贸易总量可能非常重要。

### 2. Port-boundary polygons / 港区边界

Eighteen port polygons are refined. The Port of Santos illustrates how an incomplete polygon can omit important berths and systematically undercount port calls and cargo activity.

论文修正 18 个港区多边形。Santos 港案例说明，边界不完整会遗漏泊位并系统性低估活动。

### 3. Offshore platforms / 海上平台

Offshore oil and gas platforms are treated as additional port polygons. The pilot adds:

- Brazil: 45
- Jamaica: 1
- United States: 1

Brazil is especially important because most petroleum production occurs offshore. Omitting offshore loading facilities would create downward bias in oil-export estimates.

海上油气平台被作为额外港口区域纳入。巴西尤其重要，因为其大部分石油生产位于海上；遗漏海上装载设施会系统性低估石油出口。

### 4. Transshipment netting / 转运净额修正

At transshipment hubs, observed draught changes may reflect international transshipment rather than domestic imports or exports. The paper applies a netting adjustment to Kingston using official container-throughput information.

在转运枢纽，船舶吃水变化可能对应国际转运，而非本国进出口。论文利用官方集装箱吞吐数据对 Kingston 港进行净额修正。

### 5. Specialised ports / 专业化港口

Domain knowledge is used to impose economically plausible directional rules:

- Port Rhoades is treated as a specialised bauxite and alumina export port.
- Implausible tanker exports from Samoa are set to zero because Samoa does not export petroleum products.

利用领域知识设置合理的贸易方向规则，例如出口专用港不应被错误识别为大量进口，萨摩亚也不应出现不合理的石油产品出口。

### 6. Deflator choice / 平减指数选择

Official national deflators are used only when they materially improve fit and are available sufficiently quickly for real-time production. In the pilot sample, this rule leads to official deflators being used for Japan.

只有当官方国家平减指数显著改善拟合且发布时间足够及时，才用于实时估计；试点中主要应用于日本。

## Key Findings / 主要发现

### Overall results / 总体结果

**English:**

- Performance is generally stronger for advanced economies and the large emerging market than for smaller economies.
- Brazil, Japan and the United States meet the correlation and normalised-RMSE criteria for the majority of trade-flow statistics after revisions.

## U.S. Trade-Tension Application / 美国贸易紧张时期的应用

**English:**

The U.S. application highlights a key distinction between total trade and maritime trade. During elevated trade tensions, some high-value and low-weight products may be transported by air or other modes. PortWatch may therefore miss sharp movements in headline trade while still tracking the underlying maritime component reasonably well.

This supports interpreting PortWatch as a measure of high-frequency **maritime physical activity**, not as a universal proxy for all trade under every market condition.

**中文：**

美国案例强调了总贸易与海运贸易之间的区别。在贸易紧张时期，一些高价值、低重量商品可能通过航空或其他运输方式提前进口。因此，PortWatch 可能无法捕捉总贸易的剧烈变化，但仍能较好追踪海运部分。

这意味着 PortWatch 更适合被解释为高频的**海运实体活动指标**，而不是在任何情况下都能代表全部贸易的通用代理变量。

## Critical Assessment / 批判性评价

### Strengths / 优点

1. **Economically interpretable transformation chain**  
   The method links physical cargo activity to value and volume rather than treating AIS variables as black-box predictors.

2. **Strong data-engineering contribution**  
   It shows that port geometry, offshore infrastructure, cargo direction and transshipment treatment can matter as much as model choice.

3. **Cross-country validation**  
   The approach is tested across countries with different sizes and economic structures.

4. **Explicit distinction between physical volume and price effects**  
   The use of deflators helps separate quantity changes from commodity-price changes.

5. **Policy relevance and timeliness**  
   Monthly estimates can be available around seven working days after month-end.

### Weaknesses / 缺点

1. **Not a formal out-of-sample forecast evaluation**  
   Correlation with official year-on-year statistics does not establish future predictive power.

2. **Primarily maritime coverage**  
   Air, road, rail and pipeline trade are not comprehensively captured.

3. **Coarse vessel-type price mapping**  
   A tanker can transport crude oil, refined products, LNG or chemicals, which do not share identical prices.

4. **Seasonal product-composition problem**  
   Average unit values can vary when the commodity mix changes seasonally.

5. **Contract-pricing problem**  
   Actual commodity trade prices may depend on long-term contracts, formulas, benchmarks and transfer pricing rather than contemporaneous spot indices.

6. **Reporting-timing mismatch**  
   PortWatch records loading or discharge, while official trade may be recorded at customs clearance, invoicing or ownership transfer.

7. **Small-country volatility**  
   A single shipment can dominate a monthly series, reducing statistical stability.

8. **Cross-comparability versus local accuracy**  
   More country-specific corrections may improve fit but weaken methodological consistency across countries.

## 2. Economic Transmission Mechanism / 经济传导机制

The paper supports the first part of the following mechanism:

\[
\text{AIS tanker activity}
\rightarrow
\text{physical oil-flow information}
\rightarrow
\text{supply-demand and inventory expectations}
\rightarrow
\text{oil-price adjustment}
\]

However, it validates mainly:

\[
\text{AIS activity}
\rightarrow
\text{maritime trade measurement}
\]

It does **not** directly test:

\[
\text{AIS activity}_{t-k}
\rightarrow
\text{future Brent or WTI price}_{t}
\]

Therefore, the dissertation must establish the final forecasting link using strict out-of-sample tests.

本文支持的是“AIS 活动能够反映实体海运货物流”的前半段机制，但没有直接检验滞后航运活动能否预测未来 Brent 或 WTI。因此，最终的油价预测关系必须由你的样本外实验建立。

## 3. Variable Choice for M3 / M3 变量选择

### Recommended conceptual priority / 概念上的优先顺序

1. AIS-derived tanker physical cargo volume or load estimate.
2. Direction-specific crude or liquid-bulk exports/imports.
3. Tanker capacity weighted by loading information.
4. Tanker capacity.
5. Tanker count.
6. Total-vessel count.

The paper suggests that actual physical shipment volume is conceptually closer to economic activity than vessel count alone.

概念上，基于吃水的油轮实物装载量最接近真实石油流量；如果不可得，再依次考虑方向性液体散货量、带装载信息的运力、油轮运力、油轮数量和总船舶数量。

### Feasible variables if only count and capacity are available  
### 如果当前只有 count 和 capacity

- `pw_{location}_n_tanker`
- `pw_{location}_capacity_tanker`
- `pw_{location}_n_total`
- `pw_{location}_capacity_total`
- tanker share:
  \[
  \frac{n_{tanker}}{n_{total}}
  \]
- tanker capacity share:
  \[
  \frac{capacity_{tanker}}{capacity_{total}}
  \]
- average tanker size:
  \[
  \frac{capacity_{tanker}}{n_{tanker}}
  \]
- changes, rolling means, anomalies and lagged values.

### Important qualification / 重要限定

The paper supports **vessel-type disaggregation**, but it does not empirically prove that tanker variables outperform total-vessel variables in oil-price forecasting.

论文支持按船型拆分，但没有实证证明 tanker 变量在油价预测中一定优于 total 变量。这个结论必须通过你的消融实验获得。

## 4. Avoiding Target Leakage and Mechanical Endogeneity  
## 避免目标泄漏与机械内生性

The paper constructs tanker trade value partly by updating unit values with a fuel-price index:

\[
\text{Tanker trade value}
=
\text{Tanker physical volume}
\times
\text{Fuel-related unit value}
\]

If the dissertation uses contemporaneous PortWatch tanker **trade value** to predict Brent or WTI, the feature may already contain oil-price information. This can create circularity or target leakage.

Therefore:

- Prefer physical variables: count, capacity, draught-based volume, loading and flow direction.
- Avoid contemporaneous price-constructed trade-value features.
- If such value variables are retained, lag them strictly and document their construction.
- Compare results with and without price-containing variables.

论文利用燃料价格指数更新油轮货物单位价值。因此，直接使用同期 tanker trade value 预测油价，可能等于把油价重新包装后输入模型，造成机械内生性或目标泄漏。应优先使用纯物理指标。

## 5. Lag Structure and Timing / 滞后结构与时间对齐

The paper identifies a possible reporting lag for Brazilian offshore oil exports: PortWatch records vessel loading or departure, while official statistics may record the trade later.

For oil-price forecasting, lag design must be based on **information availability**, not only statistical correlation.

Candidate features:

\[
x_{t-1},\ x_{t-3},\ x_{t-7},\ x_{t-14},\ x_{t-30}
\]

\[
\Delta x_t,\quad
\%\Delta x_t,\quad
MA_7(x_t),\quad
MA_{14}(x_t),\quad
MA_{30}(x_t)
\]

\[
z_t
=
\frac{x_t-\mu_{t,w}}{\sigma_{t,w}}
\]

Useful experiments:

- contemporaneous association;
- 1-, 7-, 14- and 30-day forecasting horizons;
- distributed-lag features;
- Granger-style lead-lag diagnostics as exploratory evidence;
- strict walk-forward validation for final claims.

论文指出巴西海上石油出口在 AIS 记录时间与官方统计时间之间可能存在滞后。对油价预测而言，滞后必须按预测时真实可获得的信息构造，而不能只根据全样本相关性选择。

## 6. Offshore Platforms and Oil Export Terminals

## 7. Chokepoint Features / 咽喉变量

The paper does not compare Hormuz, Suez, Malacca or other chokepoints, and does not justify selecting only three routes.

Chokepoints should instead be linked to distinct mechanisms:

| Chokepoint / 咽喉 | Potential mechanism / 可能机制 |
|---|---|
| Hormuz | Gulf crude supply and disruption risk / 海湾原油供给与中断风险 |
| Suez–SUMED | Middle East–Europe route and diversion / 中东—欧洲路线与改道 |
| Bab el-Mandeb | Red Sea security and rerouting / 红海安全与绕航 |
| Malacca | Asian import demand and eastbound flows / 亚洲进口需求与东向流量 |
| Cape of Good Hope | Diversion distance and transport cost / 绕航距离与运输成本 |
| Panama | Route substitution and selected energy flows / 航线替代及部分能源流 |

Selection should be justified through:

- oil-flow relevance;
- data coverage;
- multicollinearity;
- out-of-sample contribution;
- ablation tests;
- stability across market regimes.

本文不能用于证明 Hormuz、Suez 和 Malacca 三个咽喉就是最优组合。变量选择应依据石油流量机制、数据覆盖、共线性、样本外增益和跨时期稳定性。

## 9. Data Audit Before Model Complexity / 复杂模型之前的数据审计

The paper strongly implies that data quality should be addressed before increasing model complexity.

Recommended M3 audit:

1. Confirm the geographic meaning of each PortWatch polygon.
2. Distinguish transit, port call, loading, discharge and passage counts.
3. Check tanker classification consistency.
4. Inspect missing dates and revisions.
5. Confirm time zones and daily aggregation rules.
6. Separate empty and loaded vessels where possible.
7. Check whether offshore terminals are represented.
8. Detect transshipment or double counting.
9. Confirm when each variable was actually available.
10. Document whether capacity means DWT, estimated cargo, or another measure.

这篇论文最重要的方法论启示之一是：港口边界、船型、装载方向、海上平台和时间戳错误，不能由更复杂的模型自动解决。数据审计应先于模型升级。

## 10. Suggested Experimental Design / 建议的实验设计

### Incremental-value models / 增量价值模型

1. **Naïve baseline**
   - previous price;
   - historical mean;
   - random walk.

2. **Econometric baseline**
   - ARIMA / ARIMAX;
   - autoregressive price and fundamental variables.

3. **M1–M2 baseline**
   - market, macro and oil-fundamental variables.

4. **M1–M2 + total shipping**
   - tests whether generic maritime activity adds value.

5. **M1–M2 + tanker shipping**
   - tests whether oil-specific shipping is more useful.

6. **M1–M4 full model**
   - market, fundamentals, shipping and geopolitical/text variables.

### Required ablation tests / 必要的消融实验

- Remove all M3 variables.
- Tanker variables only.
- Total-vessel variables only.
- Count only.
- Capacity only.
- Individual chokepoint removal.
- Physical variables versus price-constructed trade-value variables.
- Normal periods versus crisis periods.

### Evaluation / 评估

- Walk-forward or expanding-window validation.
- MAE and RMSE.
- sMAPE where appropriate.
- Directional accuracy.
- Forecast horizons such as \(h=1,7,14,30\).
- Diebold–Mariano tests for selected model comparisons.
- SHAP analysis calculated only after leakage-safe training.

论文自身不是样本外预测研究，因此你的项目必须通过滚动验证和消融实验，判断 M3 航运数据是否在传统变量之外提供真正的增量预测价值。

## Limitations for Dissertation Use / 在毕业论文中使用该文献时的限制

1. **Different target**  
   The paper nowcasts trade; it does not forecast crude-oil prices.

2. **Different frequency**  
   The core outputs are monthly, whereas the dissertation may forecast daily or weekly Brent prices.

3. **No ML comparison**  
   It does not compare XGBoost, LSTM, TFT or other forecasting architectures.

4. **No direct causal claim**  
   It does not identify a causal effect from shipping activity to oil prices.

5. **No chokepoint selection evidence**  
   It does not establish that Hormuz, Suez and Malacca are the optimal locations.

6. **No proof that tanker outperforms total vessels**  
   Vessel-type separation is methodologically supported, but predictive superiority must be tested.

7. **Potential circularity in value estimates**  
   Fuel-price indices enter the construction of tanker trade values.

8. **Country-level findings may not transfer directly to route-level features**  
   Port aggregation and chokepoint transit measure different aspects of maritime activity.

## Final Assessment / 最终评价

**English:**

This is a highly relevant methodology paper for the dissertation's shipping-data component. Its main value is not a forecasting algorithm but a rigorous measurement framework showing how raw AIS observations should be converted, validated and corrected before they are used as economic indicators. It provides strong justification for incorporating tanker-specific physical activity into the M3 module, while also showing why shipping variables require careful treatment of geography, vessel type, loading status, timing and price contamination.

The dissertation's genuine contribution can extend this work by testing whether leakage-safe, lagged PortWatch tanker and chokepoint variables improve out-of-sample Brent forecasts beyond conventional market, macroeconomic, inventory and geopolitical predictors.

**中文：**

这篇论文对毕业论文的航运数据模块高度相关。它最重要的价值不是提供某个预测算法，而是建立了一套严格的测量框架，说明原始 AIS 观测在成为经济指标之前，应如何转换、验证和修正。它为在 M3 模块中加入油轮特定的物理活动变量提供了较强依据，同时也说明航运变量必须谨慎处理空间边界、船型、装载状态、时间错位和价格污染。

你的项目可以在此基础上进一步检验：在避免泄漏并严格滞后后，PortWatch 的油轮与咽喉变量，是否能够在传统市场、宏观、库存和地缘政治变量之外，提升 Brent 的样本外预测表现。
