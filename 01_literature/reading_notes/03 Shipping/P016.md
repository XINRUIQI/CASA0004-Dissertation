## One-Sentence Summary / 一句话概括

The paper constructs a monthly port-level panel dataset from AIS records and examines how crude oil futures-price fluctuations are associated with tanker port-call frequency, docking duration, total gross tonnage, and changes in the number of active tankers at major crude-oil-exporting ports.

本文利用 AIS 数据构建港口—月份面板数据，研究原油期货价格变化与主要原油出口港的油轮靠港次数、平均靠泊时间、总吨位及活跃油轮数量变化之间的关系。

---

## Research Question / 研究问题

### English

The central question is:

> How do fluctuations in crude oil futures prices affect tanker port-call behaviour at major crude-oil-exporting ports?

The paper examines four dimensions of tanker activity:

1. How frequently tankers call at oil-loading ports;
2. How long tankers remain docked;
3. How much total tanker capacity is active at each port;
4. Whether the number of different tankers calling at a port increases.

The direction studied in the paper is:

\[
\text{Crude oil futures price}_t
\rightarrow
\text{Tanker port-call features}_{i,t}
\]

### 中文

论文的核心问题是：

> 原油期货价格发生变化时，全球主要原油出口港的油轮靠港行为是否会相应变化？

文章从四个维度衡量油轮活动：

1. 油轮靠港频率是否变化；
2. 油轮平均靠泊时间是否变化；
3. 港口当月参与运输的油轮总吨位是否变化；
4. 是否有更多不同油轮进入该港口参与运输。

需要特别注意，论文研究的是：

\[
\text{油价}
\rightarrow
\text{航运活动}
\]

而不是本研究项目重点关注的：

\[
\text{航运活动}
\rightarrow
\text{未来油价}
\]

## Research Motivation / 研究动机

### English

Previous studies on the interaction between oil markets and tanker shipping have mainly focused on:

- Tanker freight rates;
- Aggregate crude-oil trade volumes;
- Transportation costs;
- Individual ports or regional shipping markets.

The authors argue that freight rates alone do not fully represent the physical operation of the tanker market. AIS data provide direct observations of vessel movements, port arrivals, departures, docking duration, and vessel size.

The paper therefore connects financial oil-market information with observable physical shipping activity.

### 中文

既有关于原油市场和油轮航运关系的研究，主要关注：

- 油轮运价；
- 原油海运贸易总量；
- 航运成本；
- 单个港口或特定区域的油轮市场。

作者认为，单纯使用运价并不能完整反映油轮市场的实际运行状况。AIS 数据能够直接记录船舶位置、靠港、离港、停留时间和船舶规模，因此可以观察更加微观的实体运输活动。

这篇文章的主要动机，是将金融市场中的原油价格与真实世界中的油轮运行行为联系起来。

## Core Method / 核心方法

- **Research design / 研究设计**: Monthly port-level panel analysis / 月度港口面板分析
- **Main models / 主要模型**:
  - Port fixed-effects panel regression / 港口固定效应面板回归
  - Binary Logit regression / 二元 Logit 回归
- **Independent variable / 核心解释变量**:
  - Monthly crude oil futures price, `fp`

- **Dependent variables / 因变量**:
  - Port-call frequency, `freq`
  - Average docking duration, `duration`

- **Control variables / 控制变量**:
  - Proven crude oil reserves, `reserve`
  - Crude oil trade or net exports, `nx`
  - Tanker freight index, `BTI`
- **Robustness checks / 稳健性检验**:
  - Restricting the sample to 2011–2019
  - Restricting the sample to OPEC ports

## AIS-Derived Variables / AIS 航运变量

### 1. Port-Call Frequency / 靠港频率

\[
freq_{i,t}
\]

**English definition**

The monthly number of tanker port calls at crude-oil-exporting port \(i\) during month \(t\).

It measures port-call events rather than unique vessels. If the same tanker calls at the same port twice in one month, both port calls are counted.

**中文定义**

港口 \(i\) 在月份 \(t\) 内发生的油轮靠港次数。

该变量统计的是靠港事件，而不是独立船舶数量。如果同一艘油轮在一个月内两次进入同一港口，则会被计为两次靠港。

**Economic meaning / 经济含义**

- Tanker activity intensity / 油轮活动强度
- Loading-operation frequency / 装载作业频率
- Port utilisation / 港口使用程度
- Physical crude-oil shipping demand / 实体原油海运需求

---

### 2. Average Docking Duration / 平均靠泊时间

\[
duration_{i,t}
\]

**English definition**

The monthly average number of days tankers remain docked at port \(i\).

**中文定义**

港口 \(i\) 在月份 \(t\) 内油轮平均靠泊或停留的天数。

**Potential interpretation / 可能反映**

- Loading and unloading time / 装卸作业时间
- Port congestion / 港口拥堵
- Anchorage or berth waiting / 锚地或泊位等待
- Operational efficiency / 港口运行效率
- Demurrage risk / 滞期风险
- Uncertainty in cargo arrangements / 货物安排不确定性

**Important caution / 重要注意**

The paper uses docking duration rather than a fully defined turnaround-time measure. It does not clearly separate:

- Waiting outside the port;
- Anchorage waiting;
- Berth time;
- Loading time;
- Time between arrival and final departure.

文章使用的是平均靠泊时间，并未严格区分：

- 港外等待；
- 锚地等待；
- 泊位停留；
- 装载时间；
- 从到港到最终离港的完整周转时间。

---

### 3. Total Gross Tonnage / 靠港油轮总吨位

\[
GT_{i,t}
\]

**English definition**

The sum of gross tonnage of all tanker port-call events at port \(i\) in month \(t\).

If one tanker calls at the same port twice during a month, its gross tonnage is added twice.

**中文定义**

港口 \(i\) 在月份 \(t\) 内所有油轮靠港事件对应的总 gross tonnage。

如果同一艘油轮在一个月内重复靠港，其总吨位也会被重复累计。

The variable can therefore be approximately understood as:

\[
GT_{i,t}
\approx
\text{Vessel size}
\times
\text{Activity frequency}
\]

因此，该变量同时包含：

- 船舶规模；
- 靠港频率；
- 潜在运输能力；
- 港口航运活动规模。

**Important caution / 重要注意**

Gross tonnage is not equal to:

- Deadweight tonnage;
- Actual cargo volume;
- Barrels of crude oil transported;
- Tanker loading rate.

Gross tonnage 不能直接等同于：

- 载重吨；
- 实际载货量；
- 实际运输的原油桶数；
- 油轮装载率。

---

### 4. New Tanker Indicator / 新增油轮指标

\[
new\_add_{i,t}
\]

The paper first calculates the number of different tankers calling at each port in each month.

论文首先统计每个港口每个月出现的不同油轮数量。

It then defines:

\[
new\_add_{i,t}
=
\begin{cases}
1,
&
\text{if unique tanker count}_{i,t}
>
\text{unique tanker count}_{i,t-1}
\\
0,
&
\text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\]

For example:

- January: four different tankers;
- February: five different tankers;
- Therefore, `new_add = 1` in February.

例如：

- 1 月有 4 艘不同油轮；
- 2 月有 5 艘不同油轮；
- 则 2 月的 `new_add = 1`。

**Important caution / 重要注意**

The variable does not necessarily identify a genuinely new vessel that has never previously visited the port. It only indicates that the number of different tankers is higher than in the previous month.

该变量并不能证明出现了一艘此前从未到访过的“新船”，只能表示当月不同油轮的数量比上月更多。

A more accurate name would be:

- Increase in unique tanker count;
- Fleet expansion indicator;
- Additional active-tanker indicator.

更准确的理解是：

- 独立油轮数量增长指标；
- 船队活动扩张指标；
- 新增活跃运力指标。

## Explanatory and Control Variables / 解释变量与控制变量

### Crude Oil Futures Price / 原油期货价格

\[
fp_t
\]

The core independent variable is the monthly crude oil futures price measured in USD per barrel.

核心解释变量为月度原油期货价格，单位为美元/桶。

However, the paper does not clearly identify:

- Whether the contract is Brent or WTI;
- Which maturity is used;
- Whether the series is a continuous futures contract;
- Whether monthly average or month-end prices are used;
- How contract rollover is handled.

但是，论文没有明确说明：

- 使用 Brent 还是 WTI；
- 使用哪个期限的合约；
- 是否为连续期货合约；
- 使用月均价还是月末价；
- 如何处理合约展期。

Therefore, the reading note should use the neutral description:

> Monthly crude oil futures price

而不应直接写成：

> Brent/WTI price

---

### Proven Oil Reserves / 已探明原油储量

\[
reserve_{i,t}
\]

The authors use national proven crude oil reserves to control for long-term production and export capacity.

作者使用国家已探明原油储量，控制一国长期生产能力和原油出口能力。

---

### Crude Oil Trade / 原油贸易量

\[
nx_{i,t}
\]

The variable measures national crude oil trade or net exports.

该变量用于反映一国的原油贸易或净出口活动。

The terminology is not fully consistent across the paper: some sections call it net exports, while Table 1 describes it as crude oil trade.

论文对这一变量的称呼并不完全一致：部分正文称其为净出口，Table 1 则称为原油贸易量。

---

### Tanker Freight Index / 油轮运价指数

\[
BTI_t
=
\frac{BCTI_t+BDTI_t}{2}
\]

where:

- BCTI = Baltic Clean Tanker Index;
- BDTI = Baltic Dirty Tanker Index.

其中：

- BCTI 为波罗的海成品油轮运价指数；
- BDTI 为波罗的海原油及重质油轮运价指数。

The index is intended to control for tanker transportation costs and shipping-market conditions.

该指标用于控制油轮运输成本和航运市场状况。

However, averaging BCTI and BDTI may mix clean-product tanker and crude-oil tanker markets. For a crude-oil-specific forecasting project, BDTI may have a more direct economic interpretation.

不过，将 BCTI 与 BDTI 简单平均可能混合成品油轮和原油轮两个不同市场。对于专门研究原油价格的项目，BDTI 通常具有更加直接的经济含义。

## Empirical Models / 实证模型

### 1. Port Fixed-Effects Models / 港口固定效应模型

The paper estimates three log-log panel regressions:

\[
\ln(freq_{i,t})
=
\alpha_1
+
\beta_1\ln(fp_t)
+
\mathbf{c}_1\ln(\mathbf{Controls}_{i,t})
+
\mu_i
+
\varepsilon_{i,t}
\]

\[
\ln(duration_{i,t})
=
\alpha_2
+
\beta_2\ln(fp_t)
+
\mathbf{c}_2\ln(\mathbf{Controls}_{i,t})
+
\mu_i
+
\varepsilon_{i,t}
\]

\[
\ln(GT_{i,t})
=
\alpha_3
+
\beta_3\ln(fp_t)
+
\mathbf{c}_3\ln(\mathbf{Controls}_{i,t})
+
\mu_i
+
\varepsilon_{i,t}
\]

where:

- \(i\) represents ports;
- \(t\) represents months;
- \(\mu_i\) represents port fixed effects;
- the controls are reserves, crude oil trade and tanker freight rates.

其中：

- \(i\) 表示港口；
- \(t\) 表示月份；
- \(\mu_i\) 表示港口固定效应；
- 控制变量包括原油储量、原油贸易和油轮运价。

### Why Port Fixed Effects? / 为什么使用港口固定效应？

Port fixed effects control for relatively time-invariant characteristics, including:

- Geographic location;
- Natural harbour conditions;
- Long-term infrastructure capacity;
- Historical trading importance;
- National institutional background;
- Persistent differences in port efficiency.

港口固定效应可以控制相对稳定的港口特征，包括：

- 地理位置；
- 天然港口条件；
- 长期基础设施能力；
- 历史贸易地位；
- 国家制度背景；
- 长期港口效率差异。

The coefficient is therefore primarily identified from changes within the same port over time.

因此，模型主要利用同一港口随时间发生的变化，而不是简单比较不同港口之间的差异。

---

### 2. Binary Logit Model / 二元 Logit 模型

The probability of an increase in the number of different tankers is modelled as:

\[
\ln
\left(
\frac{p_{i,t}}{1-p_{i,t}}
\right)
=
\gamma_1\ln(fp_t)
+
\gamma_2\ln(\mathbf{Controls}_{i,t})
+
\sigma_{i,t}
\]

where:

\[
p_{i,t}
=
P(new\_add_{i,t}=1)
\]

该模型估计原油期货价格是否影响某港口独立油轮数量增加的可能性。

## Key Findings / 主要发现

### Result Summary / 结果汇总

| Oil futures price rises / 原油期货价格上涨时 | Estimated relationship / 估计关系 |
|---|---|
| Tanker port-call frequency / 油轮靠港次数 | Decreases / 下降 |
| Average docking duration / 平均靠泊时间 | Increases / 上升 |
| Total gross tonnage / 靠港油轮总吨位 | Decreases / 下降 |
| Probability of additional active tankers / 活跃油轮数量增加的可能性 | Decreases / 下降 |

---

### 1. Port-Call Frequency / 靠港次数

In the full model:

\[
\hat{\beta}_{freq}
=
-0.0353
\]

The coefficient is statistically significant at the 1% level.

完整控制模型中，油价系数为：

\[
-0.0353
\]

并在 1% 水平上显著。

**Correct interpretation / 正确解释**

Because the model is log-log:

> A 1% increase in crude oil futures prices is associated with an approximately 0.0353% decrease in tanker port-call frequency, holding the included controls constant.

由于模型是双对数形式，正确解释为：

> 在其他变量不变时，原油期货价格上升 1%，油轮靠港次数平均下降约 0.0353%。

**Paper’s interpretation problem / 论文解释问题**

The paper describes this as approximately a 3.53% decrease, which appears to overstate the effect by a factor of 100.

论文将其解释为下降约 3.53%，这可能将弹性系数放大了约 100 倍。

---

### 2. Average Docking Duration / 平均靠泊时间

In the full model:

\[
\hat{\beta}_{duration}
=
0.130
\]

The coefficient is statistically significant at the 1% level.

完整控制模型中，油价系数为：

\[
0.130
\]

并在 1% 水平上显著。

**Correct interpretation / 正确解释**

> A 1% increase in crude oil futures prices is associated with an approximately 0.13% increase in average tanker docking duration.

正确解释为：

> 原油期货价格上升 1%，油轮平均靠泊时间增加约 0.13%。

The authors suggest that higher prices may lead to:

- Greater uncertainty in loading arrangements;
- Longer waiting time;
- More cautious purchasing decisions;
- A reduced urgency to proceed to the next destination.

作者认为，高油价可能导致：

- 装载安排更加不确定；
- 等待时间延长；
- 采购决策更加谨慎；
- 油轮前往下一目的地的紧迫性下降。

**Caution / 注意**

These mechanisms are proposed interpretations rather than directly tested causal channels.

这些机制主要是作者提出的解释，并未通过专门的中介变量或因果设计直接验证。

### 3. Total Gross Tonnage / 总吨位

In the full model:

\[
\hat{\beta}_{GT}
=
-0.00420
\]

The coefficient is statistically significant at the 1% level.

完整控制模型中，油价系数为：

\[
-0.00420
\]

并在 1% 水平上显著。

**Correct interpretation / 正确解释**

> A 1% increase in crude oil futures prices is associated with an approximately 0.0042% decrease in monthly total gross tonnage.

正确解释为：

> 原油期货价格上升 1%，月度靠港油轮总吨位平均下降约 0.0042%。

The paper reports a 0.42% decline, which again appears to be approximately 100 times larger than the elasticity implied by the estimated coefficient.

论文将其解释为下降约 0.42%，同样可能将实际弹性放大了约 100 倍。

### 4. Additional Active Tankers / 活跃油轮数量增加

In the controlled Logit model:

\[
\hat{\gamma}_{new\_add}
=
-0.114
\]

The coefficient is statistically significant at the 1% level.

控制变量加入后，Logit 模型中的系数为：

\[
-0.114
\]

并在 1% 水平上显著。

The negative sign suggests that higher oil futures prices are associated with lower odds that the number of different tankers at a port increases.

负号表示，油价越高，某港口独立油轮数量增加的胜算越低。

**Important interpretation issue / 重要解释问题**

The coefficient cannot be directly interpreted as:

> A 1% increase in oil prices reduces the probability of new tanker arrivals by 11.4%.

Logit coefficients describe changes in log-odds rather than direct percentage-point changes in probability.

该系数不能直接解释为：

> 油价上涨 1%，新增油轮概率下降 11.4%。

Logit 系数作用于对数胜算，而不是直接作用于概率。若要解释概率变化，需要报告平均边际效应或在给定基准概率下进行计算。

## Proposed Economic Mechanism / 作者提出的经济机制

The paper proposes the following transmission mechanism:

\[
\text{Higher crude oil price}
\rightarrow
\text{Lower short-term oil demand}
\rightarrow
\text{Lower purchasing and inventory demand}
\rightarrow
\text{Less tanker shipping activity}
\]

This is reflected by:

\[
\begin{cases}
\text{Lower port-call frequency}\\
\text{Lower total gross tonnage}\\
\text{Lower probability of additional tankers}\\
\text{Longer docking duration}
\end{cases}
\]

文章提出的传导逻辑是：

\[
\text{油价上涨}
\rightarrow
\text{短期石油需求下降}
\rightarrow
\text{采购与库存需求下降}
\rightarrow
\text{油轮运输活动减弱}
\]

具体表现为：

\[
\begin{cases}
\text{靠港次数下降}\\
\text{总吨位下降}\\
\text{新增活跃油轮可能性下降}\\
\text{平均靠泊时间延长}
\end{cases}
\]

However, the paper does not directly test each step in this transmission mechanism.

不过，论文并未逐步检验这一传导机制中的每个环节。

## Limitations / 局限性

### 1. Not a Forecasting Study / 不是油价预测研究

The paper does not conduct:

- Training-test splits;
- Rolling or expanding-window forecasts;
- Out-of-sample evaluation;
- Forecast-error comparisons;
- Directional-accuracy tests;
- Comparisons with ARIMA, XGBoost, LSTM or other forecasting models.

论文没有开展：

- 训练集与测试集划分；
- 滚动窗口或扩展窗口预测；
- 样本外评估；
- 预测误差比较；
- 方向准确率检验；
- 与 ARIMA、XGBoost、LSTM 等预测模型的比较。

Therefore, it cannot establish that AIS features improve oil-price forecasting accuracy.

因此，这篇论文不能证明 AIS 航运变量能够提高油价预测准确率。

---

### 2. Reverse Causality and Endogeneity / 反向因果与内生性

The paper studies:

\[
Price_t
\rightarrow
Shipping_t
\]

However, the relationship may be bidirectional:

\[
Price_t
\leftrightarrow
Shipping_t
\]

For example:

- Oil prices affect tanker deployment;
- Tanker activity reflects physical oil supply;
- Shipping disruptions can affect future prices;
- Traders may adjust shipping decisions based on expected future prices.

例如：

- 油价会影响油轮调度；
- 油轮活动反映实体原油供应；
- 航运中断可能影响未来油价；
- 贸易商可能根据未来价格预期调整运输活动。

The paper does not use:

- Instrumental variables;
- Natural experiments;
- Difference-in-differences;
- Structural simultaneous equations;
- Dynamic panel estimators;
- Granger-causality tests.

论文没有使用：

- 工具变量；
- 自然实验；
- 双重差分；
- 结构联立方程；
- 动态面板估计；
- Granger 因果检验。

Its findings should therefore be interpreted as associations rather than strongly identified causal effects.

因此，其结论更适合解释为统计关联，而不是已经被严格识别的因果效应。

---

### 3. No Full Time Fixed Effects / 未加入完整时间固定效应

The global oil futures price is identical across all ports in a given month.

同一个月份中，所有港口对应的全球油价变量都是相同的。

If full month fixed effects were introduced, the oil-price variable would be perfectly collinear with them.

如果加入完整月份固定效应，全球油价变量将与月份固定效应完全共线。

As a result, the estimated oil-price coefficient may absorb other common time-varying factors, such as:

- Global economic growth;
- OPEC policy;
- COVID-19;
- Geopolitical shocks;
- Global demand;
- Financial conditions;
- U.S. dollar movements.

因此，油价系数可能同时吸收以下全球共同冲击：

- 全球经济增长；
- OPEC 政策；
- COVID-19；
- 地缘政治冲击；
- 全球需求；
- 金融条件；
- 美元变化。

---

### 4. Very Low Explanatory Power / 模型解释力很低

The full-model \(R^2\) values are approximately:

| Dependent variable / 因变量 | \(R^2\) |
|---|---:|
| `freq` | 0.004 |
| `duration` | 0.008 |
| `GT` | 0.003 |

Although the coefficients are statistically significant, the models explain only a very small proportion of total variation.

虽然相关系数在统计上显著，但模型只能解释极少一部分港口活动变化。

This illustrates an important distinction:

\[
\text{Statistical significance}
\neq
\text{High predictive value}
\]

即：

\[
\text{统计显著}
\neq
\text{较高预测价值}
\]

The large number of observations may make very small effects statistically significant.

大样本可能使经济效应很小的系数也呈现统计显著。

---

### 5. Ambiguous Oil-Price Definition / 油价定义不清晰

The futures benchmark, maturity, frequency construction and rollover process are not sufficiently documented.

论文没有充分说明期货基准、合约期限、月度聚合方式和展期处理方法。

This reduces replicability and makes it difficult to determine whether the results apply equally to Brent and WTI.

这降低了研究的可复现性，也无法确认结果是否能同时适用于 Brent 和 WTI。

### 6. Coarse AIS Feature Construction / AIS 特征较为粗糙

The paper does not clearly distinguish:

- Crude tankers from product tankers;
- Laden from ballast voyages;
- Export-loading from import-unloading operations;
- VLCC, Suezmax and Aframax classes;
- Anchorage waiting from berth occupation;
- Vessel capacity from actual cargo volume.

论文没有清楚区分：

- 原油轮与成品油轮；
- 满载航次与空载航次；
- 出口装载与进口卸载；
- VLCC、Suezmax 和 Aframax 等船型；
- 锚地等待与泊位停留；
- 船舶容量与实际载货量。

These distinctions could be important for oil-price forecasting.

这些区分对于油价预测可能非常重要。

### 7. Monthly Frequency Only / 仅使用月度数据

The monthly frequency may smooth out short-lived disruptions, including:

- Sudden sanctions;
- Chokepoint closures;
- Port attacks;
- Weather-related disruptions;
- Rapid inventory adjustments.

月度频率可能会平滑掉一些短期冲击，例如：

Weekly or daily features may be more useful for short-horizon price forecasting.

对于短期油价预测，周度或日度航运变量可能更有价值。

AIS 数据并未作为开放复现数据集直接发布，论文只说明读者可以联系通讯作者申请获取。

这限制了研究的直接复现。

## Implications for M3 Feature Engineering / 对 M3 特征工程的具体启示

### 1. Do Not Use Only Tanker Count / 不要只使用油轮数量

A single variable such as:

```text
pw_hormuz_n_tanker
````

captures only one dimension of shipping activity.

类似以下变量：

```text
pw_hormuz_n_tanker
```

只能反映航运活动的一个维度。

Based on this paper, M3 features should cover at least:

1. Activity frequency;
2. Unique vessel participation;
3. Transport capacity;
4. Congestion or dwell time;
5. Changes and anomalies.

根据本文，M3 至少应覆盖：

1. 活动频率；
2. 独立船舶参与程度；
3. 运输能力；
4. 拥堵或停留时间；
5. 变化率和异常程度。

---

### 2. Distinguish Transit Count from Port-Call Frequency

### 区分航道通行量与港口靠港次数

The paper's `freq` variable measures tanker calls at oil-loading ports.

论文的 `freq` 统计油轮在原油装载港发生的靠港事件。

By contrast:

```text
pw_hormuz_n_tanker
```

likely measures tanker presence or transit through the Strait of Hormuz.

相比之下：

```text
pw_hormuz_n_tanker
```

更可能统计霍尔木兹海峡范围内出现或通过的油轮数量。

Their meanings are related but not identical:

| Port-call frequency / 靠港次数           | Chokepoint transit count / 咽喉航道通行量            |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Measures entry into a port           | Measures movement through a corridor          |
| Often linked to loading or unloading | Does not necessarily involve cargo operations |
| Port-specific                        | Route- or corridor-specific                   |
| May capture operational congestion   | May capture flow intensity and disruption     |
| Requires port geofencing             | Requires crossing-line or corridor detection  |

Therefore, the Hormuz variable should be described as:

> A proxy for tanker traffic intensity or crude-oil shipping flow.

因此，霍尔木兹油轮数量应描述为：

> 油轮交通强度或原油海运流量的代理变量。

It should not be described as a direct replication of port-call frequency.

不应将其描述为对论文靠港频率变量的直接复制。

---

### 3. Separate Event Count and Unique Vessel Count

### 同时统计事件数量与独立船舶数量

Recommended variables include:

```text
hormuz_tanker_transit_events
hormuz_unique_tankers
hormuz_transits_per_tanker
```

可以构造：

```text
hormuz_tanker_transit_events
hormuz_unique_tankers
hormuz_transits_per_tanker
```

where:

[
transits_per_tanker_t
=====================

\frac{
\text{transit events}_t
}{
\text{unique tankers}_t
}
]

This separates:

* More vessels entering the route;
* The same vessels making more repeated trips;
* Changes in vessel turnover.

这样可以区分：

* 更多油轮进入航线；
* 相同油轮重复通行次数增加；
* 船舶周转率发生变化。

---

### 4. Add Capacity-Weighted Features / 增加运力加权特征

Vessel count alone treats a small tanker and a VLCC equally.

单纯船舶数量会将小型油轮和 VLCC 视为相同的一艘船。

Recommended capacity-related features include:

```text
total_tanker_dwt
mean_tanker_dwt
median_tanker_dwt
vlcc_count
suezmax_count
aframax_count
capacity_weighted_tanker_count
```

建议构造：

```text
total_tanker_dwt
mean_tanker_dwt
median_tanker_dwt
vlcc_count
suezmax_count
aframax_count
capacity_weighted_tanker_count
```

For example:

[
CapacityWeightedActivity_t
==========================

\sum_{v=1}^{N_t}DWT_v
]

This is more economically informative than treating every tanker equally.

这比对所有油轮赋予相同权重更接近实际运输能力。

---

### 5. Construct Congestion and Dwell-Time Features

### 构造拥堵与停留时间特征

Recommended variables include:

```text
mean_port_dwell_hours
median_port_dwell_hours
anchorage_waiting_hours
berth_time_hours
long_dwell_share
dwell_time_zscore
```

建议变量包括：

```text
mean_port_dwell_hours
median_port_dwell_hours
anchorage_waiting_hours
berth_time_hours
long_dwell_share
dwell_time_zscore
```

A long-dwell share can be defined as:

[
LongDwellShare_t
================

\frac{
N(duration_v>\tau)
}{
N_t
}
]

where (\tau) is a threshold such as 48 or 72 hours.

其中，(\tau) 可以设置为 48 小时或 72 小时，用于识别异常长时间停留。

---

### 6. Use Changes and Anomalies Rather Than Levels Alone

### 不仅使用绝对水平，还应使用变化率和异常值

Absolute shipping levels are strongly influenced by long-term port size and route importance.

航运绝对水平会受到港口长期规模和航线重要性的影响。

Potential predictive features include:

```text
tanker_count_change_1w
tanker_count_change_4w
unique_tanker_growth
total_dwt_change
dwell_time_change
tanker_count_zscore_12w
dwell_time_anomaly
```

可以构造：

```text
tanker_count_change_1w
tanker_count_change_4w
unique_tanker_growth
total_dwt_change
dwell_time_change
tanker_count_zscore_12w
dwell_time_anomaly
```

For example:

[
Growth_t
========

\frac{x_t-x_{t-1}}{x_{t-1}}
]

and:

[
Z_t
===

\frac{x_t-\mu_{t-k:t-1}}{\sigma_{t-k:t-1}}
]

These variables may better capture:

* Sudden export reductions;
* Sanctions;
* Port disruptions;
* OPEC policy changes;
* Chokepoint risk.

这些变量更有可能识别：

* 突然的出口下降；
* 制裁影响；
* 港口中断；
* OPEC 政策变化；
* 航运咽喉风险。

## Implications for Forecast Design / 对预测设计的启示

### 1. Use Lagged Shipping Variables / 使用滞后航运变量

The dissertation should estimate:

[
Shipping_t
\rightarrow
OilPrice_{t+h}
]

rather than relying on contemporaneous relationships:

[
Shipping_t
\rightarrow
OilPrice_t
]

本项目应研究：

[
\text{当期航运活动}
\rightarrow
\text{未来油价}
]

而不是主要依赖同期关系。

Recommended lags for weekly prediction include:

```text
tanker_count_lag1
tanker_count_lag2
tanker_count_lag4
tanker_count_lag8
total_dwt_lag1
dwell_time_lag1
```

建议测试：

* 1 周滞后；
* 2 周滞后；
* 4 周滞后；
* 8 周滞后。

Different features may have different transmission periods:

* Chokepoint flows may reflect immediate supply movement;
* Export-port activity may lead physical delivery by several weeks;
* Port congestion may affect short-term volatility and risk premia.

不同航运变量的传导周期可能不同：

* 航道通行量可能较快反映供应流；
* 出口港活动可能领先实际交付数周；
* 港口拥堵可能主要影响短期波动率和风险溢价。

---

### 2. Avoid Contemporaneous Information Leakage

### 避免同期信息泄漏

All feature timestamps should reflect when the information would actually have been observable.

所有特征时间戳都应反映该信息在现实中真正可获得的时间。

For example:

* AIS observations from week (t);
* Feature aggregation completed after the end of week (t);
* Prediction generated for week (t+1).

例如：

* 使用第 (t) 周的 AIS 记录；
* 在第 (t) 周结束后完成特征聚合；
* 预测第 (t+1) 周的油价或收益率。

Any publication, processing or reporting delay should be incorporated into the lag structure.

如果数据存在发布延迟、处理延迟或报告延迟，也应反映在滞后结构中。

### English

Mi et al. (2022) extend the application of AIS data to the crude-oil shipping market by constructing a monthly panel of tanker activity across 545 oil-loading ports in major crude-oil-exporting countries.
