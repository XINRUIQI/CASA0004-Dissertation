## Research Motivation / 研究动机

### English

Forecasting studies frequently compare models using numerical measures such as RMSE, MAE, or mean squared prediction error. However, a lower observed error does not necessarily imply that one model is genuinely more accurate in the population. The observed difference may simply be caused by sampling variation in a particular out-of-sample period.

Existing forecast-comparison procedures were often based on restrictive assumptions, including:

- normally distributed forecast errors;
- zero-mean forecast errors;
- no serial correlation;
- no contemporaneous correlation between competing forecast errors;
- quadratic loss, usually mean squared prediction error.

Diebold and Mariano therefore propose a general statistical test of the null hypothesis that two forecasts have equal expected predictive accuracy.

### 中文

预测研究经常使用 RMSE、MAE 或均方预测误差等指标比较模型。但是，一个模型在特定测试样本中的误差较小，并不一定意味着它在总体上确实具有更高的预测精度。这种差异也可能只是特定样本外时期中的随机波动。

早期的预测比较方法通常依赖较强的假设，例如：

- 预测误差服从正态分布；
- 预测误差均值为零；
- 预测误差不存在序列相关；
- 两个竞争模型的预测误差不存在同期相关；
- 只能使用平方损失或均方预测误差衡量准确性。

Diebold 和 Mariano 因此提出了一种更加一般化的统计检验，用于检验两个预测方法的期望预测精度是否相等。

## Core Method / 核心方法

### English

- **Method**: Diebold–Mariano forecast accuracy test.
- **Purpose**: Test whether two competing forecasts have equal expected loss.
- **Forecast errors**:

  \[
  e_{1t}=y_t-\hat{y}_{1t}
  \]

  \[
  e_{2t}=y_t-\hat{y}_{2t}
  \]

- **Loss functions**:

  \[
  L(e_{1t}), \quad L(e_{2t})
  \]

- **Loss differential**:

  \[
  d_t=L(e_{1t})-L(e_{2t})
  \]

- **Null hypothesis**:

  \[
  H_0:E(d_t)=0
  \]

- **Alternative hypothesis**:

  \[
  H_1:E(d_t)\neq 0
  \]

- **DM statistic**:

  \[
  DM=
  \frac{\bar d}
  {\sqrt{\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(\bar d)}}
  \]

  where

  \[
  \bar d=\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}d_t
  \]

- **Asymptotic distribution**:

  \[
  DM\xrightarrow{d}N(0,1)
  \]

The variance of the average loss differential is estimated using the long-run variance, or equivalently the spectral density of the loss differential at frequency zero.

### 中文

- **方法**：Diebold–Mariano 预测精度检验。
- **目的**：检验两个竞争预测模型是否具有相同的期望损失。
- **预测误差**：

  \[
  e_{1t}=y_t-\hat{y}_{1t}
  \]

  \[
  e_{2t}=y_t-\hat{y}_{2t}
  \]

- **损失函数**：

  \[
  L(e_{1t}), \quad L(e_{2t})
  \]

- **逐期损失差**：

  \[
  d_t=L(e_{1t})-L(e_{2t})
  \]

- **原假设**：

  \[
  H_0:E(d_t)=0
  \]

  即两个模型具有相同的期望预测损失。

- **备择假设**：

  \[
  H_1:E(d_t)\neq 0
  \]

  即两个模型的期望预测损失不同。

- **DM 统计量**：

  \[
  DM=
  \frac{\bar d}
  {\sqrt{\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(\bar d)}}
  \]

  其中：

  \[
  \bar d=\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}d_t
  \]

- **渐近分布**：

  \[
  DM\xrightarrow{d}N(0,1)
  \]

平均损失差的方差通过损失差序列的长期方差进行估计，也可以表示为损失差序列在零频率处的谱密度。

## Loss Functions / 损失函数

### English

A major contribution of the paper is that forecast accuracy does not have to be measured only by mean squared prediction error.

The framework allows an arbitrary loss function.

#### Squared-error loss

\[
L(e_t)=e_t^2
\]

This loss corresponds to MSE and RMSE and places a large penalty on extreme forecast errors.

#### Absolute-error loss

\[
L(e_t)=|e_t|
\]

This loss corresponds to MAE and is less sensitive to extreme observations.

#### Asymmetric loss

The loss function can assign different costs to overprediction and underprediction:

\[
L(e_t)=
\begin{cases}
\alpha |e_t|, & e_t>0\\
\beta |e_t|, & e_t\leq 0
\end{cases}
\]

where \(\alpha\neq\beta\).

This allows forecast evaluation to reflect the economic or decision-making consequences of different types of error.

### 中文

本文的一项重要贡献是：预测准确性不必只能通过均方预测误差衡量。

DM 检验允许使用任意形式的损失函数。

#### 平方误差损失

\[
L(e_t)=e_t^2
\]

平方损失对应 MSE 和 RMSE，并且会对极端预测误差施加较大的惩罚。

#### 绝对误差损失

\[
L(e_t)=|e_t|
\]

绝对损失对应 MAE，对极端异常值的敏感程度低于平方损失。

#### 非对称损失

损失函数可以对高估和低估设置不同的成本：

\[
L(e_t)=
\begin{cases}
\alpha |e_t|, & e_t>0\\
\beta |e_t|, & e_t\leq 0
\end{cases}
\]

其中：

\[
\alpha\neq\beta
\]

这使得预测评价可以反映不同预测错误在经济决策中的实际成本。

## Serial Correlation and Long-Run Variance / 序列相关与长期方差

### English

Forecast-loss differentials are often serially correlated, especially when forecasts are produced for horizons longer than one period.

For an \(h\)-step-ahead forecast, overlapping forecast horizons may produce an approximately MA(\(h-1\)) forecast-error structure.

The variance of \(\bar d\) must therefore account for autocovariances in the loss differential:

\[
\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(\bar d)
=
\frac{1}{T}
\left[
\hat\gamma_d(0)
+
2\sum_{k=1}^{K}w_k\hat\gamma_d(k)
\right]
\]

where:

- \(K\) is the truncation lag;
- \(w_k\) is a weighting function;
- the paper discusses the Bartlett window.

A common practical choice for an \(h\)-step forecast is:

\[
K=h-1
\]

although the appropriate lag structure should depend on the forecast design and the dependence observed in the loss differential.

### 中文

预测损失差序列通常会存在序列相关，尤其是在预测期限超过一期时。

对于 \(h\) 步预测，由于相邻预测窗口可能发生重叠，预测误差可能近似具有 MA(\(h-1\)) 结构。

因此，在估计 \(\bar d\) 的方差时，必须考虑损失差序列的自协方差：

\[
\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(\bar d)
=
\frac{1}{T}
\left[
\hat\gamma_d(0)
+
2\sum_{k=1}^{K}w_k\hat\gamma_d(k)
\right]
\]

其中：

- \(K\) 为截断滞后阶数；
- \(w_k\) 为权重函数；
- 论文讨论了 Bartlett window。

对于 \(h\) 步预测，一种常见的实践选择是：

\[
K=h-1
\]

但最终的滞后结构还应根据预测设计以及损失差序列的实际依赖关系确定。

## Models Compared in the Simulation / 模拟实验比较的方法

### English

The Monte Carlo experiment compares four forecast-accuracy tests:

1. **F test**
   - Compares forecast-error variances.
   - Requires restrictive assumptions such as normality and independence.

2. **Morgan–Granger–Newbold test (MGN)**
   - Uses an orthogonal transformation of the competing forecast errors.
   - Can address some contemporaneous correlation but remains sensitive to other violations.

3. **Meese–Rogoff test (MR)**
   - Uses an estimated covariance matrix to account for dependence.
   - Can be severely undersized in small samples.

4. **Diebold–Mariano test (DM)**
   - Tests whether the mean loss differential equals zero.
   - Permits general loss functions and relatively general forecast-error processes.

### 中文

Monte Carlo 实验比较了四种预测精度检验：

1. **F 检验**
   - 比较两个模型的预测误差方差。
   - 依赖正态性、独立性等较严格假设。

2. **Morgan–Granger–Newbold 检验（MGN）**
   - 对两个预测误差进行正交变换。
   - 可以处理部分同期相关问题，但对其他假设违反仍较敏感。

3. **Meese–Rogoff 检验（MR）**
   - 使用估计的协方差矩阵处理预测误差依赖性。
   - 在小样本中可能严重 undersized，即实际拒绝率过低。

4. **Diebold–Mariano 检验（DM）**
   - 检验平均损失差是否等于零。
   - 允许一般损失函数以及更一般的预测误差过程。

## Key Findings / 主要发现

### 1. Forecast accuracy differences require formal statistical testing  
### 1. 预测精度差异需要进行正式统计检验

#### English

A model having a lower RMSE or MAE in one test sample is not sufficient evidence that it is more accurate in the population. The difference must be evaluated relative to the sampling variability and temporal dependence of the loss differential.

#### 中文

某个模型在一个测试样本中的 RMSE 或 MAE 更低，并不足以证明其总体预测精度更高。必须将平均损失差与损失差自身的随机波动和时间依赖性结合起来进行检验。

---

### 2. The test can accommodate general loss functions  
### 2. DM 检验允许使用一般损失函数

#### English

The DM framework is not restricted to squared prediction errors. It can be used with absolute loss, asymmetric loss, and other economically meaningful loss functions.

#### 中文

DM 检验并不局限于平方预测误差，还可以使用绝对损失、非对称损失以及其他具有经济意义的损失函数。

---

### 3. The test permits realistic forecast-error behaviour  
### 3. DM 检验允许更符合现实的预测误差特征

#### English

The proposed procedure allows forecast errors to be:

- non-Gaussian;
- non-zero mean;
- serially correlated;
- contemporaneously correlated across competing models.

This makes the test more suitable for economic and financial forecasting than traditional variance-comparison procedures.

#### 中文

本文提出的方法允许预测误差具有以下特征：

- 非正态分布；
- 非零均值；
- 序列相关；
- 不同模型误差之间存在同期相关。

因此，相比传统的预测误差方差比较方法，DM 检验更适合经济和金融时间序列预测。

---

### 4. DM performs comparatively well in the Monte Carlo experiments  
### 4. DM 检验在 Monte Carlo 实验中总体表现较好

#### English

The F, MGN, and MR tests become seriously distorted under various combinations of serial correlation, contemporaneous correlation, and non-Gaussian errors.

The DM test generally maintains an empirical size closer to the nominal significance level, particularly under non-Gaussian forecast errors.

However, the original DM test may still be moderately oversized in small samples, especially when serial dependence is strong.

#### 中文

当预测误差存在序列相关、同期相关或非高斯分布时，F、MGN 和 MR 检验的经验显著性水平可能出现严重失真。

相比之下，DM 检验的经验拒绝率总体更接近设定的名义显著性水平，尤其是在非高斯预测误差条件下表现更稳健。

但是，原始 DM 检验在小样本中仍可能出现一定程度的 oversized，即第一类错误率高于名义显著性水平，强序列相关下尤其需要谨慎。

---

### 5. Better predictive accuracy does not imply forecast encompassing  
### 5. 更高的预测精度不等于完全包含其他模型的信息

#### English

The authors emphasise that superior predictive accuracy does not necessarily imply that forecasts from another model contain no additional information.

The DM test evaluates average predictive loss. It does not directly test:

- forecast encompassing;
- causal mechanisms;
- variable relevance;
- whether one model contains all information from another model.

#### 中文

作者特别强调，一个模型具有更高的预测精度，并不意味着另一个模型的预测完全不包含额外信息。

DM 检验衡量的是平均预测损失差异，它并不直接检验：

- forecast encompassing，即预测信息包含关系；
- 因果机制；
- 单个变量的重要性；
- 一个模型是否完全包含另一个模型的全部信息。

## Interpretation of the DM Test / DM 检验结果解释

Assume that the loss differential is defined as:

假设损失差定义为：

\[
d_t=L(e_{\text{new},t})-L(e_{\text{baseline},t})
\]

| Mean loss differential and test result | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| \(\bar d<0\) and \(p<0.05\) | The new model has significantly lower expected loss |
| \(\bar d>0\) and \(p<0.05\) | The new model has significantly higher expected loss |
| \(p\geq0.05\) | Insufficient evidence that the two models have different expected predictive accuracy |

| 平均损失差和检验结果 | 解释 |
|---|---|
| \(\bar d<0\) 且 \(p<0.05\) | 新模型的期望预测损失显著低于基准模型 |
| \(\bar d>0\) 且 \(p<0.05\) | 新模型的期望预测损失显著高于基准模型 |
| \(p\geq0.05\) | 没有足够证据认为两个模型的期望预测精度不同 |

A statistically significant p-value alone does not establish that the new model is better. The direction of the average loss differential must also be reported.

仅凭显著的 p-value 不能证明新模型更好，还必须结合平均损失差的正负方向进行解释。

## Suggested Loss Differentials for the Dissertation / 项目建议使用的损失差

### Squared-error loss / 平方误差损失

For comparing an augmented model with M1:

比较扩展模型和 M1 时：

\[
d_t^{SE}
=
e_{\text{augmented},t}^{2}
-
e_{\text{M1},t}^{2}
\]

This corresponds to the RMSE/MSE evaluation.

该损失差对应 RMSE/MSE 评价。

A negative mean value indicates that the augmented model has lower squared prediction loss.

平均值为负表示扩展模型具有更低的平方预测损失。

---

### Absolute-error loss / 绝对误差损失

\[
d_t^{AE}
=
|e_{\text{augmented},t}|
-
|e_{\text{M1},t}|
\]

This corresponds to the MAE evaluation.

该损失差对应 MAE 评价。

Using both squared and absolute losses helps determine whether improvements are robust to extreme oil-price observations.

同时使用平方损失和绝对损失，可以判断预测改善是否受到少数极端油价时期的主导。

## Practical Requirements for the Dissertation / 项目实施要求

### English

For a valid DM comparison, all models must:

1. predict the same target variable;
2. use the same forecast horizon;
3. produce forecasts for the same out-of-sample dates;
4. use the same rolling or expanding forecast origins;
5. be evaluated with the same loss function;
6. respect the real-time availability of all predictors;
7. avoid future-information leakage;
8. use an appropriate long-run variance estimator.

The test should be conducted separately for each forecasting horizon.

For an \(h\)-step-ahead overlapping forecast, the long-run variance estimator should allow for serial correlation, commonly using at least \(h-1\) lags where justified.

### 中文

为了保证 DM 比较有效，所有模型必须：

1. 预测相同的目标变量；
2. 使用相同的预测期限；
3. 在完全相同的样本外日期上产生预测；
4. 使用相同的滚动窗口或扩展窗口预测起点；
5. 使用相同的损失函数进行评价；
6. 遵守所有预测变量的真实发布时间；
7. 避免未来信息泄漏；
8. 使用适当的长期方差估计方法。

每一个预测期限都应单独运行 DM 检验。

对于 \(h\) 步重叠预测，应在长期方差估计中处理序列相关；在预测设计允许时，通常至少考虑 \(h-1\) 阶滞后。

## Limitations / 局限性

### 1. Asymptotic test / 渐近检验

#### English

The original DM test relies on an asymptotic normal approximation. Its finite-sample performance may be imperfect when the out-of-sample evaluation period is short.

#### 中文

原始 DM 检验依赖渐近正态近似。当样本外评价期较短时，其有限样本表现可能不理想。

---

### 2. Possible small-sample size distortion / 小样本显著性水平失真

#### English

The Monte Carlo experiments show that the DM test can be moderately oversized in small samples, particularly when loss differentials are strongly serially correlated.

#### 中文

Monte Carlo 实验表明，在小样本以及损失差存在较强序列相关时，DM 检验可能出现一定程度的 oversized。

---

### 3. Pairwise comparison only / 主要用于两两比较

#### English

The standard DM test compares two forecasts at a time. Repeated comparisons among M1, M2, M3, and M4 create a multiple-testing issue.

#### 中文

标准 DM 检验一次比较两个预测模型。对 M1、M2、M3 和 M4 进行大量两两检验时，会产生多重检验问题。

---

### 4. Does not identify the source of improvement / 不能识别改善来源

#### English

A significant result indicates that expected predictive losses differ, but it does not explain which variables or mechanisms caused the difference.

#### 中文

显著的 DM 检验结果只能说明两个模型的期望预测损失不同，不能直接解释哪些变量或经济机制导致了这种差异。

---

### 5. Does not establish causality / 不能证明因果关系

#### English

The test evaluates predictive accuracy rather than causal effects. A model may forecast well because its predictors contain timely information without having a causal relationship with oil prices.

#### 中文

DM 检验评价的是预测精度，而不是因果效应。某个变量可能因为包含及时信息而提升预测，但这并不证明它对油价具有因果作用。

---

### 6. Sensitive to loss-function choice / 结论依赖损失函数

#### English

The ranking of models may change when squared, absolute, directional, or asymmetric losses are used.

#### 中文

在平方损失、绝对损失、方向损失或非对称损失下，模型之间的相对表现可能发生变化。

---

### 7. Long-run variance estimation / 长期方差估计问题

#### English

The result may be affected by the selected truncation lag, weighting scheme, and long-run variance estimator.

#### 中文

检验结果可能受到截断滞后阶数、权重方案和长期方差估计方法的影响。

---

### 8. Nested-model issue / 嵌套模型问题

#### English

M2, M3, and M4 may be nested extensions of M1 because they add predictors to the same baseline model.

The standard DM test was developed as a general comparison of forecast losses, but conventional asymptotic inference can be distorted in nested-model comparisons under squared-error loss.

Later methods, such as the Clark–West test, can therefore be included as a robustness check when comparing nested forecasting models.

#### 中文

由于 M2、M3 和 M4 是在 M1 的基础上增加预测变量，它们可能属于嵌套模型。

标准 DM 检验用于一般预测损失比较，但在平方损失下比较嵌套模型时，传统渐近推断可能出现偏差。

因此，可以进一步使用后续提出的 Clark–West 检验作为嵌套模型比较的稳健性检验。

---

## Notes for Dissertation Integration / 论文整合建议

- Use the DM test as the principal statistical test for pairwise out-of-sample forecast comparisons.
- 将 DM 检验作为样本外预测两两比较的主要统计检验。

- Report RMSE and MAE together with the mean loss differential, DM statistic, and p-value.
- 在报告 RMSE 和 MAE 的同时，报告平均损失差、DM statistic 和 p-value。

- Define the direction of the loss differential explicitly.
- 明确定义损失差的计算方向。

- Do not interpret \(p<0.05\) as an improvement unless the mean loss differential has the expected sign.
- 不能仅凭 \(p<0.05\) 判断模型得到改善，还必须检查平均损失差的方向。

- Conduct separate tests for each forecast horizon.
- 对每一个预测期限分别进行检验。

- Use both squared-error and absolute-error losses as robustness checks.
- 同时使用平方损失和绝对损失进行稳健性检验。

- Ensure that all models share exactly the same out-of-sample evaluation period.
- 确保所有模型使用完全相同的样本外评价时期。

- Combine the DM test with SHAP analysis:
  - DM determines whether a model is significantly more accurate.
  - SHAP explains which variables contribute to its predictions.
- 将 DM 检验与 SHAP 分析结合：
  - DM 判断模型是否显著更加准确；
  - SHAP 解释哪些变量推动模型预测。

- Use Clark–West as an additional robustness test when M2–M4 are treated as nested extensions of M1.
- 当 M2–M4 被视为 M1 的嵌套扩展时，可以加入 Clark–West 检验作为稳健性分析。

- Consider correcting for multiple comparisons if many model pairs, horizons, and loss functions are tested.
- 如果同时比较大量模型组合、预测期限和损失函数，应考虑多重检验校正。

### English

The paper changes the evaluation question from:

> Which model has the lowest observed RMSE?

to:

> Does the addition of new information produce a statistically significant and stable reduction in out-of-sample forecast loss?

For this dissertation, the DM test provides a direct way to determine whether remote-sensing, shipping, NLP, or combined multimodal information generates genuine incremental forecasting value beyond the conventional M1 baseline.

### 中文

这篇论文将模型评价问题从：

> 哪个模型在测试样本中的 RMSE 最低？

提升为：

> 新增信息是否在样本外产生了统计显著且相对稳定的预测损失下降？

对于本石油价格预测项目，DM 检验可以直接判断遥感、航运、NLP 或综合多模态信息是否在传统 M1 基准模型之外提供了真实的增量预测价值。
