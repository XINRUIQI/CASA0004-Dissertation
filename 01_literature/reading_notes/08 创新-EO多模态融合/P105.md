# Reading Note — Paper 105: CROMA (Remote Sensing / Multimodal EO Foundation Model)

## Citation

Fuller, A., Millard, K., & Green, J. (2023). *CROMA: Remote Sensing Representations with Contrastive Radar-Optical Masked Autoencoders*. NeurIPS 2023 / arXiv:2311.00566.

Fuller, A., Millard, K., & Green, J. (2023). *CROMA：基于对比式雷达-光学掩码自编码器的遥感表征学习*. NeurIPS 2023 / arXiv:2311.00566.

---

## Core Method / 核心方法

- **Model / 模型**: CROMA (Contrastive Radar-Optical Masked Autoencoder), a multimodal Earth Observation representation learning framework combining radar-optical contrastive learning with multimodal masked autoencoding.

- **模型结构**: CROMA 由三个 encoder 组成：  
  1. **Radar encoder**: 编码 Sentinel-1 SAR 雷达影像；  
  2. **Optical encoder**: 编码 Sentinel-2 多光谱光学影像；  
  3. **Radar-optical multimodal encoder**: 通过 cross-attention 融合 radar 和 optical 表征，生成 joint multimodal representation。  

- **Training objectives / 训练目标**:  
  1. **Radar↔Optical contrastive learning**: 将空间和时间对齐的 Sentinel-1 / Sentinel-2 样本作为 positive pairs，使模型学习跨传感器共享的地表语义。  
  2. **Multimodal masked autoencoding**: 分别 mask radar 和 optical patches，并通过 multimodal encoder + lightweight decoder 重建被遮挡的 patches，使模型保留细粒度空间与纹理信息。  

- **Data / 数据集**: SSL4EO, containing 1 million paired Sentinel-1 GRD and Sentinel-2 L2A samples. Sentinel-1 includes VV and VH backscatter channels; Sentinel-2 includes 12 multispectral surface reflectance bands.

- **输入变量 / Input variables**:  
  - Sentinel-1 SAR: VV, VH backscatter channels.  
  - Sentinel-2 optical: 12 multispectral bands.  
  - Patch-level masked radar / optical inputs.  
  - Outputs include radar-only, optical-only, and radar-optical joint representations.

- **Key architectural innovation / 关键结构创新**:  
  - **2D-ALiBi**: extends ALiBi positional bias to 2D satellite image patches, helping the model generalise to larger image sizes at test time.  
  - **X-ALiBi**: extends ALiBi to cross-attention, improving spatially aligned radar-optical fusion.  

- **Target / 目标任务**: The paper does not forecast crude oil prices. Its target is to learn transferable remote sensing representations for downstream classification, segmentation, probing, clustering, and nearest-neighbour retrieval tasks.

- **目标说明**: 该论文并不是油价预测论文，而是遥感基础模型 / 表征学习论文。它的目标是学习可迁移的 Earth Observation representation，为分类、分割、linear probing、nonlinear probing、kNN 和 K-means 等下游任务提供通用特征。

## Key Findings / 主要发现

1. **CROMA shows strong performance across remote sensing benchmarks.**  
   CROMA outperforms previous multispectral optical foundation models such as SatMAE, MAE, DINO, and I-JEPA across multiple classification and segmentation benchmarks.

   **CROMA 在多个遥感 benchmark 上表现优于已有方法。**  
   论文在 BigEarthNet、fMoW-Sentinel、EuroSAT、Canadian Cropland、DFC2020、DW-Expert、MARIDA 等数据集上评估 CROMA，结果显示其整体优于 SatMAE、MAE、DINO、I-JEPA 等基线模型。

2. **Combining contrastive learning and masked autoencoding is better than using either objective alone.**  
   The ablation study shows that removing either the contrastive objective or the MAE objective significantly reduces downstream performance.

   **对比学习 + 掩码自编码的组合优于单独使用其中一种目标。**  
   消融实验表明，只用 MAE 或只用 contrastive learning 都会导致分类和分割性能下降，说明二者在空间对齐的多模态遥感数据上具有互补性。

3. **Radar and optical modalities are complementary.**  
   Sentinel-2 optical imagery captures material and spectral information, while Sentinel-1 SAR captures geometry, roughness, and backscatter properties. Their spatial alignment enables effective cross-modal representation learning.

   **SAR 与 optical 是互补模态。**  
   Sentinel-2 光学影像更擅长表达地物材料、光谱反射、植被、水体和地表覆盖；Sentinel-1 SAR 更擅长表达几何结构、粗糙度、雷达散射和云下观测信息。两者空间对齐，因此适合做 cross-modal learning。

4. **CROMA supports optional multimodality.**  
   The model can be used when only radar is available, only optical is available, or both modalities are available. This is especially useful for remote sensing tasks where optical images may be missing or cloud-contaminated.

   **CROMA 支持可选多模态输入。**  
   下游任务中可以使用 radar-only、optical-only 或 radar-optical joint representation。这对遥感任务很重要，因为光学影像经常受云遮挡影响，而 SAR 可以在云下继续观测。

5. **2D-ALiBi improves spatial generalisation.**  
   The paper shows that 2D-ALiBi enables the model to extrapolate to images up to 17.6× larger than the training image size with only limited performance degradation.

   **2D-ALiBi 提高了模型对不同 AOI 尺寸的泛化能力。**  
   遥感下游任务中的图像裁剪尺寸往往不同。2D-ALiBi 通过在 attention matrix 中加入基于 patch 距离的空间偏置，使模型能够处理比训练阶段更大的图像区域。

6. **Frozen representations are useful.**  
   CROMA performs well under frozen linear probing, nonlinear probing, kNN, and K-means evaluations, suggesting that the pretrained encoder itself learns meaningful transferable representations.

   **冻结表征本身具有较强可迁移性。**  
   CROMA 在 frozen linear probing、nonlinear probing、kNN 和 K-means 中表现良好，说明模型学到的遥感 embedding 并不是只能依赖大规模 finetuning，而是具有直接迁移价值。

## How It Can Be Used in the Oil Price Forecasting Project / 对石油价格预测项目的具体借鉴

### 1. Use CROMA as the methodological basis for M4 remote sensing features  
### 1. 将 CROMA 作为 M4 遥感特征的方法依据

This dissertation can use CROMA to justify the use of pretrained multimodal EO encoders. The key argument is that remote sensing imagery should not be treated as raw pixels directly fed into a small oil-price dataset. Instead, a large pretrained EO model can extract robust spatial representations from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 imagery.

本项目可以用 CROMA 支撑 M4 遥感模块：不要直接把原始遥感像素塞进油价预测模型，而是先用大规模自监督预训练的遥感基础模型提取 AOI-level embedding，再将这些 embedding 作为结构化时序特征输入预测模型。

Possible pipeline:

```text
Sentinel-1 / Sentinel-2 images around oil-related AOIs
→ CROMA / Prithvi / SatMAE pretrained encoder
→ AOI-level remote sensing embeddings
→ weekly or monthly aggregation
→ fusion with M1–M3 variables
→ Brent / WTI price or return forecasting
```

---

### 2. Design M4 as SAR-only, optical-only, and SAR+optical branches  
### 2. 将 M4 设计成 SAR-only、Optical-only 和 SAR+Optical 三个分支

CROMA's architecture suggests that the dissertation should not treat remote sensing as a single homogeneous modality. Instead, SAR and optical imagery can be treated as two related but distinct modalities.

CROMA 的结构说明，遥感不是一个单一模态，而是可以继续拆分为 SAR 和 optical 两个子模态。对于你的项目来说，可以设计三种 M4 输入：

```text
M4-optical: Sentinel-2 optical embeddings
M4-SAR: Sentinel-1 SAR embeddings
M4-joint: Sentinel-1 + Sentinel-2 fused embeddings
```

This allows the dissertation to test whether the predictive signal comes mainly from optical imagery, SAR imagery, or their joint representation.

这样可以进一步回答：油价预测中真正有用的是光学影像、SAR 影像，还是二者融合后的表征。

---

### 3. Use frozen encoders to avoid overfitting  
### 3. 优先使用 frozen encoder，避免样本量不足导致过拟合

The oil price forecasting sample is much smaller than the dataset used to train CROMA. Therefore, training a remote sensing encoder from scratch is unrealistic and likely to overfit. A more defensible strategy is to freeze the pretrained encoder and only train a lightweight downstream forecasting head.

油价预测样本通常是周频或月频，样本量远小于 CROMA 的预训练数据。因此，本项目不适合从零训练遥感大模型。更合理的设计是：

```text
Frozen CROMA encoder
→ fixed AOI embeddings
→ XGBoost / Ridge / LSTM / TCN / Transformer forecasting head
```

This is also useful for dissertation writing because it makes the M4 component computationally feasible and methodologically grounded.

这也更适合 dissertation：方法上有文献依据，计算上可行，实证上也更容易做 ablation。

---

### 4. Convert image embeddings into time-series predictors  
### 4. 将遥感 embedding 转化为时间序列预测变量

CROMA outputs image-level or patch-level representations. For oil price forecasting, these need to be converted into time-indexed numerical features.

CROMA 输出的是 image-level 或 patch-level representation。你的油价预测模型需要的是按周或按月排列的数值特征，因此可以做如下转换：

```text
For each AOI and time t:
Sentinel image → CROMA encoder → embedding vector
```

Then construct:

```text
remote_embedding_mean_{AOI,t}
remote_embedding_pca1_{AOI,t}
remote_embedding_pca2_{AOI,t}
remote_anomaly_score_{AOI,t}
remote_change_score_{AOI,t}
sar_optical_distance_{AOI,t}
```

Possible economic interpretations:

| Feature | Interpretation |
|---------|----------------|
| **AOI embedding level** | General visual state of an oil port, chokepoint, terminal, or storage site |
| **Embedding change** | Change in port activity, infrastructure, vessel presence, or surface conditions |
| **Anomaly score** | Unusual remote-sensing pattern potentially linked to supply disruption or congestion |
| **SAR-optical divergence** | Potential data-quality issue, cloud contamination, or modality-specific abnormal signal |
| **Patch-level aggregation** | Localised changes within a port, refinery, storage area, or maritime chokepoint |

---

### 5. Link CROMA to M3 shipping variables  
### 5. 将 CROMA 与 M3 航运变量连接起来

CROMA is especially relevant to the dissertation's M3 shipping / chokepoint module because satellite imagery can provide visual-spatial context around the same maritime nodes used in PortWatch or GFW.

CROMA 与你的 M3 航运变量可以自然连接。M3 使用 PortWatch / GFW 描述船舶活动，M4 可以用遥感影像描述同一 AOI 的视觉空间状态。

Example integration:

```text
M3:
pw_hormuz_n_tanker
pw_hormuz_capacity_tanker
gfw_hormuz_total_hours

M4:
croma_hormuz_sar_embedding
croma_hormuz_optical_embedding
croma_hormuz_joint_embedding
croma_hormuz_anomaly_score
```

This helps test whether remote sensing imagery adds information beyond AIS-derived vessel counts and capacity measures.

这可以帮助检验：遥感图像是否在 AIS 船舶计数和 capacity 之外提供额外预测信息。

## Limitations / 局限性

1. **Not an oil price forecasting paper.**  
   CROMA does not directly model Brent, WTI, crude oil returns, volatility, or energy markets. Its contribution is representation learning for remote sensing.

   **不是油价预测论文。**  
   CROMA 本身并没有预测 Brent / WTI，也没有建模能源市场变量。它只能作为 M4 遥感表征提取的方法支撑，不能直接证明遥感能够预测油价。

2. **Static image focus.**  
   The paper mainly focuses on static Sentinel-1 / Sentinel-2 image pairs rather than long temporal sequences. Oil price forecasting, however, requires time-series aggregation and temporal modelling.

   **主要处理静态影像，而不是完整时间序列。**  
   你的项目需要把遥感影像转成周频或月频时序特征，并进一步接入 TCN、LSTM、Transformer、XGBoost 或 Ridge 等预测模型。

3. **Downstream tasks are classification and segmentation, not economic forecasting.**  
   The evaluation benchmarks are remote sensing tasks such as land-cover classification and semantic segmentation, not macroeconomic or commodity-price prediction.

   **下游任务不是经济预测。**  
   论文评估的是分类、分割、聚类和 probing，不是油价预测。因此在 dissertation 中引用时要说明：CROMA 支撑的是遥感表征提取，不是直接支撑油价可预测性。

4. **Potential mismatch between benchmark imagery and oil-related AOIs.**  
   CROMA is trained on broad EO imagery. Oil ports, refineries, storage tanks, and maritime chokepoints may require domain-specific AOI selection or light adaptation.

   **预训练数据与油气 AOI 可能存在领域差异。**  
   CROMA 是通用遥感基础模型，但油港、炼厂、储油罐、海峡、码头等对象具有更强行业特征，可能需要 AOI-specific aggregation、domain adaptation 或人工验证。

5. **Embedding interpretability remains challenging.**  
   CROMA embeddings are high-dimensional and not directly interpretable. Additional methods such as PCA, SHAP on downstream models, anomaly scoring, or visual inspection may be needed.

   **embedding 可解释性有限。**  
   高维遥感 embedding 本身难以解释。你的项目需要通过 PCA、SHAP、permutation importance、anomaly score 或案例图像回看来解释其经济含义。

6. **Computational cost.**  
   Full CROMA pretraining is expensive and uses large-scale GPU resources. The dissertation should therefore rely on pretrained checkpoints rather than training from scratch.

   **计算成本高。**  
   CROMA 预训练需要大规模 GPU 资源。你的项目应优先使用公开预训练模型或冻结 encoder，而不是自行预训练完整模型。

## One-Sentence Summary / 一句话总结

CROMA is a multimodal remote sensing foundation model that learns transferable Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical representations through contrastive learning and masked autoencoding; for this dissertation, it provides the strongest methodological support for building the M4 remote-sensing feature branch and testing whether satellite-derived spatial signals add incremental value to crude oil price forecasting.

CROMA 是一个将 Sentinel-1 SAR 与 Sentinel-2 光学影像结合起来的多模态遥感基础模型，通过“对比学习 + 掩码自编码”学习可迁移表征；对你的石油价格预测项目而言，它最重要的价值是为 M4 遥感模态编码器、SAR/optical 融合、AOI embedding 构建和 M4 消融实验提供方法论支撑。
