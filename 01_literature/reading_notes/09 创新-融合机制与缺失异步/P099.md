## Citation

Shukla, S. N., & Marlin, B. M. (2021). Multi-Time Attention Networks for Irregularly Sampled Time Series. *International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2021)*.

- **arXiv**: [2101.10318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.10318)
- **Published**: ICLR 2021
- **Code**: [https://github.com/reml-lab/mTAN](https://github.com/reml-lab/mTAN)

## Core Method

- **Model**: Multi-Time Attention Network (**mTAN**), implemented through a discretised Multi-Time Attention module (**mTAND**) and embedded within a variational encoder–decoder framework.
- **Problem setting**: Sparse, irregularly sampled and partially observed multivariate time series in which different variables may be observed at different times.
- **Core mechanism**:
  1. Learn a continuous-time embedding for each observation time.
  2. Treat predefined reference times as attention queries.
  3. Treat actual observation times as attention keys.
  4. Treat observed variable values as attention values.
  5. Map irregular observations onto a fixed sequence of reference-time representations.
- **Time embedding**: Combines a learnable linear component with learnable sinusoidal components to represent both non-periodic temporal progression and periodic temporal patterns.
- **Attention design**: Uses multiple time-embedding functions to learn flexible temporal similarity kernels instead of relying on fixed interpolation rules such as linear interpolation or radial basis function kernels.
- **Full architecture**: mTAND module + bidirectional GRU/RNN + variational autoencoder; a GRU and fully connected classifier are added for supervised classification tasks.
- **Tasks**:
  - Time-series interpolation and reconstruction.
  - Whole-series classification.
  - Time-point-level activity classification.
- **Evaluation**:
  - Mean squared error (MSE) for interpolation.
  - Area under the ROC curve (AUC) for mortality classification.
  - Accuracy for human activity classification.
  - Training time per epoch.

## How the mTAN Module Works

For variable \(d\), observations are represented as:

\[
s_d=(t_d,x_d)
\]

where:

- \(t_d\) is the list of actual observation times;
- \(x_d\) is the corresponding list of observed values;
- different variables may have different observation times and different numbers of observations.

The model first embeds continuous time \(t\) as:

\[
\phi_h(t)[i]=
\begin{cases}
\omega_{0h}t+\alpha_{0h}, & i=0 \\
\sin(\omega_{ih}t+\alpha_{ih}), & i>0
\end{cases}
\]

where:

- \(\omega\) and \(\alpha\) are learned parameters;
- the linear component captures non-periodic temporal progression;
- the sinusoidal components capture periodic patterns.

For a reference time \(r_k\):

- **Query**: the reference time \(r_k\);
- **Key**: an observed time \(t_{id}\);
- **Value**: the corresponding observation \(x_{id}\).

The model calculates a learned attention weight:

\[
\kappa_h(r_k,t_{id})
=
\operatorname{softmax}
\left(
\frac{
\phi_h(r_k)W
\left[\phi_h(t_{id})V\right]^\top
}{
\sqrt{d_k}
}
\right)
\]

and creates an interpolated representation:

\[
\hat{x}_{hd}(r_k)
=
\sum_i
\kappa_h(r_k,t_{id})x_{id}
\]

The representations from different variables and time-attention heads are then combined to form a fixed-dimensional embedding at each reference time.

Therefore, mTAN can be interpreted as a:

> **learnable continuous-time interpolation and representation module for sparse and asynchronous multivariate time series.**

## Key Findings

1. **mTAND-Full substantially improves interpolation performance.**

   On the PhysioNet interpolation task, mTAND-Full achieves substantially lower MSE than RNN-VAE and latent ODE models across all tested observation ratios from 50% to 90%.

2. **mTAN achieves state-of-the-art or near-state-of-the-art classification performance.**

   - PhysioNet mortality AUC:
     - mTAND-Full: **0.858**
     - mTAND-Enc: **0.854**
     - ODE-RNN: 0.833

   - MIMIC-III mortality AUC:
     - ODE-RNN: **0.8561**
     - mTAND-Full: 0.8544
     - L-ODE-ODE: 0.8559

   - Human Activity accuracy:
     - mTAND-Full: **0.910**
     - mTAND-Enc: **0.907**
     - ODE-RNN: 0.885

   The results indicate that mTAN is generally best or statistically comparable with the strongest continuous-time baselines.

3. **mTAN is considerably faster than neural ODE approaches.**

   Approximate training time per epoch:

   | Model | Minutes per epoch |
   |---|---:|
   | ODE-RNN | 16.5 |
   | L-ODE-ODE | 22.0 |
   | mTAND-Enc | 0.1 |
   | mTAND-Full | 0.2 |

   mTAND-Full is approximately 80–110 times faster than the strongest ODE-based alternatives because its attention operations can be parallelised and do not require repeated numerical ODE solving.

4. **Learnable continuous-time embeddings outperform fixed positional encodings.**

   Ablation results show that replacing the learned time embedding with a fixed Transformer-style positional encoding reduces classification performance:

   | Dataset | Fixed positional encoding | Learned time embedding |
   |---|---:|---:|
   | PhysioNet | 0.845 | **0.858** |
   | MIMIC-III | 0.843 | **0.854** |

5. **Learned temporal similarity is more flexible than fixed interpolation kernels.**

   Replacing the fixed RBF kernel used in IP-Nets with the learned mTAN similarity kernel improves or maintains classification performance, particularly on PhysioNet and the Human Activity dataset.

6. **Temporally distributed latent states preserve local temporal structure.**

   Instead of compressing an entire irregular sequence into a single latent state, the model learns latent states at several reference times. This helps preserve local peaks, transitions and short-term temporal patterns.

## Necessary Adaptations for Oil-Price Forecasting

### 1. Replace Bidirectional Processing with Causal Processing

The original paper mainly studies interpolation and classification. Its interpolation setting may use observations located both before and after a target time, and its full encoder includes a bidirectional RNN.

For next-week Brent forecasting, the model must not use information that becomes available after the forecasting origin.

A causal attention mask should therefore be imposed:

\[
\kappa(r_k,t_i)=0 \quad \text{if } t_i>r_k
\]


The bidirectional GRU should be replaced by:

- a unidirectional GRU;
- a causal TCN; or
- a causally masked Temporal Transformer.

### 2. Distinguish Event Time from Data-Availability Time

Each observation should ideally contain both:

\[
t_i^{\text{event}}
\]

and:

\[
t_i^{\text{available}}
\]

where:

- \(t_i^{\text{event}}\) is when the underlying event or measurement occurred;
- \(t_i^{\text{available}}\) is when the information became available to the forecaster.

At forecasting origin \(T\), an observation should only be accessible if:

\[
t_i^{\text{available}} \le T
\]

This is particularly important for:

- EIA inventory releases;
- monthly macroeconomic indicators;
- revised economic data;
- delayed satellite-image processing;
- shipping databases with reporting delays.

### 3. Use mTAN as an Alignment Module Rather Than Copying the Full VAE

The dissertation has approximately 20 years of weekly observations, corresponding to roughly 1,000 forecasting dates. This is much smaller than the datasets used in the paper.

A lighter implementation is therefore more appropriate:

```text
Frozen modality encoder
        |
        v
Small causal mTAN
        |
        v
Small GRU / TCN
        |
        v
Forecasting head
```

The complete VAE decoder is optional and may cause overfitting.

### 4. Use Forecasting Rather Than Interpolation Evaluation

The original interpolation task randomly hides observed points. This is not equivalent to forecasting future oil prices.

The dissertation should instead use:

- expanding-window evaluation;
- rolling-window evaluation;
- strict chronological train–validation–test splits;
- real-time data-availability constraints;
- comparison against the no-change forecast \(P_{t+1}=P_t\).

## Potential Dissertation Contribution Inspired by This Paper

The original mTAN framework addresses irregularly sampled numerical time series, mainly in medical interpolation and classification.

This dissertation could extend the framework in three ways:

### Causal Adaptation

Modify mTAN so that each reference week only attends to observations available at or before the forecasting origin.

### Release-Aware Adaptation

Incorporate both event time and publication time to prevent look-ahead bias caused by delayed releases and retrospective data revisions.

### Multimodal Adaptation

Apply separate temporal alignment modules to remote-sensing and shipping representations, and subsequently combine them with financial representations through gated cross-modal attention.

A possible methodological contribution can therefore be stated as:

> **A causal, release-aware and modality-specific continuous-time alignment framework for integrating asynchronously observed market, remote-sensing and shipping signals in next-week Brent oil-price forecasting.**

## Limitations

### No Oil-Price Forecasting Application

The paper does not test crude-oil, commodity or financial-market forecasting. It therefore cannot directly demonstrate that mTAN improves Brent or WTI forecasts.

### Interpolation Is Not Forecasting

Interpolation may use observations on both sides of a missing point, whereas future forecasting can only use information available before the forecasting origin.

### Potential Look-Ahead Bias

The original architecture uses bidirectional RNNs and is not designed for real-time causal forecasting.

### Primarily Numerical Time Series

The paper does not process satellite imagery, spatial networks, vessel trajectories or multimodal data directly.

### No Cross-Modal Fusion

mTAN learns temporal attention within an irregular time series but does not model interactions between financial, remote-sensing and shipping modalities.

### Attention Is Not Causal Explanation

High temporal attention weights indicate predictive relevance within the model but do not establish an economic or causal transmission mechanism.

### Large Original Datasets

The paper uses thousands to tens of thousands of independent sequences. A weekly oil-price dataset is much smaller and may not support the full mTAND-Full architecture.

### No Explicit Publication-Delay Modelling

The paper models measurement time but does not distinguish between when an event occurred and when the information became available to a forecaster.

### Limited Regime Analysis

The model does not explicitly examine whether temporal relevance changes across crises, supply shocks or different market regimes.

- Use it to justify avoiding compulsory forward filling and fixed weekly aggregation for sparse remote-sensing observations.
- Position mTAN as a temporal interface between irregular observations and conventional temporal encoders.
- Do not describe mTAN as a complete multimodal fusion model.

- Explain that the dissertation modifies the original framework through causal masking and data-availability constraints.
- Use a simplified mTAN encoder rather than the complete VAE architecture unless auxiliary reconstruction demonstrably improves out-of-sample forecasting.
- Compare learned continuous-time alignment with weekly aggregation, last-observation carry-forward and fixed interpolation.
- Use modality-level ablation tests to establish whether any performance gain comes from additional data, improved temporal alignment or increased model complexity.
- Evaluate against the no-change benchmark and conduct Diebold–Mariano tests for statistically meaningful forecasting improvements.

# 中文阅读笔记——论文 99：面向不规则采样时间序列的多时间注意力网络

## 文献信息

Shukla, S. N., & Marlin, B. M.（2021）. Multi-Time Attention Networks for Irregularly Sampled Time Series. International Conference on Learning Representations, ICLR 2021.

- **arXiv**：[2101.10318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.10318)
- **发表会议**：ICLR 2021
- **代码**：[https://github.com/reml-lab/mTAN](https://github.com/reml-lab/mTAN)

## 核心方法
- **模型**：Multi-Time Attention Network，简称 mTAN；论文进一步构建了离散参考时间版本 mTAND，并将其放入变分自编码器的 encoder–decoder 框架。
- **研究问题**：处理稀疏、不规则采样、部分变量缺失，并且不同变量观测时间不一致的多变量时间序列。
- **核心机制**：
  1. 将连续时间值映射为可学习的时间 embedding；
  2. 将固定参考时间点作为注意力的 query；
  3. 将实际观测时间作为 key；
  4. 将实际观测值作为 value；
  5. 将不规则观测转换为固定参考时间点上的规则表示。
- **时间嵌入**：同时使用可学习的线性分量和正弦周期分量，以表示非周期趋势和周期变化。
- **注意力机制**：通过多个时间嵌入函数学习时间点之间的相似性核，而不是预先规定线性插值或 RBF 核。
- **完整架构**：mTAND + 双向 GRU/RNN + VAE；在分类任务中进一步加入 GRU 和全连接分类器。
- **任务**：
  - 不规则时间序列插值和重建；
  - 整条时间序列分类；
  - 单个时间点的人体活动分类。
- **评价指标**：
  - 插值任务使用 MSE；
  - 死亡率分类使用 AUC；
  - 人体活动分类使用 Accuracy；
  - 同时比较每轮训练时间。

## mTAN 如何工作

对于第 \(d\) 个变量，论文将其观测表示为：

\[
s_d=(t_d,x_d)
\]

其中：

- \(t_d\) 是该变量实际被观测到的时间列表；
- \(x_d\) 是对应的观测值；
- 不同变量可以拥有不同的观测日期和不同的观测数量。

连续时间 \(t\) 首先被编码为：

\[
\phi_h(t)[i]=
\begin{cases}
\omega_{0h}t+\alpha_{0h}, & i=0 \\
\sin(\omega_{ih}t+\alpha_{ih}), & i>0
\end{cases}
\]

其中：

- \(\omega\) 和 \(\alpha\) 是可训练参数；
- 线性项用于捕捉非周期性的时间推进和长期趋势；
- 正弦项用于捕捉周期性变化。

对于参考时间点 \(r_k\)：

- **Query**：参考时间 \(r_k\)；
- **Key**：实际观测时间 \(t_{id}\)；
- **Value**：对应的实际观测值 \(x_{id}\)。

模型计算学习得到的时间注意力权重：

\[
\kappa_h(r_k,t_{id})
=
\operatorname{softmax}
\left(
\frac{
\phi_h(r_k)W
\left[\phi_h(t_{id})V\right]^\top
}{
\sqrt{d_k}
}
\right)
\]

然后生成参考时间点上的表示：

\[
\hat{x}_{hd}(r_k)
=
\sum_i
\kappa_h(r_k,t_{id})x_{id}
\]

不同变量和不同时间注意力头的结果进一步组合，形成参考时间点上的固定维度 embedding。

因此，mTAN 可以概括为：

一种针对稀疏、异步多变量时间序列的可学习连续时间插值与表示模块。

## 主要发现

### 1. mTAND-Full 显著提高了插值性能。

在 PhysioNet 插值任务中，无论保留 50%、60%、70%、80% 还是 90% 的观测点，mTAND-Full 的 MSE 都显著低于 RNN-VAE 和 Latent ODE 模型。

### 2. mTAN 在分类任务中达到最优或接近最优表现。

- **PhysioNet 死亡预测 AUC**：
  - mTAND-Full：**0.858**
  - mTAND-Enc：**0.854**
  - ODE-RNN：0.833
- **MIMIC-III 死亡预测 AUC**：
  - ODE-RNN：**0.8561**
  - mTAND-Full：0.8544
  - L-ODE-ODE：0.8559
- **Human Activity Accuracy**：
  - mTAND-Full：**0.910**
  - mTAND-Enc：**0.907**
  - ODE-RNN：0.885

因此，更准确的结论是：mTAN 在多数任务中表现最好，或与最强连续时间模型在统计上相当。

### 3. mTAN 的训练速度显著快于 Neural ODE。

每个 epoch 的近似训练时间为：

| 模型 | 每轮训练时间（分钟） |
|---|---:|
| ODE-RNN | 16.5 |
| L-ODE-ODE | 22.0 |
| mTAND-Enc | 0.1 |
| mTAND-Full | 0.2 |

mTAND-Full 比主要 ODE 模型快约 80–110 倍，因为注意力计算可以并行化，不需要重复调用数值 ODE 求解器。

### 4. 可学习的连续时间 embedding 优于固定位置编码。

消融实验结果为：

| 数据集 | 固定位置编码 | 可学习时间嵌入 |
|---|---:|---:|
| PhysioNet | 0.845 | **0.858** |
| MIMIC-III | 0.843 | **0.854** |

### 5. 学习型时间相似性核比固定插值核更加灵活。

与使用固定 RBF 插值核的 IP-Nets 相比，mTAN 学习得到的时间相似性核在多个数据集上取得相同或更好的分类结果。

### 6. 分布在多个参考时间点的潜在状态能够保留局部时间结构。

mTAN 不将整条序列压缩为单个潜变量，而是在多个参考时间点保留潜在状态，有利于表示局部峰值、短期变化和状态转折。

| **表示级融合** | mTAN 解决的是模态内部的时间对齐，之后仍可以通过 gated fusion 或 cross-modal attention 融合不同模态。 |
| **缺失观测** | 论文说明缺失数据不一定要在建模前先填补，模型可以只对实际被观测到的变量和时间点进行注意力计算。 |
| **滞后建模** | 多个时间注意力头可以学习不同时间尺度，避免对所有变量机械设置完全相同的 lag。 |
| **计算效率** | 与 Latent ODE 相比，mTAN 是一种更轻量的连续时间方法，更适合样本量有限的周度油价项目。 |

## 对 M1–M4 框架的具体启示
| 模型层 | 启示 |
|---|---|
| **M1——市场与宏观基本面** | 大多数金融市场变量已经是规则日频数据，可以继续使用 TCN、GRU 或 TFT。mTAN 更适合处理不规则发布时间、发布滞后或修订问题明显的周频和月频宏观变量。 |
| **M2——遥感数据** | 这是最适合使用 mTAN 的部分。每次有效 Sentinel-2 或 VIIRS 观测可单独编码并保留真实采集时间，再通过 causal mTAN 转换为周度遥感表示。 |
| **M3——航运与物理供应链** | 对事件级 AIS、单船轨迹或存在缺失的港口数据较有价值。如果 PortWatch 已经是完整日频序列，则日频 TCN 或周度聚合可能已经足够。 |
| **M4——多模态融合** | mTAN 应放在融合前，负责每个模态内部的不规则时间对齐；不同模态之间的关系仍需要 gated cross-attention。 |

## 应用于油价预测时必须进行的修改

### 1. 将双向模型改为因果模型

原论文主要研究插值和分类。插值可能使用目标时间前后的观测，完整模型还使用了双向 RNN。

但在下一周 Brent 预测中，模型不能使用预测起点之后的信息，因此需要加入因果掩码：

\[
\kappa(r_k,t_i)=0 \quad \text{if } t_i>r_k
\]


同时，应将双向 GRU 替换为：

- 单向 GRU；
- Causal TCN；
- 使用 causal mask 的 Temporal Transformer。

### 2. 区分事件发生时间与数据可用时间

每条记录最好同时保存：

\[
t_i^{\text{event}}
\]

和：

\[
t_i^{\text{available}}
\]

其中：

- \(t_i^{\text{event}}\) 表示事件或测量实际发生时间；
- \(t_i^{\text{available}}\) 表示预测者真正能够获得该信息的时间。

预测起点为 \(T\) 时，只允许使用满足以下条件的观测：

\[
t_i^{\text{available}} \le T
\]

这对于以下数据尤其重要：

- EIA 库存；
- 月度宏观指标；
- 后续修订的数据；
- 延迟处理的卫星影像；
- 存在报告延迟的航运数据库。

### 3. 将 mTAN 作为时间对齐模块，而不是复制完整 VAE

本项目如果使用约 2006–2026 年的周度数据，总样本量大约只有 1,000 个预测时间点，远小于原论文数据规模。

因此更适合采用轻量结构：

```text
冻结的模态编码器
        |
        v
小型 causal mTAN
        |
        v
小型 GRU / TCN
        |
        v
预测层
```

完整 VAE decoder 可以作为可选辅助任务，而不应默认加入。

### 4. 使用预测评估，而不是插值评估

原论文随机隐藏部分已有观测点进行插值，这与预测未来油价并不相同。

本项目应采用：

- expanding-window；
- rolling-window；
- 严格按时间划分训练集、验证集和测试集；
- 真实发布时间限制；
- 与 \(P_{t+1}=P_t\) 不变预测进行比较。

## 可形成的论文方法贡献

原始 mTAN 主要用于医疗领域的不规则数值时间序列插值和分类。

本项目可以从三个方向进行扩展：

### 因果化扩展

修改 mTAN，使每个参考周只能关注预测起点之前可以获得的观测。

### 发布时间感知扩展

同时纳入事件时间和信息发布时间，防止由数据发布滞后和历史修订造成的 look-ahead bias。

### 多模态扩展

对遥感和航运表示分别应用时间对齐模块，再通过 gated cross-modal attention 与金融表示融合。

因此，潜在的方法贡献可以表述为：

> **构建一个因果、发布时间感知、模态专属的连续时间对齐框架，用于整合异步观测的金融市场、遥感和航运信号，并预测下一周 Brent 原油价格。**

## 局限性

### 没有研究油价预测

原论文没有测试原油、商品或金融市场预测，不能直接证明 mTAN 会提高 Brent 或 WTI 预测表现。

### 插值不等于预测

插值可以使用缺失点前后的观测，而未来预测只能使用预测起点之前的信息。

### 存在前视偏差风险

原始架构使用双向 RNN，并不是为实时因果预测设计的。

### 主要处理数值时间序列

论文没有直接处理卫星影像、空间网络、船舶轨迹或真正的多模态数据。

### 没有跨模态融合机制

mTAN 处理的是不规则时间序列内部的时间注意力，并不负责金融、遥感和航运之间的交互。

### 注意力权重不等于因果解释

某个时间点获得较高注意力，只能说明其对模型预测可能有帮助，不能证明其具有经济因果效应。

### 原论文数据规模较大

论文使用数千至数万条独立序列，周度油价数据样本量明显更小，完整模型可能过拟合。

### 没有明确处理发布时间延迟

原文主要考虑测量时间，没有区分事件发生时间和预测者获得信息的时间。

### 缺少市场状态分析

论文没有研究时间权重是否会在金融危机、供应冲击和不同油价市场状态下发生变化。

## 论文整合备注
- 将该论文放入“不规则采样与异步时间序列表示学习”的方法文献部分。
- 用于支持：遥感数据不一定必须先前向填充或强制周度聚合。
- 将 mTAN 定位为不规则观测与常规时序编码器之间的时间接口。
- 不应将 mTAN 描述成完整的多模态融合模型。
- 不应声称该论文已经证明 mTAN 能够改善油价预测。
- 明确说明本项目将通过 causal mask 和数据可用时间约束对原方法进行修改。
- 在没有证据表明重建辅助任务有效时，优先采用简化的 mTAN encoder，而不是完整 VAE。
- 将学习型连续时间对齐与周平均、最近值填充和固定插值进行对比。
- 使用模态消融实验识别性能改进究竟来自新增数据、更合理的时间对齐，还是单纯增加了模型复杂度。
- 最终评价必须与不变预测进行比较，并使用 Diebold–Mariano 检验判断预测改进是否具有统计意义。
