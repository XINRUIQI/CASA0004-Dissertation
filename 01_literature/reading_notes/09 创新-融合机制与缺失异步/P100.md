## Research Problem / 研究问题

The paper addresses a general multimodal-learning problem:

> How can a neural network exploit complementary information from heterogeneous modalities while preserving the discriminative ability of each individual modality and remaining robust when one or more modalities are missing or corrupted?

本文研究的核心问题是：

> 在不同模态的数据结构、信息密度和噪声特征存在明显差异的情况下，如何既利用模态之间的互补信息，又保留每个模态自身的判别能力，并使模型在一个或多个模态缺失或受噪时仍能输出可靠结果？

Although the application is gesture recognition, the methodological contribution is more general. The paper focuses on three difficulties:

虽然论文的应用场景是手势识别，但其方法学贡献具有较强的普适性，主要解决三个困难：

1. Different modalities have different dimensions, structures, noise levels and optimal network capacities.  
   不同模态具有不同的维度、数据结构、噪声水平和最优模型容量。

2. Directly concatenating heterogeneous raw inputs may produce false cross-modal correlations and allow strong modalities to dominate weaker ones.  
   直接拼接异构原始输入可能产生虚假的跨模态相关性，并导致强模态压制弱模态。

3. A model trained only on complete multimodal observations may fail when one channel is missing at test time.  
   仅使用完整模态样本训练的模型，在测试阶段某个通道缺失时可能出现严重性能下降。

- Joint velocities  
  关节速度

- Joint accelerations

该分支主要捕捉全身或上半身结构，而视频分支捕捉更精细的手部和手指信息。因此，各模态提供的是互补信息，而不是简单重复的信息。

## Core Method / 核心方法

### 1. Modality-Specific Encoders  
### 1. 模态专属编码器

Instead of immediately concatenating all raw inputs, the framework assigns a separate processing pathway to each modality.

论文没有在输入层直接拼接所有原始数据，而是为每种模态设计独立的处理路径。

Conceptually:

概念上可以表示为：

\[
x^{(1)}
\rightarrow
E_1
\rightarrow
z_1
\]

\[
x^{(2)}
\rightarrow
E_2
\rightarrow
z_2
\]

\[
\cdots
\]

\[
x^{(K)}
\rightarrow
E_K
\rightarrow
z_K
\]

where:

其中：

- \(x^{(k)}\) is the raw input of modality \(k\);  
  \(x^{(k)}\) 是第 \(k\) 个模态的原始输入；

- \(E_k\) is the modality-specific encoder;  
  \(E_k\) 是该模态的专属编码器；

- \(z_k\) is the learned modality representation or embedding.  
  \(z_k\) 是模型学习得到的模态表示或 embedding。

This avoids allocating the same model capacity and architecture to fundamentally different signals.

这种设计避免对性质完全不同的数据使用完全相同的网络结构和模型容量。

---

### 2. Modality-Wise Pretraining  
### 2. 单模态分支预训练

Each modality-specific branch is first trained independently to perform gesture classification.

每个模态分支首先被单独训练，使其能够独立完成手势分类任务。

The pretraining objective can be summarised as:

预训练目标可概括为：

\[
\mathcal{L}_{pretrain}
=
\sum_{k=1}^{K}
\mathcal{L}\left[M^{(k)}\right]
+
\alpha
\sum_{h=1}^{H}
\left\|W_h\right\|_2^2
\]

where each modality-specific loss is initially minimised independently.

其中，每个模态对应的损失在初始阶段被独立优化，并加入权重正则化。

The purpose of pretraining is to ensure that:

预训练的作用是确保：

1. Every branch learns a meaningful discriminative representation before fusion.  
   每个模态在融合之前都已经学到有意义的判别表示。

2. A strong modality does not suppress the learning of weaker modalities during the initial training stage.  
   强模态不会在训练初期压制较弱模态的学习。

3. Modality-specific architecture and capacity can be tuned separately.  
   可以针对不同模态分别调整网络结构和模型容量。

4. Bottlenecks in individual branches can be identified before training the complete model.  
   可以在训练完整模型之前发现单模态分支中的性能瓶颈。

---

### 3. Gradual Fusion  
### 3. 渐进式融合

After pretraining, the modality-specific representations are connected to shared hidden layers.

完成预训练后，各个模态表示被连接到共享隐藏层。

However, the authors do not immediately initialise all cross-modal connections randomly. A fully connected random fusion layer could rapidly destroy useful pretrained representations.

但作者并未直接随机初始化所有跨模态连接。若直接使用完全随机的融合层，大量自由参数可能迅速破坏已经学好的单模态表示。

The shared weight matrix is initially organised into block structures:

共享权重矩阵在初始阶段采用分块结构：

- Diagonal blocks preserve modality-specific mappings.  
  对角块保留各模态内部已经学到的映射。

- Off-diagonal blocks represent cross-modal interactions.  
  非对角块代表不同模态之间的交互。

- Off-diagonal interactions are initially restricted and later released.  
  跨模态连接在初期受到限制，之后再逐步开放。

At the beginning of fusion training, the network approximates an aggregation of independently trained modality models. During later optimisation, the constraints are relaxed and the model learns more complex cross-modal dependencies.

在融合训练初期，模型近似于对多个单模态模型进行集成；随着训练推进，结构约束被放松，模型逐渐学习更加复杂的跨模态关系。

The principle can be summarised as:

该原则可概括为：

> First preserve the individual modality representations, and then gradually learn interactions between them.

> 先保留各模态自身的表示能力，再逐渐学习模态之间的交互关系。

---

### 4. ModDrop: Modality-Level Dropout  
### 4. ModDrop：模态级随机丢弃

The main contribution of the paper is ModDrop.

论文的主要贡献是 ModDrop。

Ordinary dropout randomly removes individual input variables or hidden units. ModDrop instead removes an entire modality-specific group.

普通 Dropout 随机删除单个输入变量或隐藏神经元；ModDrop 删除的是整个模态对应的一组输入或表示。

For modality \(k\):

对于第 \(k\) 个模态：

\[
\delta^{(k)}
\sim
\operatorname{Bernoulli}\left(p^{(k)}\right)
\]

\[
\widetilde{x}^{(k)}
=
\delta^{(k)}x^{(k)}
\]

or, when applied at the representation level:

或者在表示层使用时：

\[
\widetilde{z}_k
=
\delta^{(k)}z_k
\]

where:

其中：

- \(\delta^{(k)}=1\): modality \(k\) is retained;  
  \(\delta^{(k)}=1\)：保留第 \(k\) 个模态；

- \(\delta^{(k)}=0\): the complete modality is removed;  
  \(\delta^{(k)}=0\)：删除整个第 \(k\) 个模态；

- \(p^{(k)}\): probability that the modality remains available;  
  \(p^{(k)}\)：该模态被保留的概率；

- \(q^{(k)}=1-p^{(k)}\): probability that the modality is dropped.  
  \(q^{(k)}=1-p^{(k)}\)：该模态被删除的概率。

For \(K\) modalities, there are potentially \(2^K\) modality-availability combinations. ModDrop approximates training over these combinations by sampling different masks during stochastic optimisation.

对于 \(K\) 个模态，理论上存在 \(2^K\) 种模态组合。ModDrop 通过在随机梯度训练中不断采样不同的模态掩码，近似训练这些不同组合对应的子网络。

---

### 5. Cross-Modal Regularisation  
### 5. 跨模态正则化

The authors theoretically show that ModDrop adds a regularisation effect involving products of inputs and weights from different modalities.

作者通过理论推导指出，ModDrop 会引入一个涉及不同模态输入和权重乘积的正则项。

For uncorrelated modalities, these cross-modal terms tend to cancel out.

当不同模态不相关时，这些跨模态项在样本量足够大时趋于相互抵消。

For positively or negatively correlated modalities, ModDrop encourages corresponding weights to become aligned in a way consistent with the direction of the cross-modal relationship.

当不同模态存在正相关或负相关时，ModDrop 会促使相应权重按照跨模态关系的方向进行对齐。

Therefore, ModDrop is intended to:

因此，ModDrop 试图同时实现：

- Preserve the uniqueness of each modality-specific representation;  
  保留各模态表示的独立性；

- Learn genuine cross-modal correlations;  
  学习真实的跨模态相关性；

- Prevent false co-adaptation;  
  防止虚假的模态共适应；

- Improve robustness to missing or corrupted channels.  
  提高模型对缺失或受噪通道的稳健性。

---

### 6. Multi-Temporal-Scale Architecture  
### 6. 多时间尺度架构

The model operates through parallel temporal pathways with different sampling strides.

模型通过具有不同采样步长的并行时间路径运行。

The figure in the paper illustrates temporal strides such as:

论文架构图展示了不同的时间步长，例如：

- \(s=2\)
- \(s=3\)
- \(s=4\)

Each dynamic pose uses the same number of frames, but a larger stride covers a longer time interval.

每个动态姿态使用相同数量的帧，但较大的时间步长会覆盖更长的时间范围。

Consequently:

因此：

- Short-scale paths capture rapid movements;  
  短时间尺度路径捕捉快速动作；

- Longer-scale paths capture more slowly articulated gestures;  
  长时间尺度路径捕捉持续时间更长的动作；

- Differences in gesture speed and personal articulation style can be accommodated.  
  模型可以适应不同人的动作速度和表达方式。

Predictions produced at the different temporal scales are combined through additive late fusion.

不同时间尺度产生的预测最终通过加法式后期融合进行整合。

## Training Procedure / 训练流程

The full training procedure can be divided into the following stages:

完整训练流程可以划分为以下阶段：

### Stage 1: Train each modality independently  
### 阶段1：分别训练各模态

- Train video branches;  
  训练视频分支；

- Train articulated-pose branch;  
  训练骨架分支；

- Train audio branch;  
  训练音频分支；

- Tune architecture and capacity for each modality.  
  分别调整每个模态的网络结构与容量。

### Stage 2: Initialise fusion layers  
### 阶段2：初始化融合层

- Copy pretrained modality-specific weights;  
  载入预训练后的单模态权重；

- Initialise diagonal blocks using pretrained mappings;  
  使用预训练映射初始化对角权重块；

- Initially suppress unrestricted cross-modal connections.  
  初始阶段限制无约束的跨模态连接。

### Stage 3: Train shared layers  
### 阶段3：训练共享层

- Train the fusion and output layers;  
  训练融合层和输出层；

- Preserve the learned modality-specific information during early fusion.  
  在融合初期保留各模态已经学习到的信息。

### Stage 4: Joint fine-tuning  
### 阶段4：联合微调

- Relax fusion constraints;  
  放松融合结构约束；

- Fine-tune modality-specific branches together with the shared layers;  
  联合微调模态分支和共享层；

- Learn cross-modal dependencies from training data.  
  从训练数据中学习跨模态依赖。

### Stage 5: Apply Dropout and ModDrop  
### 阶段5：应用 Dropout 与 ModDrop

- Ordinary input dropout regularises local input features;  
  普通输入 Dropout 对局部特征进行正则化；

- ModDrop randomly removes complete modalities;  
  ModDrop 随机删除完整模态；

- The network learns to predict under multiple modality-availability conditions.  
  模型学习在不同模态可用条件下进行预测。

## Experimental Configuration / 实验设置

In the principal multimodal robustness experiment:

在主要的多模态稳健性实验中：

- Input dropout rate: 20%  
  输入 Dropout 比例：20%

- ModDrop probability: 10%  
  ModDrop 概率：10%

- The ModDrop probability was selected using the validation set.  
  ModDrop 概率通过验证集进行选择。

The authors report that hidden-unit dropout degraded performance in their compact architecture, so they primarily applied dropout to the input rather than extensively dropping hidden units.

作者发现，由于其网络结构较为紧凑，隐藏层 Dropout 反而会降低性能，因此主要在输入层使用普通 Dropout。

This is an architecture-specific empirical result and should not be interpreted as a universal rule against hidden-layer dropout.

这是一个与特定网络结构有关的实验结果，不能被解释为普遍不应使用隐藏层 Dropout。

### 1. Modality-specific pretraining substantially improves performance  
### 1. 单模态预训练能够显著改善性能

The comparison of training strategies reports the following validation accuracy:

不同训练策略的验证集准确率如下：

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No / 否 | No / 否 | No / 否 | No / 否 | 91.94% |
| No / 否 | Yes / 是 | No / 否 | No / 否 | 93.33% |
| Yes / 是 | No / 否 | No / 否 | No / 否 | 94.96% |
| Yes / 是 | Yes / 是 | No / 否 | No / 否 | 96.31% |
| Yes / 是 | Yes / 是 | Yes / 是 | No / 否 | 96.77% |
| Yes / 是 | Yes / 是 | Yes / 是 | Yes / 是 | 96.81% |

The largest improvements come from:

最大的提升主要来自：

1. Modality-wise pretraining;  
   模态专属预训练；

2. Input regularisation;  
   输入正则化；

3. Structured initialisation of the fusion layers.  
   融合层的结构化初始化。

ModDrop provides only a small additional gain when all modalities are present.

当所有模态均完整时，ModDrop 带来的额外精度提升较小。

### 2. ModDrop mainly improves missing-modality robustness  
### 2. ModDrop 的主要价值在于提高缺失模态稳健性

When all modalities are available:

所有模态均可用时：

| Method / 方法 | Accuracy / 准确率 | Jaccard Index |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dropout | 96.77% | 0.876 |
| Dropout + ModDrop | 96.81% | 0.880 |

The difference is small under complete observations.

在完整数据条件下，两种方法的差距较小。

However, the difference becomes very large when one or more modalities are missing:

但当一个或多个模态缺失时，差异明显扩大：

| Missing channel / 缺失通道 | Dropout accuracy / 普通Dropout准确率 | Dropout + ModDrop accuracy / 加ModDrop准确率 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Left-hand video / 左手视频 | 89.09% | 91.87% |
| Right-hand video / 右手视频 | 81.25% | 85.36% |
| Both hand-video channels / 双手视频 | 53.13% | 73.28% |
| Mocap or skeleton / 骨架模态 | 38.41% | 92.82% |
| Audio / 音频 | 84.10% | 92.59% |

The most important case is the missing skeleton channel:

最显著的是骨架模态缺失的情况：

- Ordinary Dropout model: 38.41% accuracy;  
  普通 Dropout 模型：38.41%；

- Dropout + ModDrop model: 92.82% accuracy.  
  Dropout + ModDrop 模型：92.82%。

This indicates that the ordinary multimodal model had become excessively dependent on the skeleton input. ModDrop forced the model to preserve useful predictive pathways through the remaining modalities.

这说明普通多模态模型已经对骨架输入产生严重依赖，而 ModDrop 迫使模型保留通过其他模态完成预测的能力。

### 3. ModDrop also improves robustness to corrupted channels  
### 3. ModDrop 还能提高模型对受噪通道的稳健性

The authors add 50% pepper noise to individual channels.

作者向不同通道加入50%的椒盐噪声。

For example:

例如：

| Corrupted channel / 受噪通道 | Dropout accuracy / 普通Dropout准确率 | Dropout + ModDrop accuracy / 加ModDrop准确率 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Both hand-video channels / 双手视频 | 94.55% | 95.06% |
| Mocap / 骨架 | 93.31% | 94.28% |
| Audio / 音频 | 94.76% | 94.96% |

The improvements under noise are smaller than the improvements under complete channel loss, but ModDrop remains consistently more stable.

在部分噪声条件下，ModDrop 的提升小于整个通道缺失时的提升，但总体表现仍更加稳定。

---

### 4. A weak standalone modality may still improve multimodal prediction  
### 4. 单独表现较弱的模态仍可能改善多模态结果

The audio-only branch performs relatively poorly in temporal localisation.

音频模态单独进行时间定位时表现相对较弱。

The paper attributes this partly to gesture–speech asynchrony:

论文认为，这部分是由于手势与语音之间存在时间不同步：

- Spoken words may occur before the gesture;  
  语音可能出现在手势之前；

- Speech may be delayed until the gesture reaches its peak;  
  语音也可能延迟到手势动作的高峰；

- Spoken phrases are often shorter than the corresponding movement.  
  语音短语通常比对应的手势动作更短。

Despite weak standalone localisation performance, adding audio still improves the complete multimodal model.

尽管音频模态单独定位手势的能力较弱，将其加入完整多模态系统后仍能改善总体结果。

This demonstrates an important distinction:

这说明需要区分：

> Standalone predictive power and incremental multimodal value are not the same concept.

> 单模态独立预测能力与该模态在多模态模型中的增量价值并不是同一个概念。

---

### 5. Similar modalities can be fused earlier than fundamentally different modalities  
### 5. 性质相近的模态可以较早融合，差异较大的模态应较晚融合

The authors fuse depth and intensity video relatively early because both are visual channels with aligned spatial structure.

作者较早融合深度视频和灰度视频，因为二者均为视觉模态，且空间结构基本对齐。

In contrast, video, articulated pose and audio are fused at a higher shared layer because their physical meaning and data structure differ substantially.

相比之下，视频、骨架和音频在较高层的共享表示中融合，因为它们的数据结构和物理含义存在显著差异。

This provides a hierarchical fusion principle:

这形成了一种分层融合原则：

- Within-modality or closely related signals: earlier fusion;  
  同一模态内部或性质相近的信号：较早融合；

- Fundamentally heterogeneous modalities: representation-level or later fusion.  
  结构差异明显的异构模态：在表示层或更高层融合。

## Recommended ModDrop Design for the Dissertation  
## 本项目建议采用的 ModDrop 设计

The dissertation should apply ModDrop to complete modality embeddings rather than randomly removing a few engineered variables.

本项目应在完整模态 embedding 层面应用 ModDrop，而不是随机删除少数工程变量。

Appropriate level:

合适的应用层级：

\[
z_{fin},\quad z_{rs},\quad z_{ship}
\]

Inappropriate interpretation:

不建议的做法：

- Randomly delete one Brent lag;
- 随机删除某一个 Brent 滞后项；

- Randomly delete one tanker-count column;
- 随机删除某一列油轮数量；

- Treat ordinary feature dropout as equivalent to ModDrop.
- 将普通特征 Dropout 视为与 ModDrop 等价。

The drop probabilities do not need to be equal across modalities:

不同模态不必使用相同的丢弃概率：

\[
q_{fin}
\neq
q_{rs}
\neq
q_{ship}
\]

A possible initial search range is:

可作为初始调参范围：

\[
q_{fin}\in[0.00,0.10]
\]

\[
q_{rs}\in[0.10,0.30]
\]

\[
q_{ship}\in[0.05,0.20]
\]

These values are not taken directly from the paper. They should be selected through time-series validation and informed by the actual missingness frequency of each data source.

这些数值并非论文给出的固定结论，应结合时间序列验证以及各数据源的真实缺失率进行选择。

The original paper's 10% ModDrop probability should not be copied mechanically because the gesture-recognition setting has a different number of modalities, sample size and missingness process.

不能机械复制原论文的10% ModDrop 概率，因为手势识别任务与石油预测任务在模态数量、样本规模和缺失机制上均不同。

## Recommended Training Strategy for the Dissertation  
## 本项目建议的训练策略

### Stage A: Train and validate each encoder separately  
### 阶段A：分别训练和验证各编码器

1. Train the finance-only model.  
   训练仅使用金融数据的模型。

2. Train the remote-sensing-only representation and prediction head.  
   训练遥感表示与遥感预测头。

3. Train the shipping-only representation and prediction head.  
   训练航运表示与航运预测头。

The remote-sensing-only and shipping-only models do not necessarily need to beat M0. Their purpose is also to verify that the encoders produce meaningful and stable representations.

遥感单模态和航运单模态模型不一定必须击败 M0；这一阶段还用于验证编码器能否学习到稳定且有意义的表示。

---

### Stage B: Freeze encoders and train the fusion head  
### 阶段B：冻结编码器并训练融合层

Initially freeze:

初始阶段冻结：

- Finance encoder;
- 金融编码器；

- Remote-sensing encoder;
- 遥感编码器；

- Shipping encoder.
- 航运编码器。

Train only:

仅训练：

- Projection layers;
- 表示投影层；

- Gating or fusion layer;
- 门控或融合层；

- Prediction head.
- 预测头。

This prevents unstable early gradients from destroying pretrained representations.

这样可以防止融合初期不稳定的梯度破坏已经学好的单模态表示。

---

### Stage C: Gradually unfreeze upper layers  
### 阶段C：逐步解冻高层网络

A possible learning-rate hierarchy is:

可以设置分层学习率：

\[
\eta_{fusion}
>
\eta_{temporal}
>
\eta_{EO}
\]

where:

其中：

- \(\eta_{fusion}\): fusion and prediction-head learning rate;  
  \(\eta_{fusion}\)：融合层和预测头学习率；

- \(\eta_{temporal}\): financial, remote-sensing temporal and shipping temporal encoder learning rate;  
  \(\eta_{temporal}\)：金融、遥感时间聚合与航运时间编码器学习率；

- \(\eta_{EO}\): pretrained Earth-observation backbone learning rate.  
  \(\eta_{EO}\)：预训练地球观测骨干网络学习率。

The EO backbone can remain frozen if the weekly oil-price dataset is too small for safe fine-tuning.

如果周频油价样本不足以安全微调大型遥感模型，可以始终冻结 EO 骨干网络。

---

### Stage D: Introduce ModDrop during joint fine-tuning  
### 阶段D：在联合微调阶段加入 ModDrop

After the complete fusion model has reached stable convergence:

在完整融合模型稳定收敛后：

- Randomly remove the remote-sensing embedding;
- 随机删除遥感 embedding；

- Randomly remove the shipping embedding;
- 随机删除航运 embedding；

- Occasionally remove both;
- 偶尔同时删除两者；

- Use finance-only batches as a realistic degraded operating condition;
- 使用仅保留金融模态的批次模拟真实退化运行状态；

- Avoid excessively removing the finance modality if it is nearly always available in deployment.
- 如果金融模态在实际使用中几乎始终可用，则不应过度删除金融模态。

## Suggested Ablation Experiments / 建议的消融实验

### 1. Fusion-method ablation  
### 1. 融合方法消融

| Model / 模型 | Description / 说明 |
|---|---|
| **Flat concatenation / 扁平拼接** | Concatenate all engineered variables into one table / 将全部工程变量拼成一张表 |
| **Prediction averaging / 预测平均** | Average predictions from separate modality models / 对不同单模态模型的预测进行平均 |
| **Embedding concatenation / 表示拼接** | Concatenate `z_fin`, `z_rs` and `z_ship` / 拼接三个模态表示 |
| **Gated fusion / 门控融合** | Learn time-varying modality weights / 学习随时间变化的模态权重 |
| **Cross-attention / 交叉注意力** | Learn conditional interactions between modality representations / 学习模态表示之间的条件性交互 |
| **Fusion + ordinary dropout / 融合加普通Dropout** | Drop individual representation dimensions / 随机删除表示中的部分维度 |
| **Fusion + ModDrop / 融合加ModDrop** | Drop complete modality embeddings / 随机删除完整模态表示 |

The original paper does not use modern cross-attention. Cross-attention would be an extension inspired by the broader principle of controlled representation-level fusion.

原论文没有使用现代意义上的 cross-attention。交叉注意力是基于“受控的表示级融合”原则所做的后续扩展，不能说是原论文直接验证的方法。

## Key Contributions to the Dissertation Framework  
## 对本论文框架的核心贡献

### 1. Provides a methodological definition of genuine multimodal learning  
### 1. 为“真正的多模态学习”提供方法依据

该论文支持以下观点：多模态学习并不只是向同一张特征表中加入更多变量列。

真正的多模态模型应当：

   显式处理跨模态依赖和模态缺失。

### 2. 支持“核心实证层—方法创新层”的双层设计

本项目可以保留扁平特征形式的 M0–M4，作为透明且可复现的核心实证基准。

模态感知网络则构成方法创新层：

即比较：

\[
\text{扁平工程特征融合}

\text{保留模态结构的表示级融合}

这一比较可以直接回答：保留模态结构是否比单纯加入更多预测变量更有利于石油价格预测。

### 3. 支持缺失模态鲁棒性贡献

Most oil-price forecasting studies assume that all predictors are available and synchronised.

许多石油价格预测研究默认所有预测变量均完整可用且时间同步。

因此，本项目可以检验模态级随机丢弃能否使多模态油价模型在真实数据可用条件下保持可靠运行。

## Limitations / 局限性

### 1. Different application domain  
### 1. 应用领域不同

The paper studies gesture recognition rather than financial forecasting.

该论文研究的是手势识别，而不是金融或石油价格预测。

The modalities are synchronised sensor channels describing the same physical event, whereas financial, satellite and shipping variables may reflect different causal processes and transmission lags.

论文中的模态是描述同一物理事件的同步传感器通道，而金融、遥感和航运变量可能对应不同的因果过程和传导滞后。

Therefore, the empirical gains cannot be directly transferred to Brent forecasting.

因此，论文中的性能提升不能直接外推到 Brent 预测任务。

---

### 2. Classification rather than regression  
### 2. 主要为分类和定位，而非回归预测

The paper predicts discrete gesture classes and temporal intervals.

论文预测离散手势类别和时间区间。

The dissertation predicts continuous oil returns, prices or volatility.

本项目预测连续的油价收益率、价格或波动率。

The loss functions, evaluation metrics and error distributions are therefore different.

二者的损失函数、评价指标和误差分布均存在差异。

### 3. Limited evidence of improvement with all modalities present  
### 3. 完整模态条件下的提升有限

With complete inputs, accuracy increases only from 96.77% to 96.81% after adding ModDrop.

在完整输入条件下，加入 ModDrop 后，准确率仅从96.77%提升至96.81%。

The central benefit is robustness, not a large improvement in normal complete-data accuracy.

因此，ModDrop 的核心价值在于提高鲁棒性，而不是显著提高完整数据下的预测精度。

For the dissertation, ModDrop should be justified primarily as a missing-modality and regularisation mechanism.

在本项目中，ModDrop 应主要被定位为缺失模态处理和正则化机制。

### 4. Fusion architecture is relatively simple by current standards  
### 4. 与当前方法相比，融合架构相对简单

The paper mainly uses shared fully connected layers with carefully structured initialisation.

论文主要使用经过特殊初始化的共享全连接层进行融合。

It does not evaluate:

论文没有检验：

- Gated multimodal units;
- 门控多模态单元；

- Cross-modal Transformers;
- 跨模态 Transformer；

- Cross-attention;
- 交叉注意力；

- Mixture-of-experts routing;
- 混合专家路由；

- Graph-based multimodal fusion.
- 基于图结构的多模态融合。

These methods would be extensions rather than replications of the original ModDrop architecture.

这些方法属于在 ModDrop 原理基础上的扩展，而不是对原论文架构的直接复现。

### 5. Artificial modality dropping may not match real missingness  
### 5. 人工模态丢弃不一定符合真实缺失机制

ModDrop usually assumes stochastic modality removal.

ModDrop 通常采用随机模态删除。

In the dissertation, missingness may be systematic:

而本项目中的缺失可能具有系统性：

- Clouds are seasonal and spatially clustered;
- 云覆盖具有季节性和空间聚集特征；

- AIS coverage may be weaker in particular regions;
- AIS 覆盖不足可能集中在特定地区；

- Publication delays depend on the data provider;
- 数据发布时间取决于具体数据提供方；

- Missing observations may be associated with geopolitical or operational disruptions.
- 数据缺失可能与地缘政治或运营中断相关。

Therefore, random ModDrop should be supplemented with realistic missingness simulations.

因此，随机 ModDrop 应与符合真实机制的缺失情景模拟结合使用。

### 6. Pretraining may be difficult with a small weekly sample  
### 6. 周频样本较小时，单模态预训练可能较困难

The gesture dataset contains many local dynamic-pose training samples.

手势数据可以从视频中构造大量局部动态姿态样本。

A weekly oil-price dataset covering approximately two decades contains only around one thousand weekly observations.

而覆盖约二十年的周频油价数据通常仅包含约一千个观测。

Large independent modality-specific neural networks may therefore overfit.

因此，大型单模态神经网络可能出现严重过拟合。

Possible safeguards include:

可采取的措施包括：

- Freeze pretrained EO backbones;
- 冻结预训练 EO 骨干网络；

- Use compact temporal encoders;
- 使用紧凑的时间编码器；

- Apply walk-forward validation;

- Restrict the representation dimension;
- 限制模态表示维度；

- Compare against simpler feature-based models;
- 与简单的工程特征模型进行比较；

- Report uncertainty across repeated runs.
- 报告多次重复训练结果的不确定性。

### 7. It does not prove that cross-attention is optimal  
### 7. 论文不能证明交叉注意力是最优融合方法

该论文支持模态专属编码、受控融合以及模态级随机丢弃。

但它并未直接证明门控交叉注意力优于拼接、平均或共享全连接层。

The dissertation must empirically compare these fusion methods.

本项目仍需通过实证实验比较不同融合方法。

## Possible Dissertation Wording / 可用于论文中的表述

### English version

Neverova et al. (2015) propose ModDrop, a modality-level regularisation strategy that randomly removes complete modality channels during training. Their framework first pretrains modality-specific pathways and subsequently performs gradual representation-level fusion through shared layers. Unlike conventional dropout, which removes individual units, ModDrop exposes the network to different combinations of available modalities, thereby discouraging false cross-modal co-adaptation and improving robustness to missing or corrupted signals. Although developed for multimodal gesture recognition, the framework is relevant to the present study because satellite and shipping observations may be unavailable, asynchronous or noisy. This dissertation therefore adapts the ModDrop principle at the embedding level, applying modality masks to financial, remote-sensing and shipping representations during joint fine-tuning.

### 中文版本

Neverova 等（2015）提出了 ModDrop，即一种在训练阶段随机删除完整模态通道的模态级正则化方法。该框架首先分别预训练各个模态专属分支，随后通过共享隐藏层进行渐进式表示融合。不同于随机删除单个神经元的普通 Dropout，ModDrop 使模型在训练过程中接触不同的模态可用组合，从而减少虚假的跨模态共适应，并提高模型对模态缺失和通道受噪的稳健性。尽管该方法最初用于多模态手势识别，但其思想与本研究高度相关，因为卫星和航运观测可能存在缺失、异步和测量误差。因此，本研究在模态 embedding 层面引入 ModDrop，在联合微调期间对金融、遥感和航运表示应用模态掩码。

## Final Assessment / 总体评价

This paper is a foundational methodological reference for robust multimodal representation learning.

该论文是多模态鲁棒表示学习中的一篇基础性方法文献。

Its most important lesson is not that adding more modalities automatically improves prediction. Instead, it demonstrates that successful multimodal learning requires:

其最重要的结论并不是“加入更多模态就一定能够提高预测精度”，而是：

1. Each modality must first learn an appropriate representation;  
   每种模态首先需要学习适合自身结构的表示；

2. Heterogeneous representations should be fused in a controlled and gradual way;  
   异构模态表示应以受控、渐进的方式融合；

3. The model should not be allowed to depend completely on one dominant modality;  
   模型不应完全依赖某一个占主导地位的模态；

4. Training should reflect the missingness and corruption that may occur at deployment;  
   训练过程应模拟实际应用中的模态缺失和数据受噪；

5. Complete-data accuracy and missing-modality robustness should be evaluated separately.  
   完整数据预测精度与缺失模态鲁棒性应被分别评价。

For the oil-price forecasting dissertation, its main role is to support the methodological contribution:

对于本石油价格预测项目，该文最重要的作用是支持以下方法贡献：

> To test whether modality-specific encoding, representation-level fusion and modality-level dropout can outperform conventional flat feature concatenation while maintaining reliable Brent forecasts under missing remote-sensing and shipping observations.

> 检验模态专属编码、表示级融合和模态级随机丢弃，能否在优于传统扁平特征拼接的同时，使 Brent 预测模型在遥感和航运数据缺失时仍保持可靠表现。
