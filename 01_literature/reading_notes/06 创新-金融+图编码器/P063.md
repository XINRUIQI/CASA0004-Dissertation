# 1. Research Objective | 研究目标

## English

This paper develops a hybrid deep-learning model for forecasting WTI crude oil futures prices over multiple horizons.

The central research question is:

> Can forecasting accuracy be improved by jointly modelling the temporal dynamics of each predictor and the time-varying relationships among crude-oil, financial, supply–demand and related-market variables?

The authors argue that the relationship between crude oil prices and their determinants is not fixed. For example, the influence of the dollar, interest rates, market uncertainty and oil production may change during financial crises, geopolitical shocks or supply disruptions.

To address this issue, the paper combines:

1. a self-attention mechanism for learning a dynamic graph structure;
2. Graph WaveNet for modelling cross-variable dependencies;
3. dilated causal convolution for capturing short- and long-term temporal patterns.

## 中文

本文提出一种混合深度学习模型，用于对 WTI 原油期货价格进行多步预测。

文章的核心研究问题是：

> 如果同时学习各个预测变量自身的时间变化，以及油价、金融、供需和相关市场变量之间随时间变化的关系，能否提高 WTI 期货价格的预测精度？

作者认为，原油价格与美元、利率、风险情绪、产量和相关商品市场之间的关系并不是固定不变的。例如，在金融危机、地缘政治冲突或供给中断期间，不同变量对油价的相对重要性可能发生明显变化。

因此，文章结合了：

1. 用于学习动态图结构的自注意力机制；
2. 用于捕捉变量间依赖关系的 Graph WaveNet；
3. 用于学习短期和长期时间依赖的膨胀因果卷积。

# 2. Dataset | 数据集

## English

- **Source**: Wind Financial Terminal.
- **Sample period**: 3 April 1990 to 4 May 2022.
- **Number of observations**: 7,726 daily records.
- **Frequency**:
  - Most variables are observed daily.
  - U.S. crude oil production is originally weekly.
  - Cubic spline interpolation is used to convert weekly production into daily observations.
- **Input window**: the previous 21 trading days, approximately one trading month.
- **Forecast horizons**:

$$
H = 1,2,3,4,5
$$

The model therefore predicts WTI futures closing prices one to five trading days ahead.

## 中文

- **数据来源**：Wind 金融终端。
- **样本区间**：1990年4月3日至2022年5月4日。
- **样本数量**：7,726 个日频观测。
- **数据频率**：
  - 大多数变量为日频；
  - 美国原油产量原本为周频；
  - 作者使用三次样条插值，将周频产量转换为日频数据。
- **输入窗口**：过去 21 个交易日，约等于一个交易月。
- **预测期限**：

$$
H = 1,2,3,4,5
$$

即分别预测未来 1—5 个交易日的 WTI 期货收盘价格。

# 5. Core Method | 核心方法

## 5.1 Model: GWNet-Attn

The proposed model is called:

> Graph WaveNet with Self-Attention, or GWNet-Attn.

The overall process can be summarised as:

$$
\text{Historical multivariate sequences}
\rightarrow
\text{Dynamic graph learning}
\rightarrow
\text{Temporal convolution}
\rightarrow
\text{Graph convolution}
\rightarrow
\text{Multi-step WTI forecasts}
$$

模型总体流程为：

$$
\text{历史多变量序列}
\rightarrow
\text{动态图学习}
\rightarrow
\text{时间卷积}
\rightarrow
\text{图卷积}
\rightarrow
\text{多步WTI价格预测}
$$

---

## 5.2 Variables as Graph Nodes | 将变量视为图节点

## English

Each variable is treated as a node in a graph. For example:

- WTI futures;
- USDX;
- LIBOR;
- VIX;
- crude-oil production;
- BDI;
- Brent futures.

The edges represent statistical dependencies among variables.

The term “spatial” in this paper does not refer to geographical space. It refers to non-Euclidean cross-node relationships in a graph.

## 中文

文章将每个变量视为一个图节点，例如：

- WTI 期货；
- 美元指数；
- LIBOR；
- VIX；
- 美国原油产量；
- BDI；
- Brent 期货。

节点之间的边表示变量之间的统计依赖关系。

需要注意，本文中的“空间关系”并不是港口坐标、地理距离或航运路线意义上的地理空间关系，而是图结构中不同变量节点之间的非欧氏关系。

---

## 5.3 Self-Attention Graph Learning | 自注意力动态图学习

## English

The original Graph WaveNet uses a learned but largely time-invariant adaptive adjacency matrix. The authors replace it with a time-varying attention matrix:

$$
W_i =
\operatorname{Softmax}
\left(
\frac{K_iQ_i^{T}}{\sqrt{D_2}}
\right)
$$

where:

$$
Q_i = X_iW^Q
$$

$$
K_i = X_iW^K
$$

For every input batch or forecasting window, the model generates a new adjacency matrix describing how strongly each variable is related to the others.

This allows the graph structure to change across:

- normal market periods;
- financial crises;
- oil-supply shocks;
- periods of high uncertainty;
- different forecast horizons.

## 中文

原始 Graph WaveNet 使用通过节点嵌入学习得到的自适应邻接矩阵，但该邻接矩阵在不同时间窗口之间基本固定。

本文使用自注意力机制，将其替换为时变注意力矩阵：

$$
W_i =
\operatorname{Softmax}
\left(
\frac{K_iQ_i^{T}}{\sqrt{D_2}}
\right)
$$

其中：

$$
Q_i = X_iW^Q
$$

$$
K_i = X_iW^K
$$

模型会根据当前输入窗口重新生成一个邻接矩阵，用来表示不同变量之间的动态关系。

因此，模型允许变量关系在以下情形下发生改变：

- 正常市场时期；
- 金融危机时期；
- 原油供给冲击时期；
- 高不确定性时期；
- 不同预测期限下。

---

## 5.4 Dilated Causal Convolution | 膨胀因果卷积

## English

Dilated causal convolution is used to model the temporal dynamics of each variable.

“Causal” means that forecasts at time $t+h$ only use information available at or before time $t$.

“Dilated” means that convolutional filters examine observations at increasing temporal intervals, allowing the receptive field to grow rapidly without requiring a very large number of parameters.

The paper uses nine stacked temporal layers to cover the 21-day input sequence.

The gated temporal convolution can be represented as:

$$
x =
\tanh(\operatorname{TCN}_a(x))
\odot
\sigma(\operatorname{TCN}_b(x))
$$

where the sigmoid component controls how much temporal information passes through the network.

## 中文

膨胀因果卷积用于学习每个变量自身的时间变化规律。

“因果”表示预测未来价格时，只能使用预测时点及其之前的信息，不能使用未来数据。

“膨胀”表示卷积核按照不断扩大的时间间隔读取历史观测，从而在不显著增加参数数量的情况下快速扩大感受野。

文章堆叠了 9 个时间卷积层，以覆盖 21 日输入窗口。

带门控的时间卷积可以表示为：

$$
x =
\tanh(\operatorname{TCN}_a(x))
\odot
\sigma(\operatorname{TCN}_b(x))
$$

其中 sigmoid 部分负责控制哪些时间信息应该通过网络。

---

## 5.5 Graph Convolution | 图卷积

## English

The graph-convolution module aggregates information from connected variables according to the dynamic attention matrix.

For example, if the learned edge from USDX to WTI is strong, information from the dollar index receives a larger weight when constructing the WTI representation.

The model uses diffusion graph convolution and aggregates information from multiple graph hops.

The optimal number of graph hops differs by horizon:

- two hops for one- and two-day forecasts;
- three hops for three- to five-day forecasts.

## 中文

图卷积模块根据动态注意力邻接矩阵，从相关变量中聚合信息。

例如，如果模型学习到 USDX 指向 WTI 的边权较高，那么在构建 WTI 节点表示时，美元指数的信息会获得更高权重。

模型使用扩散图卷积，并聚合多跳邻居的信息。

最优图卷积跳数随预测期限变化：

- 预测未来 1—2 日时，2-hop 效果较好；
- 预测未来 3—5 日时，3-hop 效果较好。

这表明较长期预测可能需要更广泛的跨变量信息传播。

---

## 5.6 Residual and Skip Connections | 残差连接与跳跃连接

## English

Residual connections are used to prevent model degradation when the network becomes deeper.

Skip connections transmit representations from different temporal layers directly to the output module.

These components allow the model to combine:

- short-term local information;
- longer-term temporal patterns;
- features learned at different network depths.

## 中文

残差连接用于避免随着网络加深而出现模型性能下降。

跳跃连接则将不同层学习到的表示直接传递给输出模块。

因此，模型能够同时利用：

- 短期局部信息；
- 较长期时间模式；
- 不同网络深度学习到的特征。

# 6. Baseline Models | 对比模型

GWNet-Attn is compared against the following models:

1. **CNN-Attn**
2. **LSTM-Attn**
3. **GRU-Attn**
4. **CNN-LSTM-Attn**

The comparison is designed to test:

- whether combining CNN feature extraction with GRU or LSTM improves performance.

## 中文

论文通过上述模型比较回答以下问题：

- 图神经网络是否优于普通的 CNN、GRU 和 LSTM？

- 动态注意力图是否优于固定自适应图？
- CNN 与 GRU/LSTM 的组合是否优于单独使用循环神经网络？

# 7. Evaluation | 评价方法

The paper uses six out-of-sample forecasting metrics:

- Root Mean Squared Error, RMSE;
- Mean Absolute Error, MAE;
- Mean Absolute Percentage Error, MAPE;
- Coefficient of Determination, $R^2$;
- Theil's Inequality Coefficient, UI;
- Index of Agreement, $d$.

The paper also uses two statistical forecast-comparison procedures:

1. **Diebold–Mariano test**
2. **Model Confidence Set procedure**

## 中文

文章使用六项样本外预测指标：

- 均方根误差 RMSE；
- 平均绝对误差 MAE；
- 平均绝对百分比误差 MAPE；
- 决定系数 $R^2$；
- Theil 不平等系数 UI；
- 一致性指数 $d$。

此外，文章还使用：

1. Diebold–Mariano 检验；
2. Model Confidence Set 检验。

这些统计检验用于判断模型之间的预测损失差异是否显著，而不仅仅是比较平均误差的大小。

# 8. Key Findings | 主要发现

## Finding 1: GWNet-Attn achieves the best overall forecasting performance

## English

GWNet-Attn produces the most accurate forecasts across all one- to five-day horizons and across all six evaluation metrics.

For the one-day-ahead forecast:

| Model | RMSE | MAE | MAPE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GWNet | 2.314 | 1.234 | 2.514% |
| GWNet-Attn | **2.154** | **1.069** | **2.228%** |

For the five-day-ahead forecast:

| Model | RMSE | MAE | MAPE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GWNet | 3.127 | 1.926 | 3.902% |
| GWNet-Attn | **2.995** | **1.797** | **3.682%** |

The DM tests indicate that GWNet-Attn significantly outperforms all baseline models at the 99% confidence level for every prediction horizon.

## 中文

GWNet-Attn 在未来 1—5 日的所有预测期限和六项评价指标上均取得最佳结果。

以未来 1 日预测为例，GWNet-Attn 的 RMSE 从普通 GWNet 的 2.314 降低到 2.154。

预测未来 5 日时，GWNet-Attn 仍然保持最优。

DM 检验显示，在所有预测期限下，GWNet-Attn 均在 99% 置信水平上显著优于全部对比模型。

# 10. Specific Implications for M0–M4 | 对 M0–M4 的具体启示

## M0: No-change benchmark

The paper does not include the benchmark:

$$
\hat{P}_{t+h}=P_t
$$

However, this benchmark is essential for the dissertation because crude-oil price levels are highly persistent.

Your project should retain:

$$
M0:\quad \hat{P}_{t+1}=P_t
$$

and test whether all complex models provide incremental predictive information beyond the current price.

### 中文

本文没有加入“不变预测”或随机游走基准：

$$
\hat{P}_{t+h}=P_t
$$

由于原油价格水平具有高度持续性，这一基准通常非常强。因此，本项目必须保留 M0，并判断复杂模型是否真正提供了超越当前价格的新增预测信息。

- WTI、天然气和成品油等相关市场。

由于 LIBOR 已经退出主要市场基准，本项目不应机械照搬，而应选择适合研究时期和频率的现行利率指标。

---

## M2: Remote-sensing augmentation

P063 does not use remote-sensing data, but its dynamic weighting idea can be extended to remote-sensing representations:

$$
z_{rs,t}=f_{rs}(X_{rs,t})
$$

The model can investigate whether remote-sensing information receives higher weights during:

- refinery disruptions;
- production changes;
- inventory accumulation or drawdown;
- regional supply shocks.

### 中文

P063 本身没有使用遥感数据，但其动态权重思想可以扩展到遥感表示：

$$
z_{rs,t}=f_{rs}(X_{rs,t})
$$

可以分析在炼厂中断、产量变化、库存积累或区域供给冲击期间，模型是否提高对遥感模态的依赖。

---

## M3: Shipping augmentation

The paper treats statistical variables as graph nodes. The dissertation can construct a more economically meaningful graph:

- ports as nodes;
- chokepoints as nodes;
- tanker flows as edges;
- vessel counts and capacities as node or edge features;
- congestion and waiting time as dynamic features.

A hybrid adjacency structure could be defined as:

$$
A_t =
\lambda A_{\text{physical},t}
+
(1-\lambda)A_{\text{learned},t}
$$

where:

- $A_{\text{physical},t}$ represents observed shipping connections;
- $A_{\text{learned},t}$ represents data-driven dynamic dependencies.

### 中文

与 P063 的变量关系图相比，本项目的航运模态可以构建真实物理网络：

- 港口作为节点；
- 海峡和关键通道作为节点；
- 油轮流量作为边；
- 船舶数量和运力作为节点或边特征；
- 拥堵和等待时间作为动态特征。

这样可以同时保留航运网络的物理机制和模型学习到的动态关系。

## M4: Multimodal fusion

P063 supports replacing fixed feature concatenation with dynamic modality weighting.

A possible structure is:

$$
z_{\text{fin},t}=f_{\text{fin}}(X_{\text{fin},t})
$$

$$
z_{\text{rs},t}=f_{\text{rs}}(X_{\text{rs},t})
$$

$$
z_{\text{ship},t}=f_{\text{ship}}(X_{\text{ship},t})
$$

followed by:

$$
[g_{\text{fin},t},g_{\text{rs},t},g_{\text{ship},t}]
=
\operatorname{Softmax}
\left(
\operatorname{Gate}
(z_{\text{fin},t},z_{\text{rs},t},z_{\text{ship},t})
\right)
$$

and:

$$
z_t =
g_{\text{fin},t}z_{\text{fin},t}
+
g_{\text{rs},t}z_{\text{rs},t}
+
g_{\text{ship},t}z_{\text{ship},t}
$$

This allows the contribution of each modality to change across market regimes.

### 中文

P063 支持将固定的特征拼接改为动态模态加权。

金融、遥感和航运数据先分别经过模态专属编码器，得到：

$$
z_{\text{fin},t},\quad
z_{\text{rs},t},\quad
z_{\text{ship},t}
$$

再通过门控网络学习动态权重：

$$
g_{\text{fin},t},\quad
g_{\text{rs},t},\quad
g_{\text{ship},t}
$$

因此，不同市场状态下，三种模态的贡献可以发生变化，而不是始终使用固定权重。

# 11. Limitations | 局限性

## 1. No random-walk or no-change benchmark | 缺少随机游走或不变预测基准

The study compares GWNet-Attn with other neural-network models but does not compare it with:

$$
\hat{P}_{t+h}=P_t
$$

As a result, it demonstrates superiority over the selected deep-learning baselines, but it does not establish whether the model beats a strong naive price-level forecast.

本文证明了 GWNet-Attn 优于所选择的神经网络对比模型，但没有证明它能够击败高度持续的“不变预测”。

---

## 2. Price-level target | 直接预测价格水平

Price levels are highly persistent and potentially non-stationary. High $R^2$ and low MAPE may partly reflect the model's ability to reproduce the current price level.

The paper does not investigate whether the model accurately predicts:

- price changes;
- log returns;
- turning points;
- directional movements.

价格水平的高持续性可能使模型获得较高的 $R^2$，但这不一定代表模型能够准确预测真实价格变化或转折点。

---

## 3. Homogeneous numerical inputs | 输入仍是同质数值序列

All predictors are structured numerical time series.

The paper does not include:

- satellite imagery;
- AIS trajectories;
- shipping networks;
- news text;
- geospatial observations.

因此，本文不属于严格意义上的多模态油价预测，而是多变量数值时间序列预测。

---

## 4. “Spatial” does not mean geographical space | “空间”并非地理空间

The graph represents statistical relationships among variables rather than physical geography.

It does not model:

- port connectivity;
- maritime routes;
- spatial proximity;
- refinery locations;
- satellite-image spatial patterns.

因此，本文不能直接证明遥感空间结构或真实航运网络图对油价预测有效。

---

## 5. Attention weights are not causal effects | 注意力权重不等于因果效应

The adjacency matrices improve model interpretability, but attention scores can be affected by:

- correlated predictors;
- common trends;
- scale and preprocessing choices;
- model architecture;
- reverse relationships.

They should be interpreted as model-dependent predictive associations rather than structural causal effects.

注意力矩阵只能解释模型在预测时使用了哪些信息，不能直接证明某个变量导致油价发生变化。

---

## 6. Interpolation and real-time availability | 插值与实时数据可得性问题

Weekly U.S. crude-oil production is converted to daily frequency using cubic spline interpolation.

If interpolation uses observations that were unavailable at the forecast date, it may create unrealistic smoothness or potential look-ahead information.

本项目应按照真实发布日期对库存、产量、AIS 和遥感数据进行对齐，避免全样本插值带来的潜在前视偏差。

---

## 7. Large daily sample versus a smaller weekly sample | 日频大样本与周频小样本差异

The paper has 7,726 daily observations.

A 2006–2026 weekly dataset contains only approximately:

$$
20\times52\approx1040
$$

observations before accounting for missing values, lag windows and test periods.

Directly reproducing the nine-layer GWNet-Attn architecture may therefore cause serious overfitting in the dissertation.

本项目应考虑：

- 更少的网络层；
- 更低的隐藏维度；
- 更少的 attention heads；
- dropout 和 weight decay；
- 冻结预训练遥感编码器；
- 严格的时间序列交叉验证。

---

## 8. WTI-specific evidence | 仅以 WTI 为目标

The study focuses exclusively on WTI futures prices.

Its findings may not transfer directly to Brent because Brent has different:

- geographical exposure;
- pricing mechanisms;
- physical delivery structure;
- connections to European, Middle Eastern and seaborne oil markets.

本项目以 Brent 为目标，因此需要重新检验变量重要性和模型表现，而不能直接套用 WTI 结果。

---

# 13. Suggested Literature-Review Positioning | 建议的文献综述定位

## English

Zhao et al. (2023) model WTI futures and ten commodity and financial predictors as a dynamic graph. Self-attention is used to estimate time-varying adjacency matrices, graph convolution captures cross-variable dependencies, and dilated causal convolution represents temporal dynamics. Their model outperforms fixed-correlation, adaptive-graph and conventional deep-learning alternatives across one- to five-day horizons. However, the inputs remain homogeneous numerical time series, and the graph represents statistical relationships among variables rather than the native spatial structure of remote-sensing imagery or the physical topology of maritime networks. The present dissertation extends this research direction from variable-level dynamic graph learning to modality-aware representation fusion across financial, Earth-observation and shipping-network data.

## 中文

Zhao 等（2023）将 WTI 期货及十个商品和金融预测变量建模为动态图，通过自注意力学习时变邻接矩阵，使用图卷积捕捉变量间依赖，并通过膨胀因果卷积学习时间动态。其模型在未来 1—5 日预测中优于固定相关图、自适应固定图和传统深度学习模型。然而，该研究的输入仍是同质化的数值时间序列，图结构表示的是变量之间的统计关系，而不是遥感影像的原生空间结构或航运网络的物理拓扑。本研究将这一方向从变量级动态图学习进一步扩展到金融、地球观测和航运网络数据之间的模态感知表示融合。
