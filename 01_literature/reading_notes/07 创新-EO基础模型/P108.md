## Core Method / 核心方法

- **Model / 模型**:  
  TerraFM is a ViT-based self-supervised Earth Observation foundation model.  
  TerraFM 是一个基于 Vision Transformer 的自监督遥感基础模型。

- **Pretraining framework / 预训练框架**:  
  DINO-style teacher–student contrastive learning. The teacher network receives global crops, while the student network receives both global and local crops.  
  采用 DINO 风格的 teacher–student 对比学习框架：teacher 处理 global crops，student 同时处理 global crops 和 local crops。

- **Modalities / 数据模态**:  
  Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2 Level-1C, and Sentinel-2 Level-2A.  
  使用 Sentinel-1 SAR 雷达影像、Sentinel-2 L1C 光学影像和 Sentinel-2 L2A 光学影像。

- **Dataset / 数据集**:  
  Major-TOM global Earth observation dataset, with globally distributed, co-registered multisensor imagery.  
  使用 Major-TOM 全球遥感数据集，包含全球分布、空间共配准的多传感器遥感影像。

- **Training scale / 训练规模**:  
  Around 1.5 million grid cells are filtered and divided into 534×534 tiles, producing 18.7 million modality-specific training tiles.  
  作者筛选约 150 万个 grid cells，并切分为 534×534 tiles，最终得到 18.7 million 个 modality-specific training tiles。

- **Key architecture components / 关键结构组件**:  
  1. **Modality-specific patch embedding / 模态专属 patch embedding**  
     Each sensor modality has its own patch embedding layer, allowing the model to handle different channel structures and physical meanings.

  2. **Modality-as-augmentation / 将模态视为自然增强**  
     Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 observations of the same location are treated as complementary views of the same scene.

  3. **Cross-attention fusion / 交叉注意力融合**  
     When multiple modalities are available, cross-attention is used to dynamically fuse modality-specific tokens.

  4. **Dual-centering / 双重中心化机制**  
     A frequency-aware centering mechanism is introduced to reduce the dominance of high-frequency land-cover classes and improve representation learning for underrepresented categories.

- **Evaluation / 评估方式**:  
  The model is evaluated on GEO-Bench and Copernicus-Bench using kNN evaluation, linear probing, UPerNet probing, and full fine-tuning.  
  论文在 GEO-Bench 和 Copernicus-Bench 上评估模型，评估方式包括 kNN、linear probing、UPerNet probing 和 full fine-tuning。

## Key Findings / 主要发现

1. **Multisensor EO data can be unified through modality-aware representation learning.**  
   **多传感器遥感数据可以通过模态感知表征学习进行统一建模。**

   TerraFM shows that Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical imagery should not be treated as simple stacked channels. Instead, each modality has distinct physical properties and should first be encoded separately before being fused.

   TerraFM 证明，Sentinel-1 SAR 和 Sentinel-2 optical 不应被简单当作普通通道拼接。不同传感器具有不同的物理机制和通道结构，因此应先进行模态专属编码，再进入统一融合空间。

2. **Modality-as-augmentation improves sensor-invariant representation learning.**  
   **将模态视为 natural augmentation 有助于学习跨传感器稳定表征。**

   By allowing the teacher and student networks to observe different modalities of the same location, TerraFM encourages the model to learn representations that remain consistent across SAR and optical views.

   通过让 teacher 和 student 看到同一地点的不同传感器观测，TerraFM 促使模型学习对 SAR 和 optical 都稳定的 sensor-invariant representations。

3. **Cross-attention fusion is more flexible than naive early fusion.**  
   **Cross-attention fusion 比简单 early fusion 更适合多模态遥感。**

   Instead of directly concatenating all modalities, TerraFM uses cross-attention to dynamically weight the contribution of each sensor at each spatial location.

   TerraFM 没有直接拼接所有模态，而是使用 cross-attention 在每个空间位置动态判断不同传感器的重要性。

4. **Dual-centering addresses long-tailed land-cover distributions.**  
   **Dual-centering 用于缓解遥感数据中的长尾类别问题。**

   EO datasets are dominated by frequent classes such as tree cover, grassland, and water, while rare but important classes such as built-up areas, mangroves, and snow/ice are underrepresented. Dual-centering helps reduce the dominance of frequent classes in the representation space.

   遥感数据中 forest、grassland、water 等类别占比较高，而 built-up、mangroves、snow/ice 等类别较少。Dual-centering 通过引入频率感知的中心化机制，减少高频类别对表示空间的主导。

5. **TerraFM achieves strong performance on downstream EO benchmarks.**  
   **TerraFM 在多个遥感下游任务中取得较强表现。**

   On GEO-Bench and Copernicus-Bench, TerraFM outperforms or matches several recent EO foundation models across classification and segmentation tasks. For example, the paper reports strong results on m-EuroSat, m-BigEarthNet, m-So2Sat, m-Cashew-Plant, and m-SA-Crop-Type.

   在 GEO-Bench 和 Copernicus-Bench 上，TerraFM 在多个分类和分割任务中超过或接近已有遥感基础模型，例如 m-EuroSat、m-BigEarthNet、m-So2Sat、m-Cashew-Plant 和 m-SA-Crop-Type。

## Limitations / 局限性

1. **Not an oil-price forecasting paper / 不是油价预测论文**  
   TerraFM does not directly forecast Brent, WTI, crude returns, or oil-market volatility. Its relevance is methodological rather than empirical for oil-price prediction.  
   TerraFM 本身不预测 Brent、WTI、原油收益率或油价波动率。它对本文的价值主要是方法论支撑，而不是直接的油价预测证据。

2. **No economic target variables / 没有经济金融目标变量**  
   The downstream tasks are EO classification and segmentation tasks, not commodity-market forecasting tasks.  
   论文的下游任务是遥感分类和分割，而不是大宗商品市场预测。

3. **High computational cost / 计算成本较高**  
   TerraFM is trained on millions of global tiles using large GPU resources. This is not directly reproducible in a dissertation-scale project.  
   TerraFM 使用数百万全球 tiles 和大规模 GPU 资源训练，硕士论文项目中难以复现完整预训练过程。

4. **Embeddings may be hard to interpret economically / EO embedding 的经济含义不一定直接可解释**  
   EO foundation model embeddings may capture spatial texture, land-cover patterns, or infrastructure changes, but these do not automatically map to oil inventories, production, or trade flows.  
   EO foundation model embedding 可能捕捉空间纹理、土地覆盖或基础设施变化，但这些特征不一定能直接对应到库存、产量或贸易流。

5. **Temporal forecasting is not the main focus / 时间序列预测不是本文重点**  
   TerraFM focuses on spatial and multisensor representation learning. It does not address lag selection, rolling-origin validation, forecast horizons, or temporal leakage, all of which are central to oil-price forecasting.  
   TerraFM 重点是空间与多传感器表征学习，并不讨论 lag selection、rolling-origin validation、预测期或时间泄漏问题，而这些是油价预测的核心。

6. **AOI-level aggregation remains unresolved / AOI 到周度特征的聚合仍需本文自行设计**  
   The paper does not explain how to aggregate EO embeddings from specific oil-related AOIs into weekly forecasting features. This dissertation still needs to design spatial pooling, temporal aggregation, and lag structures.  
   论文没有说明如何把特定油气 AOI 的 EO embedding 聚合成周度预测特征。本文仍需要自行设计 spatial pooling、temporal aggregation 和 lag structure。

## Possible Dissertation Paragraph / 可直接放入论文的段落

Recent advances in Earth Observation foundation models provide methodological support for incorporating satellite-derived representations into commodity-market forecasting. Danish et al. (2025) propose TerraFM, a scalable multisensor EO foundation model trained on globally distributed Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical imagery. The model uses modality-specific patch embeddings to handle heterogeneous sensor channels, treats different sensors as natural augmentations, and applies cross-attention fusion to dynamically combine modality-specific information. Although TerraFM is evaluated on EO classification and segmentation tasks rather than oil-price forecasting, its architecture is directly relevant to the remote-sensing branch of this dissertation. Specifically, pretrained EO encoders can be frozen and used to extract AOI-level embeddings from oil-related infrastructure such as ports, refineries, storage terminals, and chokepoints. These embeddings can then be aggregated to the weekly frequency and fused with market and shipping variables for Brent price prediction.

近年来，遥感基础模型的发展为将卫星表征引入大宗商品价格预测提供了方法论基础。Danish et al. (2025) 提出的 TerraFM 是一个面向多传感器遥感数据的可扩展 foundation model，使用全球分布的 Sentinel-1 SAR 和 Sentinel-2 optical 影像进行预训练。该模型通过 modality-specific patch embedding 处理不同传感器的通道异质性，将不同传感器视为 natural augmentations，并使用 cross-attention fusion 动态融合不同模态的信息。尽管 TerraFM 的评估任务是遥感分类和分割，而非油价预测，但其结构对本文的遥感模块具有直接借鉴意义。具体而言，本文可以冻结预训练 EO 编码器，从油港、炼厂、储油区和航运咽喉等 AOI 中提取 satellite embeddings，再将这些特征聚合到周度频率，并与市场变量和航运变量融合，用于 Brent 原油价格预测。
