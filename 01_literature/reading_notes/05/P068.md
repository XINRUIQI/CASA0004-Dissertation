## Research Motivation / 研究动机

### English

Official port-level trade statistics are often released with considerable delays and may become incomplete or unavailable in countries with weak statistical systems, geopolitical conflicts, sanctions, or strategic data suppression.

Existing maritime trade monitoring methods frequently rely on the Automatic Identification System (AIS). Although AIS provides detailed vessel-position and movement information, it depends on vessels actively transmitting signals. Ships involved in sanctions evasion, illicit trade, military activity, or “dark shipping” may disable or manipulate AIS transmissions.

This paper therefore develops a framework that measures port-level maritime activity using publicly available satellite observations and static port characteristics. The central idea is that maritime trade leaves observable physical footprints around ports, including vessel movements, cargo-handling changes, metallic structures, container accumulation, and nighttime operating activity.

### 中文

官方港口级贸易统计通常存在较长的发布延迟。在统计能力较弱、发生地缘政治冲突、受到经济制裁或存在战略性数据隐瞒的国家，贸易数据还可能不完整甚至停止发布。

现有海运贸易监测方法经常依赖船舶自动识别系统 AIS。AIS 可以提供详细的船舶位置和航行信息，但它依赖船舶主动发送信号。参与制裁规避、非法贸易、军事活动或“暗船运输”的船舶可能关闭或操纵 AIS 信号。

因此，本文建立了一个仅依靠公开卫星数据和港口静态属性的港口贸易测度框架。其核心思想是：海运贸易活动会在港口周围留下可由卫星观测的物理痕迹，包括船舶移动、装卸变化、金属设施和集装箱堆积，以及夜间运营活动。

### English

The paper addresses three main questions:

1. Can publicly available satellite imagery and port characteristics accurately nowcast monthly port-level maritime trade?
2. Do satellite variables provide information beyond static port infrastructure characteristics?
3. Can a model trained on U.S. ports be spatially extrapolated to geographically distinct or previously unseen ports?

1. 公开卫星影像和港口属性能否准确即时预测月度港口级海运贸易？
2. 卫星变量能否提供超出港口静态基础设施属性之外的动态信息？
3. 在美国港口上训练的模型能否外推至地理环境不同、训练样本中未出现的港口？

## Core Method / 核心方法

### Model / 模型

- **Main model / 核心模型**: XGBoost
- **Model type / 模型类型**: Gradient-boosted decision trees
- **Data structure / 数据结构**: Preprocessed tabular port-month observations rather than raw images
- **Reason for selection / 选择原因**:
  - Suitable for moderate sample sizes
  - Captures nonlinear relationships
  - Captures interactions between satellite signals and port characteristics
  - Handles heterogeneous numerical and categorical features
  - Does not require training a large image-based deep learning model

### 中文说明

作者并未直接将原始卫星影像输入神经网络，而是先从影像中提取具有经济含义的统计特征，再把这些特征输入 XGBoost。

选择 XGBoost 的原因包括：

- 样本规模中等；
- 输入数据是结构化表格特征；
- 变量之间可能存在非线性；
- 遥感信号与港口规模之间可能存在复杂交互；
- 树模型适合同时处理连续变量和港口分类属性。

## Dataset / 数据集

### 1. Sentinel-1 SAR Imagery / Sentinel-1 合成孔径雷达影像

- Source: Copernicus Sentinel-1 GRD
- Spatial resolution: approximately 10 metres
- Polarisation bands: VV and VH
- Main advantage:
  - Day-and-night observation
  - Relatively robust to clouds and weather
  - Sensitive to vessels, containers, cranes, and metallic infrastructure

### 2. VIIRS Nighttime Lights / VIIRS 夜间灯光

- Product: NASA VNP46A2
- Original frequency: daily
- Spatial resolution: approximately 500 metres
- Daily observations are aggregated into monthly port-level indicators.

### 3. World Port Index / 世界港口索引

- Version: December 2025
- Provider: National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency
- Coverage: more than 3,800 ports worldwide
- Variables used in the model: 91 port-level attributes

### 4. U.S. Census Port-Level Trade Data / 美国人口普查局港口贸易数据

- Monthly vessel-based imports and exports
- Port-level trade value
- Port-level trade weight
- Imports and exports are aggregated into total monthly maritime trade.

### Main sample / 主样本

- Period: January 2016–December 2024
- Unit of observation: port-month
- Number of ports: 64 U.S. ports
- Port categories: Small, Medium, and Large ports in the WPI
- Months without usable Sentinel-1 observations are excluded.

## Area of Interest Construction / 港口研究区域构建

### English

For each port, the author constructs a square area of interest centred on the WPI port coordinate, using an approximately 3 km radius.

The buffer size is selected to balance two competing concerns:

- A small AOI may omit relevant terminals, vessels, or cargo-handling areas.
- A large AOI may overlap with neighbouring ports or include unrelated residential and urban areas.

### 中文

作者以 WPI 港口坐标为中心，为每个港口建立约 3 km 半径的方形 AOI。

AOI 尺度需要在两方面之间取得平衡：

- 范围过小可能遗漏码头、船舶、锚地或装卸区域；
- 范围过大可能与邻近港口重叠，或纳入住宅区和城市灯光等非港口活动。

## Features / 特征变量

## 1. SAR Features / SAR 遥感变量

### VV Image Difference / VV 影像差分

For two consecutive VV images, the paper calculates the absolute pixel-level difference:

\[
D_{a,t}
=
\sum_{i,j}
\left|
I^{VV}_{a,t+1}(i,j)-I^{VV}_{a,t}(i,j)
\right|
\]

The monthly VV indicator is the median of consecutive-image differences within each month:

\[
VV_{a,m}
=
\operatorname{median}\{D_{a,t}:t\in m\}
\]

**Economic interpretation / 经济含义**:

- Vessel movements
- Arrival and departure activity
- Loading and unloading changes
- Short-term physical changes within the port
- Dynamic port activity

This is a signal-based change-detection indicator rather than an explicit vessel-counting or object-detection model.

该变量并不是直接识别或计算船舶数量，而是利用相邻 SAR 影像之间的变化，代理船舶移动、装卸活动和港区短期变化。

### VH Backscatter / VH 后向散射

The author constructs a monthly median VH composite and calculates average backscatter over the port AOI.

**Economic interpretation / 经济含义**:

- Metallic vessels
- Containers
- Cranes and vertical structures
- Port infrastructure
- Container accumulation
- Persistent cargo stock or possible congestion

The VV and VH indicators are therefore complementary:

- **VV** mainly captures short-run movement and change.
- **VH** mainly captures stocks, metallic objects, and persistent port characteristics.

因此：

- **VV** 更接近动态变化和流量；
- **VH** 更接近金属物体、集装箱存量、基础设施和潜在拥堵。

---

## 2. Nighttime-Light Features / 夜间灯光变量

### Monthly Mean NTL / 月均夜间灯光

Represents the average intensity of nighttime activity within the port AOI.

Possible economic meaning:

- Continuous cargo handling
- Nighttime logistics operations
- Security and terminal illumination
- General operating intensity

### Monthly Standard Deviation / 月内标准差

Measures variation in daily nighttime-light intensity within a month.

Possible interpretation:

- Fluctuating operating intensity
- Irregular cargo-handling schedules
- Concentrated periods of nighttime activity

### Lit Area Ratio / 亮灯面积比例

The paper calculates the proportion of pixels within the AOI whose median nighttime-light intensity exceeds a fixed threshold:

\[
LR_{a,m}
=
\frac{1}{|\Omega_a|}
\sum_{(i,j)\in\Omega_a}
\mathbf{1}
\left\{
\operatorname{median}_{t\in m}
I^{NTL}_{a,t}(i,j)>\tau
\right\}
\]

This variable approximates the spatial extent of illuminated port-related activity and infrastructure.

亮灯面积比例不仅反映灯光有多亮，还反映港口运营和基础设施所覆盖的空间范围。

它们本身通常不随月份变化，因此无法单独解释同一港口短期活动的波动。

## Experimental Design / 实验设计

## 1. Time-Series Nowcasting / 时间序列即时预测

### Training and test split / 训练测试划分

- Training period: January 2016–May 2022
- Test period: June 2022–December 2024
- Approximate split: first 70% for training and final 30% for testing

The data are split chronologically rather than randomly, which better reflects a real forecasting environment.

数据按照时间顺序切分，而不是随机打乱，因此比随机交叉验证更接近真实预测场景。

## 2. Model Specifications / 模型规格

| Specification | Inputs |
|---|---|
| **Satellite + Port** | SAR + NTL + WPI port characteristics |
| **Satellite Only** | SAR + NTL |
| **Port Only** | WPI port characteristics |

### Purpose / 目的

The three specifications form an ablation framework that separates:

- The contribution of dynamic satellite signals
- The contribution of static port infrastructure
- The potential complementarity between the two feature groups

## Key Findings / 主要发现

## 1. Strong Nowcasting Performance / 即时预测表现较强

### Trade Value / 贸易价值

| Specification | \(R^2\) | Correlation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Satellite + Port | 0.945 | 0.976 |
| Port Only | 0.933 | 0.976 |
| Satellite Only | 0.863 | 0.934 |

### Trade Weight / 贸易重量

| Specification | \(R^2\) | Correlation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Satellite + Port | 0.880 | 0.938 |
| Port Only | 0.908 | 0.953 |
| Satellite Only | 0.776 | 0.881 |

### Interpretation / 解释

The full model provides the strongest prediction for trade value. However, the port-only model performs almost as well for trade value and slightly better for trade weight.

This shows that adding more data sources does not automatically improve every prediction target.

完整模型对贸易价值的表现最好，但仅使用港口属性的模型已经能够获得非常高的预测精度；对于贸易重量，Port Only 模型甚至略优于完整模型。

因此，本文并未证明“变量越多越好”，而是说明不同变量组对不同目标具有不同价值。

---

## 2. Static Port Characteristics Explain Levels / 静态港口属性解释绝对水平

Time-invariant port characteristics explain a large proportion of cross-port differences in trade levels.

Important infrastructure variables include:

- Oil-terminal depth
- Channel depth
- Tug availability
- Crane facilities
- Terminal type
- Port services

Large, deep, well-equipped ports tend to have persistently higher trade volumes.

港口深度、规模、起重机、拖船和码头类型等静态属性，能够很好地解释为什么某些港口长期贸易规模更大。

---

## 3. Satellite Signals Capture Temporal Dynamics / 卫星信号捕捉时间变化

Static port attributes cannot explain why the same port becomes more or less active in a particular month.

Satellite variables provide this time-varying information:

- VV captures short-run physical changes.
- VH captures persistent metallic objects and cargo stocks.
- NTL captures operating intensity.
- Lit area ratio approximates the spatial footprint of active infrastructure.

因此，本文最重要的结论之一是：

> Port characteristics explain cross-sectional levels, while satellite signals capture within-port temporal dynamics.

即：

> 港口静态属性解释港口之间的长期水平差异，卫星信号解释同一港口随时间发生的活动变化。

---

## 4. Satellite-Only Feature Importance / 仅卫星模型中的变量重要性

For trade-value prediction in the satellite-only model:

| Feature | Approximate gain importance |
|---|---:|
| VV image difference | 30.6% |
| Mean nighttime lights | 25.0% |
| Lit area ratio | 19.1% |
| VH backscatter | 13.0% |
| NTL standard deviation | 12.3% |

Predictive power is distributed across several remote-sensing indicators rather than being driven by one single feature.

这说明遥感预测能力并非由某一个指标单独驱动，而是来自多个互补卫星信号。

---

## 5. Nonlinear Predictive Relationships / 非线性预测关系

The VV difference variable is not statistically significant in the paper's simple OLS regressions, but it becomes the most important variable in the satellite-only XGBoost model.

This suggests that its predictive contribution may arise through:

- Nonlinear threshold effects
- Interactions with port size
- Interactions with NTL
- Different effects across port types
- Different effects at high and low activity levels

因此，线性回归中不显著并不意味着变量没有预测价值。XGBoost 可以识别线性模型难以捕捉的非线性和变量交互。

However, XGBoost feature importance measures predictive contribution rather than causal effect or directional impact.

不过，XGBoost 特征重要性只能说明变量对预测的贡献，不能说明因果关系，也不能直接说明变量增加会使贸易上升还是下降。

---

## 6. Placebo Test / 安慰剂检验

The author randomly shuffles satellite variables across observations.

After shuffling:

\[
R^2=-0.019
\]

\[
Corr=-0.026
\]

Predictive performance collapses, indicating that the original satellite variables contain meaningful port-time information.

作者随机打乱卫星变量后，模型预测能力几乎完全消失。这说明模型并非只依赖时间趋势或港口平均水平，真实卫星信号确实包含有效信息。

## Spatial Extrapolation / 空间外推

## Hawaii Leave-One-Region-Out Test / 夏威夷留一区域验证

The author excludes Hawaii completely from model training and uses mainland U.S. ports to predict the ports of Honolulu and Hilo.

### Before anchoring / 校准前

For trade value:

\[
R^2=-1.380
\]

\[
Corr=0.896
\]

The high correlation indicates that the model captures temporal movement, but the negative \(R^2\) shows that it fails to recover the correct absolute level.

这意味着模型大致预测对了上升和下降趋势，但整条预测曲线可能系统性偏高或偏低。

The model especially overpredicts Hilo because the training sample contains no sufficiently comparable mainland port.

---

## Anchoring Strategy / 基准校准策略

The author uses the first observed month to estimate a constant level offset:

\[
c=\hat{Y}_{i,1}-Y_{i,1}
\]

Adjusted predictions are then calculated as:

\[
\hat{Y}^{adjusted}_{i,t}
=
\hat{Y}_{i,t}-c
\]

### After anchoring / 校准后

- Satellite + Port: \(R^2=0.874\)
- Satellite Only: \(R^2=0.937\)

The satellite-only model performs better than the model containing high-dimensional port attributes after anchoring.

这表明：

- 卫星连续变量能够较好地捕捉新地区的时间变化；
- 高维港口分类属性可能使模型过度学习训练地区的结构；
- 加入更多静态变量可能降低跨空间泛化能力。

---

## Why Changes Generalise Better Than Levels / 为什么变化比绝对水平更容易外推

The paper assumes:

\[
Y_{i,t}
=
\alpha_i+\beta X_{i,t}+\varepsilon_{i,t}
\]

where \(\alpha_i\) represents a port-specific baseline level.

When pre/post changes are calculated:

\[
\Delta Y_i
=
\bar{Y}_{i,\mathrm{post}}
-
\bar{Y}_{i,\mathrm{pre}}
\]

the port-specific fixed component cancels:

\[
\Delta Y_i
=
\beta
\left(
\bar{X}_{i,\mathrm{post}}
-
\bar{X}_{i,\mathrm{pre}}
\right)
+
\Delta\varepsilon_i
\]

Monte Carlo results show:

- Slope between predicted and actual changes: 0.988
- Correlation: 0.997

Therefore, the model may struggle to determine how large an unseen port is, but it can still estimate whether activity has increased or decreased.

因此，模型可能无法准确判断一个新港口的绝对贸易规模，但仍能够较准确地判断其活动增加了多少或减少了多少。

## Application to Russian Ports / 俄罗斯港口应用

The model trained on U.S. ports is applied to 15 Russian ports with sufficient Sentinel-1 coverage between 2017 and 2024.

The paper compares average predicted activity before and after the February 2022 sanctions.

Selected results include:

- Kaliningrad: approximately −1.44%
- Kronshtadt: approximately −0.47%
- Korsakov: approximately +0.43%
- Sovetskaya Gavan: approximately +0.87%
- De Kastri: approximately +1.5%

### Interpretation / 解释

The model identifies:

- Declines at some European-facing Russian ports
- Increases at several Far Eastern and Sakhalin ports

This is consistent with a possible reorientation of Russian energy and maritime activity toward Asian markets after sanctions.

模型发现部分面向欧洲的俄罗斯港口活动下降，而远东和萨哈林地区部分港口活动上升，这与俄罗斯能源出口向亚洲重新配置的趋势一致。

However, the predictions measure physical port activity rather than verified commercial trade.

模型测量的是港口物理活动，而不是经过确认的商业贸易。因此，它无法完全区分：

- Commercial trade
- Military logistics
- Vessel maintenance
- Congestion
- Anchorage activity
- Other non-commercial maritime movements

## Interpretability / 可解释性

### English

The paper reports XGBoost feature importance based on the gain metric.

It does **not** use SHAP.

Gain importance measures how much each feature contributes to reducing prediction error across tree splits, but it does not directly provide:

- The direction of an effect
- Observation-level explanations
- Causal interpretation
- Stable importance under correlated features

### 中文

本文使用的是基于 gain 的 XGBoost 特征重要性，并未使用 SHAP。

Gain importance 可以说明某变量在树模型分裂中对降低预测误差的贡献，但不能直接提供：

- 变量影响方向；
- 单个观测的解释；
- 因果关系；
- 共线变量条件下完全稳定的重要性排序。

在你的研究中，可以将 SHAP 作为对 Jung 方法的进一步扩展，而不能写成“Jung 使用 SHAP”。

## Key Contributions / 主要贡献

1. **Satellite-based measurement of maritime trade**  
   Extends satellite-based economic measurement to monthly port-level maritime trade.

   **卫星测度海运贸易**  
   将卫星经济活动测度扩展到港口级月度海运贸易。

   Uses publicly available Sentinel-1, VIIRS, WPI, and Census data.

   **公开数据框架**  
   主要数据均可公开获取，提高了透明度和可重复性。

   Provides a method that remains usable when AIS signals are unavailable, disabled, or manipulated.

   **不依赖 AIS 的监测**  
   在船舶关闭或操纵 AIS 信号时，遥感仍可能观测到港口物理活动。

   Demonstrates that static infrastructure explains levels, while satellite signals explain temporal variation.

   Shows that high random or time-series test performance does not guarantee successful prediction in unseen spatial domains.

   **空间外推分析**

   Provides theoretical and simulation-based evidence that changes are more transferable than absolute levels.

   **变化型预测**

## Limitations / 局限性

1. **Aggregate maritime trade target / 目标为综合海运贸易**

   The model predicts total maritime trade rather than crude-oil-specific trade.

   模型预测所有海运货物的综合贸易活动，而不是原油、成品油或 LNG 的专门流量。

2. **No commodity-level identification / 无法识别商品类别**

   It cannot separate crude oil, LNG, coal, containers, grain, or other bulk commodities.

   模型无法区分原油、天然气、煤炭、集装箱、粮食等不同货物。

3. **No bilateral trade flows / 无法识别双边流向**

   The model does not identify origins, destinations, or trading partners.

   文章不能判断货物来自哪个国家或运往哪个市场。

4. **Physical activity is not always commercial trade / 物理活动不一定等同于商业贸易**

   Military logistics, maintenance, congestion, and anchorage can produce similar satellite signals.

   军事物流、维修、拥堵和锚泊等非商业活动也可能产生类似卫星信号。

5. **Moderate temporal frequency / 时间频率有限**

   Sentinel-1 revisit frequency makes the approach more suitable for monthly or medium-term analysis than precise daily forecasting.

   Sentinel-1 的重访频率使该方法更适合月度或中期分析，而不是高频日度预测。

6. **AOI sensitivity / AOI 敏感性**

   Results may depend on port coordinates and AOI boundaries. Large AOIs may include cities or neighbouring ports, while small AOIs may omit relevant terminals.

   遥感指标可能对港口坐标和 AOI 范围较敏感。

   WPI characteristics improve in-domain level predictions but can reduce out-of-domain generalisation.

9. **No deep-learning image benchmark / 未与影像深度学习模型比较**

   The study does not compare the tabular-feature approach with CNN-based or vision-transformer models.

   文章没有与 CNN、Vision Transformer 等原始影像模型进行比较。

10. **Feature importance is not causal / 特征重要性不是因果效应**

    Gain importance cannot establish whether a satellite signal causes trade changes.

## Overall Assessment / 总体评价

### English

This paper is highly relevant to the dissertation as a methodological reference for remote-sensing feature construction, multi-source tabular prediction, XGBoost modelling, ablation analysis, AIS-independent monitoring, and spatial extrapolation.

Its most important lesson is not simply that the full model achieves a high \(R^2\). Rather, it demonstrates that:

- Different data modalities explain different components of the target.
- More features do not necessarily improve out-of-sample performance.
- Random or in-domain validation may overstate spatial generalisation.
- Absolute levels are harder to transfer than changes.
- Placebo tests are necessary to establish genuine information content.

However, an important conceptual gap remains:

\[
\text{Remote sensing / shipping activity}
\rightarrow
\text{Port or oil-flow conditions}
\rightarrow
\text{Crude oil price}
\]

Jung directly models the first relationship between satellite activity and maritime trade. The dissertation must additionally demonstrate the second relationship between oil-related physical activity and Brent price movements.

- 多源表格数据融合；
- XGBoost 预测；

- 空间外推；

- 样本内或普通测试集表现可能高估空间泛化能力；

- 安慰剂检验对于证明新数据的真实信息价值十分重要。

但 Jung 与本论文之间仍存在一个关键的机制缺口：

\text{港口活动或石油流量}

\text{原油价格}

Jung 主要证明了卫星活动与港口贸易之间的第一段关系。本论文还需要通过 M1–M4 比较、滞后设计、经济机制和稳健性检验，证明石油相关港口活动是否能够进一步预测 Brent 原油价格。
