## Research Objective / 研究目标

### English

This paper develops a unified taxonomy for multimodal machine learning. Rather than limiting multimodal research to the conventional distinction between early fusion and late fusion, the authors identify five broader technical challenges:

1. Representation
2. Translation
3. Alignment
4. Fusion
5. Co-learning

The objective is to organise a highly fragmented literature and explain how machine-learning systems can represent, relate, align, integrate, and transfer knowledge across heterogeneous data modalities.

### 中文

本文旨在建立一套统一的多模态机器学习分类体系。作者认为，仅使用传统的“早期融合—后期融合”二分法，无法完整描述多模态学习所面对的问题，因此提出五类核心技术挑战：

1. 表示学习（Representation）
2. 跨模态转换（Translation）
3. 多模态对齐（Alignment）
4. 多模态融合（Fusion）
5. 跨模态共同学习（Co-learning）

论文的目标不是提出一个具体预测模型，而是系统解释机器学习模型应如何处理结构、频率、噪声和信息含义均不同的异质数据。

## Definition of Modality / 模态的定义

### English

A modality is a particular way in which information is observed, expressed, or experienced. A problem is multimodal when it contains multiple information channels with different structures or statistical properties.

The paper mainly discusses:

- Natural language
- Images and video
- Audio and vocal signals

However, its framework is general and can be extended to other heterogeneous data sources.

### 中文

模态是信息被观测、表达或感知的一种特定形式。当一个研究问题包含多个具有不同数据结构、生成机制或统计特征的信息来源时，可以将其视为多模态问题。

论文主要讨论：

- 自然语言；
- 图像与视频；
- 音频与语音信号。

但其方法框架并不局限于这些感知模态，同样可以扩展到金融时间序列、遥感影像、航运流量和空间网络等数据。

| Challenge | English definition | 中文解释 | Main subcategories |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Representation** | Learning representations that summarise heterogeneous modalities while retaining complementary and redundant information. | 将不同模态转换为模型可以处理的向量或张量，同时保留模态内部结构及模态之间的互补、重复信息。 | Joint representations; coordinated representations |

# 1. Multimodal Representation / 多模态表示

## 1.1 Representation as an embedding / 表示与嵌入

### English

The paper uses “feature” and “representation” in a broadly similar way. A representation is a vector or tensor encoding of an object, sequence, image, sentence, or signal.

A good multimodal representation should:

- Preserve meaningful information from each modality;
- Reflect semantic similarity;
- Exploit complementarity and redundancy;
- Retain temporal and spatial coherence;
- Be robust to noise;
- Remain usable when some modalities are missing;
- Potentially reconstruct missing modalities from observed ones.

### 中文

论文将 feature 和 representation 视为含义接近的概念。表示是模型对图像、序列、文本或信号形成的向量或张量编码，也可以理解为 embedding。

一个理想的多模态表示应当：

- 保留每种模态中的关键信息；
- 使表示空间中的距离能够反映真实语义相似性；
- 利用模态之间的互补信息和重复信息；
- 保留时间连续性与空间结构；
- 对噪声具有一定鲁棒性；
- 在部分模态缺失时仍然可以使用；
- 在可能的情况下，根据已有模态推断缺失模态。

---

## 1.2 Joint Representation / 联合表示

### Definition

A joint representation combines multiple modalities into one shared representation:

\[
z_{\text{joint}}=f(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_M)
\]

The simplest joint representation is direct concatenation:

\[
z_{\text{joint}}=[x_1;x_2;\ldots;x_M]
\]

More advanced approaches first process each modality separately and then project their intermediate representations into a common latent space.

### 中文解释

联合表示把多个模态共同映射到一个共享的潜在空间中。

最简单的方法是把所有模态的特征直接拼接。更复杂的方法则是：

1. 每个模态先经过自己的编码器；
2. 获得模态专属表示；
3. 再通过共享隐藏层或融合模块；
4. 形成联合表示；
5. 使用联合表示完成预测。

### Typical methods / 典型方法

- Neural networks
- Autoencoders
- Deep belief networks
- Deep Boltzmann machines
- Recurrent neural networks
- End-to-end multimodal networks

### Strengths / 优点

- Can explicitly learn cross-modal interactions;
- Can be trained end-to-end;
- Potentially offers strong predictive performance;
- Can integrate representation learning and prediction in one model.

### Weaknesses / 缺点

- Usually requires substantial training data;
- May be difficult to train;
- Does not naturally handle missing modalities;
- Can be difficult to interpret;
- One noisy modality may contaminate the joint representation.

---

## 1.3 Coordinated Representation / 协调表示

### Definition

Coordinated representations retain separate representations for each modality but impose constraints so that the modality spaces are related:

\[
z_1=f(x_1),\qquad z_2=g(x_2)
\]

\[
f(x_1)\sim g(x_2)
\]

The symbol \(\sim\) represents a coordination constraint, such as:

- Minimising cosine distance;
- Maximising cross-modal correlation;
- Ranking corresponding pairs above non-corresponding pairs;
- Enforcing a shared hierarchy or partial order.

### 中文解释

协调表示不立即把不同模态压缩成一个向量，而是保留各模态独立的表示空间，再通过某种约束使这些空间相互协调。

例如，同一时间段的遥感、航运和金融状态应在表示空间中具有较高的一致性，而不相关时间段的表示应相互远离。

### Main types / 主要类型

- Similarity-based coordinated representations
- Structured coordinated representations
- Canonical Correlation Analysis
- Deep Canonical Correlation Analysis
- Ranking-based embeddings
- Cross-modal hashing

### Suitable situations / 适用场景

- Cross-modal retrieval;
- Zero-shot learning;
- One modality may be unavailable during inference;
- Pretraining using paired multimodal observations;
- Contrastive or correlation-based representation learning.

---

## Joint vs. Coordinated Representation / 联合表示与协调表示对比

| Aspect | Joint representation | Coordinated representation |
|---|---|---|
| Representation space | One shared multimodal space | Separate but related spaces |
| Modalities at inference | Usually all modalities are available | May work with only one modality |
| Main purpose | Prediction and fusion | Retrieval, transfer, alignment, pretraining |
| Basic example | Feature concatenation | Similarity or correlation constraint |
| Dissertation use | Final Brent forecasting model | Auxiliary pretraining or alignment objective |

# 2. Multimodal Translation / 跨模态转换

## Definition / 定义

Multimodal translation maps an object from one modality into another modality.

Examples include:

- Image to text;
- Text to image;
- Video to language description;
- Text to speech;
- Audio to visual speech.

跨模态转换是根据一种模态的信息，检索、生成或恢复另一种模态的信息。

---

## 2.1 Example-based Translation / 基于实例的转换

The model searches a dictionary or training database for similar examples.

### Subtypes

- **Retrieval-based**: directly uses the closest retrieved result;
- **Combination-based**: combines components from multiple retrieved examples.

### Advantages

- Relatively simple;
- Does not require a complex generative model;
- Can produce realistic outputs when close matches exist.

### Limitations

- Heavily dependent on the training dictionary;
- Large storage and slow retrieval may be required;
- Cannot reliably generate unseen structures;
- A suitable reference example may not exist.

---

## 2.2 Generative Translation / 生成式转换

Generative models learn a mapping from the source modality to the target modality.

### Main categories

1. Grammar-based generation
2. Encoder-decoder models
3. Continuous generation models

An encoder-decoder model can be represented as:

\[
x_{\text{source}}
\rightarrow
z
\rightarrow
\hat{x}_{\text{target}}
\]

### Relevance to the dissertation / 对本论文的相关性

Translation is not the central task of the oil-price forecasting project because the objective is not to generate one modality from another.

However, it could support auxiliary tasks such as:

- Predicting port activity from satellite observations;
- Reconstructing missing shipping observations;
- Estimating latent physical activity from multiple sensors;
- Using an autoencoder to reconstruct masked modalities.

---

# 3. Multimodal Alignment / 多模态对齐

## Definition / 定义

Alignment identifies relationships between subcomponents of two or more modalities.

Examples in the paper include:

- Words aligned with image regions;
- Recipe steps aligned with video segments;
- Speech phonemes aligned with transcripts;
- Movie scenes aligned with books or scripts.

For the oil-price forecasting dissertation, the equivalent question is:

> Which financial, remote-sensing, and shipping observations correspond to the same underlying economic process, and with what time lag?

---

## 3.1 Explicit Alignment / 显式对齐

Explicit alignment directly estimates correspondences between modality components.

### Main forms

- Unsupervised alignment;
- Supervised or weakly supervised alignment;
- Dynamic time warping;
- Learned similarity functions;
- Graphical alignment models.

### Dissertation examples

- Estimating the lag between tanker-flow disruption and Brent-price response;
- Aligning satellite-detected activity with later inventory releases;
- Matching port-level physical activity with corresponding shipping flows.

---

## 3.2 Implicit Alignment / 隐式对齐

Implicit alignment is learned as an intermediate latent process while optimising another task.

Attention is a typical implicit-alignment mechanism because it allows a model to focus on selected components of another modality without requiring explicit correspondence labels.

### Dissertation examples

- Cross-attention learns which shipping weeks are relevant to the current financial state;
- AOI attention learns which refinery or port locations matter for a specific prediction;
- Temporal attention learns which historical observations have the strongest relationship with next-week oil returns.

---

## Alignment problems in the dissertation / 本项目中的对齐问题

| Modality | Alignment issue |
|---|---|
| Financial data | Regular daily or weekly observations |
| Remote sensing | Irregular revisit intervals, cloud contamination, missing observations |
| AIS and PortWatch | Daily events, transport delays, possible reporting issues |
| Inventory and macroeconomic variables | Publication lag and revision |
| Target variable | Defined at a specific forecast origin and forecast horizon |

The dissertation should distinguish:

- **Event time**: when an economic or physical event occurred;
- **Observation time**: when the event was observed by a sensor;
- **Release time**: when the data became publicly available;
- **Forecast origin**: the final information cutoff used to generate the forecast.

Only information genuinely available at forecast time \(t\) should be included in \(X_t\).

---

# 4. Multimodal Fusion / 多模态融合

## Definition / 定义

Fusion combines information from two or more modalities to make a prediction.

The paper separates fusion into:

1. Model-agnostic fusion
2. Model-based fusion

---

## 4.1 Model-Agnostic Fusion / 模型无关融合

### Early Fusion / 早期融合

Features are combined before prediction, often by direct concatenation:

\[
X_t=
[X^{\text{fin}}_t;
X^{\text{rs}}_t;
X^{\text{ship}}_t]
\]

The combined feature matrix can then be processed by:

- Linear regression;
- XGBoost;
- LightGBM;
- Random forest;
- MLP;
- TCN.

#### Advantages

- Easy to implement;
- Requires only one prediction model;
- Can capture interactions between input variables;
- Suitable for small and medium-sized datasets;
- Compatible with feature-importance methods such as SHAP.

#### Limitations

- Treats heterogeneous modalities as ordinary columns;
- May remove spatial, temporal, image, or graph structure;
- Sensitive to feature-scale and dimensionality imbalance;
- Difficult to handle an entirely missing modality;
- Does not explicitly distinguish modality quality.

### Relation to the dissertation

The current flattened M4 feature model is an early-fusion model:

\[
M4_{\text{early}}
=
M1+M2+M3
\]

where market, remote-sensing, and shipping features are placed in one feature table.

This is a valid and necessary baseline, but it should not be presented as the full methodological contribution of multimodal representation learning.

---

### Late Fusion / 后期融合

Each modality produces its own prediction:

\[
\hat y_{\text{fin}}
=
f_{\text{fin}}(X_{\text{fin}})
\]

\[
\hat y_{\text{rs}}
=
f_{\text{rs}}(X_{\text{rs}})
\]

\[
\hat y_{\text{ship}}
=
f_{\text{ship}}(X_{\text{ship}})
\]

The predictions are then combined:

\[
\hat y
=
w_{\text{fin}}\hat y_{\text{fin}}
+
w_{\text{rs}}\hat y_{\text{rs}}
+
w_{\text{ship}}\hat y_{\text{ship}}
\]

Possible fusion rules include:

- Simple averaging;
- Weighted averaging;
- Voting;
- Noise-dependent weighting;
- Stacking regression;
- Regime-dependent dynamic weights.

#### Advantages

- Each modality can use a different model;
- Easier to handle missing modalities;
- Easier to inspect modality-level predictive contributions;
- Does not require perfectly paired training data in every case.

#### Limitations

- Ignores low-level cross-modal interactions;
- May lose information that is only meaningful when modalities are considered jointly.

---

### Hybrid Fusion / 混合融合

Hybrid fusion combines early-fusion outputs and unimodal predictions.

For example:

\[
z_{\text{joint}}
=
F(z_{\text{fin}},z_{\text{rs}},z_{\text{ship}})
\]

\[
\hat y
=
g(z_{\text{joint}},
\hat y_{\text{fin}},
\hat y_{\text{rs}},
\hat y_{\text{ship}})
\]

Hybrid fusion attempts to retain:

- Cross-modal interactions;
- Unimodal predictive strength;
- Robustness when one modality is weak or missing.

---

## 4.2 Model-Based Fusion / 模型驱动融合

Model-based methods explicitly incorporate multimodal fusion into the model architecture.

The paper identifies:

- Multiple-kernel learning;
- Graphical models;
- Neural-network-based fusion.

### Neural-network-based fusion

Each modality may first pass through a modality-specific encoder:

\[
z_{\text{fin}}=E_{\text{fin}}(X_{\text{fin}})
\]

\[
z_{\text{rs}}=E_{\text{rs}}(X_{\text{rs}})
\]

\[
z_{\text{ship}}=E_{\text{ship}}(X_{\text{ship}})
\]

The resulting representations are fused:

\[
z_{\text{fused}}
=
F(z_{\text{fin}},z_{\text{rs}},z_{\text{ship}})
\]

The target is predicted through:

\[
\hat y_{t+1}=g(z_{\text{fused}})
\]

The complete architecture can be trained end-to-end so that the prediction loss updates:

- Each modality encoder;
- The fusion module;
- The final forecasting head.

### Main advantages

- Learns task-specific representations;
- Can model nonlinear cross-modal interactions;
- Representation learning and prediction can be optimised jointly;
- Can potentially outperform fixed feature engineering when sufficient data are available.

### Main limitations

- Requires more training data;
- Higher computational complexity;
- Greater risk of overfitting;
- Difficult to interpret;
- Standard neural models do not automatically solve missing-modality problems.

---

# 5. Co-learning / 跨模态共同学习

## Definition / 定义

Co-learning uses knowledge from one modality to improve learning in another modality, especially when one modality has:

- Few labelled observations;
- Noisy inputs;
- Missing values;
- Unreliable labels;
- A shorter historical record.

---

## 5.1 Parallel Data / 平行数据

The modalities describe the same observations and have direct sample-level correspondence.

Dissertation example:

- Financial, satellite, shipping, and Brent observations all refer to the same week.

Possible methods:

- Joint representation learning;
- Multimodal autoencoders;
- Co-training;
- Cross-modal distillation;
- Contrastive learning across corresponding weeks.

---

## 5.2 Non-parallel Data / 非平行数据

The modalities do not contain directly matched observations, but they share concepts or categories.

Dissertation example:

- A remote-sensing encoder may be pretrained on a large image archive that does not overlap exactly with the Brent forecasting period;
- A shipping encoder may be pretrained on long AIS histories before joint forecasting begins.

---

## 5.3 Hybrid Data / 混合数据

Modalities are connected through an intermediate modality, dataset, or concept.

Dissertation example:

\[
\text{Satellite port activity}
\leftrightarrow
\text{Port-week entity}
\leftrightarrow
\text{AIS vessel activity}
\]

or:

\[
\text{Remote sensing}
\leftrightarrow
\text{Physical oil activity}
\leftrightarrow
\text{Oil-market state}
\]

# Key Findings / 主要结论

## English

1. Multimodal machine learning cannot be adequately described only through early and late fusion. Representation, translation, alignment, fusion, and co-learning are distinct but interconnected challenges.

2. Joint and coordinated representations serve different purposes. Joint representations are particularly suitable when all modalities are available during prediction, while coordinated representations are useful for retrieval, transfer learning, and situations where modalities are not simultaneously available.

3. Early fusion is simple and can capture low-level interactions, but it may treat fundamentally different modalities as homogeneous features.

4. Late fusion provides greater modelling flexibility and better missing-modality robustness, but it may fail to capture low-level cross-modal interactions.

5. Hybrid and model-based fusion can combine modality-specific modelling with cross-modal interaction learning.

6. Attention can function as an implicit alignment mechanism, allowing models to identify relationships between modality components without explicit alignment labels.

7. Multimodal systems may benefit from both complementary and supplementary information.

8. A second modality may not always improve average predictive accuracy. Its main benefit may appear when the dominant modality is noisy or unavailable.

9. Different modalities can exhibit different noise structures, temporal resolutions, missingness patterns, and predictive strengths.

10. Deep multimodal models offer strong representation-learning capacity but require large datasets and often have limited interpretability.

## 中文

1. 多模态机器学习不能只用早期融合和后期融合来概括。表示、转换、对齐、融合和共同学习是相互关联但又具有独立含义的技术问题。

2. 联合表示和协调表示适用于不同场景。预测阶段所有模态均可获得时，联合表示通常更适合；存在模态缺失、跨模态检索或迁移学习需求时，协调表示更有价值。

3. 早期融合简单，并且可以学习变量之间的低层交互，但容易把结构完全不同的数据当成同质化数值列。

4. 后期融合允许每种模态使用不同模型，也更容易处理模态缺失，但可能忽视只有在底层联合分析时才能发现的跨模态关系。

5. 混合融合和模型驱动融合能够同时保留模态专属建模与跨模态交互学习。

6. 注意力机制可以被视为隐式对齐方法，使模型能够在没有显式对齐标签的情况下学习不同模态元素之间的对应关系。

7. 多模态数据可能同时包含互补信息和补充性或重复性信息。

8. 增加一种模态不一定在所有情况下都提高平均预测精度。它的主要价值可能体现在主导模态噪声较大、失效或缺失的时期。

9. 不同模态具有不同的噪声结构、时间分辨率、缺失机制和预测能力，因此不能使用完全相同的处理方式。

10. 深度多模态模型具有较强的表示学习能力，但通常需要更多训练数据，并存在可解释性不足的问题。

# Complementary vs. Supplementary Information  
# 互补信息与补充性信息

## Complementary information / 互补信息

A modality contains information that is unavailable in the other modalities.

For the dissertation:

- Financial variables capture market expectations;
- Shipping data capture physical transportation flows;
- Remote sensing captures observable on-ground physical activity.

\[
\text{Market expectations}
\neq
\text{Physical flows}
\neq
\text{Observed industrial activity}
\]

## Supplementary or redundant information / 补充性或重复信息

Different modalities may observe the same underlying process.

For example:

- AIS indicates reduced tanker movement;
- Satellite imagery also indicates reduced port activity;
- Financial markets may already partially price in the same disruption.

The additional modality may therefore improve robustness rather than substantially reduce average RMSE.

This distinction implies that the dissertation should not ask only:

> Does M4 outperform M1 on average?

It should also ask:

> Under which market conditions do remote-sensing and shipping modalities provide incremental or robustness-enhancing information?

| **Robustness / 鲁棒性** | Evaluation should include missing-modality tests and regime-specific performance, not only full-sample RMSE. 评价应包含缺失模态压力测试和市场状态分组，而不仅是全样本 RMSE。 |

The project should not treat a missing observation as an ordinary zero.

For each modality, define an availability mask:

\[
m_{k,t}\in\{0,1\}
\]

For remote sensing, additional quality variables may include:

\[
a_{\text{rs},t}
=
\text{days since last valid observation}
\]

\[

=

\]

The representation can be augmented as:

\[
\tilde z_{\text{rs},t}
=
[z_{\text{rs},t};
m_{\text{rs},t};
a_{\text{rs},t};
q_{\text{rs},t}]
\]

Recommended methods:

- Availability masks;
- Time-since-last-observation variables;
- Quality-aware gates;
- Modality dropout;
- Masked-modality reconstruction;
- Separate missing-modality evaluation.

Cloud fraction may be removed as a direct economic predictor while still being retained as an observation-quality indicator.

# Gated Fusion / 门控融合

A gate can learn time-varying modality weights:

\[
\boldsymbol{\alpha}_t
=
\operatorname{softmax}
\left(
W_g
[z_{\text{fin},t};
z_{\text{rs},t};
z_{\text{ship},t};
q_t]
+b_g
\right)
\]

where:

\[
\alpha_{\text{fin},t}
+
\alpha_{\text{rs},t}
+
\alpha_{\text{ship},t}
=1
\]

The fused representation is:

\[
z_{\text{fused},t}
=
\alpha_{\text{fin},t}z_{\text{fin},t}
+
\alpha_{\text{rs},t}z_{\text{rs},t}
+
\alpha_{\text{ship},t}z_{\text{ship},t}
\]

Possible interpretation:

- Financial modality dominates in ordinary market periods;
- Shipping modality gains weight during transport disruption;
- Remote sensing gains weight when physical port or storage activity changes;
- Low-quality or stale modalities receive lower weights.

The paper provides the conceptual foundation for representation, alignment, and model-based fusion. It does not directly propose the modern gated cross-attention architecture.

# Limitations / 局限性

1. **No empirical forecasting experiment / 缺少统一实证实验**  
   The paper is a survey and does not estimate one model on one common dataset. It therefore cannot demonstrate that any fusion approach universally performs best.  
   本文是一篇综述，没有在统一数据集上训练和比较所有方法，因此不能证明某一种融合方法在所有任务上最优。

2. **Not an oil-price forecasting study / 并非油价预测研究**  
   Most examples come from language, image, audio, video, affect recognition, and multimedia retrieval.  
   论文案例主要来自语言、图像、音频、视频、情感识别和多媒体检索，并未直接研究 Brent 或 WTI 预测。

3. **Pre-Transformer taxonomy / 技术年代较早**  
   The survey predates many modern developments, including large-scale Transformers, CLIP-style contrastive learning, multimodal foundation models, modern cross-attention, and Earth-observation foundation models.  
   论文形成于 Transformer 多模态模型、CLIP 式对比学习、多模态基础模型及 EO foundation models 大规模发展之前。

4. **No guidance on economic targets / 不讨论经济预测目标**  
   It does not determine whether oil-price levels, returns, directions, volatility, or density forecasts should be used.  
   论文无法回答油价水平、收益率、方向、波动率或概率密度预测中哪一种目标最合适。

5. **No real-time forecasting protocol / 缺少实时预测规范**  
   It does not directly discuss data-release lags, revisions, forecast-origin availability, rolling-window evaluation, or look-ahead bias.  
   论文没有系统讨论发布时间滞后、数据修订、滚动窗口和前视偏差等经济预测问题。

6. **Limited sample-size guidance / 缺少样本量指导**  
   Although the authors note that neural networks require large datasets, they do not specify when deep multimodal fusion becomes preferable to simpler models.  
   虽然作者指出深度网络依赖较大样本，但没有给出深度融合何时应优于简单模型的明确标准。

7. **Overlapping categories / 分类边界存在重叠**  
   In end-to-end neural networks, representation learning and fusion are closely intertwined, making the boundary between the two categories less clear.  
   在端到端神经网络中，表示学习和融合通常同时发生，因此两者的分类边界并不完全清晰。

8. **Interpretability remains unresolved / 可解释性不足**  
   The survey acknowledges that neural multimodal models can be difficult to interpret but does not provide a complete solution.  
   论文指出深度多模态模型难以解释，但没有提供完整的解释框架。

9. **No causal interpretation / 不支持因果解释**  
   Attention, gates, and embeddings represent predictive associations and should not automatically be interpreted as causal mechanisms.  
   注意力、门控权重和嵌入反映的是预测关联，不能自动解释为经济因果机制。

# Suggested Literature Review Paragraph / 建议文献综述段落

## English

Baltrušaitis, Ahuja, and Morency provide a foundational taxonomy of multimodal machine learning organised around representation, translation, alignment, fusion, and co-learning. Their framework distinguishes joint representations, in which modalities are projected into a shared latent space, from coordinated representations, in which modality-specific spaces are linked through similarity or structural constraints. The authors further separate model-agnostic early, late, and hybrid fusion from model-based approaches that explicitly learn cross-modal interactions. This taxonomy is directly relevant to the present study because financial time series, remote-sensing observations, and shipping flows differ in structure, temporal resolution, noise, and missingness. Accordingly, the dissertation treats direct feature concatenation as an early-fusion baseline and investigates whether modality-specific encoders and learned representation-level fusion provide additional forecasting value for next-week Brent oil prices.

## 中文

Baltrušaitis、Ahuja 和 Morency 建立了一套以表示、转换、对齐、融合和共同学习为核心的多模态机器学习分类框架。该框架区分了将多个模态映射到共享潜在空间的联合表示，以及通过相似性或结构约束连接各模态独立空间的协调表示。作者进一步将模型无关的早期、后期和混合融合，与能够显式学习跨模态交互的模型驱动融合区分开来。该分类体系与本研究高度相关，因为金融时间序列、遥感观测和航运流量在数据结构、时间分辨率、噪声和缺失机制方面存在明显差异。因此，本论文将直接特征拼接视为早期融合基准，并进一步检验模态专属编码器和表示级学习式融合能否为下一周 Brent 油价预测提供额外价值。
