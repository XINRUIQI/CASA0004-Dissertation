## Core Method / 核心方法

- **Model / 模型**:  
  Prithvi-EO-2.0 is a multi-temporal geospatial foundation model based on the Masked Autoencoder (MAE) framework and Vision Transformer architecture. It is released in approximately 300M-parameter and 600M-parameter versions, based on ViT-L and ViT-H backbones.

  Prithvi-EO-2.0 是一个基于掩码自编码器（Masked Autoencoder, MAE）和 Vision Transformer 的多时相地球观测基础模型。论文提供约 3 亿参数和 6 亿参数两个主要版本，分别基于 ViT-L 和 ViT-H。

- **Pretraining dataset / 预训练数据集**:  
  The model is pretrained on NASA's Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 (HLS) archive covering 2014–2023. The final dataset contains approximately 4.2 million training samples and 46,000 validation samples at 30 m spatial resolution.

  模型使用 NASA Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2（HLS）数据进行预训练，时间范围为 2014–2023 年。最终数据集包含约 420 万个训练样本和 4.6 万个验证样本，空间分辨率为 30 m。

- **Input structure / 输入结构**:  
  Each pretraining sample contains four satellite observations of the same location, with intervals of approximately one to six months between consecutive timestamps. Each observation is represented as a 256 × 256-pixel patch.

  每个预训练样本包含同一地点的四期卫星影像，相邻时间点之间通常间隔 1–6 个月。每期影像的大小为 256 × 256 像素。

- **Spectral bands / 光谱波段**:  
  Six spectral bands common to Landsat and Sentinel-2 are used: Blue, Green, Red, Near Infrared, SWIR1, and SWIR2.

  输入包括 Landsat 和 Sentinel-2 共有的六个光谱波段：蓝光、绿光、红光、近红外、短波红外 1 和短波红外 2。

- **Sampling strategy / 采样策略**:  
  The training data are sampled to represent diverse land-cover classes and global ecoregions. Urban areas and heterogeneous landscapes are deliberately oversampled, while homogeneous sea and desert areas are downsampled. Training and validation areas are spatially separated to reduce geographic leakage.

  训练样本按照土地覆盖类别和全球生态区进行分层抽样。城市地区和土地覆盖异质性较高的地区被适当过采样，而大面积海洋和沙漠等高度同质区域被降采样。训练区和验证区在空间上相互分离，以减少地理信息泄漏。

- **Quality control / 质量控制**:  
  Samples with more than 1% missing pixels in any band or more than 20% cloud and cloud-shadow pixels are excluded. Limited missing pixels are filled using nearest-neighbour interpolation.

  任一波段缺失像素超过 1%，或云和云影像素超过 20% 的样本会被删除。少量缺失像素使用最近邻插值处理。

- **Pretraining objective / 预训练目标**:  
  The model randomly masks a large proportion of spatiotemporal image tokens and learns to reconstruct the missing multispectral pixels. The reconstruction loss is the mean squared error between predicted and observed masked pixels.

  模型随机遮挡部分时空影像 token，并根据未遮挡部分重建缺失的多光谱像素。预训练损失为预测像素与真实被遮挡像素之间的均方误差。

- **Spatiotemporal architecture / 时空模型结构**:  
  A three-dimensional convolutional patch-embedding layer converts the temporal image sequence into spatiotemporal tokens. Three-dimensional positional encodings represent the temporal, horizontal, and vertical positions of each token.

  模型使用三维卷积 patch embedding，将多时相影像序列转换为时空 token。三维位置编码分别表示 token 所处的时间、横向空间位置和纵向空间位置。

- **Temporal and location metadata / 时间与位置元数据**:  
  The model can additionally use year, day of year, latitude, and longitude. These variables are encoded using sine–cosine embeddings and added to the image tokens through learned weights.

  模型还可以使用年份、年内日期、纬度和经度信息。这些元数据经过正弦—余弦编码后，通过可学习权重加入影像 token。

- **Metadata dropout / 元数据丢弃机制**:  
  Temporal and location metadata are randomly dropped during pretraining so that the model remains usable when metadata are unavailable or incomplete.

  预训练期间会随机删除时间或位置元数据，使模型在元数据缺失或不完整时仍能正常工作。

- **Downstream tasks / 下游任务**:  
  The model is evaluated on classification, semantic segmentation, and regression tasks, including flood mapping, wildfire scar detection, burn-intensity mapping, landslide detection, crop mapping, land-cover classification, above-ground biomass estimation, and gross primary productivity estimation.

  模型在分类、语义分割和回归任务上进行评估，包括洪水制图、火灾烧毁区识别、燃烧强度制图、滑坡检测、作物制图、土地覆盖分类、地上生物量估计和总初级生产力估计。

- **Transfer-learning strategies / 迁移学习策略**:  
  Different downstream applications use full fine-tuning, parameter-efficient LoRA fine-tuning, or a frozen Prithvi encoder combined with lightweight task-specific prediction heads.

  不同下游任务分别采用完整微调、参数高效的 LoRA 微调，或冻结 Prithvi 编码器并训练轻量级任务预测头。

- **Evaluation / 评估方式**:  
  The model is benchmarked using GEO-Bench, which contains six classification and six semantic-segmentation datasets. The authors perform hyperparameter tuning followed by repeated experiments with ten random seeds. Additional subject-matter-expert-led applications are used to test real-world transferability.

  模型使用 GEO-Bench 进行统一评估，其中包括六个分类数据集和六个语义分割数据集。作者先进行超参数搜索，再使用十个不同随机种子重复实验，并通过多个专家主导的真实应用测试模型的迁移能力。

## Key Findings / 主要发现

1. **Prithvi-EO-2.0 substantially improves on Prithvi-EO-1.0.**  
   Across the aggregated GEO-Bench tasks, Prithvi-EO-2.0 improves performance by approximately 8% relative to its predecessor and achieves strong overall performance against six other geospatial foundation models.

   **Prithvi-EO-2.0 相比 Prithvi-EO-1.0 有明显提升。**  
   在 GEO-Bench 综合任务中，Prithvi-EO-2.0 相比上一代模型的整体表现提高约 8%，并在与另外六种地球观测基础模型的比较中取得较强的综合表现。

2. **Pretraining-data scale and quality are important.**  
   A Prithvi-EO-2.0 model pretrained on the larger global dataset achieves an approximately 3% higher overall GEO-Bench score than an architecturally identical model pretrained on a smaller dataset.

   **预训练数据规模和质量十分重要。**  
   在模型结构和参数规模相同的情况下，使用更大规模全球数据预训练的模型，其 GEO-Bench 综合得分提高约 3%。

3. **Larger models generally perform better, but the relationship is not universal.**  
   The 600M variants usually achieve stronger aggregated performance than the smaller variants. However, the 300M model performs better in some individual tasks, and temporal-location metadata do not improve every dataset.

   **更大的模型通常更强，但并非所有任务都遵循这一规律。**  
   600M 版本在综合指标上通常优于较小版本，但在部分具体任务中，300M 模型表现更好；时间和位置元数据也不是在所有数据集上都能提高性能。

4. **The benefits of foundation-model pretraining are particularly visible under limited labelled data.**  
   In the landslide experiment using only approximately 50 training images, the Prithvi-EO-2.0-600M model obtains an mIoU of 67.0%, compared with 59.7% for U-Net and 55.5% for U-Net++.

   **基础模型在小样本条件下的优势更加明显。**  
   在仅使用约 50 张训练影像的滑坡检测实验中，Prithvi-EO-2.0-600M 的 mIoU 达到 67.0%，高于 U-Net 的 59.7% 和 U-Net++ 的 55.5%。

5. **A model pretrained using four timestamps can be transferred to longer temporal sequences.**  
   Although Prithvi-EO-2.0 is pretrained on four-image sequences, it can be fine-tuned for applications with 12 or substantially more timestamps.

   **使用四期影像预训练的模型可以迁移到更长时间序列。**  
   尽管 Prithvi-EO-2.0 的预训练样本只包含四个时间点，但模型仍能够适配包含 12 期甚至更多时间点的下游任务。

6. **Frozen EO embeddings can be fused with separately encoded numerical variables for regression.**  
   In the gross primary productivity task, six-band HLS imagery is processed by a pretrained and frozen Prithvi encoder, while MERRA-2 environmental variables are processed by a separate convolutional branch. The two latent representations are concatenated and used to predict a continuous GPP value.

   **冻结的 EO embedding 可以与独立编码的数值变量融合，用于连续变量回归。**  
   在总初级生产力任务中，六波段 HLS 影像由预训练且冻结的 Prithvi 编码器处理，MERRA-2 环境变量由独立卷积分支处理。两种 latent representation 拼接后，用于预测连续的 GPP 数值。

7. **Prithvi representations can outperform manually aggregated image features.**  
   In the GPP experiment, Prithvi-EO-2.0 models outperform random forest and XGBoost models without vegetation indices by up to approximately 20% in \(R^2\). The 600M-TL version records the strongest result in every test year, even though Prithvi does not explicitly use NDVI, EVI, or other vegetation indices.

   **Prithvi 表示可以优于人工汇总的影像特征。**  
   在 GPP 实验中，Prithvi-EO-2.0 相比未加入植被指数的随机森林和 XGBoost，\(R^2\) 最多提高约 20%。600M-TL 版本在所有测试年份均取得最高结果，尽管模型并未显式使用 NDVI、EVI 等植被指数。

8. **Different physical modalities should not necessarily be treated as additional image bands.**  
   Adding Sentinel-1 SAR variables directly as extra input bands to the optical Prithvi encoder did not improve biomass estimation. The authors suggest that a more suitable strategy may be to use a separate SAR encoder and fuse its representation with the pretrained optical encoder output.

   **不同物理模态不应简单作为额外影像波段输入同一编码器。**  
   将 Sentinel-1 SAR 数据直接作为额外波段加入光学 Prithvi 编码器，并未改善生物量估计。作者认为，更合理的方法可能是设置独立 SAR 编码器，再将其表示与预训练光学编码器的输出融合。

## Limitations / 局限性

1. **No direct oil-price forecasting evidence / 没有直接的油价预测证据**  
   The paper evaluates Earth-observation tasks and environmental regression targets. It does not test Brent, WTI, commodity prices, financial returns, or market forecasting.

   论文评估的是地球观测和环境回归任务，没有预测 Brent、WTI、商品价格、金融收益率或其他市场变量。

2. **Indirect mechanism between imagery and oil prices / 遥感影像与油价之间的机制链条较长**  
   In most paper applications, the target directly affects the observed surface. Oil prices are influenced by expectations, policy, finance, inventories, geopolitical shocks, and global demand, so the relationship with satellite-observed infrastructure is substantially more indirect.

   在论文的大多数任务中，预测目标会直接改变地表像素；油价则同时受到预期、政策、金融市场、库存、地缘政治和全球需求影响，因此遥感设施活动与油价之间的关系更加间接。

3. **Temporal-scale mismatch / 时间尺度不完全匹配**  
   Prithvi is pretrained with observations separated by approximately one to six months and is designed to capture seasonal and longer-term changes. The dissertation forecasts next-week Brent movements, which may respond to information within hours or days.

   Prithvi 的预训练影像通常间隔 1–6 个月，主要学习季节和中长期变化；而本论文预测下一周 Brent，油价可能在数小时或数日内对新信息作出反应。

4. **Optical-data limitation / 光学数据限制**  
   The model is mainly pretrained on six-band optical HLS imagery. It does not natively model AIS networks, SAR backscatter, news, financial time series, or oil-market variables.

   模型主要基于六波段光学 HLS 影像预训练，不能直接表示 AIS 网络、SAR 后向散射、新闻文本、金融序列或石油市场变量。

5. **Resolution limitation / 空间分辨率限制**  
   The 30 m HLS resolution is suitable for regional port, industrial, storage, and land-cover context, but may be insufficient for individual vessels, small facilities, narrow pipelines, or precise storage-tank measurements.

   30 m HLS 分辨率适合观察港区、工业区、储罐群和周边土地覆盖，但可能无法精确识别单艘船舶、小型设施、狭窄管线或油罐液位。

6. **Domain shift / 数据域偏移**  
   Applying the model to original Sentinel-2 imagery requires consistent band mapping, radiometric scaling, spatial resolution, normalization, and cloud masking. Otherwise, the downstream imagery may differ materially from the HLS pretraining distribution.

   将模型应用于原始 Sentinel-2 影像时，需要统一波段映射、反射率缩放、空间分辨率、归一化和云掩膜，否则下游数据可能与 HLS 预训练分布存在明显偏移。

7. **Very high computational cost / 计算成本很高**  
   Pretraining required tens of thousands of A100 GPU-hours. Full fine-tuning of a 300M- or 600M-parameter model is unlikely to be justified for a weekly oil-price dataset containing only several hundred complete multimodal observations.

   预训练需要数万 A100 GPU 小时。对于只有数百个完整多模态周度样本的油价数据，完整微调 300M 或 600M 参数模型很难具有合理性。

8. **Larger models are not consistently superior / 大模型并非在所有任务中都更优**  
   The 600M model obtains better aggregated performance, but the 300M model performs better on some individual applications. Model size must therefore be treated as an empirical choice rather than assumed to be beneficial.

   600M 模型的综合表现更好，但 300M 模型在部分具体任务中反而占优，因此不能预设模型越大越适合本项目。

9. **Temporal and location embeddings are not universally beneficial / 时间和位置 embedding 并非普遍有效**  
   TL variants do not consistently outperform non-TL variants across every benchmark.

   带时间和位置元数据的 TL 版本并未在每个数据集上稳定优于不带 TL 的版本。

10. **Simple fusion rather than advanced cross-modal interaction / 融合结构相对简单**  
    The GPP case uses separate branches followed by flattening and concatenation. It supports representation-level fusion, but it does not directly validate gated cross-attention or more complex multimodal interaction mechanisms.

    GPP 案例采用独立分支、展平和拼接，能够支持表示级融合的合理性，但没有直接验证门控交叉注意力等更复杂的多模态交互机制。

11. **No direct comparison with persistence or random-walk financial benchmarks / 未涉及金融预测中的强基准**  
    The paper does not compare against oil-price persistence, random walk, ARIMA, futures-based forecasts, or other strong financial benchmarks.

    论文没有涉及油价不变预测、随机游走、ARIMA、期货预测或其他金融预测强基准。

## Notes for Dissertation Integration / 论文整合笔记

- Use this paper as the principal methodological reference for the remote-sensing encoder in the dissertation's representation-level multimodal model.

  将该论文作为本研究表示级多模态模型中遥感编码器的核心方法文献。

- Do not cite it as evidence that remote sensing improves oil-price forecasting. Cite it as evidence that multi-temporal EO foundation models can produce transferable spatial–spectral–temporal representations.

  不应将其作为“遥感能够提高油价预测”的直接证据，而应将其作为“多时相 EO 基础模型能够提取可迁移空间—光谱—时间表示”的方法学证据。

- Begin with the Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M encoder frozen. Precompute AOI embeddings and train only lightweight temporal aggregation, AOI attention, fusion, and prediction layers.

  第一阶段建议冻结 Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M，并预计算每个 AOI 的 embedding，只训练轻量级时间聚合、AOI attention、融合层和预测头。

- Treat the 600M version and LoRA adaptation as sensitivity analyses rather than the default specification.

  将 600M 版本和 LoRA 微调作为敏感性实验，而不是默认主模型。

- Use four recent valid observations as the initial EO sequence because this matches the pretraining configuration. Subsequently test longer windows such as 8 or 12 observations.

  初始实验可使用最近四期有效影像，以匹配预训练结构；随后测试 8 期或 12 期等更长窗口。

- Ensure that every image used for week \(t\) was genuinely available before the forecast origin. Future cloud-free composites must not be used to fill current-week observations.

  必须保证用于第 \(t\) 周预测的所有影像在预测起点之前已经可获得，不能使用未来无云影像合成当前周输入。

- Record image age, days since the most recent observation, cloud fraction, valid-pixel fraction, and an observation mask.

  建议记录影像距离预测日的时间、最近观测间隔天数、云量、有效像素比例和观测 mask。

- Compare uniform physical context windows with the existing port/refinery/terminal AOI sizes because Prithvi's common 224 × 224 input at 30 m represents approximately 6.72 km × 6.72 km.

  由于 Prithvi 常见的 224 × 224、30 m 输入约对应 6.72 km × 6.72 km，应比较统一物理范围与现有港口、炼厂、终端差异化 AOI 范围。

- Add a mechanistic validation stage before interpreting oil-price improvements. Test whether EO embeddings explain or predict tanker counts, tanker capacity, congestion, or other physical-supply indicators.

  在解释油价预测改善之前，应增加机制验证，检验 EO embedding 能否解释或预测油轮数量、油轮运力、港口拥堵或其他实体供应链指标。

- Use the following mechanism chain in the dissertation:

  建议在论文中明确以下机制链条：

\[
\text{Satellite-observed infrastructure activity}
\rightarrow
\text{physical oil supply-chain conditions}
\rightarrow
\text{market expectations}
\rightarrow
\text{Brent price changes}
\]

- The main internal prediction target can remain next-week log return:

  模型内部主要预测目标可设为下一周对数收益率：

\[
r_{t+1}=\log(P_{t+1})-\log(P_t)
\]

- The predicted price can then be reconstructed as:

  随后还原下一周价格：

\[
\widehat{P}_{t+1}=P_t\exp(\widehat{r}_{t+1})
\]

- Report both return-based and price-level metrics, together with directional accuracy, relative improvement over M0, and Diebold–Mariano tests.

  同时报告收益率误差、价格水平误差、方向准确率、相对 M0 的改善幅度以及 Diebold–Mariano 检验。

- Use multiple random seeds for neural models and report mean performance and standard deviation. A single favourable run should not be interpreted as evidence of multimodal improvement.

  深度模型应使用多个随机种子，并报告均值与标准差，不能将某一次有利运行结果直接解释为多模态模型具有稳定优势。

## Suggested Literature-Review Positioning / 建议的文献综述定位

**English**

Prithvi-EO-2.0 represents a recent generation of geospatial foundation models that jointly encode spatial, spectral, and temporal information from multi-temporal satellite imagery. Unlike approaches that reduce remote-sensing observations to a small set of manually engineered indicators, Prithvi learns transferable latent representations through large-scale masked-image pretraining. Its downstream GPP application further demonstrates that a frozen EO encoder can be combined with a separately encoded numerical-data branch for continuous regression. However, its applications remain within Earth observation and environmental monitoring, leaving unresolved whether such representations contain incremental information for commodity-market forecasting.

**中文**

Prithvi-EO-2.0 代表了新一代地球空间基础模型，其能够联合编码多时相卫星影像中的空间、光谱和时间信息。与将遥感观测压缩为少量人工构建指标的方法不同，Prithvi 通过大规模掩码影像预训练学习可迁移的 latent representation。其 GPP 下游任务进一步证明，冻结的 EO 编码器可以与独立编码的数值数据分支融合，用于连续变量回归。然而，该模型的应用仍主要集中于地球观测和环境监测领域，多时相 EO 表示能否为商品市场预测提供增量信息，仍未得到验证。

## Suggested Research-Gap Statement / 建议的研究缺口表述

**English**

Existing EO foundation-model research demonstrates that pretrained multi-temporal encoders can extract transferable representations for environmental classification, segmentation, and regression. However, the literature has not established whether foundation-model representations of economically important infrastructure, such as oil ports, refineries, and storage terminals, improve short-horizon commodity-price forecasts beyond conventional market, macroeconomic, and AIS-based shipping indicators. This dissertation addresses this gap by comparing manually engineered EO features with frozen Prithvi-EO-2.0 representations and by evaluating flat feature concatenation against modality-specific representation fusion in a strictly out-of-sample Brent forecasting framework.

**中文**

现有地球观测基础模型研究已经证明，预训练多时相编码器能够为环境分类、分割和回归任务提取可迁移表示。然而，现有文献尚未验证石油港口、炼厂和储存终端等重要经济基础设施的基础模型遥感表示，能否在传统市场、宏观经济和 AIS 航运指标之外，提高短期商品价格预测。本研究通过比较人工构建的 EO 特征与冻结的 Prithvi-EO-2.0 表示，并在严格样本外的 Brent 预测框架中比较宽表特征拼接与模态专属表示融合，从而填补这一研究缺口。
