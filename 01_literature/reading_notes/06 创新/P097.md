## Research Question

The paper investigates whether multimodal Transformers are inherently robust when one of the input modalities is partially or completely unavailable at inference time.

The central research questions are:

1. Do multimodal Transformers retain their predictive performance when a modality is missing?
2. Does the location of multimodal fusion affect robustness to missing modalities?
3. Is there a universally optimal early- or late-fusion strategy?
4. Can robustness be improved by explicitly training the model on both complete- and incomplete-modality inputs?

The paper distinguishes between:

- **Architectural compatibility**: whether a Transformer can technically accept an input with missing tokens through attention masking.
- **Predictive robustness**: whether its performance remains stable when one modality is unavailable.

The authors show that architectural flexibility does not automatically imply predictive robustness.

---

## Core Method

### Model

- **Backbone**: ViLT, a vision-and-language Transformer.
- **Input modalities**:
  - Image patches transformed through linear projection and positional embeddings.
  - Text tokens transformed through word and positional embeddings.
  - Modality-type embeddings used to distinguish image and text tokens.
- **Fusion mechanism**: Transformer self-attention over image and text tokens.
- **Proposed extension**: A robust multimodal Transformer trained through multi-task learning and differentiable fusion-policy search.

### Missing-Modality Setting

The standard experimental setting is:

- **Training**: 100% image + 100% text.
- **Testing**: 100% image + a reduced percentage of text.

The percentage of observed text is varied across:

- 10%;
- 30%;
- 50%;
- 70%;
- 90%;
- 100%.

A lower percentage represents more severe modality missingness.

The paper defines robustness by comparing performance on the missing-modality test set with performance on the complete-modality test set:

\[
\text{Robustness Gap}
=
\text{Performance}_{\text{full}}
-
\text{Performance}_{\text{missing}}
\]

A smaller performance gap indicates stronger robustness.

### Multi-Task Optimisation

For an image–text dataset, the proposed model jointly learns three tasks:

1. Image-only prediction;
2. Text-only prediction;
3. Image-and-text prediction.

The total loss is:

\[
\mathcal{L}
=
\lambda_{\text{img}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{img}}
+
\lambda_{\text{txt}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{txt}}
+
\lambda_{\text{it}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{it}}
\]

where:

- \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{img}}\) is the image-only loss;
- \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{txt}}\) is the text-only loss;
- \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{it}}\) is the complete image–text loss;
- the \(\lambda\) parameters control the relative contribution of each task.

The model contains separate task-specific classification tokens for the three tasks.

### Attention Masking

Attention masks ensure that each task-specific classification token can only access the modalities assigned to its task.

For example:

- The image-only token cannot attend to text tokens.
- The text-only token cannot attend to image tokens.
- The complete-modality token can attend to both modalities.

This prevents information leakage, in which a nominally unimodal prediction task indirectly accesses the excluded modality through self-attention.

### Fusion-Policy Search

The authors investigate where cross-modal fusion should begin within the Transformer.

Two general strategies are considered:

- **Early fusion**: Image and text interact from an early Transformer layer.
- **Late fusion**: Each modality is processed more independently, with interaction occurring only in later layers.

Rather than manually selecting the fusion layer, the authors introduce trainable policy parameters that determine the layer from which multimodal fusion begins.

The search is formulated as a bi-level optimisation problem:

\[
\theta^*(\alpha)
=
\arg\min_{\theta}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{train}}(\theta,\alpha)
\]

\[
\min_{\alpha}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{validation}}
\left(
\theta^*(\alpha),\alpha
\right)
\]

where:

- \(\theta\) represents the Transformer parameters;
- \(\alpha\) represents the fusion-policy parameters.

The model parameters are optimised on the training set, while the fusion policy is selected according to validation-set performance.

## Datasets

### MM-IMDb

- **Modalities**: Movie posters and plot descriptions.
- **Sample size**: 25,956 image–text pairs.
- **Target**: Movie genre prediction.
- **Classes**: 23 genres.
- **Task type**: Multi-label classification.
- **Metrics**:
  - F1 Macro;
  - F1 Micro;
  - F1 Weighted;
  - F1 Samples.

Text is the dominant modality because plot descriptions generally contain more genre-related information than movie posters.

### UPMC Food-101

- **Modalities**: Food images and associated text.
- **Sample size**: 90,704 image–text pairs.
- **Target**: Food category.
- **Classes**: 101.
- **Task type**: Multi-class classification.
- **Metric**: Accuracy.

Text also provides stronger unimodal predictive performance than images in this dataset.

### Hateful Memes

- **Modalities**: Meme image and embedded text.
- **Sample size**: Approximately 10,000 samples.
- **Target**: Whether a meme contains hateful content.
- **Task type**: Binary classification.
- **Metric**: AUROC.

The dataset is deliberately designed so that neither image nor text alone is sufficient. Accurate prediction depends on the contextual relationship between the two modalities.

## Evaluation

The study evaluates:

1. Performance with complete modalities;
2. Performance under partial modality missingness;
3. Unimodal performance;
4. Early versus late fusion;
5. Learned fusion policies;
6. Multi-task learning ablations;
7. Attention-mask ablations.

The main metrics are:

- F1 scores for MM-IMDb;
- Accuracy for UPMC Food-101;
- AUROC for Hateful Memes.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Multimodal Transformers are not inherently robust to missing modalities

When only 30% of the text modality remains available, the standard ViLT model experiences large relative performance reductions:

- **MM-IMDb**: 43.6% decline;
- **UPMC Food-101**: 28.3% decline;
- **Hateful Memes**: 14.2% decline.

This shows that the ability to mask missing tokens does not guarantee stable prediction performance.

### 2. A multimodal model can perform worse than a unimodal model

Under severe text missingness, the complete-modality-trained Transformer sometimes performs worse than a model trained specifically on the remaining image modality.

For example, when only 10% of text is available:

- MM-IMDb baseline multimodal model: F1 Macro = 23.1;
- MM-IMDb image-only model: F1 Macro ≈ 35.0;
- Proposed robust model: F1 Macro = 37.3.

For UPMC Food-101:

- Baseline multimodal model: Accuracy = 58.4;
- Image-only model: Accuracy ≈ 71.5;
- Proposed robust model: Accuracy = 73.3.

Therefore, the assumption that a multimodal model will degrade gracefully into a reliable unimodal model is not valid.

### 3. Transformers tend to overfit to the dominant modality

In MM-IMDb and Food-101, text has stronger standalone predictive power than image.

The multimodal model consequently learns to depend disproportionately on text. When text becomes unavailable, performance deteriorates sharply.

This behaviour is described as overfitting to the dominant modality.

### 4. The optimal fusion strategy is dataset-dependent

There is no universally superior early- or late-fusion strategy.

The learned policies indicate that:

- **MM-IMDb prefers relatively late fusion**, because the dominant text modality already provides most of the information required for classification.
- **Hateful Memes prefers early fusion**, because accurate prediction requires detailed interaction between image and text.
- **Food-101 also benefits from a relatively later fusion strategy under the examined missing-modality setting.**

The optimal fusion depth depends on:

- the relative strength of each modality;
- the degree of cross-modal complementarity;
- the amount of information available from each individual modality;
- the severity and pattern of missingness.

### 5. Multi-task training substantially improves missing-modality robustness

The proposed joint training of complete-, image-only, and text-only tasks improves performance across all three datasets.

When only 10% of text is available:

- MM-IMDb improves from 23.1 to 37.3 F1 Macro;
- Food-101 improves from 58.4 to 73.3 Accuracy;
- Hateful Memes improves from 58.0 to 59.6 AUROC.

The benefit is particularly large when the missing modality is dominant.

### 6. Multi-task learning contributes more than fusion-layer search

The ablation analysis shows that both components are useful:

- multi-task missing-modality training;
- optimal fusion-policy search.

However, multi-task learning provides the larger contribution.

When only 10% of text is observed on MM-IMDb, the paper reports that multi-task learning outperforms the fusion-policy component alone by approximately 30% in relative terms.

This suggests that exposing the model to modality-incomplete conditions during training is more important than optimising a highly sophisticated fusion architecture.

### 7. Correct attention masking is essential

On MM-IMDb with only 10% text available:

- Without proper attention masking: F1 Macro = 23.0;
- With attention masking: F1 Macro = 37.3.

The substantial difference shows that unimodal auxiliary tasks must be prevented from accessing excluded modalities.

Simply adding separate task heads is insufficient if information can still leak across the attention matrix.

## Relevance to This Dissertation

| Aspect | Connection to the Oil-Price Forecasting Project |
|--------|--------------------------------------------------|
| **Missing-modality motivation** | Remote-sensing observations can be unavailable because of cloud cover, revisit intervals, poor image quality or data-processing delays. Shipping and AIS data can also contain coverage gaps or delayed updates. The paper provides direct evidence that a multimodal Transformer should not be assumed to handle these cases automatically. |
| **Modality-specific encoders** | The dissertation proposes separate finance, remote-sensing and shipping encoders. This is compatible with the paper's principle that each modality should retain a usable representation before or during multimodal fusion. |
| **Modality dropout** | Training should deliberately remove remote-sensing or shipping inputs, rather than training only on weeks in which all three modalities are complete. |
| **Multi-task learning** | The model can jointly optimise finance-only, finance–remote-sensing, finance–shipping and complete three-modality forecasts. |
| **Attention masks** | Missing or excluded modalities should be removed through explicit attention masks, rather than represented only through zero-filled embeddings. |
| **Fusion design** | Early, intermediate and late fusion should be compared empirically. The optimal fusion depth cannot be selected solely through theoretical intuition. |
| **Dominant modality** | Historical prices and market variables are likely to dominate the oil-price task. The model may ignore remote-sensing and shipping information unless modality-specific ablations and robustness tests are conducted. |
| **Robustness evaluation** | Model performance should be evaluated under complete, partially missing and fully missing external modalities. |
| **Deployment relevance** | A slightly less accurate model under complete data may be preferable if it remains stable when remote-sensing or shipping data are delayed or missing. |
| **Methodological contribution** | Missing-modality robustness can become part of the dissertation's methodological contribution, rather than being treated only as a preprocessing problem. |

M1, M2, M3 and M4 are trained separately.

These experiments answer:

> Does adding remote-sensing or shipping information improve out-of-sample oil-price forecasts?

### Unified Missing-Modality Model

A single multimodal network is trained to operate under multiple modality combinations:

- Finance only;
- Finance + remote sensing;
- Finance + shipping;

These experiments answer:

> Can the same multimodal model remain reliable when remote-sensing or shipping information is unavailable?

The two questions are related but not equivalent.

## Proposed Adaptation for the Oil-Price Model

Let:

- \(F\) represent the financial and market modality;
- \(R\) represent the remote-sensing modality;
- \(S\) represent the shipping modality.

Because financial data are the essential base modality, the most relevant training tasks are:

1. \(F\): Finance only;
2. \(FR\): Finance + remote sensing;
3. \(FS\): Finance + shipping;
4. \(FRS\): Complete three-modality input.

The joint loss can be written as:

\[
\mathcal{L}
=
\lambda_F\mathcal{L}_F
+
\lambda_{FR}\mathcal{L}_{FR}
+
\lambda_{FS}\mathcal{L}_{FS}
+
\lambda_{FRS}\mathcal{L}_{FRS}
\]

For next-week Brent log-return forecasting:

\[
y_{t+1}
=
\log(P_{t+1})-\log(P_t)
\]

the four task-specific predictions are:

\[
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{F},
\quad
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FR},
\quad
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FS},
\quad
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FRS}
\]

The training objective becomes:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}
=&
\lambda_F
\ell
\left(
y_{t+1},
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{F}
\right)
+
\lambda_{FR}
\ell
\left(
y_{t+1},
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FR}
\right)
\\
&
+
\lambda_{FS}
\ell
\left(
y_{t+1},
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FS}
\right)
+
\lambda_{FRS}
\ell
\left(
y_{t+1},
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FRS}
\right)
\end{aligned}
\]

Possible regression losses include:

- Mean squared error;
- Mean absolute error;
- Huber loss;
- A combined return-regression and direction-classification loss.

## Missingness Indicators for the Dissertation

Zero-filling a missing embedding is insufficient because the model cannot distinguish between:

- genuinely low physical activity;
- a missing observation;
- a low-valued encoder output;
- a failed data-processing step.

Each external modality should therefore be accompanied by an availability indicator:

\[
m_t^R\in\{0,1\}
\]

\[
m_t^S\in\{0,1\}
\]

where:

- \(m_t^R=1\) means that valid remote-sensing information is available at time \(t\);
- \(m_t^S=1\) means that valid shipping information is available.

The model should also consider observation age:

\[
\Delta_t^R
=
\text{days since the latest valid remote-sensing observation}
\]

\[
\Delta_t^S
=
\text{days since the latest valid shipping observation}
\]

A modality representation may therefore include:

\[
\tilde{z}_t^R
=
[z_t^R,m_t^R,\Delta_t^R]
\]

\[
\tilde{z}_t^S
=
[z_t^S,m_t^S,\Delta_t^S]
\]

This is particularly important because an observation from two days ago should not necessarily receive the same weight as an observation from several weeks ago.

## Recommended Robustness Experiments

### Whole-Modality Missingness

| Test scenario | Available modalities |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Full input | Finance + remote sensing + shipping |
| No remote sensing | Finance + shipping |
| No shipping | Finance + remote sensing |
| Finance only | Finance |

### Random Temporal Missingness

Randomly mask:

- 10% of remote-sensing observations;
- 30% of remote-sensing observations;
- 50% of remote-sensing observations;
- equivalent proportions of shipping observations.

### Block Missingness

Simulate consecutive periods of missing data:

- Two consecutive weeks;
- Four consecutive weeks;
- Eight consecutive weeks.

This is more realistic than independent random deletion because satellite and data-platform interruptions often occur in temporal blocks.

### AOI-Level Missingness

Remove information from:

- one randomly selected AOI;
- a major refinery or terminal;
- one critical port;
- multiple AOIs simultaneously.

### Chokepoint-Level Missingness

Remove:

- Strait of Hormuz;
- Suez Canal;
- Strait of Malacca;
- tanker counts;
- tanker capacity measurements;
- multiple chokepoint indicators.

### Modality Permutation Tests

Randomly shuffle remote-sensing or shipping embeddings across time while preserving financial inputs.

If model performance remains unchanged, the model is probably not using the external modality meaningfully.

## Robustness Metrics for Oil-Price Forecasting

For an error metric such as MAE, a relative robustness gap can be defined as:

\[
\text{Robustness Gap}_{\text{MAE}}
=
\frac{
\text{MAE}_{\text{missing}}
-
\text{MAE}_{\text{full}}
}{
\text{MAE}_{\text{full}}
}
\times100
\]

A lower value indicates greater robustness.

Equivalent measures can be reported for:

- RMSE;
- MAPE, where appropriate;
- direction accuracy;
- return-sign accuracy;
- volatility forecasts.

The robustness analysis should also include:

- Comparison with M0;
- Comparison with M1;
- Diebold–Mariano tests;
- Results across market regimes;
- Performance during crises and supply disruptions.

## Recommended Fusion Experiment

The dissertation should initially compare three controlled architectures:

| Fusion design | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Early fusion** | Modality tokens interact near the beginning of the network. |
| **Intermediate fusion** | Each modality first develops a modality-specific representation, followed by one or two gated cross-attention layers. |
| **Late fusion** | Final modality embeddings or predictions are combined near the output layer. |

A practical initial architecture is:

Finance sequence  
→ Finance temporal encoder  
→ \(z_{\text{fin}}\)

Remote-sensing sequence  
→ Frozen or fine-tuned EO encoder  
→ Temporal/AOI aggregation  
→ \(z_{\text{rs}}\)

Shipping sequence or graph  
→ Shipping temporal/graph encoder  
→ \(z_{\text{ship}}\)

\[
[z_{\text{fin}},z_{\text{rs}},z_{\text{ship}}]
\rightarrow
\text{one or two gated cross-attention layers}
\rightarrow
\text{prediction head}
\]

Given the relatively small number of weekly oil-price observations, a fixed comparison of early, intermediate and late fusion is likely to be more reliable than immediately implementing a data-intensive neural architecture search procedure.

## Limitations

1. **Classification rather than forecasting**  
   The experiments concern image–text classification. The paper does not test continuous time-series forecasting, oil prices, returns or volatility.

2. **Only two modalities**  
   The framework is demonstrated with image and text, while the dissertation contains at least three major modalities: finance, remote sensing and shipping.

3. **Large benchmark datasets**  
   The datasets contain approximately 10,000–90,000 observations. A weekly oil-price dataset covering around two decades may contain only about 1,000 observations.

4. **No temporal dependence**  
   Samples are treated as relatively independent image–text pairs. Serial correlation, rolling windows and temporal leakage are not considered.

5. **Simplified missingness mechanism**  
   The paper mainly reduces the proportion of observed text. Real remote-sensing and AIS missingness may be seasonal, spatially clustered, event-dependent and non-random.

6. **No asynchronous-modality modelling**  
   Image and text belong to the same sample. The paper does not model different update frequencies, publication delays or stale observations.

7. **No point-in-time information constraint**  
   It does not address whether an observation was genuinely available at the historical forecast origin.

8. **Limited safety guarantee**  
   The authors acknowledge that multi-task learning can only help ensure that multimodal performance is not worse than unimodal performance. This may still be insufficient for safety-critical applications.

9. **No economic or statistical forecast tests**  
   The study does not report Diebold–Mariano tests, economic value, directional trading performance or regime-dependent forecast evaluation.

10. **Architecture-search overfitting risk**  
    Differentiable fusion-policy search may be unstable when the validation sample is small, as in a weekly oil-price forecasting application.

## Suggested Literature-Review Statement

Ma et al. (2022) demonstrate that multimodal Transformers are not inherently robust to missing modalities. Models trained exclusively on complete multimodal observations can experience substantial performance deterioration when one modality becomes unavailable and may even underperform separately trained unimodal models. The authors further show that the optimal depth of multimodal fusion is dataset-dependent, reflecting differences in modality dominance and cross-modal complementarity. They improve robustness by jointly optimising complete- and incomplete-modality tasks through task-specific classification tokens, attention masking and differentiable fusion-policy search. These findings are directly relevant to multimodal oil-price forecasting, where satellite and shipping observations may be unavailable because of cloud contamination, irregular revisit intervals, AIS coverage gaps or asynchronous publication. Accordingly, the proposed forecasting framework incorporates structured modality dropout, availability masks and auxiliary prediction objectives for finance-only, finance–remote-sensing, finance–shipping and complete three-modality inputs.

---

## Overall Assessment

This paper does not provide direct evidence that remote-sensing or shipping data improve Brent oil-price forecasts. Its contribution to the dissertation is methodological rather than domain-specific.

Its main value is to establish that:

1. Multimodal Transformers do not automatically tolerate missing modalities.
2. Complete-data performance is insufficient for evaluating a deployable multimodal forecasting model.
3. Missing-modality conditions should be simulated during training.
4. Auxiliary unimodal or partial-modality tasks can substantially improve robustness.
5. The optimal fusion depth depends on modality dominance and complementarity.
6. External modalities should be evaluated through ablation, permutation and missingness stress tests.

The paper therefore provides a strong methodological foundation for the dissertation's proposed robust, modality-aware oil-price forecasting network.

## 研究问题

本文研究的核心问题是：

> 当多模态 Transformer 在训练阶段使用完整模态，但在实际测试或部署阶段缺少部分模态时，模型是否仍然具有稳定的预测能力？

论文主要回答以下问题：

1. 多模态 Transformer 是否天然能够适应模态缺失？
2. 多模态融合发生在哪一层，是否会影响模型的缺失模态鲁棒性？
3. 是否存在普遍最优的早期融合或晚期融合策略？
4. 能否通过在训练阶段同时引入完整模态和缺失模态任务，提高模型的鲁棒性？

论文特别区分了两个概念：

- **架构兼容性**：Transformer 能否借助 attention mask 接收缺少部分 token 的输入。
- **预测鲁棒性**：模态缺失以后，模型性能是否仍然保持稳定。

作者证明：

> 模型在程序上能够处理缺失 token，并不意味着它在预测上对缺失模态具有鲁棒性。

## 核心方法

### 模型

- **主干模型**：ViLT 视觉—语言 Transformer。
- **输入模态**：
  - 图像被分割为 image patches，再经过线性投影和位置编码；
  - 文本经过 tokenizer、word embedding 和位置编码；
  - 使用 modality-type embedding 区分图像和文本 token。
- **融合机制**：通过 Transformer self-attention 建立图像和文本之间的联系。
- **作者提出的方法**：通过多任务学习和可微分融合策略搜索，建立对缺失模态更加稳健的多模态 Transformer。

### 缺失模态设定

基本实验设定为：

- **训练阶段**：100% 图像 + 100% 文本；
- **测试阶段**：100% 图像 + 部分文本。

文本可用比例依次设置为：

- 10%；
- 30%；
- 50%；
- 70%；
- 90%；
- 100%。

文本比例越低，代表缺失程度越严重。

论文通过比较完整模态和缺失模态测试集上的性能衡量鲁棒性：

\[
\text{Robustness Gap}
=
\text{Performance}_{\text{full}}
-
\text{Performance}_{\text{missing}}
\]

性能差距越小，说明模型的缺失模态鲁棒性越强。

### 多任务优化

对于图像—文本双模态数据，作者同时训练三个任务：

1. 仅使用图像进行预测；
2. 仅使用文本进行预测；
3. 同时使用图像和文本进行预测。

总损失函数为：

\[
\mathcal{L}
=
\lambda_{\text{img}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{img}}
+
\lambda_{\text{txt}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{txt}}
+
\lambda_{\text{it}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{it}}
\]

其中：

- \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{img}}\) 是 image-only 任务损失；
- \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{txt}}\) 是 text-only 任务损失；
- \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{it}}\) 是完整图文任务损失；
- \(\lambda\) 用于控制三个任务的相对权重。

模型为三个任务分别设置 task-specific classification token。

### Attention Mask

作者通过 attention mask 保证每个任务的分类 token 只能访问该任务允许使用的模态。

例如：

- image-only token 不能注意文本 token；
- text-only token 不能注意图像 token；
- 完整模态 token 可以同时注意图像和文本。

这样可以避免信息泄漏。

否则，一个名义上的 text-only 任务仍可能通过 self-attention 间接读取图像信息，导致单模态训练任务失去意义。

### 融合策略搜索

作者研究跨模态交互应该从 Transformer 的哪一层开始。

主要融合方式包括：

- **Early fusion**：图像和文本在较早的 Transformer 层中就开始交互；
- **Late fusion**：两个模态先相对独立地编码，在较后层才发生交互。

作者没有人工固定融合层，而是为不同 Transformer 层设置可学习的策略参数，自动决定从哪一层开始融合。

融合搜索被写成双层优化问题：

\[
\theta^*(\alpha)
=
\arg\min_{\theta}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{train}}(\theta,\alpha)
\]

\[
\min_{\alpha}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{validation}}
\left(
\theta^*(\alpha),\alpha
\right)
\]

其中：

- \(\theta\) 是 Transformer 网络参数；
- \(\alpha\) 是融合策略参数。

模型参数在训练集上学习，融合位置则根据验证集表现确定。

- UPMC Food-101：Accuracy；

## 主要发现

### 1. 多模态 Transformer 并不天然对缺失模态具有鲁棒性

当测试阶段只剩下 30% 文本时，普通 ViLT 模型的相对性能下降分别达到：

- **MM-IMDb**：43.6%；
- **UPMC Food-101**：28.3%；
- **Hateful Memes**：14.2%。

这说明：

> Transformer 可以通过 mask 接收不完整输入，但这并不意味着其预测性能可以保持稳定。

### 2. 多模态模型可能比单模态模型表现更差

在文本严重缺失时，使用完整模态训练的多模态模型，有时甚至不如专门使用剩余图像模态训练的模型。

当只有 10% 文本可用时，MM-IMDb 的结果为：

- 普通多模态模型：F1 Macro = 23.1；
- Image-only 模型：F1 Macro 约为 35.0；
- 作者提出的稳健模型：F1 Macro = 37.3。

UPMC Food-101 的结果为：

- 普通多模态模型：Accuracy = 58.4；
- Image-only 模型：Accuracy 约为 71.5；
- 作者提出的稳健模型：Accuracy = 73.3。

因此，不能假设一个多模态模型在缺少某个模态时，会自动退化为一个可靠的单模态模型。

### 3. Transformer 容易对主导模态过拟合

在 MM-IMDb 和 Food-101 中，文本单独使用时的预测能力明显高于图像。

因此，多模态模型会过度依赖文本。当文本在测试阶段缺失时，模型性能会急剧下降。

作者将这一现象描述为：

> 对主导模态的过拟合。

### 4. 最优融合策略依赖具体数据集

不存在普遍最优的 early fusion 或 late fusion。

自动搜索得到的融合策略表明：

- **MM-IMDb 更偏好较晚融合**，因为主导文本模态已经能够提供大部分分类信息；
- **Hateful Memes 更偏好较早融合**，因为预测必须依赖图像与文本之间的深度交互；
- **Food-101 在论文所考察的缺失设定下也更偏向较晚融合。**

最优融合深度取决于：

- 各模态的相对预测能力；
- 模态之间的互补程度；
- 单个模态所包含的信息量；
- 模态缺失的程度与模式。

### 5. 多任务训练明显改善缺失模态鲁棒性

作者同时训练完整模态、image-only 和 text-only 任务后，三个数据集上的缺失模态性能均得到提高。

当只剩 10% 文本时：

- MM-IMDb：F1 Macro 从 23.1 提高到 37.3；
- Food-101：Accuracy 从 58.4 提高到 73.3；
- Hateful Memes：AUROC 从 58.0 提高到 59.6。

当被删除的是主导模态时，改进尤其明显。

### 6. 多任务学习的贡献大于融合层搜索

消融实验表明，下列两个组成部分均有效：

- 缺失模态多任务训练；
- 最优融合策略搜索。

但多任务训练的贡献更大。

在 MM-IMDb 只剩 10% 文本时，论文报告多任务学习相对于单独融合策略模块取得了约 30% 的相对优势。

这说明：

> 让模型在训练时真正经历模态缺失，比设计非常复杂的融合结构更加重要。

### 7. 正确的 attention mask 至关重要

在 MM-IMDb 只剩 10% 文本时：

- 不使用正确 attention mask：F1 Macro = 23.0；
- 使用 attention mask：F1 Macro = 37.3。

这一结果说明：

> 只增加单模态辅助预测头是不够的，还必须确保这些任务无法偷看被排除的模态信息。

## 在油价模型中的具体改造方式

令：

- \(F\) 表示金融和市场模态；
- \(R\) 表示遥感模态；
- \(S\) 表示航运模态。

由于金融数据是预测任务中的基础模态，最有实际意义的训练任务包括：

1. \(F\)：仅金融；
2. \(FR\)：金融 + 遥感；
3. \(FS\)：金融 + 航运；
4. \(FRS\)：完整三模态。

联合损失可以写为：

\[
\mathcal{L}
=
\lambda_F\mathcal{L}_F
+
\lambda_{FR}\mathcal{L}_{FR}
+
\lambda_{FS}\mathcal{L}_{FS}
+
\lambda_{FRS}\mathcal{L}_{FRS}
\]

若内部预测目标为下一周 Brent 对数收益率：

\[
y_{t+1}
=
\log(P_{t+1})-\log(P_t)
\]

四个任务分别输出：

\[
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{F},
\quad
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FR},
\quad
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FS},
\quad
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FRS}
\]

对应的联合训练目标为：

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}
=&
\lambda_F
\ell
\left(
y_{t+1},
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{F}
\right)
+
\lambda_{FR}
\ell
\left(
y_{t+1},
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FR}
\right)
\\
&
+
\lambda_{FS}
\ell
\left(
y_{t+1},
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FS}
\right)
+
\lambda_{FRS}
\ell
\left(
y_{t+1},
\hat{y}_{t+1}^{FRS}
\right)
\end{aligned}
\]

回归损失可以选择：

- MSE；
- MAE；
- Huber loss；
- 收益率回归损失与方向分类损失的联合形式。

## 缺失状态变量

只将缺失 embedding 设置为零并不充分，因为模型无法区分：

- 实体活动确实很低；
- 数据缺失；
- 编码器输出本身接近零；
- 数据处理过程失败。

因此，应为外部模态增加 availability indicator：

\[
m_t^R\in\{0,1\}
\]

\[
m_t^S\in\{0,1\}
\]

其中：

- \(m_t^R=1\) 表示时点 \(t\) 有有效遥感信息；
- \(m_t^S=1\) 表示时点 \(t\) 有有效航运信息。

还应考虑观测的新鲜程度：

\[
\Delta_t^R
=
\text{距离最近一次有效遥感观测的天数}
\]

\[
\Delta_t^S
=
\text{距离最近一次有效航运观测的天数}
\]

模态表示可以构建为：

\[
\tilde{z}_t^R
=
[z_t^R,m_t^R,\Delta_t^R]
\]

\[
\tilde{z}_t^S
=
[z_t^S,m_t^S,\Delta_t^S]
\]

因为两天前的观测与两周前的观测不应被赋予完全相同的权重。

## 建议增加的鲁棒性实验

### 整体模态缺失

| 测试情景 | 可用模态 |
|----------|----------|
| Full input | 金融 + 遥感 + 航运 |
| No remote sensing | 金融 + 航运 |
| No shipping | 金融 + 遥感 |
| Finance only | 仅金融 |

### 随机时间步缺失

分别随机遮蔽：

- 10% 遥感观测；
- 30% 遥感观测；
- 50% 遥感观测；
- 航运模态采用相同缺失比例。

### 连续区块缺失

模拟连续缺失：

- 连续两周；
- 连续四周；
- 连续八周。

相较于独立随机删除，这更符合卫星和数据平台连续中断的现实情况。

### AOI 层级缺失

分别移除：

- 一个随机 AOI；
- 一个重要炼油厂或石油终端；
- 一个关键港口；
- 多个 AOI。

### Chokepoint 层级缺失

分别移除：

- Hormuz；
- Suez；
- Malacca；
- tanker count；
- tanker capacity；
- 多个 chokepoint 指标。

### 模态随机置换

保持金融输入不变，将遥感或航运 embedding 在时间维度上随机打乱。

如果模型性能几乎不变，说明模型可能没有真正利用该外部模态。

## 油价预测中的鲁棒性指标

对于 MAE，可以定义相对鲁棒性差距：

\[
\text{Robustness Gap}_{\text{MAE}}
=
\frac{
\text{MAE}_{\text{missing}}
-
\text{MAE}_{\text{full}}
}{
\text{MAE}_{\text{full}}
}
\times100
\]

数值越低，表示缺失模态后的退化越小。

同样可以计算：

- RMSE robustness gap；
- MAPE robustness gap；
- 方向准确率下降；
- 收益率正负号准确率下降；
- 波动率预测性能下降。

鲁棒性结果还应包括：

- 与 M0 比较；
- 与 M1 比较；
- Diebold–Mariano 检验；
- 不同市场阶段的结果；
- 危机和供应中断时期的表现。

## 建议的融合结构实验

项目可以先比较三个受控架构：

| 融合结构 | 定义 |
|----------|------|
| **Early fusion** | 各模态 token 在模型前部就开始交互。 |
| **Intermediate fusion** | 各模态先形成专属表示，再通过一至两层门控交叉注意力进行融合。 |
| **Late fusion** | 在最终 embedding 或预测输出附近进行融合。 |

一个较适合当前项目的初始结构为：

金融序列  
→ Finance temporal encoder  
→ \(z_{\text{fin}}\)

遥感序列  
→ 冻结或微调的 EO encoder  
→ 时间和 AOI 聚合  
→ \(z_{\text{rs}}\)

航运序列或动态图  
→ Shipping temporal/graph encoder  
→ \(z_{\text{ship}}\)

\[
[z_{\text{fin}},z_{\text{rs}},z_{\text{ship}}]
\rightarrow
\text{一至两层 gated cross-attention}
\rightarrow
\text{预测头}
\]

由于项目中的周度油价样本量较小，先通过 chronological validation 比较 early、intermediate 和 late fusion，可能比立即实施复杂的神经架构搜索更加可靠。

## 局限性

1. **研究任务是分类而非预测**  
   论文研究图像—文本分类，没有测试连续时间序列、油价、收益率或波动率预测。

2. **只有两个模态**  
   论文只研究图像和文本，而本项目至少包含金融、遥感和航运三个模态。

3. **基准数据集规模较大**  
   论文数据集包含约 10,000–90,000 个样本，而约二十年的周度油价数据只有大约 1,000 个时间点。

4. **没有考虑时间依赖**  
   图文样本被视为相对独立，论文没有处理序列相关性、滚动窗口和时间泄漏问题。

5. **缺失机制较简单**  
   论文主要通过减少可用文本比例模拟缺失，而真实遥感和 AIS 缺失可能具有季节性、空间聚集性和非随机性。

6. **没有处理模态异步**  
   图像和文本属于同一个样本，没有不同更新频率、发布时间延迟和陈旧观测的问题。

7. **没有 point-in-time 约束**  
   论文没有处理某项信息在历史预测时点是否真正已经可用。

8. **鲁棒性保证有限**  
   作者承认，多任务学习最多帮助多模态模型不低于单模态模型，但在安全关键系统中仍可能不够。

9. **没有经济预测评价**  
   论文没有使用 Diebold–Mariano 检验、经济价值、方向交易收益或市场阶段分析。

10. **融合策略搜索可能过拟合**  
    当验证集很小，如周度油价预测中，可微分融合策略搜索可能不稳定。

- 将本文放入文献综述中的**方法创新层：缺失模态鲁棒性与动态融合**。
- 用它证明 attention mask 本身不代表多模态 Transformer 已经具有鲁棒性。
- 用它支撑训练阶段实施 structured modality dropout。
- 将其多任务思想改造成：
  - Finance only；
  - Finance + Remote Sensing；
  - Finance + Shipping；
  - Finance + Remote Sensing + Shipping。
- 为各模态增加 availability mask 和 observation-age 指标。
- 通过时间顺序验证比较 early、intermediate 和 late fusion。
- 保留独立训练的 M1–M4，因为它们与统一鲁棒多模态模型回答的是不同研究问题。
- 增加 leave-one-modality-out 和 modality permutation tests。
- 在随机缺失、连续缺失、AOI 缺失和 chokepoint 缺失条件下评估模型。

- 即使 cloud fraction 不作为直接油价预测特征，也可以作为数据质量和模态可用性指标。
- 所有缺失模拟和输入构建都应遵循 point-in-time availability，避免未来信息泄漏。

## 可用于文献综述的表述

Ma et al.（2022）证明，多模态 Transformer 并不天然对缺失模态具有鲁棒性。仅在完整多模态样本上训练的模型，在某一模态不可用时可能出现显著的性能下降，甚至低于专门训练的单模态模型。作者进一步发现，最优融合深度依赖于具体数据集，反映了主导模态和跨模态互补关系的差异。为提高鲁棒性，论文使用 task-specific classification tokens、attention masking、多任务优化和可微分融合策略搜索，联合训练完整模态与缺失模态任务。该结论对多模态石油价格预测具有直接的方法学意义，因为遥感和航运观测可能受到云遮挡、重访周期不规则、AIS 覆盖缺口及异步发布时间的影响。因此，本研究的预测框架应在训练中引入结构化模态 dropout、模态可用性 mask，并分别为 Finance-only、Finance–Remote-Sensing、Finance–Shipping 和完整三模态输入设置辅助预测目标。

## 总体评价

本文没有直接证明遥感或航运信息能够提高 Brent 油价预测表现，其对本项目的价值主要是方法学上的，而非石油市场领域上的。

本文最重要的启示是：

1. 多模态 Transformer 不会自动适应模态缺失；
2. 只报告完整数据条件下的最好结果是不充分的；
3. 训练阶段必须主动模拟缺失模态；
4. 单模态和部分模态辅助任务能够明显提高鲁棒性；
5. 最优融合深度取决于模态主导性和互补关系；
6. 必须通过消融、置换和缺失压力测试确认外部模态是否被真正利用。

因此，P097 可以为本项目的稳健、模态感知油价预测网络提供重要的方法学基础。
