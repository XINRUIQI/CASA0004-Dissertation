# Reading Note — Paper P024: Nighttime Lights as a Proxy for Shipping Activity  
# 阅读笔记——P024：以夜间灯光代理锚泊区航运活动

## Citation / 文献信息

Polinov, S., Bookman, R., & Levin, N. (2022). A Global Assessment of Night Lights as an Indicator for Shipping Activity in Anchorage Areas. *Remote Sensing*, 14(5), 1079.

- **DOI**: [10.3390/rs14051079](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs14051079)
- **Published / 发表日期**: 22 February 2022 / 2022 年 2 月 22 日
- **Journal / 期刊**: *Remote Sensing*
- **Keywords / 关键词**: VIIRS/DNB; nighttime lights; shipping; spatial analysis; anchorage area  
  VIIRS/DNB；夜间灯光；航运活动；空间分析；锚泊区

---

## Research Question / 研究问题

- **English**: Can nighttime lights emitted by ships in offshore port anchorage areas be used as an open-access proxy for shipping activity?
- **中文**：港口外海锚泊区内船舶产生的夜间灯光，能否作为一种免费、标准化的航运活动代理指标？

The paper addresses two specific questions:

1. To what extent do nighttime-light values correspond to shipping activity at the anchorage-area, port, and country levels?
2. Which maritime and socioeconomic variables explain differences in anchorage-area nighttime-light intensity?

论文具体回答两个问题：

1. 锚泊区夜间灯光在多大程度上与锚泊区、港口和国家层面的航运活动相对应？
2. 哪些航运、贸易和社会经济变量能够解释不同国家锚泊区夜光强度的差异？

---

## Core Method / 核心方法

### Study Design / 研究设计

- **English**: Global remote-sensing proxy validation and spatiotemporal correlation study.
- **中文**：全球尺度的遥感代理变量验证与时空相关性研究。

The paper is not a forecasting or machine-learning study. It evaluates whether VIIRS nighttime-light measurements can represent otherwise difficult-to-obtain shipping activity.

该论文并非预测或机器学习研究，而是检验 VIIRS 夜间灯光能否代表难以直接、持续获取的航运活动。

---

### Nighttime-Light Dataset / 夜间灯光数据

- **Sensor / 传感器**: Suomi NPP VIIRS Day/Night Band
- **Temporal coverage / 时间范围**: April 2012–March 2020
- **Frequency / 频率**: Monthly
- **Spatial resolution / 空间分辨率**: Approximately 742 m at nadir
- **Platform / 处理平台**: Google Earth Engine
- **Products / 产品**:
  - `VCMCFG` for April 2012–December 2013
  - `VCMSLCFG` for January 2014–March 2020

Main VIIRS bands:

- `Avg_rad`: monthly average nighttime-light radiance
- `Cf_cfg`: cloud-free observations available during the month

主要 VIIRS 波段：

- `Avg_rad`：月均夜间灯光辐亮度
- `Cf_cfg`：当月有效无云观测信息

The authors also calculated:

\[
SOL_{i,t}=AvgRad_{i,t}\times Area_i
\]

where `SOL` is the Sum of Lights within an anchorage polygon.

作者还计算了锚泊区灯光总量：

\[
SOL_{i,t}=AvgRad_{i,t}\times Area_i
\]

其中，`SOL` 同时反映锚泊区面积和平均夜光强度。

---

### Anchorage-Area Dataset / 锚泊区数据

Anchorage points were obtained from the Global Fishing Watch public anchorages dataset.

锚泊点来自 Global Fishing Watch 的 public anchorages 数据集。

The authors:

1. converted anchorage points into a 1 km raster;
2. applied a spatial closing filter using a 7 × 7 moving window;
3. converted the resulting clusters into anchorage polygons;
4. retained anchorage areas containing at least ten anchorage points;
5. excluded areas located too close to brightly illuminated coastlines.

作者的处理步骤为：

1. 将锚泊点转换为 1 km 栅格；
2. 使用 7 × 7 移动窗口执行空间闭运算；
3. 将形成的聚集区转换为锚泊区多边形；
4. 仅保留至少包含 10 个锚泊点的区域；
5. 尽量排除靠近明亮城市或港口灯光的近岸区域。

Final global sample:

- **601 anchorage areas**
- **97 countries**
- **44,570 included anchorage points**
- **96 monthly observations per anchorage area**

最终全球样本包括：

- **601 个锚泊区**
- **97 个国家**
- **44,570 个纳入分析的锚泊点**
- **每个锚泊区最多 96 个月度观测**

Anchorage points should not be interpreted as observed monthly vessel counts. They primarily represent the spatial structure and potential capacity of an anchorage area.

需要注意，锚泊点并不等于每个月实际观察到的船舶数量。它主要代表锚泊区的空间结构和潜在锚泊容量。

### Data Cleaning and Quality Control / 数据清洗与质量控制

The authors corrected the nighttime-light series for temporal variation in natural light sources such as airglow.

作者对 airglow 等自然背景光的时间波动进行了校正。

A monthly observation was considered unreliable when:

\[
Average\ cloud\text{-}free\ days < 1
\]

当某锚泊区某月的平均无云观测天数小于 1 天时，该月夜光观测被认为不可靠。

Approximately 94.6% of all anchorage-month observations had sufficient cloud-free coverage.

全部“锚泊区—月份”观测中，约 94.6% 具有足够的无云覆盖。

Missing or underestimated months were interpolated using the six months before and six months after the affected observation. A two-tailed t-test was then used to compare the original and interpolated series.

作者利用缺失月份之前六个月和之后六个月的数据进行插值，并使用双尾 t 检验比较插值前后的均值。

No significant difference was found between the original and interpolated average nighttime-light values.

结果显示，插值没有显著改变各锚泊区的长期平均夜光水平。

A detailed temporal validation was conducted for the Port of Santos, Brazil.

作者以巴西 Santos 港进行了详细的月度时间序列验证。

Validation period:

- January 2016–March 2020
- 51 monthly observations

验证时间：

- 2016 年 1 月至 2020 年 3 月
- 共 51 个月度观测

Validation data included:

- approximately four to five Sentinel-1 images per month;
- monthly imports and exports;
- number of vessels waiting in the anchorage area;
- number of vessels waiting for more than 72 hours;

验证数据包括：

- 基于 Sentinel-1 SAR 影像识别的船舶数量；
- 每月约 4–5 景 Sentinel-1 影像；
- 港口月度进口量和出口量；
- 锚泊区等待船舶数量；
- 等待超过 72 小时的船舶数量；
- 普通货船、干散货船、油轮、客船和滚装船等分类统计。

### Statistical Analysis / 统计分析

- **Main method / 主要方法**: Spearman rank correlation
- **Software / 软件**: SPSS
- **Spatial levels / 空间层面**:
  1. individual anchorage-area level;
  2. country level;
  3. monthly port-level analysis for Santos.
- **Additional test / 额外检验**: two-tailed t-test for the interpolation procedure.

The study does not estimate a forecasting model, conduct causal inference, or evaluate out-of-sample prediction.

论文没有建立预测模型，也没有进行因果推断或样本外预测评估。

## Key Findings / 主要发现

### 1. Nighttime lights correspond to anchorage-area scale  
### 1. 夜间灯光与锚泊区规模显著相关

Across 601 anchorage areas, the number of anchorage points was strongly and significantly associated with average annual SOL:

\[
R_s=0.69,\quad p<0.01,\quad n=601
\]

在全球 601 个锚泊区中，锚泊点数量与长期平均灯光总量显著正相关：

\[
R_s=0.69,\quad p<0.01
\]

This suggests that larger anchorage areas with greater potential vessel capacity generally produce stronger aggregate nighttime-light signals.

这表明，规模较大、潜在船舶容量较高的锚泊区通常产生更强的总体夜光信号。

However, this result partly captures persistent cross-sectional differences in anchorage size and port scale, rather than only short-term changes in vessel activity.

但是，该结果在一定程度上反映的是锚泊区面积和港口长期规模差异，而不完全是短期船舶活动变化。

---

### 2. Country-level correspondence is particularly strong  
### 2. 国家层面的相关关系尤其明显

At the country level, total anchorage points and average annual nighttime lights were strongly correlated:

\[
R_s=0.84,\quad p<0.01,\quad n=97
\]

在国家层面，一个国家的锚泊点总数与其锚泊区夜光总量之间具有显著强相关：

\[
R_s=0.84,\quad p<0.01
\]

The strongest country-level relationships included:

| Variable / 变量 | Spearman correlation with NTL / 与夜光的相关系数 |
|---|---:|
| Container port throughput / 集装箱港口吞吐量 | 0.84 |
| Population / 人口 | 0.68 |
| Maximum cargo-carrying capacity / 最大载货能力 | 0.66 |
| Imports / 进口额 | 0.62 |
| GDP / 国内生产总值 | 0.61 |
| Liner shipping connectivity index / 班轮运输连通性指数 | 0.60 |
| Maximum container capacity / 最大集装箱载运能力 | 0.55 |
| Maximum vessel size / 最大船舶尺寸 | 0.51 |
| Port calls / 港口靠港次数 | 0.42 |

These findings indicate that anchorage-area nighttime lights capture broad differences in national shipping scale, trade integration, and port capacity.

这些结果说明，锚泊区夜光能够反映不同国家在航运规模、贸易网络连接程度和港口容量方面的总体差异。

The country-level results should not automatically be interpreted as evidence of short-term forecasting power, because much of the association may be driven by persistent size differences between countries.

但国家层面的高相关性不能直接被解释为短期预测能力，因为其很大一部分可能来自国家间长期规模差异。

---

### 3. Monthly vessel-count validation is moderate rather than strong  
### 3. 月度实际船舶数量验证仅达到中等相关

At the Port of Santos, monthly VIIRS nighttime lights were moderately correlated with vessel counts obtained from Sentinel-1:

\[
R_s=0.51
\]

在 Santos 港，VIIRS 月度夜光与 Sentinel-1 识别的锚泊船舶数量呈中等相关：

\[
R_s=0.51
\]

Nighttime lights were also associated with:

- vessels waiting for more than 72 hours: \(R_s=0.41\);
- bulk-solid vessels: \(R_s=0.41\);
- exports: \(R_s=0.37\).

夜光还与以下变量存在一定相关性：

- 等待超过 72 小时的船舶：\(R_s=0.41\)；
- 干散货船数量：\(R_s=0.41\)；
- 出口量：\(R_s=0.37\)。

These results imply that NTL may capture not only vessel numbers, but also vessel size, illumination behaviour, and the number of nights spent waiting in an anchorage area.

这表明 NTL 可能不仅反映船舶数量，还同时反映船舶尺寸、照明行为以及船舶在锚泊区等待的夜晚数量。

A useful conceptual representation is:

\[
NTL\approx Vessel\ Count\times Waiting\ Time\times Lighting\ Intensity
\]

可以将夜光的含义概括为：

\[
NTL\approx 船舶数量\times 等待时间\times 船舶照明强度
\]

---

### 4. NTL does not reliably identify tanker activity  
### 4. 夜光不能可靠识别油轮活动

For Santos, the correlation between VIIRS nighttime lights and the number of tankers was:

\[
R_s=-0.07
\]

在 Santos 港，VIIRS 夜光与油轮数量的相关系数为：

\[
R_s=-0.07
\]

The correlation with the total number of ships reported by the port was also relatively weak:

\[
R_s=0.22
\]

夜光与港口官方统计的船舶总数之间的相关性也较弱：

\[
R_s=0.22
\]

Therefore, the study supports NTL as a proxy for broad anchorage activity, but it does not demonstrate that NTL is a reliable proxy for tanker traffic or petroleum shipping specifically.

因此，该研究支持将 NTL 视为综合锚泊活动的代理变量，但没有证明 NTL 能够可靠代表油轮活动或石油运输活动。

---

### 5. NTL can reveal broad temporal changes in maritime activity  
### 5. 夜光能够识别航运活动的总体时间变化

Among the 601 anchorage areas:

- 270 showed a statistically significant increase in NTL;
- 112 showed a statistically significant decrease;
- 219 had no statistically significant trend.

在 601 个锚泊区中：

- 270 个锚泊区的夜光显著增加；
- 112 个锚泊区的夜光显著下降；
- 219 个锚泊区没有显著时间趋势。

The results demonstrate the potential of VIIRS data for monitoring broad spatial and temporal changes in global maritime activity, especially where consistent port statistics are unavailable.

这些结果表明，在缺乏一致港口统计数据的情况下，VIIRS 数据具有监测全球航运活动总体空间分布和时间变化的潜力。

---

### 6. Offshore anchorage selection reduces coastal-light contamination  
### 6. 选择离岸锚泊区能够降低沿岸灯光污染

The Fujairah case study showed that the anchorage area was approximately 10 km offshore, while visible city-light influence declined after approximately 6–7 km from the coastline.

Fujairah 案例表明，其锚泊区距离海岸约 10 km，而城市灯光影响大约在距离海岸 6–7 km 后明显减弱。

The anchorage-area radiance was approximately 2–7 nW cm\(^{-2}\) sr\(^{-1}\), compared with more than 50 nW cm\(^{-2}\) sr\(^{-1}\) in the urban and coastal area.

锚泊区夜光约为 2–7 nW cm\(^{-2}\) sr\(^{-1}\)，而城市和沿岸地区夜光超过 50 nW cm\(^{-2}\) sr\(^{-1}\)。

Fujairah is particularly relevant to petroleum research because tankers are the main vessel category operating in the selected anchorage area.

Fujairah 与石油研究尤其相关，因为该锚泊区的主要船型为油轮。

## Suggested Mechanism for the Oil-Price Project  
## 对石油价格预测项目的机制启示

The paper does not support a direct relationship of:

\[
NTL\rightarrow Oil\ Price
\]

论文不支持简单地将夜光直接连接到油价：

\[
NTL\rightarrow 石油价格
\]

A more defensible mechanism is:

\[
Anchorage\ NTL
\rightarrow
Shipping\ Activity/Waiting/Congestion
\rightarrow
Oil\ Trade\ Flows
\rightarrow
Supply\text{-}Demand\ Expectations
\rightarrow
Oil\ Price
\]

更合理的机制链条为：

\[
锚泊区夜光
\rightarrow
航运活动/等待时间/港口拥堵
\rightarrow
石油贸易流
\rightarrow
供需预期
\rightarrow
石油价格
\]

The interpretation depends on the economic function of each port:

- At an export terminal, stronger NTL may indicate increased loading and future supply, or alternatively congestion and delayed supply.
- At an import or refining port, stronger NTL may indicate stronger import demand or inventory replenishment.
- At a general cargo port, NTL may primarily capture non-oil shipping and therefore add little petroleum-specific information.

具体解释取决于港口功能：

- 在原油出口港，夜光增强可能表示装船和未来供应增加，也可能表示拥堵和供应延迟；
- 在进口港或炼化港，夜光增强可能表示进口需求增加或补库存；
- 在综合货运港，夜光可能主要反映非石油航运，对石油价格的特异性较弱。

## Suggested Variables for Dissertation Implementation  
## 项目可构建的变量

### Raw NTL Features / 原始夜光变量

\[
ntl\_avg\_rad_{i,t}
\]

\[
ntl\_sol_{i,t}=ntl\_avg\_rad_{i,t}\times area_i
\]

---

### Dynamic Features / 动态变化变量

\[
ntl\_mom_{i,t}
=
\frac{NTL_{i,t}}{NTL_{i,t-1}}-1
\]

\[
ntl\_yoy_{i,t}
=
\frac{NTL_{i,t}}{NTL_{i,t-12}}-1
\]

\[
ntl\_anomaly_{i,t}
=
\frac{NTL_{i,t}-\mu_{i,month}}
{\sigma_{i,month}}
\]

Dynamic variables are more suitable for oil-price forecasting because they reduce persistent differences caused by AOI size, fixed port infrastructure, and regional lighting practices.

动态变量更适合油价预测，因为它们能够减弱 AOI 面积、固定港口设施和地区照明制度造成的长期差异。

- `ntl_snr_i`

这些变量可作为模型输入、数据筛选条件、观测权重或交互项。

- `夜光异常 × 油轮占比`
- `夜光异常 × 出口港虚拟变量`
- `夜光异常 × 进口港虚拟变量`
- `夜光异常 × AIS 等待时间`
- `夜光异常 × 地缘政治事件变量`

## Suggested Experimental Design / 建议的实验设计

The paper establishes construct validity but not forecasting validity. The dissertation should therefore compare:

| Experiment / 实验 | Feature Set / 特征集合 |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Oil-price lags only / 仅油价滞后项 |
| M1 | Market, macroeconomic, inventory, volatility, and uncertainty variables / 市场、宏观、库存、波动率和不确定性变量 |
| M1 + Raw NTL | M1 plus raw anchorage-area nighttime lights / M1 加原始锚泊区夜光 |
| M1 + Dynamic NTL | M1 plus NTL changes, anomalies, and quality variables / M1 加夜光变化、异常值和质量变量 |
| M1 + M2 | Complete market and remote-sensing feature set / 完整市场与遥感变量 |
| M1 + M2 + M3 | Add AIS, shipping, or chokepoint-flow features / 再加入 AIS、航运和咽喉流量变量 |
| Full Multimodal Model | Market, remote sensing, shipping, text, and event features / 市场、遥感、航运、文本和事件变量 |

Recommended evaluation:

- expanding-window or rolling-window validation;
- strict chronological train-validation-test split;
- RMSE, MAE, MAPE, and directional accuracy;
- Diebold–Mariano forecast comparison;
- one-, three-, and six-month horizons;
- SHAP or permutation importance;
- modality-level and AOI-level ablation tests.

建议采用：

- 扩展窗口或滚动窗口验证；
- 严格按时间排序的训练、验证和测试划分；
- RMSE、MAE、MAPE 和方向准确率；
- Diebold–Mariano 预测比较检验；
- 1、3 和 6 个月预测期；
- SHAP 或 permutation importance；
- 模态层面和 AOI 层面的消融实验。

## Limitations / 局限性

1. **Coarse spatial resolution / 空间分辨率较粗**

   VIIRS has an approximately 742 m footprint, preventing the identification of individual vessels and limiting the analysis of small or densely clustered objects.

   VIIRS 的空间分辨率约为 742 m，无法识别单艘船舶，也难以分析较小或高度密集的目标。

2. **Cloud contamination / 云覆盖影响**

   Frequent cloud cover in coastal and tropical regions causes missing or underestimated monthly observations.

   沿海和热带地区频繁出现的云层会导致月度数据缺失或低估。

3. **Coastal and urban light pollution / 城市和沿岸灯光污染**

   Port facilities and coastal cities may emit much stronger light than vessels, making the method ineffective for anchorage areas located close to brightly illuminated coastlines.

   港口设施和沿海城市的灯光可能远强于船舶灯光，使靠近明亮海岸的锚泊区无法有效使用该方法。

4. **No global vessel-type information / 缺乏全球船型信息**

   The global analysis does not distinguish tankers, container ships, bulk carriers, or other vessel categories.

   全球分析没有区分油轮、集装箱船、散货船和其他船型。

5. **Heterogeneous lighting behaviour / 船舶照明行为存在差异**

   Vessel types, local port regulations, lighting technology, and operational practices may produce different light intensities for the same number of ships.

   船型、地方港口规定、照明技术和运营方式可能导致相同船舶数量产生不同的灯光强度。

6. **Sensor spectral limitations / 传感器光谱限制**

   VIIRS DNB is panchromatic and does not adequately capture blue-wavelength light, which is an important component of LED illumination.

   VIIRS DNB 为全色传感器，无法充分测量 LED 照明中较重要的蓝光成分。

7. **Observation-angle effects / 观测角度影响**

   Variations in satellite zenith and azimuth viewing angles may change the amount of ship light received by the sensor.

   卫星天顶角和方位角的变化可能影响传感器接收到的船舶灯光强度。

8. **Only one detailed port-level validation case / 仅有一个详细港口验证案例**

   The direct monthly comparison with vessel counts was conducted only for Santos, limiting generalisability to tanker-dominated or petroleum-specific ports.

   论文仅在 Santos 港进行了实际船舶数量的月度验证，难以直接推广至油轮主导或石油专用港口。

9. **Correlation rather than forecasting or causality / 仅验证相关性**

   The paper does not test lead-lag relationships, Granger causality, out-of-sample forecasting performance, or incremental predictive value.

   论文没有检验领先滞后关系、Granger 因果、样本外预测性能或增量预测价值。

10. **Aggregation and scale effects / 聚合与尺度效应**

    Strong country-level correlations may primarily reflect persistent differences in country size, port capacity, and trade volume.

    国家层面的强相关可能主要反映国家规模、港口容量和贸易量的长期差异。

11. **SOL is affected by polygon area / SOL 受到多边形面积影响**

    Because SOL equals mean radiance multiplied by anchorage-area size, its correlation with anchorage scale may partly arise mechanically.

    由于 SOL 等于平均辐亮度乘以锚泊区面积，其与锚泊规模的相关性可能部分来自计算方式本身。

12. **Interpolation creates a forecasting-leakage risk / 插值存在预测信息泄漏风险**

    The study uses six preceding and six subsequent months to interpolate cloudy observations. This is acceptable for retrospective analysis but would use future information in a forecasting setting.

    论文使用缺失月份前后各六个月的数据进行插值。这适用于事后分析，但在预测任务中会使用未来信息并产生数据泄漏。

13. **Limited temporal coverage / 时间覆盖有限**

    VIIRS data begin in 2012, preventing direct coverage of earlier oil-market events in a possible 2005–2025 dissertation sample.

    VIIRS 数据始于 2012 年，因此无法直接覆盖 2005–2025 长样本中的早期石油市场事件。

## Notes for Dissertation Integration / 论文整合笔记

- Use this paper as a core methodological reference for the M2 nighttime-light and remote-sensing module.
- Cite it to justify the extraction of VIIRS `Avg_rad`, `SOL`, and cloud-free observation variables over offshore anchorage polygons.
- Use offshore anchorage areas rather than entire ports wherever possible.
- Treat Fujairah as the paper's most directly relevant petroleum-port example because the selected anchorage area is offshore and primarily serves tankers.
- Do not claim that the paper proves NTL is a proxy for tanker traffic or predicts oil prices.
- Interpret NTL as a composite indicator of vessel activity, waiting time, congestion, and lighting intensity.
- Combine NTL with AIS vessel type, tanker share, vessel waiting time, or port-function classifications.
- Use dynamic NTL anomalies and changes rather than relying only on raw cross-sectional radiance levels.
- Retain cloud-free observation counts and missingness flags as explicit quality-control variables.
- Replace the paper's two-sided interpolation with past-only imputation to avoid look-ahead leakage.
- Conduct a separate post-2012 multimodal experiment for the NTL-enhanced model.
- Demonstrate value through strict out-of-sample comparison between M1 and M1 + M2.
- Use modality-level SHAP values and ablation tests to determine whether remote-sensing variables provide information beyond market, macroeconomic, and shipping variables.

- 将该论文作为 M2 夜间灯光与遥感模块的核心方法文献。
- 用它支持在离岸锚泊区提取 VIIRS `Avg_rad`、`SOL` 和无云观测变量。
- 在条件允许时优先采用离岸锚泊区，而不是整个港口范围。
- Fujairah 是本文与石油项目最直接相关的港口案例，因为其锚泊区远离海岸且主要服务于油轮。
- 不应声称该论文证明 NTL 可以代理油轮流量或预测石油价格。
- 应将 NTL 解释为船舶活动、等待时间、拥堵和照明强度共同形成的综合指标。
- 应将 NTL 与 AIS 船型、油轮占比、等待时间或港口功能分类结合。
- 优先使用夜光异常值和动态变化，而不是仅依赖原始亮度水平。
- 将无云观测次数和缺失标记作为明确的数据质量变量。
- 将论文的双向插值修改为仅使用历史信息的插值方法，避免前视偏差。
- 针对 2012 年以后的样本单独开展加入 NTL 的多模态预测实验。
- 通过 M1 与 M1 + M2 的严格样本外比较验证夜光是否提供增量价值。
- 使用模态层面的 SHAP 和消融实验，检验遥感变量能否提供超出市场、宏观和航运变量的信息。

## Recommended Dissertation Positioning / 建议的文献定位

**English**:

This paper should be positioned as a core methodological reference for constructing and validating the dissertation's remote-sensing shipping-activity variables, rather than as an oil-price forecasting paper. It establishes the construct validity of offshore anchorage nighttime lights, while also demonstrating why vessel-type information, quality control, and out-of-sample validation are necessary before NTL can be used in a petroleum-price forecasting model.

**中文**：

该论文应被定位为本项目构建和验证遥感航运活动变量的核心方法文献，而不是石油价格预测文献。它为离岸锚泊区夜间灯光提供了变量合理性依据，同时也说明，在将 NTL 用于石油价格预测之前，必须补充船型信息、实施严格的数据质量控制，并进行样本外增量预测检验。

## One-Sentence Summary / 一句话总结

**English**: VIIRS nighttime lights provide a scalable and open-access proxy for broad shipping activity in offshore anchorage areas, but they do not directly identify tanker movements or demonstrate oil-price forecasting power.

**中文**：VIIRS 夜间灯光能够作为离岸锚泊区综合航运活动的可扩展、免费代理变量，但不能直接识别油轮活动，也尚未证明能够提高石油价格预测能力。
