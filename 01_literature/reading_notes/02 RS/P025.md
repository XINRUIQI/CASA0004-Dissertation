## Research Question / 研究问题

### English

Can cloud cover above floating-roof oil tanks predict future crude-oil returns by reducing the availability of satellite-based inventory information?

The paper does not treat cloudiness primarily as a conventional weather variable. Instead, cloud cover is interpreted as a barrier to satellite observation:

\[
\text{Cloud cover}
\rightarrow
\text{Lower satellite observability}
\rightarrow
\text{Less inventory information}
\rightarrow
\text{Greater information uncertainty}
\rightarrow
\text{Changes in oil returns}
\]

### 中文

浮顶油罐上方的云层会阻碍卫星观测，减少市场能够获得的库存信息。论文研究这种“卫星信息不可获得性”能否预测未来原油收益率。

论文并不是把云量简单视为天气变量，而是提出以下机制：

\[
\text{云覆盖增加}
\rightarrow
\text{卫星可观测性下降}
\rightarrow
\text{库存信息流减少}
\rightarrow
\text{信息不确定性上升}
\rightarrow
\text{未来原油收益率变化}
\]

## Core Contribution / 核心贡献

### English

The paper shifts attention from the economic information directly extracted from satellite imagery to the **availability of satellite information itself**.

Most remote-sensing studies ask what economic activity can be measured from images. This paper instead asks whether the market is affected when economically valuable satellite information becomes temporarily unavailable.

It therefore provides a direct link between:

\[
\text{Remote-sensing information availability}
\rightarrow
\text{Oil-market return predictability}
\]

### 中文

论文没有直接使用遥感影像估计出的库存作为预测变量，而是研究：

> 当云层使市场无法通过卫星观察库存时，这种信息缺口是否会影响未来油价收益率。

因此，它把以下两部分直接连接起来：

\[
\text{遥感信息可获得性}
\rightarrow
\text{原油市场收益率预测}
\]

这是少数直接将遥感信息环境与石油价格形成联系起来的文献之一。

## Data / 数据

### 1. Oil-storage areas / 原油储存区

The paper studies floating-roof tanks in eight major U.S. oil-storage areas:

1. Cushing, Oklahoma — COK
2. Houston, Texas — HTX
3. Texas City, Texas — TTX
4. Beaumont, Texas — BTX
5. Corpus Christi, Texas — CTX
6. Lake Charles, Louisiana — LLA
7. Baton Rouge, Louisiana — BLA
8. Mississippi River, Louisiana — MRL

These locations belong mainly to PADD 2 and PADD 3, which together hold a substantial share of U.S. crude-oil inventories.

论文研究美国八个主要储油区的浮顶油罐，包括 Cushing 和 Gulf Coast 地区。研究对象并不只有 Cushing。

---

### 2. Tank-location data / 油罐位置数据

- High-resolution historical maps from ArcGIS Online
- YOLOv5 object-detection model
- Annual updates of tank coordinates from 2014 to 2021

Because tanks may be constructed, abandoned, or modified over time, the authors update the geographical distribution of tanks annually rather than treating infrastructure locations as permanently fixed.

由于油罐会新建、废弃或发生变化，作者使用逐年高分辨率地图更新油罐坐标，而不是假设油罐分布在整个样本期保持不变。

---

### 3. Cloud-cover data / 云覆盖数据

- **Source / 来源**: NASA Terra satellite
- **Product / 产品**: MODIS Cloud Mask
- **Temporal resolution / 时间分辨率**: Daily observations aggregated to weekly measures
- **Spatial resolution / 空间分辨率**: Up to approximately 250 m for relevant MODIS observations

MODIS cloud states are converted into numerical values:

| Cloud classification | Numerical value |
|---|---:|
| Confident clear | 0 |
| Probably clear | 1 |
| Probably cloudy | 2 |
| Confident cloudy | 3 |

Higher values therefore indicate heavier cloud cover.

MODIS 云分类被转换为 0–3 的数值，数值越高表示云层越厚。

---

### 4. Construction of cloudiness indicators / 云量指标构建

For each storage area, cloudiness is calculated as a weighted average across floating-roof tanks, where tank footprint is used as the weight.

\[
K_{i,t}
=
\sum_j w_{ij}Cloudiness_{ij,t}
\]

where:

- \(i\) = oil-storage area;
- \(j\) = individual floating-roof tank;
- \(w_{ij}\) = weight based on the footprint of tank \(j\);
- \(K_{i,t}\) = cloudiness above storage area \(i\).

The authors construct:

- eight area-specific cloudiness indicators;
- one aggregate cloudiness indicator across the eight areas.

每个储油区内部按照浮顶油罐 footprint 对云量加权，并进一步构建八个地区指标和一个总体云量指标。

---

### 5. Oil-market data / 石油市场数据

- **Price source / 价格来源**: U.S. Energy Information Administration, EIA
- **Main asset / 主要资产**: WTI spot crude oil
- **Additional asset / 扩展资产**: WTI Contract 1 futures
- **Main target / 主要预测目标**: Next-week WTI return

Weekly returns are calculated using Tuesday-to-Tuesday log-price differences:

\[
r_{t+1}
=
\ln(P_{t+1})-\ln(P_t)
\]

The timing is chosen to reflect the weekly structure surrounding EIA inventory announcements.

论文预测的是下一周 WTI 收益率，而不是 Brent，也不是直接预测 WTI 价格水平。

---

## Why Floating-Roof Tanks Matter / 为什么关注浮顶油罐

### English

The roof of a floating-roof tank rises and falls with the level of oil inside the tank. Under clear daylight conditions, the outer and inner shadows of the tank can be used, together with the solar angle, to estimate the height of the floating roof and therefore the approximate inventory level.

Cloud cover prevents observation of these shadows and reduces the availability of satellite-based oil-inventory estimates.

Fixed-roof tanks do not provide the same inventory information because their roofs do not move with the stored liquid.

### 中文

浮顶油罐的顶部会随罐内液面升降：

- 库存增加时，浮顶升高；
- 库存减少时，浮顶降低。

在晴朗的白天，可以结合油罐内外阴影和太阳高度角估计浮顶高度，进一步推断库存。

云层会遮挡油罐及其阴影，使市场无法及时获得卫星库存估计。固定顶油罐的顶部不会随库存变化，因此不能通过相同方法估计库存。

## Variables / 变量

### Main predictor / 核心预测变量

- \(K_{i,t}\): cloudiness above floating-roof tanks in storage area \(i\)
- \(K_{A,t}\): aggregate cloudiness across the eight storage areas

Interpretation:

\[
K_t \uparrow
\Rightarrow
\text{Satellite information availability} \downarrow
\]

云量越高，卫星库存信息的可获得性越低。

---

### Control variables / 控制变量

The predictive regressions include six competing oil-market and uncertainty predictors:

1. **EPU** — Economic Policy Uncertainty
2. **VIX** — CBOE equity-market volatility index
3. **MU** — Macroeconomic uncertainty
4. **OI** — Unexpected oil-inventory change
5. **OR** — Lagged WTI oil return
6. **AR** — Asymmetric oil-return variable, represented by the positive component of oil returns

控制变量涵盖政策不确定性、股票市场波动率、宏观不确定性、意外库存变化和滞后石油收益率。

The correlations between cloudiness measures and the conventional predictors are generally low, suggesting that cloudiness contains relatively distinct information.

云量变量与传统预测变量的相关性较低，说明其可能提供传统市场数据之外的新信息。

## Core Method / 核心方法

### 1. In-sample predictive regression / 样本内预测回归

The baseline model is:

\[
r_{t+1}
=
\alpha+\beta_KK_t+\varepsilon_{t+1}
\]

The expected sign is:

\[
\beta_K<0
\]

A multivariate version adds the control variables:

\[
r_{t+1}
=
\alpha+\beta_KK_t+
\sum_{j=1}^{N}\gamma_jX_{j,t}
+\varepsilon_{t+1}
\]

- **Estimator / 估计方法**: Ordinary Least Squares
- **Inference / 统计推断**: Newey–West heteroskedasticity- and autocorrelation-robust standard errors
- **Hypothesis / 假设**: One-sided test of whether cloudiness negatively predicts oil returns

---

### 2. Out-of-sample forecasting / 样本外预测

The authors recursively estimate the predictive model using information available before each forecast date.

The benchmark is the historical-average model, which assumes no return predictability.

Forecast accuracy is evaluated using:

\[
R^2_{OOS}
=
1-
\frac{MSPE_{model}}
{MSPE_{historical\ average}}
\]

A positive \(R^2_{OOS}\) means that the predictive model performs better than the historical-average benchmark.

样本外预测采用递归估计，每一期只能使用预测时点之前已经获得的信息，从而避免前视偏差。

---

### 3. Statistical forecast tests / 预测统计检验

- Clark–West test
- Forecast-encompassing test
- Harvey–Leybourne–Newbold test

These tests evaluate whether cloudiness forecasts provide statistically significant and unique information beyond the benchmark and conventional predictors.

这些检验用于判断云量模型是否真正优于历史均值模型，以及云量是否包含其他预测变量没有提供的信息。

---

### 4. Alternative aggregation methods / 替代聚合方法

The paper tests three approaches for combining information from the eight storage areas:

1. Simple aggregate cloudiness
2. Equal-weight forecast combination
3. Diffusion index based on the first principal component

论文不仅使用简单总体云量，还使用等权预测组合和 PCA 第一主成分验证结果是否依赖特定聚合方式。

---

### 5. Robustness tests / 稳健性检验

The paper applies several alternative inference methods:

- Wild bootstrap with 2,000 simulations
- Stambaugh bias adjustment
- IVX-Wald test
- Weighted least squares
- Alternative autoregressive benchmark
- Longer forecast horizons
- WTI futures returns
- Time trend interaction to test whether predictability weakens over time

这些检验用于处理非正态性、序列相关、预测变量持续性、异方差和模型基准选择等问题。

---

### 6. Placebo and contrast tests / 安慰剂与对照检验

Three major placebo tests are conducted:

#### Earlier period

The model is tested over February 2007–December 2013, before satellite-derived oil information became widely available to investors.

If cloudiness predicted returns during this period, the result could reflect ordinary weather effects rather than satellite-information availability.

#### Fixed-roof tanks

Cloudiness above fixed-roof tanks is tested because these tanks do not reveal inventory through moving-roof shadows.

#### Nighttime cloudiness

Nighttime cloudiness is tested because floating-roof inventory cannot be inferred from solar shadows at night.

三项安慰剂检验分别使用：

- 卫星库存信息尚未普遍应用的早期时期；
- 固定顶油罐；
- 夜间云量。

其目的是排除普通天气、地区气候和其他遗漏因素的解释。

---

### 7. Mechanism tests / 机制检验

The paper tests two channels:

#### Information-uncertainty and inventory channel

\[
Cloudiness_t
\rightarrow
Inventory\ uncertainty_t
\rightarrow
Inventory_{t+1}
\rightarrow
Oil\ returns_{t+1}
\]

#### Information-flow and volatility channel

\[
Cloudiness_t
\rightarrow
Less\ information\ flow
\rightarrow
Lower\ trading\ activity
\rightarrow
Lower\ future\ volatility
\]

The interaction between cloudiness and lagged volatility is also examined to determine whether investors rely more heavily on past information when new satellite information is unavailable.

---

### 8. Economic-value test / 经济价值检验

The paper constructs a mean–variance portfolio containing:

- WTI oil futures;
- three-month U.S. Treasury bills.

The predicted oil-futures return determines the portfolio's ex-ante futures weight.

Performance is evaluated using:

- certainty-equivalent return, CER;
- Sharpe ratio, SR;
- different risk-aversion coefficients;
- different constraints on long, short and leveraged positions.

---

## Key Findings / 主要发现

### 1. Cloudiness negatively predicts next-week WTI returns

Seven of the eight area-specific cloudiness indicators have significant negative coefficients in the univariate in-sample models.

After adding conventional control variables, six of the eight indicators remain significantly negative.

八个储油区中，有七个地区的云量在单变量模型中显著负向预测下一周 WTI 收益率；加入控制变量后，六个地区仍然显著。

---

### 2. Aggregate cloudiness has significant in-sample predictive power

For the aggregate cloudiness measure:

\[
\hat{\beta}_K=-0.040
\]

\[
t=-2.611
\]

\[
R^2=1.275\%
\]

The coefficient is statistically significant at the 1% level.

After adding the six control variables:

\[
\hat{\beta}_K=-0.038
\]

\[
t=-2.748
\]

The relationship therefore cannot be explained solely by conventional uncertainty, inventory or return predictors.

总体云量显著负向预测未来收益率，而且加入 EPU、VIX、宏观不确定性、库存和滞后收益率后，结果仍然成立。

---

### 3. Cloudiness improves out-of-sample forecasting

The aggregate cloudiness model obtains:

\[
R^2_{OOS}=0.889\%
\]

with a Clark–West statistic of:

\[
CW=2.185
\]

which is significant at the 5% level.

Most competing single-variable predictors fail to outperform the historical-average benchmark over the same period.

总体云量模型的样本外预测误差低于历史均值基准，说明云量包含一定的真实增量预测信息。

---

### 4. Forecast-encompassing results support unique information content

When the historical-average model is used as the benchmark, the optimal combination assigns a weight of 1 to the aggregate cloudiness model.

When the aggregate cloudiness model is used as the benchmark, conventional predictors receive weights close to zero.

This indicates that the cloudiness forecast contains information not captured by the competing predictors.

预测包含检验表明，总体云量模型在较大程度上包含传统变量的预测信息，而传统变量难以包含云量模型提供的信息。

---

### 5. Alternative aggregation methods remain effective

The out-of-sample results are:

- Equal-weight forecast combination:

\[
R^2_{OOS}=0.821\%
\]

- PCA-based diffusion index:

\[
R^2_{OOS}=0.751\%
\]

Both approaches continue to outperform the historical-average benchmark.

使用等权预测组合或 PCA 汇总八个 AOI 后，结果仍然成立，说明结论并非完全依赖简单平均方法。

---

### 6. Predictive power is short-lived

Aggregate cloudiness significantly predicts returns at two- and three-week horizons.

For horizons longer than three weeks, the predictive power disappears.

云覆盖造成的信息缺口主要是短期的。一旦出现新的晴朗观测或 EIA 发布库存数据，不确定性便会逐渐消失。

---

### 7. Results extend to WTI futures

For WTI Contract 1 futures, the aggregate cloudiness model achieves:

\[
R^2_{OOS}=1.108\%
\]

with:

\[
CW=2.326
\]

The result is statistically significant and confirms that cloudiness also predicts economically tradable oil-futures returns.

---

### 8. Placebo tests support the satellite-information mechanism

- Cloudiness does not significantly predict returns during the earlier 2007–2013 period.
- Fixed-roof-tank cloudiness has weaker predictive ability.
- Nighttime cloudiness has no meaningful predictive ability.

These findings weaken explanations based on ordinary weather, seasonality or regional climate.

安慰剂检验说明，真正重要的并不是一般天气，而是白天云层是否阻碍了具有库存信息价值的浮顶油罐观测。

---

### 9. Information-uncertainty channel

A one-point increase in aggregate cloudiness is associated with an approximately:

\[
0.398\%
\]

increase in U.S. oil inventory in the following week.

The interpretation is that greater uncertainty encourages precautionary inventory holdings.

总体云量上升与下一周美国原油库存增加相关，支持“信息不确定性—预防性库存”机制。

---

### 10. Information-flow channel

Higher cloudiness is associated with lower future oil-market volatility.

The interaction between lagged volatility and cloudiness suggests that volatility becomes more persistent under heavy cloud cover, potentially because investors rely more strongly on historical information when new satellite information is unavailable.

云量较高时，新信息流减少，未来短期波动率下降；同时，市场更依赖已有信息，波动率持续性增强。

---

### 11. The forecasts have economic value

Under the futures-weight constraint:

\[
-1<\omega_t<1
\]

the annualised certainty-equivalent return gains are approximately:

- 422 basis points for risk aversion \(\gamma=1\);
- 261.9 basis points for \(\gamma=3\);
- 111.9 basis points for \(\gamma=6\).

The corresponding annualised Sharpe-ratio gains are approximately:

- 0.194;
- 0.251;
- 0.231.

Therefore, the statistical forecasting improvement also produces measurable portfolio value under the authors' assumptions.

---

## Interpretation / 结果解释

### English

The findings do not imply that cloudy weather directly reduces physical oil production or demand.

Instead, cloud cover changes the information set available to market participants:

1. Clear imagery allows investors to infer oil inventories before official announcements.
2. Cloud cover blocks this information.
3. Market participants become more uncertain about inventories.
4. Firms may hold more precautionary stocks.
5. Less new information enters trading decisions.
6. Oil returns and volatility subsequently change.

### 中文

论文并不是认为阴天直接减少石油供给或需求，而是认为云层改变了市场的信息环境：

1. 晴朗影像使投资者能够提前估计库存；
2. 云层阻断库存观测；
3. 市场对库存状态更加不确定；
4. 企业可能增加预防性库存；
5. 新信息进入市场的速度下降；
6. 未来收益率和波动率受到影响。

## Specific Implications for M2 Remote-Sensing Features / 对 M2 遥感变量的具体启示

### 1. Separate signal variables from quality variables

#### Economic or physical-activity signals

- `ntl_avg_rad_{aoi}`
- `ntl_sum_rad_{aoi}`
- `s2_storage_proxy_{aoi}`
- `refinery_activity_{aoi}`
- `port_activity_{aoi}`

These variables attempt to measure actual oil-related activity.

#### Observation-quality or availability variables

- `ntl_valid_obs_{aoi}`
- `s2_clear_obs_count_{aoi}`
- `cloud_fraction_{aoi}`
- `clear_coverage_ratio_{aoi}`
- `days_since_last_clear_obs_{aoi}`
- `max_observation_gap_{aoi}`

These variables measure how reliable and complete the remote-sensing signal is.

可以将 M2 变量明确分成：

\[
\text{Remote-sensing activity signal}
\]

和：

\[
\text{Remote-sensing observation quality}
\]

而不是把所有遥感指标作为同一类型的经济活动变量。

---

### 2. Potential model specification

\[
OilReturn_{t+h}
=
f(
M1_t,
RSActivity_t,
RSQuality_t,
M3_t,
M4_t
)
\]

A regression-style mechanism test could be:

\[
r_{t+h}
=
\alpha
+\beta_1RSActivity_t
+\beta_2RSInformationGap_t
+\gamma'MarketControls_t
+\varepsilon_{t+h}
\]

where:

\[
RSInformationGap_t
=
1-ClearCoverage_t
\]

or:

\[
RSInformationGap_t
=
\sum_iw_i(1-ClearCoverage_{i,t})
\]

---

### 3. Important distinction between Sentinel-2 and VIIRS

#### Sentinel-2

Sentinel-2 clear-observation counts are relatively close to the mechanism in this paper because cloud cover directly determines whether daytime optical features such as tanks, shadows and surface activity can be observed.

Therefore:

- `s2_clear_obs_count_{aoi}`
- `s2_cloud_fraction_{aoi}`
- `days_since_last_clear_s2_{aoi}`

may be interpreted as optical-observation availability or potential information-gap variables.

#### VIIRS nighttime lights

`ntl_valid_obs_{aoi}` should primarily be interpreted as:

- data-quality control;
- monthly-composite reliability;
- observation-coverage measure.

It should not automatically be labelled as a market inventory-information uncertainty proxy.

The paper's nighttime placebo test indicates that nighttime cloudiness does not share the same floating-roof inventory mechanism.

因此，Sentinel-2 无云观测次数与本文机制较接近，而 VIIRS 的 `valid_obs_count` 更适合首先定义为数据质量控制变量。

---

## Recommended Dissertation Tests / 建议在本项目中实施的检验

### Model ablation / 模块消融

| Model | Input features |
|---|---|
| B0 | Historical mean or random-walk benchmark |
| B1 | Lagged oil returns |
| M1 | Market, macroeconomic and oil-fundamental variables |
| M1 + M2 | Add remote-sensing variables |
| M1 + M3 | Add shipping and chokepoint variables |
| M1 + M4 | Add NLP, news and geopolitical variables |
| Full model | M1 + M2 + M3 + M4 |

The key M2 contribution is:

\[
\Delta Performance_{M2}
=
Performance(M1+M2)-Performance(M1)
\]

核心问题不是完整模型是否准确，而是遥感模块相对于传统基准增加了多少预测能力。

---

### Placebo tests / 安慰剂检验

1. Replace oil AOIs with non-oil industrial AOIs.
2. Shift AOI boundaries to nearby random locations.
3. Randomly shuffle remote-sensing dates.
4. Use irrelevant infrastructure categories.
5. Compare daylight optical availability with nighttime observation quality.
6. Test future remote-sensing variables against earlier oil returns.
7. Test whether effects are stronger around EIA announcements or high-uncertainty periods.

---

### Mechanism tests / 机制检验

Potential intermediate targets include:

- EIA crude-oil inventories;
- port calls;
- tanker counts;
- refinery utilisation;
- export volumes;
- oil-price volatility.

For example:

\[
RemoteSensing_t
\rightarrow
Inventory_{t+1}
\rightarrow
OilReturn_{t+2}
\]

or:

\[
RemoteSensing_t
\rightarrow
ShippingActivity_{t+1}
\rightarrow
OilReturn_{t+2}
\]

This would provide stronger economic interpretation than directly placing all variables into a black-box forecasting model.

## Limitations / 局限性

### 1. Limited geographical scope / 地理范围有限

The study covers only eight U.S. storage areas concentrated in PADD 2 and PADD 3.

It does not test whether the findings generalise to:

- global oil infrastructure;
- Middle Eastern storage hubs;
- European terminals;
- Brent crude oil;
- international shipping networks.

研究结果不能直接推广到全球石油供应链或 Brent 市场。

---

### 2. WTI-specific target / 仅研究 WTI

The main target is WTI spot returns, with WTI futures used as an extension.

The dissertation's Brent-focused design therefore represents an important external-validity extension.

---

### 3. Relatively short sample / 样本期较短

The main sample covers 2014–2021, providing only several hundred weekly observations.

This limits:

- the use of highly parameterised models;
- regime-specific analysis;
- generalisation across multiple commodity cycles;
- evaluation over long structural transitions.

---

### 4. Cloudiness is a proxy, not direct inventory / 云量并非实际库存

The main predictor measures whether inventory information can potentially be observed; it does not measure:

- actual tank fill levels;
- actual inventory changes;
- production;
- consumption;
- physical oil flows.

Therefore, the paper studies the availability of information rather than directly measuring supply and demand.

---

### 5. Market information availability is not directly observed / 未直接观察市场的信息使用情况

The paper assumes that sophisticated investors use satellite-based inventory estimates, but it does not directly observe:

- which investors purchased satellite data;
- when they accessed the data;
- how they incorporated the information into trades;
- the market share of satellite-informed investors.

Cloud cover is therefore an indirect proxy for market information availability.

---

### 6. Mainly linear forecasting models / 主要使用线性模型

The predictive framework is based mainly on linear OLS regressions.

It does not extensively model:

- nonlinear thresholds;
- interactions among storage areas;
- structural breaks;
- market regimes;
- complex temporal dependence;
- cross-modal feature interactions.

A multimodal ML framework can test whether the effect is nonlinear or state dependent.

---

### 7. Aggregation may overlook spatial heterogeneity / 聚合方式可能忽略空间异质性

The aggregate index is largely based on combining the eight area-level measures without fully modelling their different economic importance.

Storage areas may differ in:

- capacity;
- throughput;
- connection to pipelines;
- relevance to WTI pricing;
- investor monitoring intensity.

The dissertation could improve this by testing capacity-, throughput- or network-centrality-weighted aggregation.

---

### 8. Modest forecasting gains / 预测提升幅度有限

Although statistically significant, the out-of-sample \(R^2\) values are below approximately 1–1.1%.

This is meaningful in return forecasting but should not be interpreted as highly accurate prediction of weekly oil returns.

The practical importance depends on:

- transaction costs;
- model stability;
- data-acquisition costs;
- leverage constraints;
- real-time data availability.

---

### 9. Mechanism evidence is not fully causal / 机制证据并非严格因果识别

The placebo tests and control variables strengthen the proposed interpretation, but the study remains observational.

It cannot fully rule out all unobserved factors correlated with both cloud cover and future oil-market conditions.

---

### 10. Data reproducibility / 数据复现限制

The paper states that the datasets are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.

The complete tank-level dataset and processing pipeline are not provided as a directly downloadable public replication package, which may limit exact replication.

---

## Notes for Dissertation Integration / 论文整合建议

- Use this paper as a core reference for the direct connection between remote-sensing information availability and oil-return forecasting.
- Position it primarily in the literature-review section on remote sensing, alternative data and oil-market information.
- Use it to justify separating remote-sensing activity variables from observation-quality variables.
- Treat Sentinel-2 cloud-free observation measures as more directly comparable to the paper's mechanism.
- Treat VIIRS `valid_obs_count` primarily as a measurement-quality variable unless additional evidence supports a market-information interpretation.
- Replicate the paper's hierarchical spatial design: AOI-specific indicators first, aggregate factors second.
- Compare simple averages, capacity-weighted averages, PCA and model-based feature aggregation.
- Use recursive out-of-sample evaluation rather than random train–test splitting.
- Add Clark–West or Diebold–Mariano tests to assess whether M2 significantly improves the M1 baseline.
- Include non-oil AOIs, shuffled dates and irrelevant infrastructure as placebo tests.
- Test whether remote sensing predicts intermediate fundamentals such as inventories, shipping activity or refinery utilisation.
- Use SHAP interaction values to test whether remote-sensing information gaps become more important during high-volatility, high-GPR or major supply-disruption periods.
- Present the dissertation as an extension from one satellite-information proxy and one national market to a global, multimodal Brent forecasting framework.

## Critical Takeaway / 核心批判性结论

The paper should not be interpreted as evidence that every remote-sensing valid-observation count predicts oil prices.

Its supported mechanism is narrower:

\[
\text{Daytime optical observability of economically valuable oil information}
\rightarrow
\text{Short-horizon WTI return predictability}
\]

For this dissertation:

- Sentinel-2 cloud-free observations may represent information availability;
- VIIRS valid-observation counts should initially represent measurement reliability;
- the economic interpretation must be validated through mechanism and placebo tests.

不能将该文简单解释为“任何遥感有效观测次数都能预测油价”。它真正支持的是：

\[
\text{具有石油经济价值的白天光学信息是否可被观察}
\rightarrow
\text{短期 WTI 收益率变化}
\]

这一区分应在本项目的变量定义、模型解释和文献综述中明确保留。
