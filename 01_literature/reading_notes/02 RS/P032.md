## Research Question / 研究问题

### English

The paper asks two closely related questions:

1. Which satellite night-time lights product, DMSP-OLS or VIIRS DNB, provides a more reliable proxy for subnational economic activity?
2. Under which spatial, demographic, and sectoral conditions can night-time lights be used reliably in economic research?

The study therefore examines not only **which data product is better**, but also **where night-time lights work and where they fail**.

### 中文

本文主要回答两个相互关联的问题：

1. 在 DMSP-OLS 与 VIIRS DNB 两类夜间灯光数据中，哪一种能够更可靠地代理地方经济活动？
2. 在什么空间尺度、人口密度和产业结构条件下，夜间灯光数据才适合用于经济研究？

因此，这篇论文不仅比较“哪一种灯光数据更好”，还重点讨论：

> 夜间灯光在哪些地区和研究场景中有效，以及在哪些情况下可能失效。

## Research Motivation / 研究动机

### English

Night-time lights are widely used in economics as proxies for local GDP, urbanisation, regional development, poverty, and spatial inequality, particularly where official subnational statistics are unavailable or unreliable.

However, much of the economics literature has traditionally relied on DMSP-OLS data, despite several measurement problems:

- spatial blurring;
- coarse spatial resolution;
- top-coding and saturation in bright urban areas;
- lack of onboard calibration;
- limited dynamic range;
- inconsistencies between satellites and years;
- weak temporal comparability.

VIIRS DNB was designed as a calibrated radiometer and provides finer spatial resolution, a much wider dynamic range, and more consistent radiance measurements.

The paper investigates whether these technical improvements translate into better economic measurement.

### 中文

夜间灯光经常被经济学研究用来代理：

- 地区 GDP；
- 城市化水平；
- 地区发展程度；
- 贫困；
- 空间经济不平等。

这种方法在缺少可靠地方统计数据的发展中国家尤其常见。

但传统经济学文献大量依赖 DMSP-OLS，而 DMSP 存在明显的数据质量问题：

- 空间模糊；
- 空间分辨率较低；
- 明亮地区数值饱和和封顶；
- 缺少机载校准；
- 动态范围较窄；
- 不同卫星和年份之间缺乏一致性；
- 时间序列可比性较弱。

相比之下，VIIRS DNB 是经过校准的辐射计，具有更高的空间精度、更大的动态范围和更一致的辐射值。

因此，作者希望检验：

> VIIRS 在技术层面的改进，是否能够真正提高夜间灯光测量经济活动的能力。

## Night-Time Lights Products / 夜间灯光产品

| Characteristic | DMSP-OLS | VIIRS DNB |
|---|---|---|
| Historical coverage / 历史覆盖 | 1992–2013 | 2012 onward / 2012 年以后 |
| Measurement / 测量值 | Digital Number, 0–63 | Calibrated radiance / 校准辐射值 |
| Quantisation / 量化精度 | 6-bit | 14-bit |
| Approximate pixel size / 近似像元尺度 | Coarser; approximately 2.7 km before common resampling / 较粗 | Approximately 0.7 km / 约 0.7 km |
| Dynamic range / 动态范围 | Less than two orders of magnitude / 不足两个数量级 | Approximately seven orders of magnitude / 约七个数量级 |
| Saturation / 饱和问题 | Severe in bright areas / 明亮地区严重 | No comparable DMSP-style saturation / 基本不存在 DMSP 式饱和 |
| Calibration / 校准 | No consistent onboard calibration / 缺乏一致机载校准 | Calibrated radiometer / 经过校准 |
| Spatial blurring / 空间模糊 | Severe / 严重 | Substantially lower / 明显较低 |
| Fine-scale analysis / 小尺度研究 | Generally unreliable / 通常不可靠 | More suitable, subject to cleaning / 相对适合，但仍需清洗 |

- Primary-sector versus industrial and service-sector activity  
  第一产业与工业、服务业活动。

## Core Method / 核心方法

### 1. GDP–Lights Regression / GDP—灯光回归

The main specification is:

$$
\ln(GDP_{it})
=
\alpha
+
\beta \operatorname{IHS}(Lights_{it})
+
\delta_t
+
\varepsilon_{it}
$$

where:

- $GDP_{it}$ is real regional GDP;
- $Lights_{it}$ is the sum of night-time lights;
- $\delta_t$ is a year dummy;
- $\beta$ measures the elasticity-like relationship between lights and GDP.

主要模型为：

$$
\ln(GDP_{it})
=
\alpha
+
\beta \operatorname{IHS}(Lights_{it})
+
\delta_t
+
\varepsilon_{it}
$$

其中：

- $GDP_{it}$ 表示地区实际 GDP；
- $Lights_{it}$ 表示空间单元内的灯光总量；
- $\delta_t$ 表示年份虚拟变量；
- $\beta$ 衡量灯光与 GDP 之间的弹性关系。

The authors use the inverse hyperbolic sine transformation because some low-density areas have zero observed lights.

由于部分低密度地区观测到的灯光为零，作者对灯光变量使用 inverse hyperbolic sine，IHS，变换。

IHS behaves similarly to a logarithm for positive values while retaining zero observations.

IHS 对较大的正数与对数变换较为接近，同时能够保留零值观测。

### 2. Comparative Validation / 比较与验证

The paper compares:

- DMSP versus VIIRS;
- pooled versus year-by-year regressions;
- urban versus rural units;
- high-density versus low-density areas;
- coarse versus fine spatial aggregation;
- primary-sector versus industrial and service-sector GDP;
- cleaned versus uncleaned VIIRS monthly data.

论文比较了：

- DMSP 与 VIIRS；
- 合并回归与逐年横截面回归；
- 城市与农村地区；
- 高密度与低密度地区；
- 较粗与较细的空间尺度；
- 第一产业与工业、服务业 GDP；
- 清洗后与未经清洗的 VIIRS 月度数据。

### 3. Spatial Inequality Analysis / 空间不平等分析

The study uses:

- Gini coefficients;
- Theil indices;
- Lorenz curves;
- intra-city grid comparisons.

论文使用：

- Gini 系数；
- Theil 指数；
- Lorenz 曲线；
- 城市内部网格比较。

### 4. DMSP Top-Coding Correction / DMSP 封顶修正

The authors test Pareto-adjusted DMSP data designed to correct values top-coded at the maximum Digital Number of 63.

作者使用经过 Pareto 调整的 DMSP 数据，尝试修正最大值被封顶在 DN = 63 的问题。

### 5. Monthly VIIRS Cleaning Validation / 月度 VIIRS 清洗验证

Monthly VIIRS data are masked using a cleaned annual VIIRS composite to remove background noise and temporary light sources before being aggregated into annual measures.

作者利用经过清洗的年度 VIIRS 产品作为 mask，剔除背景噪声和短暂光源，再将月度数据聚合为年度指标。

## Model Type / 模型类型

- **Econometric model**: Log–log or IHS–log regression  
  **计量模型**：对数回归或 IHS—对数回归。
- **Comparison design**: Cross-product and cross-location validation  
  **比较设计**：不同产品、地区和空间尺度之间的验证。
- **Inequality analysis**: Gini, Theil, and Lorenz curves  
  **不平等分析**：Gini、Theil 和 Lorenz 曲线。
- **Not a machine-learning forecasting model**  
  **不是机器学习预测模型**。
- **No genuine rolling or out-of-sample forecasting exercise**  
  **没有开展真正的滚动或样本外时间序列预测**。
- **No causal interpretation is claimed**  
  **不主张灯光与 GDP 之间存在因果关系**。

## Key Findings / 核心发现

### 1. VIIRS generally outperforms DMSP  
### 1. VIIRS 整体上明显优于 DMSP

In the full Indonesian sample:

- the pooled DMSP elasticity is approximately -0.059 and statistically insignificant;
- the DMSP regression has an $R^2$ of approximately 0.01;
- the pooled VIIRS elasticity is approximately 0.179 and statistically significant;
- the VIIRS regression has an $R^2$ of approximately 0.05.

在印度尼西亚全部地区样本中：

- DMSP 的合并回归系数约为 -0.059，且不显著；
- DMSP 回归的 $R^2$ 约为 0.01；
- VIIRS 的合并回归系数约为 0.179，且显著为正；
- VIIRS 回归的 $R^2$ 约为 0.05。

The low overall fit partly reflects the fact that urban and rural areas have very different lights–GDP relationships.

总体拟合度较低的一个重要原因，是城市与农村地区的灯光—GDP 关系存在巨大差异。

---

### 2. VIIRS performs particularly well in cities  
### 2. VIIRS 在城市地区的表现尤其突出

For Indonesian Kota:

- DMSP elasticity is approximately 0.94;
- VIIRS elasticity is also approximately 0.94;
- DMSP $R^2$ is approximately 0.36;
- VIIRS $R^2$ is approximately 0.68.

对于印度尼西亚城市型 Kota：

- DMSP 弹性约为 0.94；
- VIIRS 弹性也约为 0.94；
- DMSP 的 $R^2$ 约为 0.36；
- VIIRS 的 $R^2$ 约为 0.68。

Although both products show a positive city-level relationship, the unexplained variation is approximately twice as large with DMSP.

尽管两种数据在城市中都与 GDP 正相关，但 DMSP 的未解释误差大约是 VIIRS 的两倍。

This indicates that DMSP can sometimes identify broad differences between cities but measures them with substantially more noise.

这说明 DMSP 有时能够识别城市之间的大致经济差异，但测量噪声明显更大。

---

### 3. Night-time lights are unreliable in low-density rural areas  
### 3. 夜间灯光不适合无条件代理低密度农村经济活动

For mainly rural Kabupaten:

- DMSP has a significantly negative relationship with GDP;
- VIIRS has a small positive but statistically imprecise relationship;
- both products have very low predictive fit.

对于以农村为主的 Kabupaten：

- DMSP 与 GDP 显著负相关；
- VIIRS 与 GDP 的关系较弱且统计上不精确；
- 两种产品的拟合度都很低。

After splitting regions by population density, night-time lights are negatively related to GDP in the lowest-density areas, including when VIIRS is used.

按照人口密度进一步划分后，即使使用 VIIRS，最低密度地区中的灯光与 GDP 也可能呈负相关。

The problem is therefore not only sensor quality. Many rural and agricultural activities do not generate sufficient night-time illumination to be observed reliably from satellites.

因此，问题不只是卫星传感器质量。很多农村和农业经济活动本身不会产生足够强的夜间灯光，无法被卫星可靠识别。

---

### 4. Night-time lights are more useful for industrial and service activity than for agriculture  
### 4. 夜间灯光更适合代理工业和服务业，而不是农业

The China analysis finds that night-time lights are poor proxies for primary-sector activity, including agriculture, forestry, and fishing.

中国数据表明，夜间灯光不适合代理农业、林业和渔业等第一产业活动。

The relationship is considerably stronger for the combined industrial and service sectors.

灯光与工业和服务业活动之间的关系明显更强。

This suggests that night-time lights primarily capture concentrated electricity use, urban infrastructure, industrial facilities, transport, and commercial activity rather than all forms of economic output.

这意味着夜间灯光主要反映：

- 集中用电；
- 城市基础设施；
- 工业设施；
- 交通运输；
- 商业活动。

它并不能代表所有类型的经济产出。

---

### 5. The advantage of VIIRS becomes larger at finer spatial scales  
### 5. 空间尺度越精细，VIIRS 相对于 DMSP 的优势越明显

For 288 Chinese prefectures:

- DMSP GDP regression $R^2$: approximately 0.62;
- VIIRS GDP regression $R^2$: approximately 0.76.

在中国 288 个地级行政单元中：

- DMSP 的 GDP 回归 $R^2$ 约为 0.62；
- VIIRS 的 GDP 回归 $R^2$ 约为 0.76。

At the finer county and district level in Chongqing:

- DMSP $R^2$: approximately 0.53;
- VIIRS $R^2$: approximately 0.81.

在更精细的重庆区县尺度：

- DMSP 的 $R^2$ 约为 0.53；
- VIIRS 的 $R^2$ 约为 0.81。

The gap between the products widens as the spatial unit becomes smaller.

随着空间单元变小，两种数据之间的表现差距进一步扩大。

Aggregation can partly average out measurement errors, whereas blurring and geolocation errors become especially damaging for small areas.

较大的空间聚合可以在一定程度上平均掉测量误差，而对于较小的区域，空间模糊和定位误差会造成更严重的影响。

---

### 6. Night-time lights are better for cross-sectional differences than short-term temporal changes  
### 6. 夜间灯光更擅长识别横截面差异，而不是短期时间变化

The regressions show that:

- between-unit $R^2$ dominates;
- within-unit $R^2$ is approximately zero;
- year dummy coefficients are generally insignificant.

回归结果表明：

- 地区之间的解释力明显高于地区内部的时间变化解释力；
- within-unit $R^2$ 接近零；
- 年份虚拟变量通常不显著。

Night-time lights are therefore better at identifying:

> which city or region is more economically active,

than at identifying:

> whether the same location experienced a small short-term increase in activity.

因此，夜间灯光更适合识别：

> 哪个城市或地区长期更活跃，

而不一定适合识别：

> 同一个地区在短期内是否发生了较小的经济活动变化。

---

### 7. DMSP substantially understates spatial inequality  
### 7. DMSP 会严重低估空间经济不平等

For all Indonesian spatial units, the Theil index is approximately:

- 1.42 using DMSP;
- 1.54 using Pareto-adjusted DMSP;
- 2.19 using VIIRS.

对于印度尼西亚全部地区，Theil 指数约为：

- DMSP：1.42；
- Pareto 修正后的 DMSP：1.54；
- VIIRS：2.19。

For urban areas, the estimated Gini coefficient is approximately:

- 0.58 using DMSP;
- 0.71 using VIIRS.

对于城市地区，灯光空间分布的 Gini 系数约为：

- DMSP：0.58；
- VIIRS：0.71。

DMSP compresses spatial variation because:

1. blurring spreads lights into surrounding darker areas;
2. top-coding prevents the brightest locations from recording their true intensity.

DMSP 会从两个方向压缩空间差异：

1. 空间模糊把亮区灯光扩散到周围暗区；
2. 数值封顶使最明亮地区无法记录真实亮度。

---

### 8. Correcting top-coding alone does not solve the DMSP problem  
### 8. 只修正饱和和封顶问题无法解决 DMSP 的主要缺陷

The Pareto adjustment closes only about one-sixth of the overall inequality gap between DMSP and VIIRS.

Pareto 修正只能弥补 DMSP 与 VIIRS 总体空间不平等差距的大约六分之一。

This implies that spatial blurring is at least as important as, and possibly more important than, top-coding.

这说明空间模糊问题至少与数值封顶同样重要，甚至可能更加严重。

A statistically adjusted DMSP product therefore cannot be assumed to reproduce the fine spatial information contained in VIIRS.

因此，即使对 DMSP 进行统计校正，也不能认为它能够恢复 VIIRS 所包含的精细空间信息。

---

### 9. DMSP obscures important intra-city features  
### 9. DMSP 会掩盖重要的城市内部空间结构

In the Jakarta DMSP image:

- approximately 82% of pixels have the maximum value of 63;
- approximately 17% have a value of 62.

在 Jakarta 的 DMSP 图像中：

- 约 82% 的像元为最高值 63；
- 约 17% 的像元为 62。

The city therefore appears as an almost undifferentiated block of light.

因此，整个城市几乎表现为一个没有内部差异的大型亮斑。

Even Pareto-adjusted DMSP fails to represent several important spatial features, including the brightness of the Tanjung Priok port.

即使使用 Pareto 修正后的 DMSP，仍然无法正确识别包括 Tanjung Priok 港口在内的重要空间特征。

VIIRS more clearly distinguishes:

- the central business district;
- parks and public squares;
- major transport axes;
- the port;
- smaller high-intensity locations.

VIIRS 能够更清楚地区分：

- 中央商务区；
- 公园和公共广场；
- 主要交通轴线；
- 港口；
- 局部高亮设施。

---

### 10. Monthly VIIRS data require careful cleaning  
### 10. VIIRS 月度数据必须经过严格清洗

For Chongqing, the relationship between monthly-derived VIIRS estimates and the official cleaned annual composite has:

- $R^2 \approx 0.86$ when monthly data are unmasked;
- $R^2 \approx 0.98$ when monthly data are masked.

在重庆样本中，由月度 VIIRS 数据构造的年度指标与正式年度产品之间：

- 未使用 mask 时，$R^2$ 约为 0.86；
- 使用 mask 后，$R^2$ 约为 0.98。

For predicting GDP:

- cleaned annual composite or masked monthly estimate: $R^2 \approx 0.79$;
- unmasked monthly estimate: $R^2 \approx 0.64$.

在预测 GDP 时：

- 正式年度产品或经过 mask 的月度聚合值：$R^2$ 约为 0.79；
- 未清洗的月度聚合值：$R^2$ 约为 0.64。

This demonstrates that noise filtering and the treatment of temporary light sources materially affect empirical results.

这说明背景噪声、短暂光源和异常值处理会实质性影响最终的实证结果，而不是无关紧要的数据预处理细节。

| **M2 data selection / M2 数据选择** | The paper provides strong methodological support for using VIIRS rather than DMSP for ports, refineries, storage terminals, oil export facilities, and other relatively small infrastructure AOIs. / 论文为港口、炼厂、储油区和石油出口终端等小尺度 AOI 优先使用 VIIRS 提供了直接的方法依据。 |
| **Fine-scale AOI design / 精细 AOI 设计** | The widening VIIRS–DMSP performance gap at smaller spatial scales shows that DMSP is unsuitable for facility-level monitoring. / 空间尺度越小，DMSP 的误差越严重，因此不适合用于设施级监测。 |
| **Feature meaning / 变量含义** | NTL should be interpreted as a proxy for concentrated lighting, infrastructure operation, industrial activity, and transport rather than total economic or oil-market activity. / NTL 更接近集中照明、基础设施运行、工业和交通活动代理，而不是完整的石油市场活动指标。 |
| **Temporal feature engineering / 时间特征工程** | Because NTL performs better cross-sectionally than temporally, the project should emphasise within-AOI anomalies and changes rather than only raw radiance levels. / 由于 NTL 对横截面差异的解释强于短期变化，项目应重点使用站点内部异常值和变化率，而不是仅使用绝对亮度。 |
| **Data cleaning / 数据清洗** | Cloud, stray-light, background-noise, fire, and temporary-light treatment must be part of the formal feature pipeline. / 云、杂散光、背景噪声、火灾和短暂光源处理应成为正式特征工程的一部分。 |
| **Gas-flare treatment / 火炬燃烧处理** | Unlike GDP studies, oil research should not automatically discard all gas-flare signals because flaring may contain information about extraction or refining activity. / 与 GDP 研究不同，石油项目不能机械删除所有火炬燃烧信号，因为它们可能反映开采或炼化活动。 |
| **Validation strategy / 代理变量验证** | NTL features should first be validated against AIS vessel movements, refinery utilisation, port calls, production, or inventory data. / 在进入油价模型前，应先检验 NTL 与 AIS、炼厂利用率、港口挂靠、产量或库存之间的关系。 |
| **Sensor discontinuity / 传感器断点** | DMSP and VIIRS should not be directly concatenated into a single unadjusted series. / 不应把 DMSP 与 VIIRS 原始值直接拼接为一条连续时间序列。 |
| **Sample-period design / 样本期设计** | A full-period model without VIIRS and a separate VIIRS-era model should be estimated. / 应分别建立不含 VIIRS 的完整时期模型，以及 VIIRS 可用期的增量模型。 |
| **Ablation testing / 消融实验** | The predictive value of M2 should be tested by comparing otherwise identical models with and without NTL over the same evaluation period. / 应在完全相同的训练和测试时期内比较加入与不加入 NTL 的模型，隔离 M2 的真实预测贡献。 |
| **Interpretability / 可解释性** | SHAP or permutation importance can identify whether specific AOIs contribute stable information, but high importance alone does not prove a valid physical mechanism. / SHAP 或 permutation importance 可以识别重要 AOI，但高重要性本身不能证明变量具有真实石油活动机制。 |

## Specific Implications for M2 Remote-Sensing Features  
## 对 M2 遥感特征的具体启示

### 1. Prefer VIIRS for the main model  
### 1. 主模型优先使用 VIIRS

The main remote-sensing experiment should be based on VIIRS-era data.

遥感主实验应以 VIIRS 可用时期为基础。

DMSP may be retained for:

- historical descriptive analysis;
- large-scale annual robustness checks;
- separate harmonisation experiments.

DMSP 可以用于：

- 历史描述分析；
- 大尺度年度稳健性检验；
- 单独的跨传感器协调实验。

However, it should not be treated as equivalent to VIIRS for small facility-level AOIs.

但不应把它视为与 VIIRS 等价的小尺度设施监测数据。

---

### 2. Avoid relying only on raw NTL levels  
### 2. 不要只依赖 NTL 绝对值

Recommended temporal features include:

- `ntl_mom_change`;
- `ntl_yoy_change`;
- `ntl_rolling_zscore`;
- `ntl_seasonal_anomaly`;
- `ntl_deviation_from_site_baseline`;
- `lit_pixel_share_change`;
- `high_radiance_area_change`.

建议构造：

- `ntl_mom_change`：环比变化；
- `ntl_yoy_change`：同比变化；
- `ntl_rolling_zscore`：滚动标准化异常；
- `ntl_seasonal_anomaly`：相对季节性基准的异常；
- `ntl_deviation_from_site_baseline`：相对站点历史基准的偏离；
- `lit_pixel_share_change`：亮像元占比变化；
- `high_radiance_area_change`：高辐射区域面积变化。

A site-standardised anomaly can be defined as:

$$
NTL\_anomaly_{i,t}
=
\frac{NTL_{i,t}-\mu_{i,t}^{\text{historical}}}
{\sigma_{i,t}^{\text{historical}}}
$$

站点标准化异常值可以定义为：

$$
NTL\_anomaly_{i,t}
=
\frac{NTL_{i,t}-\mu_{i,t}^{\text{historical}}}
{\sigma_{i,t}^{\text{historical}}}
$$

This compares each facility with its own historical baseline rather than comparing the absolute brightness of structurally different facilities.

这种指标比较的是每个设施相对于自身历史状态的异常，而不是直接比较结构完全不同的 AOI 绝对亮度。

---

### 3. Use multiple spatial summaries  
### 3. 使用多种空间统计指标

Potential AOI-level features include:

| Feature | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| `ntl_sum_core` | Total radiance within the facility core / 核心设施内总辐射 |
| `ntl_mean_core` | Mean radiance / 平均辐射 |
| `ntl_median_core` | Median radiance, less sensitive to extremes / 中位数辐射 |
| `ntl_p90_core` | Bright-facility intensity / 高亮设施强度 |
| `ntl_p95_core` | Extreme bright-pixel signal / 极高亮像元信号 |
| `lit_pixel_share` | Share of pixels above an activity threshold / 超过活动阈值的像元占比 |
| `high_intensity_area` | Area of high-radiance pixels / 高辐射区域面积 |
| `ntl_spatial_cv` | Internal spatial heterogeneity / AOI 内部空间异质性 |
| `ntl_core_minus_buffer` | Facility brightness relative to surrounding background / 核心设施相对周边背景亮度 |
| `valid_obs_count` | Number of valid observations / 有效观测数量 |
| `cloud_free_fraction` | Share of cloud-free observations / 无云观测比例 |
| `ntl_age_days` | Age of the latest available NTL observation / 最新可用 NTL 距预测日的天数 |

Using several summaries is preferable because:

- sums are affected by AOI size;
- means can be diluted by dark pixels;
- percentiles capture bright operational cores;
- lit-area measures capture the extensive margin;
- core–buffer differences reduce contamination from surrounding cities.

使用多种指标的原因是：

- 总和容易受到 AOI 面积影响；
- 平均值可能被大量暗像元稀释；
- 高分位数可以捕捉设施核心亮区；
- 亮区面积可以衡量活动范围；
- 核心区与缓冲区差值可以减弱城市背景灯光污染。

### 4. Validate the proxy mechanism before forecasting oil prices  
### 4. 在预测油价前先验证代理机制

The proposed causal-information chain is:

$$
NTL\ change
\rightarrow
oil\ infrastructure\ activity
\rightarrow
supply,\ inventory,\ or\ shipping\ information
\rightarrow
oil\ price
$$

建议验证的逻辑链为：

$$
NTL变化
\rightarrow
石油基础设施活动
\rightarrow
供给、库存或航运信息
\rightarrow
油价
$$

Each link should be tested rather than assumed.

每一段关系都需要验证，不能直接假设成立。

#### Port AOIs / 港口 AOI

Validate NTL against:

- AIS vessel counts;
- tanker counts;
- port calls;
- vessel dwell time;
- anchorage activity;
- tanker departures.

将 NTL 与以下指标验证：

- AIS 船舶数量；
- 油轮数量；
- 港口挂靠次数；
- 船舶停留时间；
- 锚泊区活动；
- 油轮离港数量。

#### Refinery AOIs / 炼厂 AOI

Validate NTL against:

- refinery utilisation;
- throughput;
- maintenance and shutdown records;
- flare activity;
- petroleum-product output.

将 NTL 与以下指标验证：

- 炼厂利用率；
- 原油加工量；
- 检修和停产记录；
- 火炬燃烧活动；
- 成品油产量。

#### Storage AOIs / 储油区 AOI

Validate NTL against:

- reported inventory changes;
- tank-level or tank-shadow estimates;
- loading and unloading activity;
- nearby tanker movements.

将 NTL 与以下指标验证：

- 公布的库存变化；
- 油罐液位或阴影估计；
- 装卸活动；
- 周边油轮移动。

### 5. Separate oil-related flaring from irrelevant noise  
### 5. 将石油相关火炬燃烧与无关噪声分开

In general economic applications, gas flares are often treated as unwanted temporary light sources.

在一般经济研究中，gas flare 经常被当作需要删除的短暂异常光源。

For this oil-market project, flaring may contain economically meaningful information.

但在石油项目中，火炬燃烧可能包含有意义的活动信息。

Possible separate variables include:

- `flare_radiance`;
- `flare_active_pixel_count`;
- `flare_duration`;
- `flare_anomaly`;
- `flare_site_count`.

可以单独构造：

- `flare_radiance`：火炬辐射值；

- stray light;
- cloud contamination;
- extreme single-pixel noise;

通常应过滤或控制：

- 野火；

## Limitations / 局限性

### 1. Not an oil-price forecasting study  
### 1. 不是油价预测研究

The paper studies regional GDP and spatial inequality, not crude-oil prices.

论文研究的是地区 GDP 和空间不平等，而不是原油价格。

It does not directly demonstrate that VIIRS variables improve Brent or WTI forecasts.

它不能直接证明 VIIRS 变量能够提高 Brent 或 WTI 的预测精度。

---

### 2. Primarily a proxy-validation study rather than a genuine forecasting exercise  
### 2. 主要是代理变量验证，而不是真正的预测实验

The paper uses regression fit and explanatory power rather than:

- rolling-origin forecasting;
- time-series cross-validation;
- strict out-of-sample evaluation;
- forecast-comparison tests.

论文主要比较回归拟合度和代理能力，没有使用：

- 滚动窗口预测；
- 时间序列交叉验证；
- 严格样本外测试；
- 预测模型显著性比较。

The word “predict” in the paper mostly refers to explaining or estimating GDP from lights, not forecasting future GDP.

论文中的 “predict” 主要是指利用灯光估计或解释 GDP，而不是预测未来 GDP。

---

### 3. Incomplete temporal overlap between DMSP and VIIRS  
### 3. DMSP 与 VIIRS 的核心样本年份没有完全重合

The main Indonesian comparison uses:

- DMSP for 2011–2012;
- VIIRS for 2015–2016.

印度尼西亚核心比较使用：

- 2011—2012 年 DMSP；
- 2015—2016 年 VIIRS。

Differences may therefore partly reflect changes across years, although the authors conduct a supplementary overlapping-year test for 2013.

因此，两种产品的差异可能部分混合了年份差异，尽管作者使用 2013 年重叠期进行了补充验证。

---

### 4. Annual and cross-sectional focus  
### 4. 主要基于年度和横截面数据

The study does not evaluate weekly, daily, or intraday NTL features.

论文没有研究周频、日频或日内灯光特征。

Its results cannot automatically justify converting monthly NTL into a true weekly signal.

其结论不能直接支持把月度 NTL 解释为真实的周频变化信号。

---

### 5. Context-dependent meaning of night-time lights  
### 5. 夜间灯光含义具有场景依赖性

The same radiance value may reflect:

- permanent infrastructure;
- household lighting;
- road lighting;
- industrial production;
- ships;
- fishing activity;
- fires;
- gas flaring;
- temporary construction.

相同的辐射值可能来自：

- 固定基础设施；
- 居民照明；
- 道路照明；
- 工业生产；
- 船舶；
- 渔业活动；
- 火灾；
- 火炬燃烧；
- 临时施工。

The economic interpretation of NTL must therefore be established separately for each AOI type.

因此，必须针对每种 AOI 类型单独验证 NTL 的实际含义。

---

### 6. Administrative-unit sums may not directly transfer to facility AOIs  
### 6. 行政区灯光总和不能直接套用到设施 AOI

The paper mainly aggregates lights within administrative boundaries.

论文主要在行政区边界内汇总灯光。

For ports, refineries, and storage facilities:

- AOI size;
- buffer selection;
- nearby urban lighting;
- coastline and water pixels;
- overlapping facilities

may substantially affect the extracted features.

对于港口、炼厂和储油区：

- AOI 面积；
- 缓冲区选择；
- 周边城市灯光；
- 海岸线和水域像元；
- 相邻设施重叠

都可能显著影响变量。

---

### 7. The masking strategy may remove economically meaningful temporary activity  
### 7. Mask 方法可能删除具有经济意义的临时活动

The annual-product mask improves measurement consistency, but a light source absent from the reference annual image may not always be noise.

年度产品 mask 可以提高数据一致性，但某个未出现在参考年度图像中的灯光，并不一定都是噪声。

In oil infrastructure, temporary lighting may reflect:

- loading operations;
- maintenance;
- emergency production;
- drilling;
- flaring;
- temporary vessel concentration.

在石油基础设施中，临时灯光可能反映：

- 装卸作业；
- 检修；
- 紧急生产；
- 钻井；
- 火炬燃烧；
- 临时船舶聚集。

The filtering rule should therefore be adapted to the oil-market context.

因此，清洗规则必须根据石油市场场景进行调整。

---

### 8. No causal identification  
### 8. 不提供因果识别

The lights–GDP regressions do not imply that lights cause economic output.

灯光—GDP 回归不意味着灯光导致经济产出。

Similarly, a correlation between NTL and oil prices would not itself prove that physical oil-market activity caused the price movement.

同样，NTL 与油价相关也不能单独证明实体石油活动导致了价格变化。

## Suggested Literature Review Paragraph / 可用于文献综述的表述

Gibson et al. (2021) systematically compare DMSP-OLS and VIIRS DNB night-time light data as proxies for subnational economic activity. Using regional evidence from Indonesia, China, and South Africa, they find that VIIRS generally provides higher predictive power and lower measurement noise, particularly in cities, high-density regions, and small spatial units. The weaker performance of DMSP is attributed to spatial blurring, top-coding, limited dynamic range, lack of calibration, and inconsistencies between satellites and years. However, the authors also show that even VIIRS performs poorly in low-density and agriculture-dominated regions, indicating that the suitability of night-time lights depends on both sensor quality and the nature of the underlying activity.

Gibson 等人（2021）系统比较了 DMSP-OLS 和 VIIRS DNB 夜间灯光数据作为地方经济活动代理变量的表现。基于印度尼西亚、中国和南非的地区数据，研究发现 VIIRS 通常具有更高的解释能力和更低的测量噪声，特别是在城市、高人口密度地区和较小空间单元中。DMSP 表现较差主要源于空间模糊、数值封顶、动态范围有限、缺乏校准以及不同卫星和年份之间的不一致。但作者同时发现，即使使用 VIIRS，夜间灯光在低人口密度和农业主导地区仍然表现较差，说明 NTL 的适用性不仅取决于传感器质量，也取决于被测经济活动的性质。

For the oil-price forecasting project, this study provides methodological support for using VIIRS to construct remote-sensing indicators for concentrated oil infrastructure, including ports, refineries, storage terminals, and export facilities. More importantly, it suggests that cross-sectional differences in brightness should not be interpreted automatically as evidence that short-term changes in radiance measure operational changes. The remote-sensing module should therefore focus on quality-controlled within-AOI anomalies, changes in lit area, high-radiance pixels, and core–buffer contrasts. These features should be validated against AIS movements, refinery utilisation, inventories, production, or port activity before their incremental contribution to out-of-sample oil-price forecasting is evaluated.

对于石油价格预测项目，本文为使用 VIIRS 构建港口、炼厂、储油终端和出口设施等集中型石油基础设施的遥感指标提供了方法依据。更重要的是，论文说明地点之间的长期亮度差异不能自动证明短期辐射变化能够衡量设施运行变化。因此，M2 遥感模块应重点构建经过质量控制的 AOI 内部异常值、亮区面积变化、高辐射像元和核心区—缓冲区差值，并首先使用 AIS、炼厂利用率、库存、产量或港口活动验证这些变量，再检验其对油价样本外预测的增量贡献。

## One-Sentence Summary / 一句话总结

**English:**
