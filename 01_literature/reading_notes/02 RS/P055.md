## Research Objective / 研究目标

### English

This paper develops a method for estimating the physical volume of large cylindrical oil tanks from a single high-resolution optical satellite image.

The method uses two types of geometric information:

1. The external crescent-shaped shadow cast by the tank is used to estimate tank height.
2. The circular boundary of the tank roof is used to estimate tank radius.

The final structural volume is calculated by approximating the tank as a cylinder:

\[
V=\pi r^2h
\]

The study is motivated by the difficulty of obtaining complete and internationally comparable information on oil-storage infrastructure.

### 中文

本文提出了一种利用单期高分辨率光学卫星影像估算大型圆柱形油罐物理容积的方法。

方法使用两类几何信息：

1. 利用油罐外壁投射在地面上的月牙形阴影估算油罐高度；
2. 利用罐顶的圆形边界估算油罐半径。

最后将油罐近似为圆柱体，并计算其结构容积：

\[
V=\pi r^2h
\]

研究的主要动机是：全球油罐数量、尺寸和储存容量数据通常不完整，也缺少统一、可比较的公开数据库。

> **Important distinction / 重要区别**
>
> The paper estimates nominal or structural tank capacity, not current oil inventory, liquid level, or floating-roof fill ratio.
>
> 本文估算的是油罐的理论结构容量，不是当前实际库存、液面高度或浮顶油罐充填率。

## Core Method / 核心方法

### 1. Image preprocessing / 影像预处理

The original satellite images are processed through:

- Radiometric correction;
- Atmospheric correction;
- Geometric correction;
- Panchromatic–multispectral image fusion;
- Area-of-interest cropping.

原始卫星影像依次经过：

- 辐射校正；
- 大气校正；
- 几何校正；
- 全色与多光谱影像融合；
- 研究区裁剪。

The fusion step combines the spatial detail of the panchromatic image with the spectral information of the multispectral image.

影像融合的目的是同时保留全色影像的空间细节和多光谱影像的颜色、光谱信息。

---

### 2. HSV-based shadow enhancement / 基于 HSV 的阴影增强

The fused RGB image is transformed into the HSV colour space:

- \(H\): hue;
- \(S\): saturation;
- \(V\): brightness or value.

融合后的 RGB 影像被转换到 HSV 色彩空间：

- \(H\)：色相；
- \(S\)：饱和度；
- \(V\)：明度。

Tank shadows generally have relatively low brightness and distinctive hue characteristics. The authors construct the following ratio image:

\[
R_{\text{shadow}}=\frac{H+1}{V+1}
\]

油罐阴影通常具有较低的明度和相对特殊的色相特征。作者构造以下比值影像：

\[
R_{\text{shadow}}=\frac{H+1}{V+1}
\]

This ratio strengthens dark shadow regions while helping distinguish them from other surface objects.

该比值可以增强低亮度阴影区域，并帮助将阴影与其他深色地物区分开。

---

### 3. Shadow segmentation / 阴影分割

The shadow extraction process contains three major steps:

1. Otsu automatic thresholding;
2. Connected-component area filtering;
3. Morphological closing with a \(3\times3\) structural element.

阴影提取包括三个主要步骤：

1. Otsu 自动阈值分割；
2. 基于连通区域面积的噪声过滤；
3. 使用 \(3\times3\) 结构元素进行形态学闭运算。

The Otsu method separates potential shadow pixels from non-shadow pixels. Area filtering removes small isolated noise, while morphological closing fills gaps and produces more continuous crescent-shaped shadow boundaries.

Otsu 方法将阴影候选像素与非阴影像素分离；面积过滤用于删除小型孤立噪声；形态学闭运算用于填补缺口并形成更加连续的月牙形阴影轮廓。

---

### 4. Shadow contour extraction / 阴影轮廓提取

The authors use the OpenCV `findContours()` function to extract the external contour of each tank shadow.

作者使用 OpenCV 的 `findContours()` 函数提取每个油罐阴影的外部轮廓。

The crescent-shaped contour is divided into:

- Upper shadow edge;
- Lower shadow edge;
- Left and right endpoints.

月牙形轮廓被划分为：

- 阴影上边缘；
- 阴影下边缘；
- 左右端点。

This is necessary because oil-tank shadows differ from the approximately rectangular shadows commonly used in conventional building-height studies.

这一处理非常重要，因为油罐阴影呈月牙形，与传统建筑物测高中常见的近似矩形阴影不同。

---

### 5. Sub-pixel shadow-length estimation / 亚像元阴影长度估算

For each candidate point on the lower shadow edge, the algorithm searches for a corresponding point on the upper edge along the direction of the solar rays.

算法以阴影下边缘的候选点为起点，沿太阳光线方向在阴影上边缘搜索相应点。

The pixel-based Euclidean distance is:

\[
D(p,q)=\sqrt{(s-x)^2+(t-y)^2}
\]

The ground shadow length is:

\[
S=D\times k
\]

where:

- \(D\) is the image distance in pixels;
- \(k\) is the ground resolution in metres per pixel;
- \(S\) is the physical shadow length.

其中：

- \(D\) 是以像素表示的影像距离；
- \(k\) 是每个像素对应的地面距离；
- \(S\) 是实际阴影长度。

The authors use sub-pixel subdivision to improve edge-location precision. A large number of possible upper-edge and lower-edge combinations are measured.

作者采用亚像元细分定位提高阴影边缘测量精度，并计算大量上下边缘候选点之间的阴影长度。

Three summary methods are evaluated:

- Maximum shadow length;
- Mean shadow length;
- Median shadow length.

文章比较了三种汇总方式：

- 最大阴影长度；
- 平均阴影长度；
- 阴影长度中位数。

The median method is found to be the most accurate and robust because it is less sensitive to incorrect edge pixels and extreme observations.

结果表明，中位数法最准确、最稳健，因为它较少受到错误边缘像素和极端值影响。

---

### 6. Tank-height estimation / 油罐高度估算

Under the assumptions of flat terrain, vertical tank walls and a complete visible shadow, tank height is calculated using:

\[
h=S\tan\alpha
\]

where:

- \(h\) is tank height;
- \(S\) is ground shadow length;
- \(\alpha\) is solar elevation angle.

在地面平坦、罐壁垂直且阴影完整可见的假设下，油罐高度通过以下公式计算：

\[
h=S\tan\alpha
\]

其中：

- \(h\) 为油罐高度；
- \(S\) 为地面阴影长度；
- \(\alpha\) 为太阳高度角。

The method also requires consideration of the relative solar and satellite azimuths, because the observation geometry determines whether the full shadow can be seen.

由于太阳与卫星的相对方位会影响阴影是否能够被完整观测，因此方法还需要考虑太阳方位角和卫星方位角。

---

### 7. Tank-radius estimation / 油罐半径估算

The top of a cylindrical tank appears approximately circular in the satellite image. The authors use the OpenCV `HoughCircles()` function to identify the circle and estimate its radius.

圆柱形油罐的顶部在卫星影像中近似为圆形。作者使用 OpenCV 的 `HoughCircles()` 函数识别圆形并估算半径。

The circle is represented as:

\[
(x-c_1)^2+(y-c_2)^2=r^2
\]

where:

- \((c_1,c_2)\) is the centre of the tank roof;
- \(r\) is its radius.

其中：

- \((c_1,c_2)\) 是罐顶圆心；
- \(r\) 是罐顶半径。

The Hough transform searches the parameter space for the most likely circle through a voting mechanism.

Hough 变换通过参数空间投票机制寻找最可能的圆心和半径。

---

### 8. Tank-volume calculation / 油罐容积计算

After obtaining tank height and radius, structural volume is estimated as:

\[
V=\pi r^2h
\]

在得到油罐高度和半径后，油罐结构容积被估算为：

\[
V=\pi r^2h
\]

For an oil-storage site containing multiple tanks, total theoretical capacity can be estimated as:

\[
C_{\text{site}}=\sum_{j=1}^{N}\pi r_j^2h_j
\]

对于包含多个油罐的储油基地，总理论容量可以估算为：

\[
C_{\text{site}}=\sum_{j=1}^{N}\pi r_j^2h_j
\]

This represents geometric or nominal capacity rather than actual usable capacity or current stored oil.

该结果代表几何容量或名义容量，不等于实际可运营容量，也不等于当前储油量。

## Evaluation Metrics / 评价指标

### Shadow extraction / 阴影提取

The study uses recall and precision-like shadow-detection measures:

\[
C_F=\frac{TP}{TP+FN}
\]

\[
C_A=\frac{TP}{TP+FN+FP}
\]

where:

- \(TP\): correctly identified shadow pixels;
- \(FP\): non-shadow pixels incorrectly identified as shadow;
- \(FN\): shadow pixels incorrectly identified as non-shadow.

其中：

- \(TP\)：被正确识别的阴影像素；
- \(FP\)：被错误识别为阴影的非阴影像素；
- \(FN\)：被错误识别为非阴影的阴影像素。

### Height, radius and volume / 高度、半径和容积

The authors evaluate estimates using:

- Absolute error;
- Relative error;
- Root mean squared error for tank height.

作者使用以下指标评估估算结果：

- 绝对误差；
- 相对误差；
- 油罐高度的均方根误差。

## Key Findings / 主要发现

### 1. The proposed shadow-extraction method substantially improves precision  
### 1. 所提出的阴影提取方法显著提高了精确率

| Method / 方法 | Recall / 召回率 | Precision / 精确率 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Histogram threshold / 直方图阈值法 | 95.73% | 60.07% |
| Morphological method / 传统形态学法 | 96.20% | 54.02% |
| Proposed method / 本文方法 | **96.95%** | **96.05%** |

Traditional methods detect most true shadows but also incorrectly classify many dark surfaces as shadows.

传统方法虽然能够识别大部分真实阴影，但容易将深色地面、水体或其他暗色区域错误识别为阴影。

The HSV ratio, area filtering and morphological-processing pipeline greatly reduces these false detections.

HSV 比值、面积过滤与形态学处理组合显著减少了误识别。

---

### 2. The sub-pixel median method is the best shadow-length estimator  
### 2. 亚像元中位数法是表现最好的阴影长度估算方法

Tank-height RMSE values reported for the five methods are:

| Method / 方法 | Height RMSE / 高度 RMSE |
|---|---:|
| Pixel-number method / 像素数量法 | 5.58 m |
| Direct manual measurement / 人工直接测量法 | 0.53 m |
| Median method / 中位数法 | **0.23 m** |
| Maximum method / 最大值法 | 3.63 m |
| Mean method / 平均值法 | 0.90 m |

The median method is less affected by contour noise and extreme shadow-length observations.

中位数法不容易受到轮廓噪声和极端阴影长度的影响。

---

### 3. Tank height can be estimated accurately in an ideal setting  
### 3. 在较理想场景下，油罐高度可以被较准确地估算

For the median method:

- Mean absolute height error: approximately 0.238 m;
- Relative height error: within approximately 1.15%;
- Height RMSE: approximately 0.23 m.

中位数法的结果为：

- 高度平均绝对误差约为 0.238 m；
- 高度相对误差约控制在 1.15% 以内；
- 高度 RMSE 约为 0.23 m。

---

### 4. Hough circle detection estimates tank radius accurately but misses some tanks  
### 4. Hough 圆检测能够准确估算半径，但未识别全部油罐

Among the 24 tanks, 22 were successfully identified for radius estimation.

在 24 个油罐中，共有 22 个成功完成罐顶圆形识别和半径估算。

Reported radius results include:

- Mean estimated radius: approximately 39.75 m;
- Actual radius: approximately 40 m;
- Mean absolute error: approximately 0.277 m;
- Mean relative error: approximately 0.69%.

半径估算结果包括：

- 平均估算半径约为 39.75 m；
- 实际半径约为 40 m；
- 平均绝对误差约为 0.277 m；
- 平均相对误差约为 0.69%。

---

### 5. Tank structural volume is estimated with relatively low error  
### 5. 油罐结构容积的估算误差相对较低

The reported tank-volume errors are:

- Absolute error: approximately 416–3,050 m³;
- Relative error: approximately 0.38%–2.78%.

文章报告的油罐容积误差为：

- 绝对误差约为 416–3,050 m³；
- 相对误差约为 0.38%–2.78%。

These results demonstrate the technical feasibility of measuring large oil-tank dimensions from sub-metre optical satellite imagery.

这些结果说明，使用亚米级光学卫星影像测量大型油罐尺寸在技术上具有可行性。

## Main Contribution / 主要贡献

### English

The paper makes four main contributions:

1. It adapts shadow-based building-height estimation to crescent-shaped oil-tank shadows.
2. It introduces a sub-pixel median method that is more robust than maximum, mean or pixel-count methods.
3. It combines shadow-based height estimation with Hough-based roof-radius detection.
4. It provides a complete workflow for converting high-resolution imagery into estimates of oil-storage infrastructure capacity.

### 中文

本文的主要贡献包括：

1. 将基于阴影的建筑物测高方法扩展到月牙形油罐阴影；

3. 将阴影测高与基于 Hough 变换的罐顶半径检测结合；

## Limitations / 局限性

### 1. Structural capacity is not actual inventory

### 1. 结构容量不等于实际库存

The estimated volume represents the maximum geometric capacity of the tank.

估算结果代表油罐的最大几何容量。

It does not reveal:

* Current liquid level;
* Current stored volume;
* Weekly inventory change;
* Inflow or outflow;
* Floating-roof position;
* Available spare capacity.

它无法直接得到：

* 当前液面高度；
* 当前实际储油量；
* 周度库存变化；
* 石油流入或流出；
* 浮顶位置；
* 剩余可用容量。

Therefore:

[
\text{structural capacity}
\neq
\text{fill level}
\neq
\text{actual inventory}
]

因此：

[
\text{油罐结构容量}
\neq
\text{充填率}
\neq
\text{实际库存}
]

---

### 2. The paper is not a floating-roof fill-level study

### 2. 本文不是浮顶油罐液位研究

The analysed shadow is the external shadow cast by the entire tank wall onto the ground.

本文分析的是整个油罐外壁投射到地面上的外部阴影。

It is not the internal shadow between the tank wall and a floating roof.

它不是罐壁与浮顶之间形成的内部阴影。

Consequently, the paper cannot be used as direct methodological support for variables such as:

```text
frt_fill_level_fujairah
frt_fill_level_rotterdam
```

这些变量需要专门的浮顶油罐内部阴影、立体几何或 SAR 液位估算文献支持。

---

### 3. Very high spatial resolution is required

### 3. 对空间分辨率要求很高

The experiment uses a 0.81 m panchromatic image.

实验使用了 0.81 m 全色影像。

This resolution allows the algorithm to observe:

* Detailed crescent-shaped shadow edges;
* Tank-roof circular boundaries;
* Small differences in shadow length;
* Sub-pixel geometric positions.

该分辨率能够观察：

* 细致的月牙形阴影边缘；
* 罐顶圆形边界；
* 较小的阴影长度差异；
* 亚像元几何位置。

A 10 m Sentinel-2 image is unlikely to reproduce the same single-tank geometric measurements or reported accuracy.

10 m Sentinel-2 影像很难复现相同的单罐几何测量和论文所报告的精度。

Sentinel-2 may still support coarse storage-site detection or AOI-level change indicators, but this would be a different application.

Sentinel-2 仍可能用于粗粒度储罐区识别或 AOI 变化指标，但这与本文的单罐精确测量方法不同。

---

### 4. Single-site and single-image validation

### 4. 仅在单一基地和单期影像上验证

The method is tested using one satellite image from one Chinese oil-storage base.

方法仅使用一个中国储油基地的一期卫星影像进行测试。

It is not systematically validated across:

* Countries;
* Climate zones;
* Surface materials;
* Tank designs;
* Satellite sensors;
* Seasons;
* Solar geometries;
* Complex industrial environments.

文章没有系统验证不同：

* 国家；
* 气候区；
* 地表材料；
* 油罐设计；
* 卫星传感器；
* 季节；
* 太阳几何条件；
* 复杂工业环境。

This limits the external validity and global scalability of the reported accuracy.

这限制了结果的外部有效性和全球推广能力。

---

### 5. Favourable geometric assumptions

### 5. 依赖较理想的几何条件

The method assumes:

* Relatively flat terrain;
* Vertical tank walls;
* Sufficient spacing between tanks;
* Complete shadows on visible ground;
* Limited interference from adjacent structures;
* Appropriate solar and satellite geometry.

方法假设：

* 地面相对平坦；
* 罐壁垂直；
* 油罐之间具有足够间距；
* 阴影完整落在可见地面；
* 相邻设施干扰较少；
* 太阳与卫星观测几何条件适合。

Dense refinery or port environments may violate these assumptions.

密集的炼厂或港区环境可能不满足这些条件。

---

### 6. Hough parameters are site-specific

### 6. Hough 参数具有场景依赖性

The Hough-circle parameters were selected through repeated experiments and were tuned to the size and resolution of the tanks in the study image.

Hough 圆检测参数通过多次实验确定，并针对研究影像中的油罐尺寸和空间分辨率进行调节。

Different AOIs, sensors or tank sizes would probably require parameter recalibration.

更换 AOI、卫星传感器或油罐尺寸后，很可能需要重新调整参数。

The Hough transform also failed to identify all 24 tanks.

Hough 变换也未能成功识别全部 24 个油罐。

---

### 7. No machine-learning model is implemented

### 7. 本文没有实际实施机器学习模型

The study relies on traditional image processing, geometric modelling and computer-vision algorithms.

本文依赖传统图像处理、几何建模和计算机视觉算法。

Machine learning is mentioned only as a possible future method for improving tank identification.

机器学习仅在未来研究中被提出，用于提高油罐识别率和半径估算精度。

---

### 8. Geometric capacity may differ from usable capacity

### 8. 几何容量可能不同于实际可用容量

The cylindrical formula does not account for:

* Safety margins;
* Maximum operating level;
* Unusable bottom volume;
* Internal equipment;
* Maintenance status;
* Temporarily inactive tanks.

圆柱体公式没有考虑：

* 安全余量；
* 最高运营液位；
* 底部不可用容积；
* 油罐内部设备；
* 维护状态；
* 临时停用油罐。

Thus, estimated geometric capacity should not automatically be interpreted as operational storage capacity.

因此，估算的几何容量不能直接等同于实际运营储存能力。

---

### 9. No oil-price forecasting evaluation

### 9. 未进行油价预测检验

The paper does not evaluate whether tank-capacity information improves forecasts of:

* Brent price;
* WTI price;
* Oil returns;
* Price direction;
* Volatility;
* Futures spreads.

本文没有检验油罐容量信息能否改善以下变量的预测：

* Brent 价格；
* WTI 价格；
* 石油收益率；
* 价格方向；
* 波动率；
* 期货价差。

It should therefore be treated as a measurement-method reference rather than direct oil-price forecasting evidence.

因此，本文应被视为遥感测量方法依据，而不是油价预测有效性的直接证据。

Use this paper to support the statement that:

> High-resolution optical remote sensing can recover physical characteristics of oil-storage infrastructure, including tank height, radius and nominal capacity.

不要使用本文支持以下论述：

## One-Sentence Takeaway / 一句话总结

### English

Wang et al. (2019) demonstrate that sub-metre optical imagery, HSV-based shadow segmentation, sub-pixel median shadow measurement and Hough circle detection can estimate the structural capacity of large oil tanks, providing a useful foundation for static infrastructure variables and capacity-weighted remote-sensing features, but not for direct measurement of current inventories or floating-roof fill levels.

### 中文

Wang 等（2019）证明了亚米级光学影像结合 HSV 阴影分割、亚像元阴影长度中位数法和 Hough 圆检测，可以估算大型油罐的结构容量；该方法适合为石油价格预测项目提供静态基础设施变量和容量加权依据，但不能直接测量当前库存或浮顶油罐充填率。
