### 1.6 Word count and editing strategy 字数与删改策略

- Official length: **10,000–12,000 words**.
  官方篇幅：**10,000–12,000 词**。
- Writing **fewer** than 10,000 is **not** penalised; writing **more than 12,000** receives a **penalty**.
  少于 10,000 **无罚分**；超过 12,000 有**罚分**。
- Aim closer to **10,000** rather than pushing toward the upper limit; **prefer fewer words rather than more**.
  目标宜接近 **10,000**，不要往上限堆字；**宁少勿多**。
- Adding words is only worthwhile if they say something meaningful.
  只有说出有意义内容时，加字才值得。
- Reader-centred editing principle: What would the reader be interested in? What information do they need in order to understand the argument?
  读者中心编辑原则：读者会对什么感兴趣？理解论证需要哪些信息？
- The literature review (~4,000 words discussed) may be too long relative to methods and results; **edit down**.
  讨论中提到文献综述约 4,000 词，相对方法与结果可能过长，应**删减**。
- Sentence-level editing check: What does this sentence say? What does it add? Is it necessary?
  句子自检：这句话在说什么？增加了什么？是否必要？
- On fusion / alternative methods in the literature review:
  关于文献综述中的融合 / 其他方法：
  - Focus on methods **most relevant to the chosen methodology**.
    聚焦与**所选方法最相关**的内容。
  - Other fusion strategies can be acknowledged in **one short sentence**, without detailed treatment of every model framework.
    其他融合策略可用**一两句**点到为止，不必详写每一种模型框架。
- **Appendix does not count** toward the word limit (figures and bibliography also excluded).
  **附录不计字数**（图表与参考文献也不计）。
- Appendix guidance:
  附录用法：
  - Include things referred to in the main text but not needed in full detail there (e.g. supplementary tables such as a GDP table, extra results).
    放正文中提到、但不需在正文展开的内容（例如 GDP 表等补充表、额外结果）。
  - Avoid adding unnecessary material just to fill the appendix.
    不要为了充实附录而堆无关材料。

### 1.13 Sentence-level writing feedback 句子层面的写作反馈

- Make sure sentences make sense to the reader; prefer **simple sentences**.
  确保句子对读者通顺；优先使用**简单句**。
- Avoid overusing commas `,`, colons `:`, and semicolons `;`; heavy punctuation makes sentences harder to read.
  少用逗号、冒号、分号；标点过密会更难读。
- Specific colon feedback: a colon normally introduces a **list**. If the following text is not a list, do not use `:` that way; split the sentence or use a comma / full stop instead.
  关于冒号的具体反馈：冒号通常用来**引出列表**。若后面不是列表，不要这样用 `:`；应拆句，或改用逗号 / 句号。
- On terminology such as “error” vs specific metric names (RMSE, etc.):
  关于 “error” 与具体指标名（RMSE 等）：
  - Using the general word **error** is acceptable when discussing incorrect prediction in a broad sense.
    在泛谈预测偏差时，使用通用词 **error** 可以。
  - But confusing sentences still need rewriting so the meaning is explicit.
    但含糊句子仍需改写，使含义明确。
- Example discussed: distinguishing **population predictability** from **forecast ability**.
  讨论示例：区分 **population predictability（总体可预测性）** 与 **forecast ability（预测能力）**。
  - These are two different properties.
    这是两个不同属性。
  - Intended meaning (as clarified): an economic variable may be related to future oil prices without necessarily producing lower forecast errors; adding variables may reduce in-sample error without guaranteeing good out-of-sample / future prediction; reducing model error does not necessarily capture future oil-market volatility.
    澄清后的本意：经济变量可能与未来油价相关，却不一定带来更低预测误差；增加变量可能降低样本内误差，但不保证样本外 / 未来预测仍好；降低模型误差也不等于抓住未来油市波动。
  - First think clearly what is meant, then rewrite in plain language.
    先想清楚要说什么，再用平实语言改写。
- Acronyms (CNN, GCN, etc.):
  缩写（CNN、GCN 等）：
  - **Spell out in full the first time**, ideally with the citation to the source paper, then use the acronym if used regularly.
    **首次完整拼出**，最好同时给出来源论文引用，之后若常用再用缩写。
  - If the term is rare, prefer spelling it out each time.
    若很少出现，宜每次写全称。
