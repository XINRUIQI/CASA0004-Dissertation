Sentence structure
Work on making sure the sentences make sense to the reader. Avoid using too many ',' or ':', or ';'. Simple sentences are easier to read. 

Policy implications 
This can relate to the context, and political context more broadly rather than one specific government policy. Think about the current global situation, how is conflict affecting current oil prices and international trade? Why might having a better model of oil prices be useful for this? How might a government use such a model? 
If you're unsure about the policy implications I suggest reading some news items - UCL gives you free access to the financial times which could be a good place to start.

Word count 
Length should be 10,000-12,000 words. I would aim for less words rather than more, you might need to edit sections you have already written, for example the literature review. Think about what information is most useful for a reader. 
If worried about the word count focus only on the methodologies which are relevant to your chosen method. 

Story vs. technical 
The methodology is important - should be clear enough that someone else can recreate your work. 
However the story is what makes it interesting to the reader - why should the person reading it care about your research question? 
The easiest way to address this is by providing lots of context in the introduction - what is the current situation with oil prices? And in the discussion cover what your findings contribute to this.
